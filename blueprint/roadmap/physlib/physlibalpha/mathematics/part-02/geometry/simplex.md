> Simplices as convex hulls of affinely independent points, and the standard simplex.

## i. Overview

> A simplex is the convex hull of finitely many affinely independent points, its extreme points. Every
> point of a simplex is a mixture of the extreme points with unique weights, its barycentric
> coordinates. The model is the standard simplex of probability vectors in `ι → ℝ`, whose extreme
> points are the standard basis vectors. Being a simplex is invariant under affine equivalences.

## ii. Key results

> - `IsSimplex` states that a convex set is generated by finitely many affinely independent extreme
>   points.
> - `IsSimplex.eq_of_affineCombination_eq` proves that the weights are unique.
> - `affineIndependent_of_convexCombination_eq` proves that unique weights give affine independence.
> - `stdSimplexSet` is the standard simplex in `ι → ℝ`.
> - `extremePoints_stdSimplexSet` proves that its extreme points are the standard basis vectors.
> - `isSimplex_of_affineEquiv_stdSimplexSet` proves that a set affinely equivalent to a standard
>   simplex is a simplex.
