# The trace pairing is surjective

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.WStarAlgebra.TracePairingSurjectivity`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/WStarAlgebra/TracePairingSurjectivity.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/WStarAlgebra/TracePairingSurjectivity.lean): 20 declarations, 7 with a docstring.

> Every continuous functional on the trace-class operators is a trace pairing `Tr (A ·)`.

## i. Overview

> A continuous functional on the trace-class operators defines a bounded sesquilinear form by
> evaluation on rank-one operators, and the Riesz theorem turns it into a bounded operator `A`. The
> functional and `Tr (A ·)` agree on rank-one operators, whose span is dense: every trace-class
> operator is approximated by finite truncations of its Hilbert–Schmidt factorization. So every
> continuous functional is a trace pairing.

## ii. Key results

> - `TraceClass.sesquilinearFormOfFunctional` : the sesquilinear form of a functional.
> - `TraceClass.rankOneSpan_dense` : rank-one operators span a dense subspace.
> - `TraceClass.tracePairing_surjective_concrete` : every continuous functional on the trace class is
>   a trace pairing.

> The density theorem is obtained by truncating a Hilbert–Schmidt factor in a Hilbert basis. The
