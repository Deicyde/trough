# Implications between selected laws

We collect here some notable implications between the selected laws in [Selected laws](../selected-laws/README.md). By [Birkhoff's completeness theorem](../basic-theory-of-magmas/sound-complete.md), every implication can basically be established by a finite number of rewrites. In most cases, the sequence of rewrites is quite straightforward, and the implication is very easy, but we record some less obvious examples.

- [Sheffer stroke axiom](sheffer.md)

A *natural central groupoid* is, up to isomorphism, a magma with carrier $S \times S$ for some set $S$ and operation

$$
(a,b) \diamond (c,d) = (b,c).
$$

These are examples of central groupoids, [E168](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?168) $({x = (y \diamond x) \diamond (x \diamond z)})$.
