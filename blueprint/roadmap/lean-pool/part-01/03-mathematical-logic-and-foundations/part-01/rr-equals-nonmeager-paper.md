# The nonmeagre lower bound for the rearrangement number

> Formalizes the inequality non(M) ≤ rr in ZFC via relational systems, category and slalom bounds, finite-vector constructions, and rearrangements of conditionally convergent real series. The reverse inequality from the accompanying manuscript is outside the upstream Lean formalization.
