# Verified Stallings folding for finitely generated subgroups of F2

> Constructs a finite inverse automaton recognizing the subgroup generated by any finite list of signed words in the rank-two free group. Proves exact subgroup preservation and correctness of canonical reduced-word membership testing. Reusable infrastructure includes inverse-automaton walks, reduction-compatible traversal, finite fold congruences, coset potentials, flower graphs, and basis roses. Folding uses exhaustive finite search over Boolean relations; no complexity bound, core pruning, subgroup basis, intersection, or index algorithm is claimed.
