## Lucchini's coprime generation bound (Kourovka 16.53)

The statement that for every finite
group $G$, all subgroups $A$ and $B$ of $G$ of coprime orders that together generate $G$, and every
natural number $d$, if each of $A$ and $B$ is generated by at most $d$ elements, then $G$ is
generated by at most $d + 1$ elements.

Formalized by `MathlibExt.GroupTheory.CoprimeGenerationRankKourovka16Problem53Wanted.coprimeGeneratedRankBound`, a definition declared in [WantedExt/GroupTheory/CoprimeGenerationRankKourovka16Problem53Wanted.lean, line 39](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/WantedExt/GroupTheory/CoprimeGenerationRankKourovka16Problem53Wanted.lean#L39). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/GroupTheory/generation/coprime-generation/index.html#coprime-generated-rank-bound).

## Lucchini's coprime generation bound fails

There exist a finite group $G$, subgroups $A$ and
$B$ of $G$ of coprime orders that together generate $G$, and a natural number $d$ such that each
of $A$ and $B$ is generated by at most $d$ elements, but no set of at most $d + 1$ elements
generates $G$. That is, Kourovka Problem 16.53 has a negative answer.

Muliarchyk constructs such groups for every $d \ge 2$, with $A$ and $B$ each needing exactly $d$
generators and $G$ needing exactly $d + 2$; there $A$ and $B$ are metabelian of exponents $6$ and
$55$ and lie inside a finite power of $\mathrm{PSL}_2(11)$.
