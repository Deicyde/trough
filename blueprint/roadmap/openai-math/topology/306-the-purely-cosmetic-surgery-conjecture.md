## Purely cosmetic surgery on knots in the three-sphere

We prove the purely cosmetic surgery conjecture for knots in the three-sphere. Distinct Dehn surgery slopes on a nontrivial smooth knot never give orientation-preservingly homeomorphic manifolds.
