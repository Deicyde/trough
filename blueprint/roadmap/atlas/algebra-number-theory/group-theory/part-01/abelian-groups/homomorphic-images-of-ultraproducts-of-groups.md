## Ultraproduct kernel

For an ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ and a sequence
$(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of groups, the normal subgroup of $\prod_i G_i$ generated by the
sequences $(g_i)_i$ with $\{i : g_i = 1\} \in U$. These sequences already form a normal subgroup,
so the kernel consists of exactly them.

## Ultraproduct of a sequence of groups

For an ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ and a sequence
$(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of groups, the ultraproduct $\left(\prod_i G_i\right)/U$ is the quotient
of the direct product $\prod_i G_i$ by the ultraproduct kernel, the normal subgroup of sequences
$(g_i)_i$ with $\{i : g_i = 1\} \in U$.

## Kourovka Problem 20.13(a) (Bergman)

The proposition that for every abelian group $A$, every
sequence $(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of (not necessarily abelian) groups and every nonprincipal
ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ (one containing every cofinite set), if there is a surjective
homomorphism from the ultraproduct $\left(\prod_i G_i\right)/U$ onto $A$, then there are a sequence
$(H_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of abelian groups and a nonprincipal ultrafilter $V$ on $\mathbb{N}$
with a surjective homomorphism from $\left(\prod_i H_i\right)/V$ onto $A$.

## Abelian images of ultraproducts are images of abelian ultraproducts

Let $A$ be an abelian
group, let $(G_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a sequence of (not necessarily abelian) groups, and let $U$
be a nonprincipal ultrafilter on $\mathbb{N}$, that is, one containing every cofinite set. If there
is a surjective homomorphism from the ultraproduct $\left(\prod_i G_i\right)/U$ onto $A$, then there
are a sequence $(H_i)_{i \in \mathbb{N}}$ of abelian groups and a nonprincipal ultrafilter $V$ on
$\mathbb{N}$ with a surjective homomorphism from $\left(\prod_i H_i\right)/V$ onto $A$.

## Abelian images of products of finite groups

Let $A$ be an abelian group. If $A$ is a
homomorphic image of a direct product $\prod_{i \in I} G_i$ of an infinite family $(G_i)_{i \in I}$
of finite (not necessarily abelian) groups, then $A$ is a homomorphic image of the direct product
$\prod_{j \in J} H_j$ of some family $(H_j)_{j \in J}$ of finite abelian groups.

Corson proves more: such an $A$ is a homomorphic image of a direct product of finite cyclic groups.

## Groups locally an image of the ultrapower $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$

Let $U$ be an
ultrafilter on $\mathbb{N}$ and $B$ a group. Then $B$ is locally an image of the ultrapower
$\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$ when every element $b \in B$ lies in a subgroup $H$ of $B$ that is a
homomorphic image of $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$: there is a surjective homomorphism $\psi$ from
the additive group $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}$ of integer sequences onto $H$ such that $\psi(f) = 1$
for every sequence $f$ with $f_i = 0$ for $U$-almost every $i$.

## Bergman's question on images of countable ultraproducts (Kourovka 21.12)

There is a
nonprincipal ultrafilter $U$ on $\mathbb{N}$ such that every group $B$ that is locally an image of
the ultrapower $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$ (every element of $B$ lies in a subgroup of $B$ that is
a homomorphic image of $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$) is a homomorphic image of an ultraproduct
$(\prod_{i \in \mathbb{N}} G_i)/U$: there are groups $G_i$, $i \in \mathbb{N}$, and a surjective
homomorphism $\varphi \colon \prod_i G_i \to B$ such that $\varphi(f) = 1$ for every family $f$
with $f_i = 1$ for $U$-almost every $i$.

The question is posed for a fixed nonprincipal $U$; it is stated here for some $U$, so that its
refutation covers every $U$.

## Bergman's question 21.12 has a negative answer

For every nonprincipal ultrafilter $U$
on $\mathbb{N}$ there is a group $B$ that is locally an image of the ultrapower
$\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$ (every element of $B$ lies in a subgroup of $B$ that is a homomorphic
image of $\mathbb{Z}^{\mathbb{N}}/U$) but is not a homomorphic image of any ultraproduct
$(\prod_{i \in \mathbb{N}} G_i)/U$ of groups: for no groups $G_i$, $i \in \mathbb{N}$, is there a
surjective homomorphism $\varphi \colon \prod_i G_i \to B$ with $\varphi(f) = 1$ for every family
$f$ such that $f_i = 1$ for $U$-almost every $i$.

Corson shows that the abelian group $\bigoplus_n C_n$, with $C_n$ finite cyclic of unbounded
orders, is such a group for every nonprincipal $U$: it is not a homomorphic image of any
nonprincipal countable ultraproduct. The Kourovka Notebook marks 21.12 solved ("No, it need not").
