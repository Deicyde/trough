> Builds the finite-dimensional standard module `V(n)` and the actions of an `sl₂` triple.

## i. Overview

> The standard highest-weight module of weight `n` has underlying space `Fin (n + 1) → K`.
> Explicit endomorphisms act on its coordinate vectors by the usual `sl₂` raising, lowering,
> and weight formulas. The bracket relations extend this action from the three generators to
> the Lie subalgebra generated by any `IsSl2Triple`.

## ii. Key results

> - `StandardSl2Module` and its coordinate vectors give the underlying vector space.
> - `ρE`, `ρF`, and `ρH` are the standard raising, lowering, and weight operators.
> - `Φ` maps a generated `sl₂` subalgebra to the endomorphisms of the standard module.
