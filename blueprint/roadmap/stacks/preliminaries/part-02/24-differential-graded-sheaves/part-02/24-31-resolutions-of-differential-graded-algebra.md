## Tag 0FU1, a lemma

[Tag 0FU1](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FU1) in the Stacks Project.

**`SheafOfModules.freeDGAlgebra_colimit_isGoodToposBaseChange`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/FreeDifferentialGradedAlgebraGood.lean, line 241](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/FreeDifferentialGradedAlgebraGood.lean#L241).

> **Tag 0FU1.** *"In the situation above the differential graded `O`-algebra `A = colim Aᵢ` has
> the following property: for any morphism `(f, f^♯) : (Sh(C'), O') ⟶ (Sh(C), O)` of ringed topoi,
> the pullback `f^*A` is flat as a graded `O'`-module and is K-flat as a complex of
> `O'`-modules."*
>
> The situation: a family `𝒮` of sheaves of graded sets indexed by a nonempty well-ordered set of
> levels (the source's `S₀, S₁, …`, indexed by `ℕ`), and a derivation datum `δ` on the free graded
> `O`-algebra `A = O⟨⨿ᵢ Sᵢ⟩` whose values are closed (`hδ`) and triangular (`htri`: the value on a
> generator of level `k` lies, locally, in the subalgebra generated by the letters of level `< k`,
> which is the source's `δₖ : Sₖ ⟶ A_{k-1}`), with the Leibniz extension of `δ` as differential.
>
> The first conjunct is `A = colim Aᵢ`: `A` is the colimit of the stages `Aᵢ`, the subcomplexes
> spanned by the words in the letters of level at most `i`. The second is the property: `A`,
> regarded as a right differential graded module over `(O, 0)`, is good in the sense of Tag 0FSA,
> that is flat as a graded `O`-module, K-flat as a complex of `O`-modules, and the same for its
> pullback along every morphism of ringed topoi.

```lean
theorem freeDGAlgebra_colimit_isGoodToposBaseChange :
    Nonempty (IsColimit (stagesCocone 𝒮 δ hδ htri)) ∧
      IsGoodToposBaseChange.{u, v₁, u₁}
      (toUnitModule (Osharp := X.O) (freeDGAlgebra (Osharp := X.O) 𝒮 δ hδ))
```

## Tag 0FU2, a lemma

[Tag 0FU2](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FU2) in the Stacks Project.

**`SheafOfModules.exists_good_quasiIso_differentialGradedAlgebra`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/DifferentialGradedAlgebraGoodResolution.lean, line 104](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/DifferentialGradedAlgebraGoodResolution.lean#L104).

> **Tag 0FU2.** *"Let `(C, O)` be a ringed site. Let `(B, d)` be a differential graded
> `O`-algebra. There exists a quasi-isomorphism of differential graded `O`-algebras
> `(A, d) ⟶ (B, d)` such that `A` is graded flat and K-flat as a complex of `O`-modules and such
> that the same is true after pullback by any morphism of ringed topoi."*
>
> The homomorphism is unital; graded flatness, K-flatness and their persistence after pullback are
> the goodness of Tag 0FSA of `A` over `(O, 0)`.

```lean
theorem exists_good_quasiIso_differentialGradedAlgebra
    (B : DifferentialGradedAlgebra.{u, u} (toRingCat Y.J Y.O)) :
    ∃ (A : DifferentialGradedAlgebra.{u, u} (toRingCat Y.J Y.O)) (f : A ⟶ B),
      (∀ U : Y.Cᵒᵖ, (f.toHomologicalComplex.f 0).val.app U (A.one.eval U) = B.one.eval U) ∧
        QuasiIso f.toHomologicalComplex ∧
          IsGoodToposBaseChange.{u, v₁, u₁}
            (DifferentialGradedAlgebra.toUnitModule (Osharp := Y.O) A)
```
