# Artin's induction theorem with its induced-character-span target

Over a
algebraically closed field in which `|G|` is invertible (in particular, in characteristic zero),
`|G| • f` belongs to the additive subgroup generated by characters induced from irreducible
characters of cyclic subgroups.

```lean
TauCeti.ClassFunction.natCard_nsmul_mem_indCharacterSpanInt_isCyclic.{u, v} {k : Type u}
  {G : Type v} [Field k] [Group G] [Finite G] [IsAlgClosed k] [Invertible ↑(Nat.card G)] {f : G → k}
  (hf : f ∈ TauCeti.virtualCharacters k G) :
  Nat.card G • f ∈ TauCeti.ClassFunction.indCharacterSpanInt k G fun C => IsCyclic ↥C
```
