# The spectral measure of a unitary representation of an abelian group

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.Representation.AbelianSpectral`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/Representation/AbelianSpectral.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/Representation/AbelianSpectral.lean): 16 declarations, 6 with a docstring.

> A unitary representation of a discrete abelian group on a Hilbert space is the integral of the
> characters of the group against a projection-valued measure on the Pontryagin dual.

## i. Overview

> The unitary operators `π(g)` of a representation of an abelian group commute and are closed under
> adjoints, so they have a joint spectral measure on the character space of the C⋆-algebra they
> generate. A character of that algebra restricts to a unitary character of the group, which gives
> a map from the character space to the Pontryagin dual of the group. Pushing the joint spectral
> measure forward along this map gives a projection-valued measure `P` on the dual with
> `π(g) = ∫ χ(g) dP(χ)`. Every bounded operator commuting with the representation commutes with `P`.

## ii. Key results

> - `UnitaryRep.family` : the commuting ⋆-family of the operators of the representation.
> - `UnitaryRep.spectralMeasure` : the projection-valued measure on the Pontryagin dual.
> - `UnitaryRep.boundedIntegral_character` : `π(g) = ∫ χ(g) dP(χ)`.
> - `UnitaryRep.spectralMeasure_commute` : `P` commutes with every operator commuting with `π`.
