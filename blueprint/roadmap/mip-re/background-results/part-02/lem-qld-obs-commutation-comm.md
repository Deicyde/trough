# Signed commutation, the commuting case

Under the hypotheses of Lemma [Win implications](lem-qld-win.md), with the first player's measurements projective, summed over the *commuting* tuples with the uniform weight $\left\vert {{\mathcal{C}}} \right\vert^{-1}$,

$$
\sum_{\omega \,:\, \gamma(\omega) = 0} \frac{1}{\left\vert {{\mathcal{C}}} \right\vert}
\left\| {\bigl( [X^{r_X}(u_X),\, Z^{r_Z}(u_Z)] \bigr) \otimes {I}_\textsc{B} |\psi\rangle} \right\|^2
\;\leq\; 9519168\, \varepsilon\;.
$$

## Depends on

- [Win implications](lem-qld-win.md)

## Proof

Items 5, 4 and 1 chain each point probe to a *marginal* of the $\mathtt{Pair}$ measurement on the other side: three links at $172\varepsilon$ each, so $1548\varepsilon$ after a three-term triangle inequality. The first player's $\mathtt{Pair}$ measurement is projective, and so is its reading as the pair $(\beta_X, \beta_Z)$, a coarse-graining of it, whose marginals are the two one-basis readings; that is the projective measurement Lemma [Commutation analysis](../../foundations/part-02/lem-commutation-analysis.md) needs, and the lemma applies. Then [(3)](../../foundations/part-02/README.md)'s commutator form with Lemma [Consistency of the point observables](lem-qld-obs-consistency.md)'s probe carries the conclusion to the observables. The constant is $12 \cdot 344 + 12 \cdot 344 + 768 \cdot (2 \cdot 1548 + 2 \cdot 1548 + 72 \cdot 86)$; its last term is the inconsistency of the two players' $\mathtt{Pair}$ measurements, $86\varepsilon$ at the Born level by item 1, which no step now needs and which is kept as slack.
