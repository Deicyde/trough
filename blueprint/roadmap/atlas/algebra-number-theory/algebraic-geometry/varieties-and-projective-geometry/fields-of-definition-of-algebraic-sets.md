## Ideal over $k$ of a subset of affine space

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure
$\bar k$, and let $Z \subseteq \bar k^n$. The ideal $I_k(Z)$ of $k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ consists of
the polynomials $f$ with coefficients in $k$ whose image in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ vanishes at
every point of $Z$; that is, $I_k(Z)$ is the preimage of the vanishing ideal of $Z$ in
$\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ under the coefficient map
$k[X_1, \dots, X_n] \to \bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.

## Membership in the ideal over $k$

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure $\bar k$, let
$Z \subseteq \bar k^n$, and let $f \in k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$. Then $f$ lies in the ideal $I_k(Z)$
over $k$ of $Z$ if and only if the image of $f$ in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ under the coefficient
map lies in the vanishing ideal of $Z$, that is, vanishes at every point of $Z$.

## Sets defined over $k$

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure $\bar k$, let
$Z \subseteq \bar k^n$, and let $I_k(Z)$ be the ideal of polynomials in $k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ whose
image in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ vanishes at every point of $Z$. The set $Z$ is defined over $k$
if the vanishing ideal of $Z$ in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ equals the ideal generated by the image
of $I_k(Z)$ under the coefficient map $k[X_1, \dots, X_n] \to \bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.

## Defined over $k$ as an equality of ideals

## Polynomials in the ideal over $k$ vanish on the set

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic
closure $\bar k$, let $Z \subseteq \bar k^n$, and let $f \in I_k(Z)$. Then for every point
$P \in Z$, the image of $f$ in $\bar k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$ evaluated at $P$ is $0$.

## The ideal over $k$ of the empty set

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure $\bar k$.
For every $n$, the ideal $I_k(\emptyset)$ of the empty subset of $\bar k^n$ is all of
$k[X_1, \dots, X_n]$.

## The ideal over $k$ reverses inclusions

Let $k$ be a field with algebraic closure
$\bar k$. If $Z_1 \subseteq Z_2 \subseteq \bar k^n$, then $I_k(Z_2) \subseteq I_k(Z_1)$.
