# TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear

A graded projective resolution is **linear** if its term in homological degree `n` is
generated by its homogeneous piece of internal degree `n`.

```lean
TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution.IsLinear.{uk, uA, uM, u_1} {k : Type uk} {A : Type uA}
  [CommRing k] [Ring A] [Algebra k A] {𝒜 : ℤ → Submodule k A} {M : Type uM} [AddCommGroup M]
  [_root_.Module k M] [_root_.Module A M] {G : TauCeti.InternalGrading k M ℤ}
  (r : TauCeti.GradedProjectiveResolution 𝒜 G) : Prop
```
