# isPathConnected-aux

The set $\{s\in \mathbb{C}\mid \Re(s)>0 ∧ s \ne 1\}$ is path-connected.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

Construct explicit paths from $2$ to any point, either a line segment or two joined ones.
