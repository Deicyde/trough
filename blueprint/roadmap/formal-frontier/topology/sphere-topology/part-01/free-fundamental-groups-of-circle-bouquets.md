# Free fundamental groups of circle bouquets

For any label type I and independent ambient and label universes, FreeGroup I is multiplicatively equivalent to the fundamental group of the point-wide-pushout CircleBouquet.space I at its common point, sending each free generator to its included counterclockwise complex-unit-circle loop, including for empty I.
