# FreyPackage

A *Frey package* $(a,b,c,p)$ is three nonzero pairwise-coprime integers $a$, $b$, $c$, with $a\equiv3\pmod4$ and $b\equiv0\pmod2$, and a prime $p\geq5$, such that $a^p+b^p=c^p$.
