# Frey

Given a Frey package $(a,b,c,p)$, the corresponding *Frey curve* (considered by Frey and, before him, Hellegouarch) is the elliptic curve over $\mathbb{Q}$ defined by the equation $Y^2=X(X-a^p)(X+b^p).$
