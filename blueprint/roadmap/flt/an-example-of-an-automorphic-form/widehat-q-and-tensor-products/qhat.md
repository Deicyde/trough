# QHat

The profinite completion $\widehat{\mathbb{Q}}$ of $\mathbb{Q}$ is the tensor product $\mathbb{Q}\otimes_{\mathbb{Z}}\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$, or $\widehat{\mathbb{Q}}=\mathbb{Q}\otimes\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$ for short.
