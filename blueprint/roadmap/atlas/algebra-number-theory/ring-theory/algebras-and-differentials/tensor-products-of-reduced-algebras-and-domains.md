## Reduced algebras stay reduced under base change over a perfect field

Tensoring a reduced algebra with a field extension over a perfect field
preserves reducedness.

## Tensor products of domains over an algebraically closed field

Over an algebraically closed field, the tensor product of a finite-type
domain with a domain is a domain.

Formalized by `Algebra.TensorProduct.isDomain_of_isAlgClosed_of_finiteType`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Algebra/TensorProduct/Domain.lean, line 93](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Algebra/TensorProduct/Domain.lean#L93). ## Field-with-domain tensor products over an algebraically closed field

domain is a domain.
