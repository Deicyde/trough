# The formal total derivative on the gauge-boson jet algebra

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.JetDeriv`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra/JetDeriv.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra/JetDeriv.lean): 25 declarations (1 instance with no name), 15 with a docstring.

> The total spacetime derivative `∂_ν` as a derivation of the gauge-boson jet algebra.

## i. Overview

> The formal total spacetime derivative extends from the component functions of the gauge
> bosons to their whole jet algebra as a derivation. It is
> `SymmetricAlgebra.derivationOfLinear` applied to the shift `∂_s A_μ^φ ↦ ∂_{s + {ν}} A_μ^φ`
> on the field-derivative space, which is right multiplication by the derivative symbol `∂_ν`
> on the `SpaceTimeDerivAlgebraℝ` factor.
>
> The four directional derivatives commute and iterate along a multiset of directions. The
> jet algebra is generated by the gauge fields and their iterated derivatives, and the
> derivative extends to the complexification `ℂ ⊗[ℝ] LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra` by base change, where
> the ambient Lagrangian theory uses it.

## ii. Key results

> - `GaugeBoson.FieldDerivSpace.jetDeriv` : the derivative shift on the component space.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.jetDeriv` : the formal total derivative, a derivation.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.iteratedJetDeriv` : the iterated derivative along a multiset.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.iteratedJetDeriv_ofA` : `∂_s A_μ^φ` as a generator.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.adjoin_iteratedJetDeriv_eq_top` : the algebra is generated by the
>   gauge fields and their derivatives.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.complexJetDeriv` : the derivative on the complexification.

> The jet algebra is generated by the gauge fields and their derivatives. Every
