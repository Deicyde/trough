# Weighted energy

Let $\Delta\subseteq \widehat{G}$ and $m\geq 1$. Let $\nu:G\to \mathbb{C}$. Then

$$
E_{2m}(\Delta;\nu)=\sum_{\gamma_1,\ldots,\gamma_{2m}\in \Delta}\left\lvert \widehat{\nu}(\gamma_1+\cdots-\gamma_{2m})\right\rvert.
$$
