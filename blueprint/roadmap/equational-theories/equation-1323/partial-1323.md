# Partial solution

A *partial solution* is a finite family ${\mathcal F}$ of tuples $(x,y,z,a,b,c) \in (\mathbb{Q}^\times)^3 \times G^6$ with $a,b,c,0$ distinct, such that the tuples $(\phi_a(b)^n x,a, \phi_b(c)^n y,b)$, $(\phi_b(c)^n y,b,\phi_c(a)^n z,c)$, $(\phi_c(a)^n z,c,\phi_a(b)^{n+1} x, a)$ for $(x,y,z,a,b,c) \in {\mathcal F}$ and $n \geq 0$ are all distinct.
