# Exact Pauli operators

From the simultaneous measurement one constructs, for $W \in \{X, Z\}$, measurements and binary observables on the expanded space which satisfy the Pauli group relations *exactly* — linearity in the argument, and $XZ$ commutation or anticommutation with the phase $(-1)^{\operatorname{tr}(e e' (\tilde{u} \cdot \tilde{v}))}$ at the two combining field elements — and which are $\delta$-close to the strategy's $(\mathtt{Point}, W)$ measurements on the expanded state. The Lean proves it for a simultaneous pair measurement of a projective strategy in any bipartite model; the closeness is stated on the physical model of Definition [The models of stages 4 and 5](def-qld-physical-model.md), for the pair measurement on its first cut and the strategy's measurements with the registers inert. The statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Depends on

- [The simultaneous global measurement](lem-qld-simultaneous.md)
- [The marginals track the point measurements](lem-qld-helper.md)
- [Win implications](../part-02/lem-qld-win.md)
- [Schwartz–Zippel](../part-05/lem-schwartz-zippel.md)
- [The models of stages 4 and 5](def-qld-physical-model.md)

## Proof

Exactness of the linearity comes from projectivity of the simultaneous measurement together with the group law of the Pauli basis; Theorem [Exact linearity from approximate linearity](../part-02/thm-linearity.md) is *not* used here. The remaining ingredient bounds the mass the simultaneous measurement places on non-multilinear outcomes, via Lemma [Schwartz–Zippel](../part-05/lem-schwartz-zippel.md) applied in the correct direction — distinct polynomials of degree $md$ *agree* on at most an $md/q$ fraction, so it is the agreement and not the disagreement that the bound controls (Remark [What stage 1.2 leaves open, and where its weight is](../README.md)).
