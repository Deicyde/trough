# Sendov's conjecture and the Phelps–Rodriguez conjecture

> Proves Sendov's conjecture in full generality: if every zero of a complex polynomial of degree at least two lies in the closed unit disk, then every zero has a critical point within distance one. Also proves the Phelps–Rodriguez strengthening, with strict inequality unless the zero lies on the unit circle and the polynomial is a scalar multiple of z^n - a^n, and recovers Rubinstein's boundary theorem. Formalizes Tao's digestion of Lech Mazur's proof: the problem is reduced to a numerical inequality in the degree and one real parameter, settled by exact rational Bernstein certificates for degrees up to one hundred and by an analytic large-degree reduction beyond. Includes the top case of Maclaurin's inequality and multiset AM–GM, a defect inequality on the closed unit disk, a sharp bound on log(sinh h / h), kernel-checked polynomial certificate infrastructure, and a packed-exponentiation bridge for exact moment integrals.
