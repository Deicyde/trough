# exists_integrable_exp_sq_of_map_rotation_eq_self

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on $F$ such that $\mu \times \mu$ is invariant under the rotation of angle $-\frac{\pi}{4}$. Then there exists $C > 0$ such that the function $x \mapsto \exp (C \Vert x \Vert ^ 2)$ is integrable with respect to $\mu$.
