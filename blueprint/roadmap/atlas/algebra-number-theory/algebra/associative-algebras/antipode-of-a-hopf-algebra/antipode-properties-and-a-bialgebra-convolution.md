## Algebra homomorphisms from a Hopf algebra are convolution units

## Convolution inverse of an algebra homomorphism via the antipode

Every algebra homomorphism from a possibly noncommutative Hopf algebra to a commutative
algebra has a convolution
inverse given by precomposition with the antipode.

Formalized by `HopfAlgebra.exists_comp_antipode_convMul_eq_one`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Algebra/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Antipode.lean, line 197](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/Algebra/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Antipode.lean#L197). ## Convolution-power criterion for bialgebras

A convolution power is trivial on all points iff it is trivial on the
universal point.

Formalized by `Bialgebra.forall_withConv_pow_eq_one_iff_toConv_id_pow_eq_one`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Algebra/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Antipode.lean, line 234](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/Algebra/Algebra/HopfAlgebra/Antipode.lean#L234).
