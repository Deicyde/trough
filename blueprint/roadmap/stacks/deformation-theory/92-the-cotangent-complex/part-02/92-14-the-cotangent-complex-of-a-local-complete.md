The attribute's comment: “the lemma for a general base ring A and a general polynomial algebra B over it with an augmentation B → A (the letters A and B are swapped relative to the source): the source's map Z[x\_1,...,x\_n] -> Z sending every x\_i to 0 is the case A = Z, B = Z[x\_1,...,x\_n], spelled out as `CommAlgCat.nonempty_cotangentComplex_mvPolynomial_int_iso_single`, which also proves that the kernel of that map is the source's ideal I = (x\_1,...,x\_n); I/I^2 is the conormal module of the augmentation. NOTHING is carried: the triangle the source's proof invokes is Proposition 08QX for the tower A -> B -> A, `CommAlgCat.productFundamentalTriangle_distinguished`, which holds for arbitrary ring maps — the triangle of Tag 08SD needs the middle map injective, whereas the augmentation B → A is surjective, so it is the proposition and not Tag 08SD that applies”.

> **Stacks Project, Tag 08SI.** Let `B` be a polynomial algebra over `A` with an augmentation
> `B → A`, and let `I` be its kernel. Then `L_{A/B}` is isomorphic in `D(A)` to `I/I²` placed in
> cohomological degree `-1`.
>
> This is `CommAlgCat.nonempty_cotangentComplex_retraction_iso_single` with the fundamental
> triangle of the tower `A → B → A` supplied by `CommAlgCat.hasFundamentalTriangleAt`.

```lean
theorem nonempty_cotangentComplex_retraction_iso_single_unconditional
    (hB : Algebra.IsPolynomialAlgebra A B) :
    Nonempty (DerivedCategory.Q.obj (cotangentComplex B (CommAlgCat.of B A)) ≅
      (DerivedCategory.singleFunctor (ModuleCat.{u} ↥(CommAlgCat.of B A)) (-1)).obj
        (ModuleCat.of ↥(CommAlgCat.of B A)
          (surjectionExtension B (CommAlgCat.of B A)
            (surjective_algebraMap_of_isScalarTower_self A B)).Cotangent))
```

theorem nonempty_cotangentComplex_iso_single_of_isKoszulRegular_sequence_unconditional

## Tag 08SK, a lemma

The attribute's comment: “the lemma in full: A -> B surjective with kernel I a Koszul-regular ideal (Tag 07CV (2), `Ideal.IsKoszulRegular`: at every prime containing I, some principal localization A\_g has I A\_g generated by a Koszul-regular sequence). The conclusion is an isomorphism in D(B) between L\_\{B/A} (the cotangent complex of the standard resolution, Tag 08PN) and I/I^2 placed in cohomological degree -1, that is I/I^2[1]; I/I^2 is the conormal module of the surjection, as the cotangent module of the corresponding algebra extension. NOTHING is carried: the hypotheses are the source's two. The localization lemma Tag 08SF is proved at every principal localization A\_g -> B\_g by `CommAlgCat.hasLocalizedCotangentComplexAt`, from `CommAlgCat.nonempty_extendScalars_cotangentComplex_iso_localization`; Proposition 08QX at the towers A\_g -> P -> A\_g that Tag 08SJ uses is `CommAlgCat.hasFundamentalTriangleAt`, from `CommAlgCat.productFundamentalTriangle_distinguished`. Everything else is proved outright: the Koszul cover generates the unit ideal, the localized map is surjective with kernel the localized ideal, Tag 08SJ at it, the flatness of B -> B\_g, and the Zariski descent of the vanishing of H^i(L\_\{B/A}) for i <= -2”.

> **Stacks Project, Tag 08SK.** Let `A → B` be a surjective map of commutative rings whose
> kernel `I` is a Koszul-regular ideal, Tag 07CV (2). Then `L_{B/A}` is quasi-isomorphic to
> `I/I²[1]`, that is, isomorphic in `D(B)` to the conormal module `I/I²` placed in cohomological
> degree `-1`.
>
> The proof is the source's: locally on `Spec(A)` the ideal `I` is generated by a Koszul-regular
> sequence, so [Stacks, Tag 08SJ] at the localizations `A_g → B_g` and the localization lemma
> [Stacks, Tag 08SF] give `H^i(L_{B/A}) = 0` for `i ≤ -2`, whence the identification with
> `I/I²[1]`. It is `CommAlgCat.nonempty_cotangentComplex_iso_single_of_isKoszulRegular` with its two
> inputs supplied by `CommAlgCat.hasLocalizedCotangentComplexAt` (Tag 08SF) and
> `CommAlgCat.hasFundamentalTriangleAt` (Proposition 08QX), so the hypotheses are the source's
> own two.

theorem nonempty_cotangentComplex_iso_single_of_isKoszulRegular_unconditional

    (hI : Ideal.IsKoszulRegular (RingHom.ker (algebraMap A (B : Type u)))) :

## Tag 08SL, a proposition

[Tag 08SL](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/08SL) in the Stacks Project.

**`CommAlgCat.isPerfect_and_hasTorAmplitude_cotangentComplexDerived_of_isLCI`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Cotangent/LocalCompleteIntersectionPerfectUnconditional.lean, line 311](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/Cotangent/LocalCompleteIntersectionPerfectUnconditional.lean#L311). The attribute's comment: “the full proposition: if A → B is a local complete intersection (RingHom.IsLocalCompleteIntersection, Tag 07D0), then L\_\{B/A} is a perfect object of D(B) (Tag 0657 (1)) with Tor amplitude in [-1, 0] (Tag 0652 (1), canonical derived tensor product). The isomorphism of L\_\{B/A} with the naive complex of a finite polynomial presentation is constructed here from Tag 08RB (Stacks.Cotangent.naiveComparisonIso), the fundamental triangle of A → P → B (Proposition 08QX), Tag 08QH and Tag 08SK”. References: SGA 6 0 (4.3), “special case”; SGA 6 VIII (2.4), “special case”; SGA 6 XIV (2.1), “special case”.

> If `A → B` is a local complete intersection map, then the cotangent complex `L_{B/A}` is a
> perfect object of `D(B)` with Tor amplitude in `[-1, 0]`.
>
> Choose a finite polynomial presentation `P = A[x₁, …, x_n] → B`. The
> naive complex `NL(P → B) = (J/J² → ⨁ B dxᵢ)` consists of finite projective modules, by Tag 08RK,
> `CommAlgCat.isPerfect_and_hasTorAmplitude_cotangentComplexDerived_of_isLocalCompleteIntersection`;
> and `L_{B/A}` is isomorphic to it in `D(B)`
> (`CommAlgCat.cotangentComplexDerivedIsoNaiveComplexOfIsLocalCompleteIntersection`): by Tag 08RB
> `NL(P → B) ≅ τ_{≥ -1} L_{B/A}`, and by the fundamental triangle 08QS of `A → P → B` together with
> Tags 08QH and 08SK, `H^i(L_{B/A}) = 0` for `i ≤ -2`.

```lean
theorem isPerfect_and_hasTorAmplitude_cotangentComplexDerived_of_isLCI
    (A : Type u) [CommRing A] (B : CommAlgCat.{u} A)
    (hlci : RingHom.IsLocalCompleteIntersection (algebraMap A ↥B)) :
    StacksDraft.Perfect.IsPerfect (cotangentComplexDerived.{u, u} A B) ∧
      StacksDraft.TorAmplitude.HasTorAmplitudeCanonical ↥B
        (cotangentComplexDerived.{u, u} A B) (-1) 0
```
