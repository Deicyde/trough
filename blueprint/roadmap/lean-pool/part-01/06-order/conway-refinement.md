# Conway refinement for omnific integers

> Proves multiplicative refinement for omnific integers: every equality ab = cd admits a two-by-two factor refinement. Develops factorization and refinement of generalized power-series integer parts and connects these to surreal-number and standalone Mathlib formulations.
