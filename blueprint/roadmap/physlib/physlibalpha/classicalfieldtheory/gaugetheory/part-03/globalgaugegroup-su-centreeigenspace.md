# The eigenspaces of the centre of `SU(N)`

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GlobalGaugeGroup.SU.CentreEigenspace`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GlobalGaugeGroup/SU/CentreEigenspace.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GlobalGaugeGroup/SU/CentreEigenspace.lean): 14 declarations, 14 with a docstring.

> The subspaces on which the central element `ω 1` of `SU(N)` acts by a scalar, and the tensors of
> `SU(N)` with no invariants for that reason.

## i. Overview

> The centre of `SU(N)` is generated by `ω 1`, for `ω` a primitive `N`-th root of unity
> (`GlobalGaugeGroup.SU.centreGenerator`). `centreEigenspace` names the subspace on which it acts by
> a scalar `ε` (A). For a representation on an algebra by algebra maps the scalar of a product is the
> product of the scalars (B), and a vector that the group fixes and that lies in an eigenspace with
> `ε ≠ 1` is zero, also modulo a stable submodule `S` (C).
>
> The element `ω 1` acts on a fundamental index by `ω`, on an anti-fundamental index by `ω̄ = ω⁻¹`
> and on an adjoint index by `1`, so on the tensors with colours `c` by `ω` to the number of
> fundamental minus anti-fundamental colours of `c` (D). When this power is not one there are no
> invariant tensors but zero. For `N ≥ 3` this is the case for two fundamental, or two
> anti-fundamental, indices, `ω² ≠ 1` (E).

## ii. Key results

> - `suTensor.centreEigenspace` : the subspace on which the centre acts by `ε`.
> - `suTensor.mul_le_centreEigenspace` : the scalar of a product is the product of the scalars.
> - `suTensor.mem_of_invariant_of_mem_sup_centreEigenspace` : a subspace on which the centre acts by
>   `ε ≠ 1` carries no invariant beyond a stable submodule `S`.
> - `suTensor.tensorRep_centreGenerator` : the centre acts on the tensors by `ω` to the number of
>   fundamental minus anti-fundamental colours.
> - `suTensor.invariants_eq_bot_of_zpow_ne_one` : the tensors on which the centre does not act
>   trivially have no invariants but zero.
> - `suTensor.invariants_fund_pow_two_eq_bot` : for `N ≥ 3` there are no invariant tensors with two
>   fundamental indices but zero, and its anti-fundamental twin.
