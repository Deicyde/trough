# TaxicabIntegral

Let $0 < R$. Let $f:\overline{\mathbb{D}_R}\to\mathbb{C}$ be analytic on neighborhoods of points in $\overline{\mathbb{D}_R}$. Define the functon $I_f:\mathbb{D}_R\to\mathbb{C}$ by

$$
I_f(z)=z\int_0^1f(tz)\,dt.
$$
