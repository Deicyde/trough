# IsFreeGroup.maximal\_isMulCommutative\_iff

The maximal abelian subgroups of a nontrivial free group are exactly the cyclic subgroups
generated by elements that are not proper powers.

```lean
IsFreeGroup.maximal_isMulCommutative_iff.{u_1} {G : Type u_1} [Group G] [IsFreeGroup G]
  [Nontrivial G] {M : Subgroup G} :
  Maximal (fun K => IsMulCommutative ↥K) M ↔ ∃ u, ¬Group.IsProperPower u ∧ M = Subgroup.zpowers u
```
