# slash-operator

Let $F$ be a function on $\mathfrak{H}$ and $\gamma\in\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$. Then the *slash operator* acts on $F$ by

$$
(F|_k\gamma)(z):=j_k(z,\gamma)\,F(\gamma z).
$$
