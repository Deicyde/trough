# fermatLastTheoremThree

There are no solutions in positive integers to $a^3+b^3=c^3$.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

The proof in mathlib was formalized by a team from the “Lean For the Curious Mathematician” conference held in Luminy in March 2024 (its dependency graph can be visualised [here](https://pitmonticone.github.io/FLT3/blueprint/dep_graph_document.html)).
