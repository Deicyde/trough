# varphi-d24

Define the quasimodular form $\varphi$ on the upper half-plane by

$$
\varphi(z)
:= \frac{\big(25E_4(z)^4 - 49E_6(z)^2E_4(z)\big) + 48E_6(z)E_4(z)^2E_2(z) + \big({-49}E_4(z)^3 + 25E_6(z)^2\big)E_2(z)^2}{\Delta(z)^2}.\qquad\text{(1)}
$$
