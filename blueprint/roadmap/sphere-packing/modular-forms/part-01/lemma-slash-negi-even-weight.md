# slash-negI-even-weight

For even $k$, $F|_{k}(-I) = F$.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node that has an article here.

## Proof

Follows from the definition of the slash operator: $(F|_{k}(-I))(z) = (-1)^{-k}F((-I)z) = F(z)$.
