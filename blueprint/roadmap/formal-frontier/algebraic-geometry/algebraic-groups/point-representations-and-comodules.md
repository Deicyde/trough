# Point representations and comodules

For a commutative Hopf algebra over a commutative ring and a fixed arbitrary module, natural point representations on the module's scalar extensions, tested on commutative base algebras in any universe containing the Hopf algebra, correspond bijectively to right-comodule structures on that module.
