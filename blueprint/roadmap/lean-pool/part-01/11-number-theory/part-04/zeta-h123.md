# Thakur's hypotheses on power sums of F_q[t]

> Formalizes four components of Thakur's hypotheses on power sums over finite polynomial rings, including uniqueness and monotonicity results for the carry-free digit optimization problems and the Laurent-series expansion of finite-field power sums.
