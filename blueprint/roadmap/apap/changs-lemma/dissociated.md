# Dissociation

We say that $A\subseteq G$ is dissociated if, for any $m\geq 1$, and any $x\in G$, there is at most one $A'\subset A$ of size $\left\lvert A'\right\rvert=m$ such that

$$
\sum_{a\in A'}a=x.
$$
