# discreteApproxSequence_of

Let $\tau$ be a stopping time bounded by $t \in T$ and $(\tau_n)$ be a discrete approximation sequence of $\tau$. Then, the sequence of stopping times $\tau_n \wedge t$ is also a discrete approximation sequence of $\tau$.
