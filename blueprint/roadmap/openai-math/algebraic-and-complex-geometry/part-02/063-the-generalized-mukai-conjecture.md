## The generalized Mukai conjecture

We prove the generalized Mukai conjecture: every positive-dimensional smooth complex Fano manifold of dimension *n*, Picard number *ρ*, and pseudoindex *ι* satisfies $\rho(\iota-1)\le n$. Equality holds precisely for the product of *ρ* copies of $\mathbb P^{\iota-1}$.
