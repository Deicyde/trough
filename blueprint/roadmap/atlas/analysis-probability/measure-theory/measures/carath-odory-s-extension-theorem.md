## Every algebra of sets is a semiring of sets

If $\mathcal{A}$ is an algebra of sets on
$\alpha$, then $\mathcal{A}$ is a semiring of sets: $\emptyset \in \mathcal{A}$, $s \cap t \in
\mathcal{A}$ for all $s, t \in \mathcal{A}$, and for all $s, t \in \mathcal{A}$ the difference
$s \setminus t$ is the union of finitely many pairwise disjoint members of $\mathcal{A}$.

## Every algebra of sets is a π-system

If $\mathcal{A}$ is an algebra of sets on $\alpha$,
then $\mathcal{A}$ is a $\pi$-system: for all $s, t \in \mathcal{A}$ with $s \cap t$ nonempty, we
have $s \cap t \in \mathcal{A}$.

## Existence of the Carathéodory extension

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an algebra of sets on
$\alpha$ and let $\mu_0$ be an additive content on $\mathcal{A}$ with values in $[0, \infty]$
that is $\sigma$-subadditive: whenever $s_0, s_1, \dots \in \mathcal{A}$ and
$\bigcup_i s_i \in \mathcal{A}$, we have $\mu_0(\bigcup_i s_i) \le \sum_i \mu_0(s_i)$. Then there
is a measure $\mu$ on the $\sigma$-algebra $\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ generated by $\mathcal{A}$ such
that $\mu(s) = \mu_0(s)$ for every $s \in \mathcal{A}$.

## Carathéodory extension theorem

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an algebra of sets on $\alpha$ and
let $\mu_0$ be an additive content on $\mathcal{A}$ with values in $[0, \infty]$ that is
$\sigma$-subadditive: whenever $s_0, s_1, \dots \in \mathcal{A}$ and
$\bigcup_i s_i \in \mathcal{A}$, we have $\mu_0(\bigcup_i s_i) \le \sum_i \mu_0(s_i)$. Suppose
there are sets $B_0, B_1, \dots \in \mathcal{A}$ with $\bigcup_i B_i = \alpha$ and
$\mu_0(B_i) < \infty$ for every $i$. Then there is exactly one measure $\mu$ on the
$\sigma$-algebra $\sigma(\mathcal{A})$ generated by $\mathcal{A}$ such that
$\mu(s) = \mu_0(s)$ for every $s \in \mathcal{A}$.
