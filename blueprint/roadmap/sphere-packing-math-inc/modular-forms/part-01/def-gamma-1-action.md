# Gamma-1-Action

The modular group $\Gamma_1:=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ acts on $\mathfrak{H}$ by linear fractional transformations

$$
\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{smallmatrix}\right)z:=\frac{az+b}{cz+d}.
$$
