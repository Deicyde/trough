# 1516-no-255

There exists a 1516 magma that does not satisfy the 255 equation

$$
x = ((x \diamond x) \diamond x) \diamond x.
$$

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

Let us define the element $x_0 := (0, 1, 0) \in G'$. Since $S x_0 = 0$, we know that $0 \diamond x_0 = 0$ (see the first part of the proof of [Obtaining Axiom B](axiom-b.md)). Therefore, $x_0$ does not satisfy the 255 equation, as we have

$$
((x_0 \diamond x_0) \diamond x_0) \diamond x_0 = (0 \diamond x_0) \diamond x_0 = 0 \diamond x_0 = 0 \neq x_0.
$$
