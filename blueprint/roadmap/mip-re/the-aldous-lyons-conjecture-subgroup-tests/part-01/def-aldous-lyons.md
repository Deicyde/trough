# Invariant random subgroups and the Aldous–Lyons conjecture; I:467–530

The space $\mathrm{Sub}(F)$ of subgroups of the free group on $s$ generators is a closed subspace of $\{0,1\}^F$ with the product topology. An invariant random subgroup is a Borel probability measure on it invariant under conjugation; the finitely described ones are the measures $\Phi(\sigma)$ of finite actions. The Aldous–Lyons conjecture asserts that for every $s$ the finitely described IRSs are weak-$*$ dense in the IRSs.
