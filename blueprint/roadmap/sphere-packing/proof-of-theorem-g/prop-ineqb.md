# ineqB

Consider the function $B:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb{C}$ defined as

$$
B(t) := -t^2\phi_0(i/t)+\frac{36}{\pi^2}\,\psi_I(it)\qquad\text{(3)}
$$

Then

$$
B(t) > 0\qquad\text{(4)}
$$

for all $t > 0$.
