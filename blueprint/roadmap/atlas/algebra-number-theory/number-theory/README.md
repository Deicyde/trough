# Number Theory

7,999 results of ATLAS's vault, 4,376 proved, 3,619 stated but not proved and 4 without a declaration, in this subject's topics.

Number theory studies integers, primes, Diophantine equations, arithmetic functions, and their analytic and algebraic structure.

The subject runs from elementary arithmetic to its analytic and algebraic theories.
[Congruences and power residues](part-01/congruences/README.md) build up to
[residue symbols and quadratic reciprocity](part-01/congruences/power-residues-reciprocity/README.md). The distribution of
primes, from the [prime number theorem and explicit bounds](part-01/distribution-of-primes/prime-number-theorem/README.md), is
approached through the [Riemann zeta function](part-01/zeta-l-functions/riemann-zeta/README.md) and
[Dirichlet characters and L-functions](part-01/zeta-l-functions/dirichlet-l-functions/README.md), with exponential-sum
estimates such as [Vinogradov's mean value theorem](part-01/exponential-sums/vinogradov-s-mean-value-theorem.md) as a
tool.

Diophantine questions take several forms: equations such as
[Pell's equation](part-01/diophantine-equations/pell-quadratic-equations/README.md), Diophantine approximation through
[Roth's theorem and linear forms in logarithms](part-01/diophantine-approximation/roth-linear-forms/README.md), and
representation by sums of powers in [Waring's problem](part-01/quadratic-forms-sums-of-squares/waring-figurate/README.md).
[Algebraic number theory](part-01/algebraic-number-theory/README.md) and [p-adic numbers](part-01/p-adic-numbers/README.md) give the
global and local structure behind them, and [arithmetic geometry](part-02/arithmetic-geometry/README.md) reaches elliptic
curves, with [Mazur's torsion theorem](part-02/arithmetic-geometry/mazur-s-torsion-theorem.md).

The largest field is [linear recurrence sequences](part-01/linear-recurrences/README.md): Fibonacci and Lucas numbers, Pell,
Jacobsthal and Tribonacci-type sequences, Lucas sequences and their primitive divisors, and Diophantine problems about
the terms. Arithmetic functions, digits and numeration systems, modular forms and partitions, special numbers such as
the Bernoulli and Catalan numbers, and integer sequences make up much of the rest.
