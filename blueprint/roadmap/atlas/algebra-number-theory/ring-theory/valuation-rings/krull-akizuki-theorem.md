## Krull–Akizuki theorem

The Krull–Akizuki theorem: a subalgebra of a finite extension of the
fraction field of a Noetherian domain of Krull dimension at most one is
Noetherian of Krull dimension at most one, and every nonzero quotient has
finite length over the base.

## Valuation subrings of finite extensions of dimension-one domains are DVRs

A valuation subring of a finite extension of the fraction field of a
Noetherian domain of Krull dimension at most one, containing the base ring
and different from the whole field, is a discrete valuation ring.
