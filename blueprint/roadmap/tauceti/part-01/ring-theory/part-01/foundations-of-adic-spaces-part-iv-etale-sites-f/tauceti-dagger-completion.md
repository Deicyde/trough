# TauCeti.Dagger.completion

The **completion** `Â = T_n ⧸ I T_n` of a dagger algebra `A ≅ W_n ⧸ I`: the quotient of the
Tate algebra `T_n = K⟨X₁, …, Xₙ⟩` by the ideal generated by the kernel `I` of the chosen
presentation `W_n ↠ A`, with `n = presentationDim K A`.
