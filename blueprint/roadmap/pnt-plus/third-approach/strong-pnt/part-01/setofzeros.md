# SetOfZeros

Let $R>0$ and $f:\mathbb{C}\to\mathbb{C}$. Define the set of zeros $\mathcal{K}_f(R)=\{\rho\in\mathbb{C}:|\rho|\leq R,\,f(\rho)=0\}$.
