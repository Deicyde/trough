**`minorIdeal_eq_of_quotientLinearEquiv`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/MinorIdealInvariance.lean, line 474](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/LinearAlgebra/Matrix/MinorIdealInvariance.lean#L474). References: SGA 7 VI (4.16), “Section 5, special case”.

> **Stacks 07Z8.** The ideal generated by the `(n - k) × (n - k)` minors of a presentation
> matrix of a finite module is independent of the presentation: if `A` has `n` rows, `B` has `m`
> rows, and the cokernels of the two column maps are isomorphic `R`-modules, then
> `I_{n-k}(A) = I_{m-k}(B)`. This is what makes `fittingIdeal` well defined.

```lean
theorem minorIdeal_eq_of_quotientLinearEquiv {R : Type*} [CommRing R]
    {n m : ℕ} {J K : Type*}
    (A : Matrix (Fin n) J R) (B : Matrix (Fin m) K R) (k : ℕ)
    (h : ((Fin n → R) ⧸ Submodule.span R
        (Set.range (fun j : J => fun i : Fin n => A i j))) ≃ₗ[R]
      ((Fin m → R) ⧸ Submodule.span R
        (Set.range (fun j : K => fun i : Fin m => B i j)))) :
    minorIdeal A (n - k) = minorIdeal B (m - k)
```

> **Stacks 0H73.** The zeroth Fitting ideal of `R ⧸ I` is `I`; for the direct sum of two such
> quotients, the zeroth and first Fitting ideals are respectively the product and sum of the
> defining ideals.

```lean
theorem fittingIdeal_quotient_and_prod {R : Type*} [CommRing R] (I J : Ideal R) :
    fittingIdeal (M := R ⧸ I) 0 = I ∧
      fittingIdeal (M := (R ⧸ I) × (R ⧸ J)) 0 = I * J ∧
      fittingIdeal (M := (R ⧸ I) × (R ⧸ J)) 1 = I + J
```

> Let `M` be a finite module over a commutative ring `R`, let `k` be a natural
> number, and let `p` be a prime ideal of `R`. The following are equivalent: the `k`th Fitting ideal
> of `M` is not contained in `p`; the fibre of `M` at `p` has dimension at most `k`; the
> localization of `M` at `p` is generated by `k` elements; and this generation property holds on
> some principal neighbourhood of `p`.

```lean
theorem fittingIdeal_local_generation_tfae
    {R : Type u} [CommRing R] {M : Type v} [AddCommGroup M] [Module R M]
    [Module.Finite R M] (k : ℕ) (p : Ideal R) [p.IsPrime] :
    [
      ¬ fittingIdeal (R := R) (M := M) (k : ℤ) ≤ p,
      Module.finrank p.ResidueField (p.ResidueField ⊗[R] M) ≤ k,
      ∃ g : Fin k → LocalizedModule.AtPrime p M,
        Submodule.span (Localization.AtPrime p) (Set.range g) = ⊤,
      ∃ f : R, f ∉ p ∧ ∃ g : Fin k → LocalizedModule.Away f M,
        Submodule.span (Localization.Away f) (Set.range g) = ⊤
    ].TFAE
```

References: SGA 7 VI (5.1), “5.1(b), special case”.

> A finite module is finite locally free of rank `r` if and only if its
> `(r - 1)`st Fitting ideal is zero and its `r`th Fitting ideal is the unit ideal; equivalently, all
> Fitting ideals below `r` are zero and all those from `r` onward are the unit ideal.

```lean
theorem finiteLocallyFreeOfRank_fittingIdeal_tfae
    {R : Type u} {M : Type v} [CommRing R] [AddCommGroup M] [Module R M]
    [Module.Finite R M] (r : ℕ) :
    [
      Module.IsFiniteLocallyFreeOfRank R M r,
      fittingIdeal (R := R) (M := M) ((r : ℤ) - 1) = ⊥ ∧
        fittingIdeal (R := R) (M := M) (r : ℤ) = ⊤,
      (∀ k : ℤ, k < r → fittingIdeal (R := R) (M := M) k = ⊥) ∧
        (∀ k : ℤ, (r : ℤ) ≤ k → fittingIdeal (R := R) (M := M) k = ⊤)
    ].TFAE
```

> free of rank `k`, and `M` is the direct sum of the quotient and the torsion submodule.
