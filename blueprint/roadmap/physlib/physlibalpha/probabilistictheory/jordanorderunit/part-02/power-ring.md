# `J[a]` is a commutative associative algebra

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.JordanOrderUnit.Power.Ring`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/JordanOrderUnit/Power/Ring.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/JordanOrderUnit/Power/Ring.lean): 16 declarations (2 instances with no name), 4 with a docstring.

> The span `J[a]` of the powers of `a` is a commutative associative unital real algebra.

## i. Overview

> By power-associativity the Jordan product restricted to the span `J[a]` of the powers of `a` is
> associative. So `J[a]` is a commutative associative unital real algebra, to which Mathlib's theory
> of commutative algebras applies.

## ii. Key results

> - `JordanAlgebra.GeneratedByOne` : the subalgebra `J[a]`.
> - `JordanAlgebra.GeneratedByOne.instCommRing` : its commutative ring structure.
> - `JordanAlgebra.GeneratedByOne.instAlgebra` : its real algebra structure.
