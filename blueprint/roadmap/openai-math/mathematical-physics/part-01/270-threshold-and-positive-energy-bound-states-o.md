# 270. Threshold and positive-energy bound states of the BFSS matrix model

2 manuscripts, with no Lean, quoted from [openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math) at commit [4eaee875](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae).

Proves that the undeformed relative $\mathrm{SU}(N)$ BFSS model has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state for every finite *N* ≥ 2, resolving the threshold-bound-state conjecture. For $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, a companion proves infinitely many normalizable positive-energy eigenstates with unbounded energies, contradicting the original BFSS paper's exclusion of additional bound states at *N* = 2.

## The unique threshold bound state of the SU(N) BFSS model

For every finite *N* ≥ 2, we prove that undeformed $\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (N)$ BFSS matrix quantum mechanics has exactly one normalizable zero-energy state after removing the center of mass. This establishes the threshold-bound-state conjecture.

[The manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/preprints/The-unique-threshold-bound-state-of-the-SU-N-BFSS-model-September-24-2026/paper.pdf).

## Positive eigenvalues of the relative SU(2) BFSS Hamiltonian

We prove that the relative $\mathop{\mathrm{SU}}\nolimits (2)$ BFSS Hamiltonian has infinitely many positive eigenvalues tending to infinity, with square-integrable eigenvectors. The operator is defined by closing the gauge-invariant supercharge form. This refutes, at *N* = 2, the exclusion of normalizable positive-energy states stated in the original BFSS paper.
