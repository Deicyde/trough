# CPlus-generator

The *standard generators* are the $(p-3)/2$ real cyclotomic units

$$
\varepsilon_2,\ \varepsilon_3,\ \ldots,\ \varepsilon_{(p-1)/2}
$$

of [Definition real-cyclotomic-unit](def-real-cyclotomic-unit.md), indexed in the formalisation by $i \in \{0, \ldots, (p-5)/2\}$ via $a = i + 2$.
