> In a commutative square of rings
> ```
> S --φ--> S'
> ↑        ↑
> |        |
> R -----> R'
> ```
> with `φ : S → S'` surjective, the map `Ω[S⁄R] → Ω[S'⁄R']` is surjective and its kernel is the
> `S`-submodule generated by the `da` with `a ∈ S` such that `φ(a)` lies in the image of `R'`
> (among them the `di` with `i ∈ ker φ`).

```lean
theorem map_surjective_and_ker_eq_span_of_surjective
    (h : Function.Surjective (algebraMap S S')) :
    Function.Surjective (map R R' S S') ∧
      LinearMap.ker (map R R' S S') =
        Submodule.span S (⇑(D R S) '' {a | algebraMap S S' a ∈ Set.range (algebraMap R' S')})
```

> Let `R → P → S` be commutative rings, let `I = ker(P → S)`, and suppose there is an
> `R`-algebra map `g : S → P ⧸ I²` that is a right inverse to `P ⧸ I² → S`. Then the exact sequence

> `KaehlerDifferential.mapBaseChange R P S` to `LinearMap.snd`. Nothing is required of `P`.

```lean
    [IsScalarTower R P S] (g : S →ₐ[R] P ⧸ RingHom.ker (algebraMap P S) ^ 2)
```
