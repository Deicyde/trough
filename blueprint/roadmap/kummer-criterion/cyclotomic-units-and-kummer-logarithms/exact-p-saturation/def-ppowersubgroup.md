# pPowerSubgroup

For a subgroup $H$ of an abelian group, $H^p$ denotes the exact image of the $p$th-power map:

$$
H^p=\{h^p:h\in H\}
$$

(an actual image, not the subgroup generated by it; for abelian groups the two coincide).
