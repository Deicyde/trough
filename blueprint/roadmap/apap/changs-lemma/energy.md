# Energy

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and $A\subseteq G$. Let $m\geq 1$. We define

$$
E_{2m}(A)=\sum_{a_1,\ldots,a_{2m}\in A}1_{a_1+\cdots-a_{2m}=0}.
$$
