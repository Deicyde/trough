# The swap isometry

There is $\delta_{qld} = O\bigl( \delta_S^{1/4} + md/q \bigr)$ and local unitaries $V_\textsc{A}$, $V_\textsc{B}$ on the expanded spaces such that, writing $M = 2^m$:

1. there is a state $|\textsc{aux}\rangle$ on the two parties' non-ancilla registers with $\left\| { V_\textsc{A} \otimes V_\textsc{B} |\widehat{\psi}\rangle - |\textsc{aux}\rangle \otimes |\textsc{EPR}_q\rangle^{\otimes M} } \right\|^2 \leq \delta_{qld}$ — the bound is on the *squared* norm, which is why Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md), whose item bounds the norm itself, carries $\delta_{qld}^{1/2}$ and halves the constant $b$;
2. conjugation by $V_\textsc{A}$, resp. $V_\textsc{B}$, carries the strategy's total Pauli measurement $M^{(\textsc{Pauli},W)}_h$ to ${I} \otimes \tau^W_h$ on the EPR registers, within $\delta_{qld}$ in the state-dependent distance relative to $|\textsc{aux}\rangle \otimes |\textsc{EPR}_q\rangle^{\otimes M}$.

Conjugation carries the exact Pauli observables of Lemma [Exact Pauli operators](lem-qld-exact-paulis.md) to the honest Pauli operators *exactly*, with no error at all; that identity is a step of item 2's proof, not a weaker form of it. The Lean proves it for the two cuts of a projective strategy in any bipartite model (Lemma [Self-consistency of the exact Pauli observables](lem-qld-pauli-selfcons.md)), in the vocabulary of Definition [The Pauli basis test is sound in a model](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-qld-sound-in.md). For a unit vector $|\textsc{aux}\rangle$ of the extension of the strategy's model by the padded registers (Definition [The models of stages 4 and 5](def-qld-physical-model.md)), the swap isometry is a local isometry from the strategy's model to the register model on $\mathbb{F}_q^M$ of that extension in the state $|\textsc{aux}\rangle$: the strategy's model into its physical model with the registers inert, then conjugation by the two swap unitaries $V_\textsc{A}$ and $V_\textsc{B}$, then the regrouping into item 1's cut. Item 1 is the squared distance of the transported state to that register model's state $|\textsc{EPR}_q\rangle^{\otimes M} \otimes |\textsc{aux}\rangle$, and item 2, for each player and each basis, the summed squared state-dependent distance there of the transported $(\textsc{Pauli}, W)$ measurement, read as cube data, to the honest projector on the player's half of the register. One $|\textsc{aux}\rangle$ serves both items and both players. The statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

The standard swap-isometry argument for item 1. It is standard *because* the representation is exact: an approximate representation would not give a unitary, which is the whole reason Lemma [Exact Pauli operators](lem-qld-exact-paulis.md) is stated with exact relations. Item 2 is not exact and is the longer half: it chains the win implications' Pauli-basis consistency and the point measurements' self-consistency through the marginals of Lemma [The marginals track the point measurements](lem-qld-helper.md), and it is where the $md/q$ of $\delta_{qld}$ comes from, by Lemma [Schwartz–Zippel](../part-05/lem-schwartz-zippel.md). Item 1 alone gives $O(\sqrt{\delta_S})$.
