# Poitou's Explicit Odlyzko Bound for Root Discriminants

> Formalizes the unconditional explicit-formula argument for a lower bound on root discriminants of totally complex number fields. Develops the completed Dedekind zeta function and its functional equation, Poitou's explicit formula, the Tartar test function, and a certified numerical estimate proving that degree at least 18 forces root discriminant at least 8.25.
