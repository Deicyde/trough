# Witt's formula for free Lie algebras

## Free Lie monomials

Let $K$ be a field, $r$ a natural number, and $L$ the free Lie algebra
over $K$ on generators $x_0, \dots, x_{r-1}$. The relation "$x$ is a homogeneous Lie monomial of
multidegree $d$", for $d \in \mathbb{N}^r$ and $x \in L$, is defined inductively: each generator
$x_i$ is a monomial of multidegree $e_i$, which is $1$ at $i$ and $0$ elsewhere; and if $x$ is a
monomial of multidegree $a$ and $y$ is a monomial of multidegree $b$, then the bracket $[x, y]$
is a monomial of multidegree $a + b$, computed pointwise. There are no other monomials.

## Multihomogeneous components of a free Lie algebra

Let $K$ be a field, $r$ a natural
number, and $L$ the free Lie algebra over $K$ on generators $x_0, \dots, x_{r-1}$. For a
multidegree $d \in \mathbb{N}^r$, the multihomogeneous component of $L$ of multidegree $d$ is the
$K$-linear span of the homogeneous Lie monomials of multidegree $d$, that is, of the iterated
brackets of the generators in which each $x_i$ occurs exactly $d_i$ times.

These components distinguish multidegrees, not only the total bracket length.

## Witt's multigraded value

For natural numbers $n_1, \dots, n_r$, let
$n = n_1 + \dots + n_r$ and $g = \gcd(n_1, \dots, n_r)$. The rational number
$M(n_1, \dots, n_r)$ is $0$ if $n = 0$, and otherwise
$M(n_1, \dots, n_r) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{d \mid g} \mu(d) \binom{n/d}{n_1/d, \ldots, n_r/d}$,
where the sum runs over the positive divisors $d$ of $g$, $\mu$ is the Möbius function, and the
last factor is the multinomial coefficient of $n_1/d, \dots, n_r/d$.

## Dimension of a multihomogeneous component of a free Lie algebra

Let $K$ be a field and
let $n_1, \dots, n_r$ be natural numbers with $n_1 + \dots + n_r > 0$. Then the multihomogeneous
component of multidegree $(n_1, \dots, n_r)$ of the free Lie algebra over $K$ on $r$ generators
has dimension over $K$ equal to Witt's multigraded value $M(n_1, \dots, n_r)$.

## Witt's multigraded dimension formula

Let $K$ be a field, let $r > 0$, and let
$n_1, \dots, n_r$ be natural numbers with $n_1 + \dots + n_r > 0$. Then the multihomogeneous
component of multidegree $(n_1, \dots, n_r)$ of the free Lie algebra over $K$ on $r$ generators
has dimension over $K$ equal to Witt's multigraded value $M(n_1, \dots, n_r)$.

Proof: The Lyndon-word basis identifies the component with the Lyndon words of
the prescribed content, which are counted by Möbius inversion.
