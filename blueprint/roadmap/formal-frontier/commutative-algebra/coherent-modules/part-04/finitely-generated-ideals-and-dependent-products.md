# Finitely generated ideals and dependent products

Over a `CommSemiring R`,
[`Submodule.smul_pi_of_fg`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/coherent-modules/blob/b670b34e8741624c22b88375b9086d6d393cbbc3/CoherentModules/LinearAlgebra/Pi.lean) says that
multiplication by a finitely generated ideal commutes with the full dependent
product of submodules of additive commutative `R`-modules. The index type is
arbitrary, including empty, and the semiring, index and carrier universes are
independent.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/coherent-modules/blob/b670b34e8741624c22b88375b9086d6d393cbbc3/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/coherent-modules](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/coherent-modules) at commit [b670b34e](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/coherent-modules/tree/b670b34e8741624c22b88375b9086d6d393cbbc3), Apache-2.0.*
