## σ-algebra of the configuration outside a region

The σ-algebra on `ConfigSpace E` generated by the coordinates outside `Λ`: the information
about a configuration that is visible from outside the region `Λ`.

Formalized by `StatMech.outsideSigma`, a definition declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean, line 53](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean#L53). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/random-fields/gibbs-conditional-distributions/index.html#outside-sigma).

## Conditional distribution given the outside of a region

The conditional distribution of a finite measure `μ` on `ConfigSpace E` given the
configuration outside `Λ`: a regular version of `μ(· | outsideSigma Λ)`, namely Mathlib's
`condExpKernel μ (outsideSigma Λ)`. For a Gibbs measure this is the Gibbs specification in `Λ`
(DLR condition).

Formalized by `StatMech.gibbsConditional`, a definition declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean, line 66](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean#L66). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/random-fields/gibbs-conditional-distributions/index.html#gibbs-conditional).

## Averaging the outside conditional distribution recovers the measure

Disintegration: averaging the conditional distribution over `μ` gives back `μ`.

Formalized by `StatMech.gibbsConditional_comp_trim`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean, line 78](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/CondDistribution.lean#L78). ## Disintegration in product form for the outside conditional distribution
