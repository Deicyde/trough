# Embeddings

1. An embedding carries Born probabilities, both players' squared state norms, the cross-party deviations and the norm of the state, and families of measurements pushed forward along it keep their value in every game and their inconsistency on every index. Along every local isometry carrying the state to the state, a sum of products of the two players' operators keeps its state norm and its quadratic form.
2. Adjoining an inert ancilla in a unit vector, reading an extension of an extension as one extension, relabelling the registers and exchanging the players in an extension are embeddings, in the vectors of Lemma [Moving registers along local isometries](../part-03/lem-ancilla-isometries.md); and so is the change of cut from the extension in the regrouped vector into a reading (Definition [Readings of an extension](def-model-reading.md)).
3. For unitaries $U$ of the first player and $V$ of the second, the isometry $\pi(\pi_A(U)\pi_B(V))$ with the conjugations $X \mapsto U X U^*$ and $Y \mapsto V Y V^*$ is a local isometry of the model to itself, with unital homomorphisms; it carries the state to the state only when that isometry fixes the state.
4. Relabelling the outcomes of two families along one bijection keeps their inconsistency, and pushing a POVM forward along a composite of unital homomorphisms is pushing it forward twice.

## Proof

Item 1 is Lemma [Local isometries carry the Born rule](../part-03/lem-local-isometry.md), the homomorphisms being unital so that pushed-forward POVMs are POVMs; the isometry intertwines each product of the two players' operators with the product of their images. In item 2 the conditions on the state are those of Lemmas [Moving registers along local isometries](../part-03/lem-ancilla-isometries.md) and [Readings of an extension](lem-model-reading.md). In item 3, $(\pi_A(U)\pi_B(V))^*\pi_A(U)\pi_B(V) = {I}$ because $U^*U = {I}$, $V^*V = {I}$ and the two players' operators commute, which also gives the intertwining; $UU^* = {I}$ and $VV^* = {I}$ make the conjugations unital.
