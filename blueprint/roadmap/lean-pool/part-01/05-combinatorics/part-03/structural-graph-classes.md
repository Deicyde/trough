# Split, cograph and threshold graph structures

> Connects split partitions, cotrees and threshold creation sequences on Mathlib SimpleGraph. Proves the finite forbidden-induced-subgraph characterizations, split iff chordal and co-chordal, and completeness of cotrees and creation sequences. Supplies certificate-preserving operations, twins and nonempty union/join decompositions for cographs, maximum-clique split partitions, and induced/isomorphic transport of existing chordal elimination orders and clique trees. Classical graph theory is formalized; no new mathematical theorem, canonical certificate or recognition-time bound is claimed.
