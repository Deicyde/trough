# Projective modules lift across nilpotent ideals

For any ring R and nilpotent two-sided ideal I, every projective R/I-module is isomorphic to the canonical reduction of a projective R-module. Finitely generated inputs admit finitely generated lifts. Neither commutativity nor a finite-generation assumption on I is needed.
