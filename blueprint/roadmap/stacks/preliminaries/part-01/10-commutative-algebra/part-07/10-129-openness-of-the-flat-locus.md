> **Stacks 00R9.** Let `k` be a field and `S` a finite type `k`-algebra which is Cohen–Macaulay
> (every local ring `S_𝔭` satisfies `depth_{𝔪}(S_𝔭) = dim S_𝔭`) and equidimensional of dimension
> `d`. Let `f = f₁, …, f_i` be elements of `S` cutting out a nonempty closed subset
> `V(f₁, …, f_i)` of `Spec S` of dimension at most `d - i`. Then that dimension is exactly `d - i`,
> and `f₁, …, f_i` is a regular sequence in `S_𝔮` for every prime `𝔮 ⊇ (f₁, …, f_i)`.

```lean
public theorem ringKrullDim_quotient_add_length_eq_and_isRegular
    {k S : Type*} [Field k] [CommRing S] [Algebra k S] [Algebra.FiniteType k S]
    (hS : ∀ (p : Ideal S) [p.IsPrime],
      Module.IsMaximalCohenMacaulay (Localization.AtPrime p) (Localization.AtPrime p))
    (hequi : TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional (PrimeSpectrum S)) (d : ℕ)
    (hd : ringKrullDim S = d) (f : List S) (hf : Ideal.ofList f ≠ ⊤)
    (hdim : ringKrullDim (S ⧸ Ideal.ofList f) + f.length ≤ (d : WithBot ℕ∞)) :
    ringKrullDim (S ⧸ Ideal.ofList f) + f.length = (d : WithBot ℕ∞) ∧
      ∀ (q : Ideal S) [q.IsPrime], Ideal.ofList f ≤ q →
        ∀ (Sq : Type*) [CommRing Sq] [Algebra S Sq] [IsLocalization.AtPrime Sq q],
          RingTheory.Sequence.IsRegular Sq (f.map (algebraMap S Sq))

Cohen-Macaulayness of the fibre (Definition 00NC) is spelled out over its primes at `Localization.AtPrime` and equidimensionality of dimension `d` as Definition 0058 plus `ringKrullDim = d`, as in tags 00ND and 00R9; the hypothesis is guarded by the fibre being nonzero, where the source's condition on its components has content. See the module docstring.”.

> **Stacks 00RA.** Let `R → S` be a finite type ring map and let `d` be such that every
> nonempty fibre `S ⊗_R κ(𝔭)` is Cohen–Macaulay (each of its local rings `A` satisfies
> `depth_{𝔪_A}(A) = dim A`) and equidimensional of dimension `d`. Let `f = f₁, …, f_i` be elements
> of `S`. Then the set of points `𝔮` of `V(f₁, …, f_i)` at which `f₁, …, f_i` is a regular sequence
> in `S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮`, where `𝔭 = R ∩ 𝔮`, is open in `V(f₁, …, f_i)`.

```lean
public theorem isOpen_setOf_isRegular_fiber_of_isMaximalCohenMacaulay_of_equidimensional
    {R S : Type*} [CommRing R] [CommRing S] [Algebra R S] [Algebra.FiniteType R S] (d : ℕ)
    (hfib : ∀ (p : Ideal R) [p.IsPrime], Nontrivial (p.Fiber S) →
      (∀ (Q : Ideal (p.Fiber S)) [Q.IsPrime],
          Module.IsMaximalCohenMacaulay (Localization.AtPrime Q) (Localization.AtPrime Q)) ∧
        TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional (PrimeSpectrum (p.Fiber S)) ∧
        ringKrullDim (p.Fiber S) = (d : WithBot ℕ∞))
    (f : List S) :
    IsOpen {q : ↥(PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus (Ideal.ofList f : Set S)) |
      RingTheory.Sequence.IsRegular
        (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.1.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal)))
        (f.map (algebraMap S (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.1.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal)))))}
```

> Let `R → S` be a flat ring map of finite type with `R` Noetherian, whose nonzero fibres
> `S ⊗_R κ(𝔭)` are Cohen–Macaulay and equidimensional of dimension `d`, and let
> `0 → S^{n_{e+1}} →[φ e] S^{n_e} → ⋯ → S^{n_1} →[φ 0] S^{n_0}` be a complex of finite free
> `S`-modules. Then the set of primes `𝔮` of `S` at which the complex
> `F_𝔮 ⊗_R κ(𝔭) = F ⊗_S S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮`, `𝔭 = 𝔮 ∩ R`, is exact is open in `Spec S`.

```lean
public theorem Algebra.isOpen_setOf_injective_and_exact_baseChange_fiber
    {R S : Type*} [CommRing R] [CommRing S] [Algebra R S] [IsNoetherianRing R]
    [Algebra.FiniteType R S] [Module.Flat R S] (d : ℕ)
    (hfib : ∀ (p : Ideal R) [p.IsPrime], Nontrivial (p.Fiber S) →
      (∀ (Q : Ideal (p.Fiber S)) [Q.IsPrime],
          Module.IsMaximalCohenMacaulay (Localization.AtPrime Q) (Localization.AtPrime Q)) ∧
        TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional (PrimeSpectrum (p.Fiber S)) ∧
        ringKrullDim (p.Fiber S) = (d : WithBot ℕ∞))
    (e : ℕ) (n : ℕ → ℕ) (φ : ∀ i, (Fin (n (i + 1)) → S) →ₗ[S] (Fin (n i) → S))
    (hφ : ∀ k < e, φ k ∘ₗ φ (k + 1) = 0) :
    IsOpen {q : PrimeSpectrum S |
      Function.Injective ((φ e).baseChange (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal)))) ∧
      ∀ k < e, Function.Exact
        ((φ (k + 1)).baseChange (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal))))
        ((φ k).baseChange (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.asIdeal))))}
