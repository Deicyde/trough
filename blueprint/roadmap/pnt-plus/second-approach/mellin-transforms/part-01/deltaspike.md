# DeltaSpike

Let $\nu$ be a bumpfunction supported in $[1/2,2]$. Then for any $\epsilon>0$, we define the delta spike $\nu_\epsilon$ to be the function from $\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ to $\mathbb{C}$ defined by

$$
\nu_\epsilon(x) = \frac{1}{\epsilon}\nu\left(x^{\frac{1}{\epsilon}}\right).
$$
