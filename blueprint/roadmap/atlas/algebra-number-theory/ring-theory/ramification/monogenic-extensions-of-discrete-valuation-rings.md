# Monogenic extensions of discrete valuation rings

## Algebras generated by a root with invertible derivative are formally unramified

Let $R$ and $S$ be commutative rings, let $S$ be an $R$-algebra, and let $x \in S$ generate $S$
as an $R$-algebra, that is $R[x] = S$. If some polynomial $P \in R[X]$ satisfies $P(x) = 0$ and
$P'(x)$ is a unit of $S$, then $S$ is formally unramified over $R$.

## Étale finite local algebras are generated by one element with separable reduction

Let $R$ and $S$ be local rings and let $S$ be an $R$-algebra whose structure map $R \to S$ is
injective, which is finitely generated and flat as an $R$-module and finitely presented as an
$R$-algebra. Let $k$ be the residue field of $R$. Then $S$ is étale over $R$ if and only if
there is an element $x \in S$ with $R[x] = S$ such that the image in $k[X]$ of the minimal
polynomial of $x$ over $R$ (a monic polynomial in $R[X]$ of least degree with root $x$) is
separable.

## Monogenic generator with a uniformizing residue polynomial

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete
valuation rings with maximal ideals $\mathfrak m_A$, $\mathfrak m_B$ and residue fields
$\kappa_A$, $\kappa_B$, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra that is finitely generated as an
$A$-module and whose structure map is a local homomorphism. If $\kappa_B / \kappa_A$ is
separable, then there are $\alpha \in B$ and a polynomial $F \in A[X]$ such that, writing
$\bar\alpha$ for the residue of $\alpha$ in $\kappa_B$: $A[\alpha] = B$;
$\kappa_A[\bar\alpha] = \kappa_B$; $F$ is monic; the reduction of $F$ modulo $\mathfrak m_A$ is
the minimal polynomial of $\bar\alpha$ over $\kappa_A$; $F'(\alpha)$ is a unit of $B$; and
$\mathfrak m_B$ is the principal ideal generated by $F(\alpha)$.

In general $F$ is not the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $A$: $F(\alpha)$ is a nonzero
uniformizer, whereas the minimal polynomial vanishes at $\alpha$.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/RingTheory/ramification/monogenic-extensions-of-discrete-valuation-rings/index.html#exists-generator-with-residue-minpoly-un).

## Monogenicity of extensions with separable residue field extension

Let $A$ and $B$ be
discrete valuation rings with residue fields $\kappa_A$, $\kappa_B$, and let $B$ be an
$A$-algebra that is finitely generated as an $A$-module and whose structure map is a local
homomorphism. If $\kappa_B / \kappa_A$ is separable, then there is $\alpha \in B$ with
$A[\alpha] = B$.

No unramifiedness is assumed: in the ramified case a lift of a generator of $\kappa_B$ over
$\kappa_A$ is perturbed so that the lifted minimal polynomial takes a uniformizer as value.

## Lifts of residue generators generate unramified extensions

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete
valuation rings with maximal ideals $\mathfrak m_A$, $\mathfrak m_B$ and residue fields
$\kappa_A$, $\kappa_B$, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra that is finitely generated as an
$A$-module and whose structure map is a local homomorphism. Suppose $\kappa_B / \kappa_A$ is
separable and the ideal $\mathfrak m_A B$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak m_A$ equals
$\mathfrak m_B$. If $\beta \in B$ has residue $\bar\beta \in \kappa_B$ with
$\kappa_A[\bar\beta] = \kappa_B$, then $A[\beta] = B$.

## Étale criterion for extensions of discrete valuation rings

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete
valuation rings with residue fields $\kappa_A$, $\kappa_B$, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra that
is finitely generated as an $A$-module and whose structure map is a local homomorphism. If
$\kappa_B / \kappa_A$ is separable and the rank of $B$ as an $A$-module equals the degree
$[\kappa_B : \kappa_A]$, then $B$ is étale over $A$.

## Characterization of unramified extensions of a complete DVR

Let $A$ and $B$ be
discrete valuation rings, with $A$ complete with respect to its maximal ideal, and let $B$
be an $A$-algebra via a local homomorphism $A \to B$, such that $B$ is finitely generated
and torsion-free as an $A$-module. Let $k_A$ and $k_B$ be the residue fields of $A$ and $B$.
Then the following are equivalent: (a) $k_B$ is separable over $k_A$ and the rank of $B$
as an $A$-module equals $[k_B : k_A]$; (b) there is $\alpha \in B$ with $B = A[\alpha]$ such
that the reduction modulo the maximal ideal of $A$ of the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$
over $A$ is separable over $k_A$.

## Adjoining a root of unity of order coprime to the residue characteristic is unramified

Let $A$ and $B$ be discrete valuation rings, with $A$ complete with respect to its maximal
ideal, and let $B$ be an $A$-algebra via a local homomorphism $A \to B$, such that $B$ is
finitely generated and torsion-free as an $A$-module. Let $k_A$ and $k_B$ be the residue fields
of $A$ and $B$. Let $n$ be a natural number coprime to the characteristic of $k_A$, and let
$\zeta \in B$ be a primitive $n$-th root of unity with $B = A[\zeta]$. Then $k_B$ is separable
over $k_A$ and the rank of $B$ as an $A$-module equals $[k_B : k_A]$.

Coprimality is meant literally, with $\gcd(n, 0) = n$, so when $k_A$ has characteristic $0$
the hypothesis forces $n = 1$.
