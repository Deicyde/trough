> An *inverse system of sites with finite coverings*, following the Stacks
> Project tag `situation-inverse-limit-sites`.
>
> The data consists of:
>
> * a cofiltered index category `I`;
> * for each `i : I`, a (small) category `C i` equipped with a Grothendieck
>   topology `J i` such that every covering sieve admits a finite generating family;
> * for each morphism `a : i ⟶ j` in `I`, a functor `u a : C j ⥤ C i` continuous in the sense of
>   Stacks 00WV and giving a morphism of sites in the sense of Stacks 00X1 (note the reversed
>   direction: the morphism of sites `f a : C i ⟶ C j` is given by `u a : C j ⥤ C i`);
> * compatibility with composition `u (b ≫ a) = u a ⋙ u b` and identities
>   `u (𝟙 i) = 𝟭 (C i)`.

**`InverseSystemOfFiniteSites`**, a structure in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/InverseSystem/FiniteCoveringsInverseSystem.lean, line 191](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/InverseSystem/FiniteCoveringsInverseSystem.lean#L191).

> An *inverse system of sites with finite coverings* (Stacks 0A34), carrying the finiteness
> condition the source actually imposes.
>
> In Stacks 00VH a site is a category together with a collection of *coverings*, each of which is a
> *finite* family of morphisms with a common target, and its Grothendieck topology is the one those
> coverings generate. So the content of "the coverings of `C i` are finite" is that the finite
> covering families generate `J i`, that is: every covering sieve `S` of `J i` **contains** a finite
> covering family. That is the `finiteCoverings` field here.
>
> `InverseSystemOfSites.finiteCoverings` instead asks that every covering sieve be **generated** by a
> finite family, which is strictly stronger: `InverseSystemOfSites` is the special case of this
> structure in which every covering sieve happens to be finitely generated. Nothing of the kind is
> available on the sites this situation is meant for — on `Spec k[t]` the sieve generated by all the
> standard opens `D(f)` is a covering sieve of the Zariski, hence also of the étale, topology and is
> dominated by no finite subfamily — so no inverse system of étale sites satisfies the stronger
> condition and Stacks 09YL could not be applied to one. Every such system does satisfy the
> condition below.
>
> Every other field, and the record-level data, is literally that of `InverseSystemOfSites`, so the
> two structures are interchangeable wherever `finiteCoverings` is not used; and a strong system is a
> weak one, `InverseSystemOfSites.toInverseSystemOfFiniteSites`.

**`CategoryTheory.Sites.InverseSystem.InverseSystemOfFiniteSites.exists_precoverage_of_colimit`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/InverseSystem/ColimitsOfSites.lean, line 967](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/InverseSystem/ColimitsOfSites.lean#L967).

> **Colimits of sites.** Let `D` be an inverse system of sites with finite coverings over a
> cofiltered index category (Stacks 0A34) — so each covering sieve of `D.J i` contains a finite
> covering family, which is what makes the coverings of Stacks 00VH generate `D.J i` — and let the
> functors `u i : D.C i ⥤ C` exhibit `C` as the colimit of the underlying categories: they are
> compatible with the transition functors (`hu`), every object of `C` comes from some `D.C i`
> (`hobj`), and the morphisms of `C` between objects coming from `D.C i` are the colimit over
> `a : j ⟶ i` of the morphisms of `D.C j` between the transported objects (`hhom`, `hhom_inj`).
>
> Then the families of morphisms of `C` that are images under the `u i` of the (finite) coverings of
> the `D.C i` are the coverings of a site structure on `C` in the sense of Stacks 00VH: they contain
> every one-element family given by an isomorphism, they are stable under refinement by coverings,
> and for every covering of `X` and every `f : Y ⟶ X` the fibre products of the members of the
> covering with `f` exist and again form a covering, of `Y`.
>
> This is the form of Stacks 09YL that applies to an inverse system of étale sites;
> `InverseSystemOfSites.exists_precoverage_of_colimit` below is its special case for a system all of
> whose covering sieves happen to be finitely generated.

```lean
public theorem InverseSystemOfFiniteSites.exists_precoverage_of_colimit {C : Type w}
    [Category.{w'} C] (D : InverseSystemOfFiniteSites) (u : ∀ i, D.C i ⥤ C)
    (hu : ∀ {i j : D.I} (a : i ⟶ j), D.u a ⋙ u i = u j)
    (hobj : ∀ X : C, ∃ (i : D.I) (Y : D.C i), (u i).obj Y = X)
    (hhom :
      ∀ (i : D.I) (X Y : D.C i) (f : (u i).obj X ⟶ (u i).obj Y),
        ∃ (j : D.I) (a : j ⟶ i) (g : (D.u a).obj X ⟶ (D.u a).obj Y),
          (u j).map g =
            eqToHom (Functor.congr_obj (hu a) X) ≫ f ≫ eqToHom (Functor.congr_obj (hu a) Y).symm)
    (hhom_inj :
      ∀ (i : D.I) (X Y : D.C i) (g₁ g₂ : X ⟶ Y),
        (u i).map g₁ = (u i).map g₂ → ∃ (j : D.I) (a : j ⟶ i), (D.u a).map g₁ = (D.u a).map g₂) :
    ∃ K : Precoverage C,
      -- the coverings of `C` are the images of the finite coverings of the `D.C i`
      (∀ (X : C) (R : Presieve X),
          R ∈ K X ↔
            ∃ (i : D.I) (Y : D.C i) (h : (u i).obj Y = X) (S : Presieve Y),
              S ∈ (D.J i).toPrecoverage Y ∧
                S ∈ Precoverage.finite (D.C i) Y ∧ R = h ▸ S.map (u i)) ∧
        -- Stacks 00VH (1): a one-element family given by an isomorphism is a covering
        K.HasIsos ∧
        -- Stacks 00VH (2): coverings are stable under refinement by coverings
        (∀ (X : C) (R : Presieve X) (T : ∀ ⦃Y : C⦄ (f : Y ⟶ X), R f → Presieve Y),
          R ∈ K X → (∀ ⦃Y : C⦄ (f : Y ⟶ X) (hf : R f), T f hf ∈ K Y) → R.bind T ∈ K X) ∧
        -- Stacks 00VH (3): the fibre products of a covering along any morphism exist, and cover
        ∃ _ : K.HasPullbacks,
          ∀ (X Y : C) (f : Y ⟶ X) (R : Presieve X) [R.HasPullbacks f],
            R ∈ K X → R.pullbackArrows f ∈ K Y
```

      ∀ (X : C) (R : Presieve X),
        R ∈ K X ↔
          ∃ (i : D.I) (Y : D.C i) (h : (u i).obj Y = X) (S : Presieve Y),
            S ∈ (D.J i).toPrecoverage Y ∧ S ∈ Precoverage.finite (D.C i) Y ∧ R = h ▸ S.map (u i))
    -- the sheaf `F` on `C`, and the canonical maps `φ_a : f_a^{-1}f_{i,*}F → f_{j,*}F` in
    -- adjoint form: `ψ a` is the identity `f_{i,*}F → f_{a,*}f_{j,*}F`
    (F : Sheaf K.toGrothendieck (Type w))
    (ψ : ∀ {i j : D.I} (a : j ⟶ i), (u i).op ⋙ F.obj ⟶ (D.u a).op ⋙ ((u j).op ⋙ F.obj))
    (hψ :
      ∀ {i j : D.I} (a : j ⟶ i) (V : D.C i) (x : F.obj.obj (op ((u i).obj V))),
        (ψ a).app (op V) x =
          F.obj.map (eqToHom (congrArg op (Functor.congr_obj (hu a) V).symm)) x)
    -- a sheaf `H` on `C` and a family of maps `f_{i,*}F → f_{i,*}H` compatible with the `φ_a`
    (H : Sheaf K.toGrothendieck (Type w))
    (g : ∀ i, (u i).op ⋙ F.obj ⟶ (u i).op ⋙ H.obj)
    (hgcomp :
      ∀ {i j : D.I} (a : j ⟶ i) (V : D.C i) (x : F.obj.obj (op ((u i).obj V))),
        (g i).app (op V) x =
          H.obj.map (eqToHom (congrArg op (Functor.congr_obj (hu a) V)))
            ((g j).app (op ((D.u a).obj V)) ((ψ a).app (op V) x)))
    (y : F ⟶ H)
    (hy : ∀ (i : D.I) (V : D.C i) (x : F.obj.obj (op ((u i).obj V))),
      (g i).app (op V) x = y.hom.app (op ((u i).obj V)) x) :
    y = pullbackPushforwardDesc D u hu hobj hhom _hhom_inj K _hK F ψ hψ H g hgcomp
