# Beyond the Bethe approximation of the permanent

> Constructs a deterministic polynomial-time approximation algorithm for the permanent of every nonnegative rational matrix, with exponential base strictly smaller than the square root of two. The development includes stable-polynomial inequalities, rational convex optimization, finite-precision arithmetic, and a Turing-machine complexity proof.
