# RestrictedProduct.relabelIso

Let $V$ and $W$ be index sets, and let $f:W\to V$ be a map with finite fibres. Let $X_v$ be sets, with subsets $C_v$, let $Y_w$ be sets with subsets $D_w$, and say for all $v\in V$ we're given a bijection $X_v\to\prod_{w|f(w)=v}Y_w$, identifying $C_v$ with $\prod_{w:f(w)=v}D_w$. Then there's an induced bijection between the restricted products $\prod'_v(X_v,C_v)$ and $\prod'_w(Y_w,D_w)$.
