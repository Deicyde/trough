# Topos-theoretic bridges for presheaf topoi

> Morita equivalence of small categories, defined as equivalence of their presheaf topoi, is characterised by equivalence of Karoubi envelopes, through the retracts of representable presheaves. The presheaf topos of a small category is Boolean exactly when the category is a groupoid, and De Morgan exactly when it satisfies the right Ore condition; both properties are invariant under Morita equivalence. An equivalence of the sheaf topoi of two small sites gives an order isomorphism between the Grothendieck topologies finer than the two given ones, matching their sheaf conditions. The subtopoi of a sheaf topos correspond to the finer topologies, and the essential points of a presheaf topos form a category equivalent to the opposite of the Karoubi envelope. For geometric theories, the categorical semantics in sheaf topoi is developed; every geometric theory has a classifying site, unique up to equivalence of topoi, on which every subobject of a power of the generic model is definable; the quotients of a theory correspond to the topologies finer than its classifying one, and the quotients of Morita-equivalent theories correspond. Geometric formulas translate into the infinitary logic of Tau Ceti with the same satisfaction, and the existence half of Diaconescu's theorem is proved, extending every internally flat continuous functor from a site to a sheaf topos to the inverse image of a geometric morphism; as an application, G-sets classify G-torsors, whose theories are Morita equivalent exactly for isomorphic groups. These are instances of O. Caramello's topos-theoretic bridges, an invariant of a topos read on two of its presentations.
