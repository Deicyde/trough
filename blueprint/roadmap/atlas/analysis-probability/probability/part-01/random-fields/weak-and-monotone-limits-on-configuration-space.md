## Weak convergence of probability measures on configuration space

Weak convergence of a sequence of probability measures on `ConfigSpace E`: convergence in the
topology of `ProbabilityMeasure (ConfigSpace E)`, whose open sets are generated by the integrals of
bounded continuous functions.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/random-fields/weak-and-monotone-limits-on-configuration-space/index.html#weak-converges-to).

## Weak convergence implies convergence of integrals

## Convergence of integrals implies weak convergence

If the integral of every bounded continuous function converges, the measures converge
weakly.

## Weak limit of an increasing chain dominates its terms

## Stochastic domination from below passes to weak limits

If `ρ ≼ μₙ` for all `n` and `μₙ → ν` weakly, then `ρ(A) ≤ ν(A)` for every increasing clopen
event `A`.

## Probability measures agreeing on clopen sets are equal

Two probability measures on `ConfigSpace E` that agree on clopen sets are equal: the clopen
cylinder events form a generating π-system.

Formalized by `StatMech.ProbabilityMeasure.ext_of_forall_isClopen`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean, line 175](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean#L175). ## Subsequential weak limits are determined by clopen-event limits

If the probabilities of clopen events converge to `L`, every subsequential weak limit `ξ`
satisfies `ξ(A) = L(A)` on clopen `A`.

Formalized by `StatMech.isClopen_apply_eq_of_subseq_tendsto`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean, line 192](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean#L192). ## Convergence on clopen events implies weak convergence

If the probability of every clopen event converges along a sequence of probability measures on
`ConfigSpace E`, the sequence converges weakly: all subsequential limits (which exist by
Prokhorov) agree on clopen sets, hence coincide.

Formalized by `StatMech.tendsto_weakly_of_forall_isClopen_tendsto`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean, line 208](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/Probability/Configuration/MonotoneLimit.lean#L208). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/random-fields/weak-and-monotone-limits-on-configuration-space/index.html#tendsto-weakly-of-forall-is-clopen-tends).
