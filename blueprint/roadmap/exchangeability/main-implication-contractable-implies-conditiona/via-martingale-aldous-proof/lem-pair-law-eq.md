# Pair law equality for contractable sequences

For a contractable sequence and $k \le m$, the joint distribution of $(X_k, X_{m+1}, X_{m+2}, \ldots)$ equals that of $(X_m, X_{m+1}, X_{m+2}, \ldots)$.
