# htpy_jet_sec_loc

A $1$-jet section from $E$ to $F$ is a function from $E$ to $F × \operatorname{Hom}(E, F)$. A homotopy of $1$-jet sections is a smooth map $\mathcal{F} : ℝ × E → F × \operatorname{Hom}(E, F)$.
