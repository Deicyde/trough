Zeta cubed: $\zeta(s)^3 = \zeta(2s) \sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\tau(n^2) n^{-s}$ for $\Re(s) > 1$.
  \begin{verbatim}
  This is IK (1.30).
  \end{verbatim}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\uses{d_apply_prime_pow,d,LSeries.term_isMultiplicative_if_fun_isMultiplicative,LSeries_d_summable,d_isMultiplicative,LSeriesSummable_two_pow_omega,Complex.one_add_prime_cpow_ne_zero,sumOnPrimePows_apply,sumOnPrimePows}
\leanok
Direct Euler-product proof (independent of Ramanujan / Baby Rankin--Selberg).
  Locally $\tau((p^k)^2)=\tau(p^{2k})=2k+1$, and $\sum_k(2k+1)x^k=(1+x)/(1-x)^2$ for $|x|<1$.
  Multiplying by the local factor $1/(1-x^2)$ of $\zeta(2s)$ recovers $1/(1-x)^3$, the local
  factor of $\zeta(s)^3$. Absolute convergence follows by comparing $\tau(n^2)\le d_3(n)$.
\end{proof}
