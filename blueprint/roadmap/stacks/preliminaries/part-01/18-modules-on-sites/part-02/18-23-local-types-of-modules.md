> A sheaf of modules on a site is **locally generated by sections** when every object has a
> covering on whose members the restricted module admits generating global sections.  The
> covering of an object `U` is recorded as a family of objects of the slice site over `U` which
> covers its terminal object.  This is Stacks 03DL(3).
