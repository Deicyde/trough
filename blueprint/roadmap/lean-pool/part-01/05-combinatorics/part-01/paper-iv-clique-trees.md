# Clique trees, subtree representations and chordal treewidth

> Develops rooted clique forests with the running-intersection property, constructs them from perfect elimination orders of finite chordal graphs, characterizes chordality by their existence, and obtains maximal-clique, separator, and counting results. Also characterizes chordality under retained-clique gluing of induced pieces, with exact vertex and edge inclusion-exclusion identities. The original library was extracted as a reusable byproduct of the published Paper IV formalization of the chordal-graph case of Erdős problem \#81; this library is not by itself a proof of that paper's partition bound. Gavril's characterization supplies exact finite-tree subtree representations and the converse to chordality. Finite subtree Helly places every finite clique in a bag of any tree decomposition and gives optimal chordal treewidth. The supplied representation includes an empty connector; no minimal-host or runtime bound is claimed.
