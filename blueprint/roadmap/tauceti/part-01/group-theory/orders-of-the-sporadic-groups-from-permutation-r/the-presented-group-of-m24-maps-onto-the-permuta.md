# The presented group of `M₂₄` maps onto the permutation model

The group defined by the
presentation of `M₂₄` carried by `TauCeti.SporadicName.presentation` has a surjective homomorphism
onto `M24`.

```lean
TauCeti.Sporadic.exists_surjective_M24 : ∃ f, Function.Surjective ⇑f
```
