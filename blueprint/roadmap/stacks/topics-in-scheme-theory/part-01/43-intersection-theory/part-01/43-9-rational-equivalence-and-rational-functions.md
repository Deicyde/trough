The attribute's comment: “both assertions, against the group of Section 43.8 (`Stacks.Chow.rationalEquivalencesByClosedPoints`, generated by `[W_a]_k - [W_b]_k` over ARBITRARY pairs of distinct closed points `a`, `b` of `P^1`): every principal divisor `div_W(f)` of a nonzero rational function on a `(k + 1)`-dimensional integral closed subscheme `W ⊆ X` lies in it, and the subgroup these principal divisors generate is all of it. `X` is a variety over an algebraically closed field `Kf`; principal divisors are pushed forward to cycles on `X` as `Stacks.Chow.principalDivisorPushforward`, and `principal divisor` is `Stacks.Chow.principalDivisorCycles`.”.

> **Stacks Project, Tag 0AZI.** Let `X` be a variety over an algebraically closed field, and
> let `k` be an integer. The principal divisor `div_V(f)` of a nonzero rational function `f` on an
> integral closed subscheme `V ⊆ X` of dimension `k + 1`, viewed as a `k`-cycle on `X`, is
> rationally equivalent to zero in the sense of Section 43.8; conversely these principal divisors
> generate the group of `k`-cycles rationally equivalent to zero in that sense.

```lean
theorem principalDivisorCycles_subset_and_closure_eq_rationalEquivalencesByClosedPoints
    (Kf : Type u) [Field Kf] [IsAlgClosed Kf] (C : ChowSituation.{u})
    (hC : C.S = AlgebraicGeometry.Spec (CommRingCat.of Kf)) {X : Scheme.{u}}
    (p : X ⟶ C.S) [LocallyOfFiniteType p]
    [AlgebraicGeometry.IsVariety Kf (p ≫ eqToHom hC)] (k : ℤ) :
    principalDivisorCycles C p k ⊆ rationalEquivalencesByClosedPoints Kf C hC p k ∧
      AddSubgroup.closure (principalDivisorCycles C p k) =
        rationalEquivalencesByClosedPoints Kf C hC p k
```
