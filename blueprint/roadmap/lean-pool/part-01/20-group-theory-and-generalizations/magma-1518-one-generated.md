# One-generated (1518 + 3862)-magmas are trivial or the Z/3 shift

> Proves, in core Lean, a classification conjectured by Terence Tao in the Equational Theories Project: in every magma satisfying ETP laws 1518 (x = (y ◇ y) ◇ (x ◇ (y ◇ x))) and 3862 (x ◇ x = (x ◇ (x ◇ x)) ◇ x), the set {x, S x, S (S x)} with S x = x ◇ x is closed, carries the table u ◇ v = S v, and satisfies S (S (S x)) = x, so every one-generated such magma is the trivial magma or the three-element cyclic shift x ◇ y = y + 1. No finiteness is assumed. Tao stated the conjecture for 1518 with all four of laws 47, 614, 817 and 3862; Bolan observed that 1518 and 3862 already imply the other three, so the hypotheses here are equivalent to his. The project also checks by kernel evaluation two finite magmas that satisfy 1518 and violate all four targets (15 and 39 elements), members of an explicit family of extensions of the shift; the 15-element example uses the square root 2 of −1 in F_5. The ETP countermodel Refutation939 corresponds to the conjugate member using the square root 3. The general family is not formalized.
