# IsDedekindDomain.FiniteAdeleRing.IntegraltensorProductAlgEquiv\_aux1

The natural map $B\otimes_A\mathbb{A}_K^\infty\to R$ is a $B$-algebra isomorphism.

## Depends on

- [IsDedekindDomain.pi\_tensorProduct](isdedekinddomain-pi-tensorproduct.md)

## Proof

This follows from the previous corollary and the fact that tensor products commute with filtered colimits.
