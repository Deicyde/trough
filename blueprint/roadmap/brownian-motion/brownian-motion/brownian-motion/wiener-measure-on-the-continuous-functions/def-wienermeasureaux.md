# Auxiliary Wiener measure

The pushforward of the measure $P_B$ of Definition [gaussianLimit](../../projective-family-of-the-brownian-motion/projective-family-of-gaussian-measures/def-gaussianlimit.md) by the Brownian motion $B$ is a measure on the continuous functions on $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{R}_+}$, with the sigma-algebra induced by the product sigma-algebra on $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{R}_+}$.
