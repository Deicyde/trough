## Presentations counted by Arzhantseva and Olshanskii

For natural numbers $m$, $n$ and $d$,
this is the set of $n$-tuples $(r_1, \dots, r_n)$ of elements of the free group $F_m$ on
generators $x_1, \dots, x_m$ such that the reduced word of each $r_i$ is cyclically reduced (the
empty word counts as cyclically reduced) and the reduced word lengths satisfy
$|r_1| + \dots + |r_n| = d$. Each tuple stands for the presentation
$\langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r_1, \dots, r_n \rangle$.

## Groups whose subgroups with fewer than $m$ generators are free

For a natural number $m$,
a group $G$ has this property if, for every finite set $S \subseteq G$ with fewer than $m$
elements, the subgroup of $G$ generated by $S$ is a free group.

## Olshanskii's genericity problem (Kourovka 11.75)

This is the proposition, in the form
proved by Arzhantseva and Olshanskii, that the following holds for all natural numbers $m \ge 2$
and $n \ge 1$. Let $F_m$ be the free group on $x_1, \dots, x_m$. For each $d$, let $N_d$ be the
number of $n$-tuples $(r_1, \dots, r_n)$ of elements of $F_m$ whose reduced words are cyclically
reduced and have total length $|r_1| + \dots + |r_n| = d$, and let $N_d^f$ be the number of those
tuples for which every subgroup of $\langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r_1, \dots, r_n \rangle$
generated by fewer than $m$ elements is free. Then $N_d^f / N_d \to 1$ as $d \to \infty$.

## Generically, subgroups with fewer generators are free (Kourovka 11.75)

Let $m \ge 2$ and
$n \ge 1$ be natural numbers and let $F_m$ be the free group on $x_1, \dots, x_m$. For each $d$,
let $N_d$ be the number of $n$-tuples $(r_1, \dots, r_n)$ of elements of $F_m$ whose reduced words
are cyclically reduced and have total length $|r_1| + \dots + |r_n| = d$, and let $N_d^f$ be the
number of those tuples for which every subgroup of
$\langle x_1, \dots, x_m \mid r_1, \dots, r_n \rangle$ generated by fewer than $m$ elements is free.
Then $N_d^f / N_d \to 1$ as $d \to \infty$.

Resolved true: Arzhantseva and Olshanskii prove this; the Kourovka Archive records 11.75 as proved
by this paper.
