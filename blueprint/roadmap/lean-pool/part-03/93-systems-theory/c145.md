# Exact single-port graph identification from two response derivatives

> Identifies a weighted graph Laplacian and signed port up to simultaneous vertex relabeling from the first and mixed second terminal response derivatives in all smooth temporal directions at one positive horizon. The general result assumes a common known positive decay, connected symmetric nonnegative weighted graphs, cyclic ports, and a separation, spectral-band and contraction criterion on the source. The project also identifies one explicit six-vertex source under a separate set of assumptions.
