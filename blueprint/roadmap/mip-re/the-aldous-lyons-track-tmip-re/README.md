# The Aldous–Lyons track: $\mathrm{TMIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$

This chapter is the plan and the progress of a second target: the main theorem of Bowen, Chapman and Vidick's *The Aldous–Lyons Conjecture II: Undecidability* (BCV25), a computable reduction from the halting problem to *tailored* non-local games that sends halting machines to games with a perfect Z-aligned permutation strategy commuting along edges, and non-halting machines to games of value at most $\frac12$. With the companion paper (BCLV24) it refutes the Aldous–Lyons conjecture. The estimate, the reuse map and the phases are in `planning/aldous-lyons-track.md`. There is no adversarial-verification ledger for these papers: the paper, at its arXiv version, is the authority on the mathematics, and line numbers II:$n$ below refer to its LaTeX source.

*Status.* Phase 0: the definitions and the statement are formalized (`MIPRE/TailoredGameValue.lean` and `MIPRE/Tailored/`), and one lemma is proved, that the definitions are not vacuous (Lemma [The trivial strategy; II:1148](part-01/lem-tailored-trivial-zpc.md)). Phase 1, first part: the toolbox of permutation strategies is proved — signed permutations (Lemma [Signed permutation matrices](part-01/lem-signed-perm.md)), the Fourier transform and data processing (Lemmas [The Fourier transform; II:974–1006](part-01/lem-fourier-pvm.md) and [Data processing; Claims II:2902 and II:2918](part-01/lem-data-processing.md)), the criterion for a permutation strategy to be perfect (Lemma [When a permutation strategy is perfect](part-01/lem-perm-strategy-perfect.md)), Lemma [ZPC strategies are PCC strategies](part-01/lem-zpc-pcc.md), and the Magic Square (Proposition [The Magic Square game; Examples II:1300–1370](part-01/prop-magic-square-zpc.md)). Phase 1, second part: a tailored verifier is a normal form verifier, the canonical decider written as a program, with the same games, values and completeness (Lemmas [The canonical decider decides the $n$-th game](part-01/lem-canonical-decider.md) and [A tailored verifier and its normal form verifier have the same games](part-01/lem-of-tnfv.md)). Phase 1, third part: the halting protocol at a fixed level is proved for a search program meeting its specification (Theorem [The tailored halting theorem at a fixed level; II:2021–2066](part-02/thm-tailored-halting-level.md)). Phase 1, fourth part: the tabulation, the search program and the halting reduction (Theorem [Halting from tailored compression; II:1887–2066](part-02/thm-tailored-halting.md)), and $\mathrm{TMIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$ for the computable class (Theorem [$\mathrm{TMIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$, as classes; II:787, II:1838, II:6997](part-02/thm-tmipstar-eq-re.md)). Phase 1, last part: $\mathrm{TMIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$ for the paper's polynomial-time class (Theorem [$\mathrm{TMIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$ for the polynomial-time class; II:787, II:1505](part-02/thm-tmipstar-poly-eq-re.md)). So Phase 1 is proved, with a tailored gap compression (Definition [Tailored gap compression, value form; II:1852](part-02/def-tailored-compression.md)) as its one hypothesis. Phase 2: the $k$-fold tailored product, the tensor powers of permutation strategies, completeness and soundness of repeated tailored verifiers for programs meeting their specification (Theorem [Completeness and soundness of repeated tailored verifiers](part-03/thm-tailored-rep-from-spec.md)), and the programs themselves with their running times, so that parallel repetition of tailored verifiers is proved (Theorem [Parallel repetition of tailored verifiers; II:11103, direct form](part-03/thm-tailored-rep.md)). The other results below carry no proof marks; they are the targets of Phases 3–5.

The formalization departs from the paper's text in the places Remark Where the formalization departs from the paper lists, all of them choices this project made for $\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$ already: soundness is proved in value form, against all finite-dimensional strategies, with direct parallel repetition, so that neither the anchored repetition theorem of (BVY17) nor the almost-synchronous rounding of (Vid22) is needed.
