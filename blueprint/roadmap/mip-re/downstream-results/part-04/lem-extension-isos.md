# Isomorphisms between extensions

The following pairs of bipartite models are isomorphic (Definition [Ancilla extension of a bipartite model](../part-02/def-ancilla-extension.md)).

1. The extension of a model by one-point registers in the vector $1$, and the model.
2. The extension in $e'$ of the extension in $e$, and the extension by the product registers in the product vector; the extension in $e$, and the extension in $e$ relabelled along bijections of the two registers; and the extension in $e$ with the players exchanged, and the extension of the exchanged model in the exchanged vector, which has the norm of $e$.
3. For an isomorphism from $M$ to $M'$, the extensions of $M$ and of $M'$ in one vector: the unitary acts on each copy of the Hilbert space, and each player's isomorphism entrywise.
4. The extension in $e$ of the tensor-product model of $\xi$, and the tensor-product model of the expanded vector $e \otimes \xi$; and the tensor-product model of $\psi$ on $\mathbb{C}^{d_A} \otimes \mathbb{C}^{d_B}$, and that of $\psi$ relabelled along bijections of $d_A$ and $d_B$, each player's matrices relabelled along their factor.

## Proof

The local isometries of Lemmas [Moving registers along local isometries](../part-03/lem-ancilla-isometries.md) and [The ancilla extension of a tensor-product model](../part-03/lem-tensor-expand.md) carry the state to the state and have inverses of the same kind. The algebra isomorphisms are three: the one-by-one matrices over an algebra are the algebra; relabelling the rows and columns of square matrices along a bijection is a $*$-algebra isomorphism; and so is applying a $*$-isomorphism to the entries. A relabelling of the factors of a tensor-product model permutes the coordinates of $\mathbb{C}^{d_A \times d_B}$.
