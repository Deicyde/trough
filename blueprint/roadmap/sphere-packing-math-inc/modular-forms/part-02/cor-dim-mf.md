# dim-mf

We have

$$
\begin{aligned}
\dim M_2(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 0,\qquad\text{(9)}\\
\dim M_4(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 1,\qquad\text{(10)}\\
\dim M_6(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 1,\qquad\text{(11)}\\
\dim M_8(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 1,\qquad\text{(12)}\\
\dim S_4(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 0,\qquad\text{(13)}\\
\dim S_6(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 0,\qquad\text{(14)}\\
\dim S_8(\mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z})) &= 0.\qquad\text{(15)}\end{aligned}
$$
