# The decoded strategy

The decoding of a readable and a linear polynomial at an input question is the table of the readable one on the Boolean cube, cut to the question's readable length, followed by the linear one's. The decoded strategy is the strategy for the typed oracularized game of the input, on the state of $T$, in which an isolated role measures its polynomial measurement and answers the decoding of its two polynomials, read on their slots' blocks, and the oracle measures its own and answers the pair of decodings of its polynomials in the two roles' slots.
