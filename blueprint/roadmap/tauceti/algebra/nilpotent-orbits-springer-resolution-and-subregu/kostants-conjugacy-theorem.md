# Kostant's conjugacy theorem

Two `sl₂` triples `(h, e, f)` and `(h', e, f')` with the same
nilpositive element in a finite-dimensional Lie algebra over a field of characteristic zero are
conjugate by an automorphism of `L` that fixes `e` and lies in the subgroup
`TauCeti.expAdSubgroup K e` generated by the automorphisms `exp (ad y)` with
`y ∈ ker (ad e) ⊓ [e, L]`.

```lean
IsSl2Triple.exists_lieEquiv_apply_eq.{u_1, u_2} {K : Type u_1} {L : Type u_2} [Field K] [CharZero K]
  [LieRing L] [LieAlgebra K L] [FiniteDimensional K L] {h e f : L} [LieAlgebra ℚ L]
  (t : IsSl2Triple h e f) {h' f' : L} (t' : IsSl2Triple h' e f') :
  ∃ φ, φ.toLinearEquiv ∈ TauCeti.expAdSubgroup K e ∧ φ e = e ∧ φ h = h' ∧ φ f = f'
```
