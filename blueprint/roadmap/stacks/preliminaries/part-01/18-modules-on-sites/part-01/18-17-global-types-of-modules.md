> A sheaf of modules is **free** when it is a coproduct of copies of the structure sheaf,
> that is, when some family of global sections exhibits it as such a coproduct.  The coproduct
> is specified by its universal property, so no sheafification instance is needed.  This is
> Stacks 03DE (1).

**`SheafOfModules.IsPresentedByGlobalSections`**, a def in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/GlobalTypesOfModules.lean, line 137](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/GlobalTypesOfModules.lean#L137). The attribute's comment: “global presentation, at a single universe of sections”.

> A sheaf of modules **has a global presentation** when it is a cokernel of a map between two
> coproducts of copies of the structure sheaf, that is, when it is the right-hand term of an exact
> sequence `⊕_{j ∈ K} O ⟶ ⊕_{i ∈ I} O ⟶ F ⟶ 0`.  The coproducts and the cokernel are specified by
> their universal properties, so no sheafification instance is needed.  This is Stacks 03DE (6).

```lean
def IsPresentedByGlobalSections (F : SheafOfModules.{w} R) : Prop
```
