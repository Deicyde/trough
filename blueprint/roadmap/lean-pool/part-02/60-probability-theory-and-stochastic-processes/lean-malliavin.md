# Malliavin calculus and the Clark–Ocone representation

> Constructs the Malliavin derivative, Gaussian integration by parts, closability, Wiener chaos, Brownian Itô integration and martingale representation. The Clark–Ocone endpoint uses a generating pre-Brownian process with continuous linear coordinates on a separable real Gaussian Banach space, its natural filtration, and pairs in the closed Malliavin graph; the conditional-expectation sections agree almost everywhere at positive times.
