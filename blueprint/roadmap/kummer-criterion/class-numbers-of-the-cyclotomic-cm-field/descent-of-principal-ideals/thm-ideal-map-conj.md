# ideal-map-conj

If $I$ is an ideal of $\mathcal O_{K^+}$, then the extended ideal $I\mathcal O_K$ is fixed by complex conjugation.

## Depends on

- [cm-real-subfield](../the-real-subfield-and-the-class-numbers/def-cm-real-subfield.md)

## Proof

The extension $I\mathcal O_K$ is generated by elements coming from $K^+$, and those elements are fixed by conjugation. Conjugation is a ring automorphism of $\mathcal O_K$, so it maps $I\mathcal O_K$ onto the ideal generated by the conjugates of the generators, which is $I\mathcal O_K$ itself.
