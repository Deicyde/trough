A small prime $L < p \leq \sqrt{n}$ can be in deficit by at most
  $M\log n$.
\end{sublemma}
\begin{proof}
\uses{factorization-def}
\leanok
Routine computation using Legendre's formula, noting that at most
  $\log n / \log 2$ powers of $p$ divide any given number up to $n$.
\end{proof}
