# CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_connectedComponentOfOne

**The image of Spin is the identity component of `SO(Q)`** under the hypotheses of
`isOpen_ker_orthogonalSpinorNorm`, as soon as the Spin group is generated by its identity
component together with the kernel of the Spin action, for instance when it is connected.

```lean
CliffordAlgebra.range_spinToSpecialOrthogonal_eq_connectedComponentOfOne.{u_1, u_2} {K : Type u_1}
  {V : Type u_2} [NontriviallyNormedField K] [LocallyCompactSpace K] [Invertible 2] [AddCommGroup V]
  [_root_.Module K V] [FiniteDimensional K V] (Q : QuadraticForm K V)
  (hQ : QuadraticMap.Nondegenerate) (hsq : IsOpen ↑(Subgroup.square Kˣ))
  (hgen :
    Subgroup.connectedComponentOfOne ↥(spinGroup Q) ⊔
        (CliffordAlgebra.spinToSpecialOrthogonal Q).ker =
      ⊤) :
  (CliffordAlgebra.spinToSpecialOrthogonal Q).range =
    Subgroup.connectedComponentOfOne ↥(TauCeti.QuadraticMap.specialOrthogonalGroup Q)
```
