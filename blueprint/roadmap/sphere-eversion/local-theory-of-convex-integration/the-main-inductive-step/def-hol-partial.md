# hol_partial

Let $E'$ be a linear subspace of $E$. A map $\mathcal{F} = (f, φ) : E → F × \operatorname{Hom}(E, F)$ is $E'$–holonomic if, for every $v$ in $E'$ and every $x$, $Df(x)v = φ(x)v$.
