## `WrittenOnTheWallII.GraphConjecture314.conjecture314`

Solved: stated here; a Lean proof is at [https://github.com/glyaea/wow-ii-314/blob/7b35f1252e4aaae3c787faf8091f9c7cd4edc41f/solution.lean](https://github.com/glyaea/wow-ii-314/blob/7b35f1252e4aaae3c787faf8091f9c7cd4edc41f/solution.lean).

> WOWII [Conjecture 314](http://cms.uhd.edu/faculty/delavinae/research/wowII/all.html#conj314):
>
> For every finite simple connected graph $G$ with $n > 1$ vertices,
> if $G$ is triangle-free and $\mathrm{path}(G) \le 4$, then $G$ is well totally
> dominated.
>
> Here $\mathrm{path}(G) = \mathrm{largestInducedPathSize}\, G$ is the **size of a
> largest induced path** in $G$, defined locally above.
>
> **Disambiguation.** Earlier revisions of this file used the `SimpleGraph.path`
> invariant, but that is the *floor of the average distance*, not the size of a
> largest induced path — a different quantity that makes Conjecture 314 vacuous
> in many cases.
>
> **Proof summary.** In a connected, $K_{3}$-free, induced-$P_{5}$-free graph $G$:
>
> 1. No minimal total dominating set has fewer than two vertices.
> 2. No minimal total dominating set has more than three vertices.
> 3. Minimal total dominating sets of sizes two and three cannot coexist.
>
> Thus, each minimal total dominating set in $G$ has the same size.
>
> The Lean proof was generated by OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol.

```lean
theorem conjecture314 [Nontrivial α] (G : SimpleGraph α) [DecidableRel G.Adj]
    (hG : G.Connected)
    (hTriFree : ∀ a b c : α, G.Adj a b → G.Adj b c → G.Adj c a → False)
    (hPath : largestInducedPathSize G ≤ 4) :
    IsWellTotallyDominated G
```
