## Generating relation of a direct limit

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of
topological spaces indexed by a preorder $I$, with maps $f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$ for $i \le j$
forming a directed system ($f_{ii}$ is the identity and $f_{jk} \circ f_{ij} = f_{ik}$ for
$i \le j \le k$). This relation on the disjoint union $\bigsqcup_{i \in I} X_i$ relates
$(i, x)$ to $(j, f_{ij}(x))$ for all $i \le j$ and $x \in X_i$, and relates no other pairs.

## Direct limit equivalence relation

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a family of topological
spaces indexed by a preorder $I$, with maps $f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$ for $i \le j$ forming a
directed system. This is the smallest equivalence relation on the disjoint union
$\bigsqcup_{i \in I} X_i$ that relates $(i, x)$ to $(j, f_{ij}(x))$ for all $i \le j$ and
$x \in X_i$.

## Direct limit of topological spaces

Let $I$ be a preorder, $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ a family
of topological spaces, and $f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$ for $i \le j$ continuous maps forming a
directed system ($f_{ii}$ is the identity and $f_{jk} \circ f_{ij} = f_{ik}$ for
$i \le j \le k$). The direct limit $\varinjlim X_i$ is the quotient of the disjoint union
$\bigsqcup_{i \in I} X_i$ by the equivalence relation generated by $(i, x) \sim (j, f_{ij}(x))$
for $i \le j$ and $x \in X_i$, with the quotient topology of the disjoint union topology.

The directed-system laws and the continuity of the transition maps are hypotheses of the
construction: the underlying quotient does not use them, but without them it need not be a
direct limit of topological spaces.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Topology/general-topology/direct-limits-of-topological-spaces/index.html#space).

## Canonical map into the direct limit

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a directed system of
topological spaces over a preorder $I$ with continuous transition maps
$f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$. For $i \in I$, the canonical map $\iota_i : X_i \to \varinjlim X_i$
into the direct limit sends $x$ to the class of $(i, x)$.

## Canonical maps into the direct limit are continuous

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a
directed system of topological spaces over a preorder $I$ with continuous transition maps
$f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$. For every $i \in I$, the canonical map
$\iota_i : X_i \to \varinjlim X_i$ into the direct limit is continuous.

## Canonical maps commute with the transition maps

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a directed
system of topological spaces over a preorder $I$ with continuous transition maps
$f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$. The canonical maps $\iota_i : X_i \to \varinjlim X_i$ into the direct
limit satisfy $\iota_j(f_{ij}(x)) = \iota_i(x)$ for all $i \le j$ and $x \in X_i$.

## Map out of the direct limit

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a directed system of
topological spaces over a preorder $I$ with continuous transition maps
$f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$, and let $Y$ be a topological space. A family of maps
$\psi_i : X_i \to Y$ with $\psi_j(f_{ij}(x)) = \psi_i(x)$ for all $i \le j$ and $x \in X_i$
induces the map $\varinjlim X_i \to Y$ from the direct limit that sends the class of $(i, x)$
to $\psi_i(x)$.

## The induced map out of the direct limit is continuous

Let $(X_i)_{i \in I}$ be a
directed system of topological spaces over a preorder $I$ with continuous transition maps
$f_{ij} : X_i \to X_j$, and let $Y$ be a topological space. If the maps
$\psi_i : X_i \to Y$ are continuous and satisfy $\psi_j(f_{ij}(x)) = \psi_i(x)$ for all
$i \le j$ and $x \in X_i$, then the induced map $\varinjlim X_i \to Y$ from the direct limit,
sending the class of $(i, x)$ to $\psi_i(x)$, is continuous.
