# Automorphisms of the bounded operators

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.HilbertSpace.Dynamics.Automorphism`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/Dynamics/Automorphism.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/Dynamics/Automorphism.lean): 11 declarations (1 instance with no name), 10 with a docstring.

> ⋆-automorphisms of bounded operators are unitary conjugations; conjugation flows are unique.

## i. Overview

> Every `⋆`-automorphism of the bounded operators on a Hilbert space is conjugation `A ↦ U A U⋆` by a
> unitary, and two unitaries give the same automorphism exactly when they differ by a phase. So the
> automorphism group is the projective unitary group.
>
> A norm-continuous unitary one-parameter group `U` acts by `αₜ(a) = U(t) a U(t)⋆`. This flow is the
> unique solution of the Heisenberg equation `d/dt αₜ(a) = ⁅αₜ(a), i G⁆` with `α₀ = id`, where `G` is
> the generator of `U` and the bracket is the ring commutator.

## ii. Key results

> - `conjStarAlgAut_surjective` : every `⋆`-automorphism is unitary conjugation.
> - `conjStarAlgAut_eq_iff` : two unitaries give the same automorphism iff they differ by a phase.
> - `projectiveUnitaryEquivStarAlgAut` : the automorphism group is the projective unitary group.
> - `UnitaryOneParameterGroup.toAutomorphism_unique` : the conjugation flow is the unique solution of
>   the Heisenberg equation.
