## Sambale's question on the order of $2$-groups of bounded abelian rank (Kourovka 19.75)

The statement that for all natural numbers $e$ and $r$, every finite $2$-group $P$ of exponent
$2^e$ in which every abelian subgroup is generated by at most $r$ elements has order
$|P| \le 2^{r(e+1)}$.

## Sambale's order bound for $2$-groups of bounded abelian rank fails (Kourovka 19.75)

There
exist natural numbers $e$ and $r$ and a finite $2$-group $P$ of exponent $2^e$ in which every
abelian subgroup is generated by at most $r$ elements, such that $|P| > 2^{r(e+1)}$.

The Kourovka Archive answers 19.75 "No, it is not true", as follows from Olshanskii's examples of
finite $p$-groups whose abelian subgroups are small compared to the group (pointed out by Mann).
