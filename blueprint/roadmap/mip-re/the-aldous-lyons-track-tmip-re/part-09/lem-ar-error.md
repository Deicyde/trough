# The soundness error and the contract's loss

There is $E_{2,0}$ such that for constants with $E_2 \geq E_{2,0}$ the field-size conditions of Theorem [Completeness and soundness of the output verifier](../part-08/thm-ar-output.md) hold, and there are $a \geq 1$ and $C$ with $24 \sqrt{e(16^9 \varepsilon)} \leq \delta(\varepsilon, n)$, $\delta$ the loss of the answer reduction contract with $b = c_{\mathrm{LD}}/2$, or $\delta \geq 1$, for $n \geq \max(C, 2)$, $\lambda, \sigma \geq 1$.

## Depends on

- [The parameters of the answer reduction](../part-08/def-ar-params.md)
- [Soundness of the answer-reduced game](../part-07/thm-ar-sound.md)
- [Answer-reduction quantitative assembly](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-08/lem-ar-error-assembly.md)
- [First facts about the parameters](../part-08/lem-ar-params-basic.md)

## Proof

The error is at most $2^j$ times the simultaneous test's errors and the field terms; each of these is at most a polynomial in $2^j$ times $\varepsilon^{c_{\mathrm{LD}}} + q^{-c_{\mathrm{LD}}} + 2^{-c Q}$, and $q = 2^t$ with $t \geq E_{2,0} (j + Q + 1)$ makes the field terms at most $2^{-(2Q + 9)}$. With $m$ polynomial in $Q \sigma$, the threshold of the MIP\* answer reduction's error comparison concludes; for $\mu = 0$ or $\varepsilon \geq 1$ the loss is at least $1$.
