# b-d24

For $r>2$, define

$$
b(r) := -4\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi r^2}{2}\right)^{\!2}\int_{0}^{i\infty}\psi_I(z)e^{\pi i r^2 z}\,dz.\qquad\text{(5)}
$$
