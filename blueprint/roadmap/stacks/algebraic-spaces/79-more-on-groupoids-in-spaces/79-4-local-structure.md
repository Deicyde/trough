> **Stacks 0CK9 (Local structure).**  Let `(U, R, s, t, c, e, i)` be a groupoid in algebraic
> spaces over `S`, let `ū` be a geometric point of `U`, and let `A = 𝒪_{U, ū}`,
> `B = 𝒪_{R, e(ū)}` and `C = 𝒪_{R ×_{s, U, t} R, (e, e)(ū)}` be the étale local rings, with the
> homomorphisms `s, t : A → B`, `c : B → C`, `e : B → A`, `i : B → B` and `(e, e) : C → A` induced
> by the structure morphisms.  Then:
>
> 1. `A`, `B`, `C` are local rings and `s, t, c, e, i, (e, e)` are local homomorphisms;
> 2. `e ∘ t = 1` and `e ∘ s = 1`;
> 3. `B = s(A) ⊕ I = t(A) ⊕ I` for `I = ker (e : B → A)`;
> 4. `𝔪_B ⊗ B + B ⊗ 𝔪_B` is a maximal ideal of `B ⊗_{s, A, t} B`, the localization at it has
>    separably closed residue field, and `C` is the henselization of that localization through
>    the homomorphism induced by `b₁ ⊗ b₂ ↦ pr₁(b₁) pr₂(b₂)`;
> 5. the ideal `J` of `C` generated by `I ⊗ B + B ⊗ I` is the kernel of `(e, e) : C → A`;
> 6. `c` sends `I` into `J`.
