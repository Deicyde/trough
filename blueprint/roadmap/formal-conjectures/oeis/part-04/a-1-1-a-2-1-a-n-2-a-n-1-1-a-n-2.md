# $a(1)=1, a(2)=1, a(n)=(2^{a(n-1)} + 1)/a(n-2)$

> The sequence is defined by $a(1) = 1, a(2) = 1$, and for $n \ge 3$,
>
> $$a(n) = \frac{2^{a(n-1)} + 1}{a(n-2)}$$
>
