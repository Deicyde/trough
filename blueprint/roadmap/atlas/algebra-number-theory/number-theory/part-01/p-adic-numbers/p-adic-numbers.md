## Cassels' embedding theorem

Let $K$ be a field of characteristic zero and $S$ a finite
subset of $K$. Then there are a prime $p \ge 3$ and an injective ring homomorphism from the subring
of $K$ generated by $S$ into the ring $\mathbb{Z}_p$ of $p$-adic integers.

## Sources

- J. W. S. Cassels, "An embedding theorem for fields", Bull. Austral. Math. Soc. 14 (1976), 193–198.

## p-adic integrality is equivalent to norm at most one

An element of the algebraic closure of `ℚ_[p]` is integral over `ℤ_[p]`
if and only if its norm is at most one.

## Strassmann's theorem

Let $p$ be a prime and let $(b_m)_{m \ge 0}$ be a sequence in
$\mathbb{Q}_p$, not identically zero, with $b_m \to 0$. Then the power series
$\sum_{m \ge 0} b_m y^m$ has only finitely many zeros $y \in \mathbb{Q}_p$ with $|y|_p \le 1$.

## Sources

- R. Strassmann, J. Reine Angew. Math. 159 (1928).
