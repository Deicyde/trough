# Category theory toolkit

> A collection of general results in category theory. Any adjunction restricts to an equivalence between the objects fixed by its unit and those fixed by its counit; the four usual conditions defining an idempotent adjunction are equivalent, and then the fixed objects form a reflective and a coreflective subcategory. Additive functors out of the Karoubi envelope into an idempotent complete category are equivalent to additive functors out of the original category. A category is direct exactly when the relation "there is a non-identity morphism" is well-founded, and the semi-simplex category is direct. Restriction of representations along a group homomorphism is full iff the homomorphism is surjective, and an equivalence iff it is bijective. In abelian categories, a pullback with an epimorphism opposite is a pushout, and semi-cartesian squares (pushout-to-corner mono, equivalently corner-to-pullback epi) are characterized by exactness and as composites of a pushout and a pullback square. Finally, a category with zero morphisms and binary biproducts in which every morphism with zero kernel and zero cokernel is invertible is preadditive. Two supporting files package results Mathlib already proves in other forms, for use by these statements, Beck's monadicity and comonadicity theorems as equivalences (a variant of Mac Lane, Theorem VI.7.1, for an equivalence rather than an isomorphism of categories), and the exactness criteria for pullback and pushout squares in abelian categories.
