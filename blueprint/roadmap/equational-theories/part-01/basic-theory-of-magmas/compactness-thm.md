# Compactness theorem

Let $\Gamma$ be a theory, and let $E$ be a law. Then $\Gamma \models E$ if and only if there exists a finite subset $\Gamma'$ of $\Gamma$ such that $\Gamma' \models E$.

## Depends on

- [Models](models-def.md)

## Proof depends on

- [Birkhoff's completeness theorem](sound-complete.md)

## Proof

The claim is obvious for $\models$, and the claim then follows from [Birkhoff's completeness theorem](sound-complete.md).
