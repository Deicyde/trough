# The models of stages 4 and 5

Let $\mathcal{N}$ be a bipartite model and $I$ a finite set, the register of the analysis ($I = \mathbb{F}_q^{\{0,1\}^m}$, so that $\mathbb{C}^I = (\mathbb{C}^q)^{\otimes M}$). Let $T$ be the ancilla of the projective dilation of Lemma [Projective dilation against a fixed state, in a star-ordered ring](../../downstream-results/part-03/lem-kraus-dilation.md) for POVMs whose outcomes are the answers of the seeded CL test at $(q, 4m, d, 1)$, with $K + 1$ Kraus terms, and $t_0 \in T$ its reference state, at the zero answer. The registers are named as in the paper: $A$ is the first player's own space, $A'$ and $A''$ the two halves of an EPR pair on $I$, and $E_A$ a copy of $T$; likewise $B$, $B'$, $B''$ and $E_B$ for the second player. Every model below depends on the padding size $K$.

1. The *padded model* is the register model of $\mathcal{N}$ on $I$ (Definition [The register model](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-register-model.md)) extended by $E_A$ for the first player and $E_B$ for the second, in the basis state $|t_0\rangle \otimes |t_0\rangle$ (Definition [Ancilla extension of a bipartite model](../../downstream-results/part-02/def-ancilla-extension.md)). Up to the associativity of extensions it is the extension of $\mathcal{N}$ by the registers $(E_A, A')$ and $(E_B, A'')$ in the unit vector $|t_0 t_0\rangle \otimes |\textsc{EPR}_I\rangle$, so the seeded CL test is sound in it (Definition [The low-individual-degree test is sound in a model](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-lidt-sound-in.md)) whenever it is sound in every extension of $\mathcal{N}$ by a unit vector, and then also in the padded model of $\mathcal{N}$ with the players exchanged.
2. The *physical model* is the extension of $\mathcal{N}$ in which each player holds three registers, $(A'', E_A, A')$ and $(B'', E_B, B')$, in the *physical state*: each player's own EPR pair, $(A'', A')$ and $(B'', B')$, and the two padding registers in $|t_0\rangle \otimes |t_0\rangle$. The physical state is a unit vector, symmetric under the exchange of the two players' blocks, and each pair satisfies the mirror identity: an operator $P$ on the near half $A'$ acts on the state as $P^{\mathsf T}$ on the far half $A''$, whatever the other player applies, and likewise for $B'$ and $B''$.
3. The *first cut* is the physical model read (Definition [Readings of an extension](../../downstream-results/part-04/def-model-reading.md)) with the first player's far half $A''$ handed to the second player: the same Hilbert space, state and representation, the first player's operators being those of $(E_A, A')$ and the second player's those of $(A'', B'', E_B, B')$. The *second cut* is the first cut of $\mathcal{N}$ with the players exchanged, a reading of the same state.
4. The padded model and the register model embed in the first cut, along embeddings $j_1$ and $\iota_1$ (Definition [Embeddings of bipartite models](../../downstream-results/part-04/def-model-embedding.md)), local isometries that carry the state to the state and keep both units: $\iota_1$ is the inert extension of the register model by $|t_0 t_0\rangle$ followed by $j_1$, and it sends a first-player operator $Z$ of $A'$ to ${I}_{E_A} \otimes Z$.
5. *Item 1's cut* is the register model on $I$, its pair being $(A'', B'')$, of the extension of $\mathcal{N}$ by the padded registers $(E_A, A')$ and $(E_B, B')$. Regrouping the physical registers into it is a local isometry (Definition [Local isometries of bipartite models](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-local-isometry.md)) from the physical model, with no condition on the state.
6. The physical model of $\mathcal{N}$ with the players exchanged is the physical model of $\mathcal{N}$ with the players exchanged: the exchange of the two blocks of registers is an isomorphism of models between them (Definition [Isomorphisms of bipartite models](../../downstream-results/part-04/def-model-iso.md)), the identity on the players' algebras, because the physical state is symmetric.
