# Separating the two point variables

Let $(G_A, G_B)$, $\Phi$ and $\Delta$ be as in Lemma [Linearity in the two combining coefficients](lem-qld-global-linear.md), where now $\Delta$ bounds the estimate of Lemma [Ordered products with global outcome multipliers](lem-qld-global-products.md) for both product orders. Call an outcome $g$ good if $g = \alpha g_X(x) + \beta g_Z(z)$ with $g_X$ reading only the $x$ block and $g_Z$ only the $z$ block: every monomial of $g$ has exponents $(1, 0)$ on $(\alpha, \beta)$ and no other nonzero exponent outside the $x$ block, or exponents $(0, 1)$ and no other nonzero exponent outside the $z$ block. Let $\mathrm{Good}$ be the set of good outcomes. Then, with $\eta = (2 + 2d + 8md)/q$,

$$
(1 - 2\eta) \sum_{g \notin \mathrm{Good}} \langle G_{A,g} \otimes {I} \rangle_\Phi
\leq 4\Delta ,
$$

and likewise for $G_B$. In particular a good outcome reads no dummy coordinate, and the good outcomes are in bijection with the pairs $(g_X, g_Z)$ of polynomials of individual degree at most $d$ on $\mathbb{F}_q^m$. The Lean proves it for a global measurement in any bipartite model in a unit state, and for a `GlobalPair` in any model; the statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

An outcome that is not good is not linear in $(\alpha, \beta)$, or is linear, $g = \alpha g_1 + \beta g_2$, with $g_2$ reading a coordinate outside the $z$ block, or with $g_1$ reading a coordinate outside the $x$ block. The first two kinds are handled against the order $\widehat M^X_a \widehat M^Z_b$ and the third against $\widehat M^Z_b \widehat M^X_a$, each by the assembly of Lemma [Linearity in the two combining coefficients](lem-qld-global-linear.md): it suffices to show $\mathbb E_u \|(S \otimes B_u(g(u)))\Phi\|^2 \leq \eta \langle S \otimes {I} \rangle$ for $S = G_{A,g}$ and each bad $g$, the non-linear ones being Lemma [Linearity in the two combining coefficients](lem-qld-global-linear.md) itself. Since $1 - 2\eta$ may be taken nonnegative, the three weights add.

Fix $g = \alpha g_1 + \beta g_2$ linear with $g_2$ reading a coordinate outside the $z$ block, and a base point $u_0$; then $g(u_0[\alpha, \beta]) = \alpha g_1(u_0) + \beta g_2(u_0)$. The fibre $\{\alpha a + \beta b = \alpha g_1(u_0) + \beta g_2(u_0)\}$ always contains the diagonal pair $(g_1(u_0), g_2(u_0))$, and contains any other pair for at most $q$ of the $q^2$ values of $(\alpha, \beta)$, the equation being a nontrivial linear form in $(\alpha, \beta)$. With the Pythagoras and weight bookkeeping of Lemma [Linearity in the two combining coefficients](lem-qld-global-linear.md), the average over $(\alpha, \beta)$ is at most $2/q \cdot \langle S \otimes {I} \rangle$ plus the diagonal weight $W(g_1(u_0), g_2(u_0)) = \langle S \otimes \widehat M^Z_{g_2} \widehat M^X_{g_1} \widehat M^Z_{g_2} \rangle \leq \langle S \otimes \widehat M^Z_{g_2(u_0)} \rangle$. Read the uniform $u_0$ as a uniform $z$ block with the other coordinates uniform and independent. For a fixed $z$ block, $u \mapsto g_2(u)$ is a polynomial in the other coordinates; as $g_2$ reads a coordinate outside the $z$ block, some coefficient of a non-constant monomial of this polynomial is a nonzero polynomial in the $z$ block, which vanishes for at most a $4md/q$ fraction of the $z$ blocks by Schwartz–Zippel, and where it does not vanish the polynomial is non-constant and takes each value $b$ with probability at most $4md/q$, again by Schwartz–Zippel. So $\mathbb E_{u_0} \langle S \otimes \widehat M^Z_{g_2(u_0)}(z) \rangle \leq \sum_b \mathbb E_z \Pr[g_2 = b \mid z] \langle S \otimes \widehat M^Z_b(z) \rangle \leq 8md/q \cdot \langle S \otimes {I} \rangle$, using $\sum_b \widehat M^Z_b(z) = {I}$. Altogether $\eta \geq 2/q + 8md/q$. For $g_1$ reading a coordinate outside the $x$ block, exchange the roles of $(\widehat M^X, a, \alpha)$ and $(\widehat M^Z, b, \beta)$: the order $\widehat M^Z_b \widehat M^X_a$ along $(\alpha, \beta)$ is the order $\widehat M^X_a \widehat M^Z_b$ along $(\beta, \alpha)$ with the two families exchanged, and the base point is read as a uniform $x$ block. Bob's version applies the same argument with the players exchanged.
