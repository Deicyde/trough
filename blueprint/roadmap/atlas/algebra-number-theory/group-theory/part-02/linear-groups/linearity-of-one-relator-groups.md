## The relator of Kourovka Problem 18.86

This is the element $[[a, b], b]$ of the free group
on two generators $a$ and $b$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$; explicitly,
$[[a, b], b] = c^{-1} b^{-1} c b$ with $c = a^{-1} b^{-1} a b$.

## The one-relator group of Kourovka Problem 18.86

This is the group
$G = \langle a, b \mid [[a, b], b] = 1 \rangle$ with generators $a, b$ and the single defining
relation $[[a, b], b] = 1$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$; that is, $G$ is the quotient of the
free group on $a$ and $b$ by the normal subgroup generated by $[[a, b], b]$.

## Kourovka Problem 18.86 (statement)

The statement that the group $G$ with generators $a, b$
and the single defining relation $[[a, b], b] = 1$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$, is linear:
there are a field $K$, a natural number $n$ and an injective group homomorphism
$G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(K)$.

## The one-relator group of Kourovka Problem 18.86 is linear

Let $G$ be the group with generators
$a, b$ and the single defining relation $[[a, b], b] = 1$, where $[x, y] = x^{-1} y^{-1} x y$. Then
$G$ is linear: there are a field $K$, a natural number $n$ and an injective group homomorphism
$G \to \mathrm{GL}_n(K)$.
