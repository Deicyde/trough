# Finite presentation of commutative-ring variable renaming

[`MvPolynomial.finitePresentation_rename_iff`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/RenameFinitePresentation.lean)
states the relative finite-presentation criterion: subsingleton coefficients,
or finitely many missed target variables together with finitely many source
collision participants. Both collision endpoints are counted. See the
[finite-presentation section](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#relative-finite-presentation-of-variable-renaming)
for the coefficient-ring boundary and infinite-variable examples.
