# NumberField.InfinitePlace.Completion.baseChange

Let $v$ be an infinite place of $K$. There is a natural $L$-algebra homomorphism $L\otimes_K K_v \to \prod_{w\mid v}L_w$, whose restriction to $1\otimes_K K_v$ corresponds to the map in [NumberField.InfinitePlace.Completion.piExtension](numberfield-infiniteplace-completion-piextension.md).
