# Crystal bonds and connectivity

## i. Overview

> A bond specifies a connection between two crystal sites, such as a spin interaction or
> an allowed hopping connection. Its endpoints may belong to the same cell or to different
> cells. The choice of bonds describes the crystal's connectivity; geometry supplies their
> physical positions and lengths.
>
> In a chosen unit cell, a bond is specified by two site labels `i`, `j` and a cell
> displacement `δ : G`. It connects site `i` in cell `n` to site `j` in cell `n + δ`, for
> every cell `n`. When `δ = 0`, both sites belong to the same cell.
>
> `PeriodicGraph.ofBonds` constructs a connectivity graph from a finite list of bonds.

## ii. Key results

> - `PeriodicGraph` : a translation-invariant, locally finite graph on the sites of a crystal.
> - `PeriodicGraph.ofBonds` : the graph generated by a finite list of unit-cell bonds.
> - `PeriodicGraph.card_neighbors_cell` : the coordination number depends only on the label.

> A periodic pattern of undirected connections between crystal sites, with finitely many

```lean
structure PeriodicGraph {G : Type*} [AddGroup G] (C : Crystal G) where
  /-- The undirected connections between crystal sites. -/
  toSimpleGraph : SimpleGraph C.Site
  /-- Translating both sites preserves their connection. -/
  adj_vadd : ∀ (a : G) (x y : C.Site),
    toSimpleGraph.Adj (a +ᵥ x) (a +ᵥ y) ↔ toSimpleGraph.Adj x y
  /-- Connectivity can be checked for any pair of sites. -/
  [decidableAdj : DecidableRel toSimpleGraph.Adj]
  /-- Each site is connected to only finitely many other sites. -/
  [locallyFinite : toSimpleGraph.LocallyFinite]
```
