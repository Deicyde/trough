## Relative generator number

For subgroups $G$ and $N$ of a group $A$, the relative generator
number of $G$ modulo $N$ is the least $k$ such that some $k$ elements of $A$ together with $N$
generate $G$, and $0$ if no finite set of elements does. For a normal subgroup $N \le G$ with
$G / N$ finitely generated it is $d(G / N)$, the minimal number of generators of $G / N$.

## The generator-number formula for almost simple groups (Kourovka 11.27)

The proposition
that for every finite non-abelian simple group $S$, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$, and every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$,
$d(G) = \max(2, d(G / S))$. Here $d(G)$ is the minimal number of generators of $G$, and
$d(G / S)$, the minimal number of generators of $G / S$, is taken to be the least number of
elements of $G$ that together with $S$ generate $G$.

## Generator number of almost simple groups (Kourovka 11.27)

Let $S$
be a finite non-abelian simple group, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$. For every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$,
$d(G) = \max(2, d(G / S))$, where $d(G)$ is the minimal number of generators of $G$ and
$d(G / S)$, the minimal number of generators of $G / S$, is taken to be the least number of
elements of $G$ that together with $S$ generate $G$.

Dalla Volta and Lucchini proved this using the classification of finite simple groups.

## Almost simple groups are generated by 3 elements (Kourovka 11.27)

Let $S$ be a finite
non-abelian simple group, identified with its inner automorphism group
$\mathrm{Inn}(S) \le \mathrm{Aut}(S)$. Every subgroup $G$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$ containing $S$ is
generated by at most $3$ elements.

Dalla Volta and Lucchini proved this using the classification of finite simple groups.

## Almost simple groups

A group $G$ is almost simple when there is a nonabelian finite simple
group $S$ such that $G$ is isomorphic to a subgroup $A$ of the automorphism group $\mathrm{Aut}(S)$
that contains the inner automorphism group $\mathrm{Inn}(S)$.
