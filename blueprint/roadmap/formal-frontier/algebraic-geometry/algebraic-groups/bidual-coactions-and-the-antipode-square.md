# Bidual coactions and the antipode square

For a specified right comodule on a finite projective module over a commutative semiring, canonical bidual evaluation identifies its bidual with the original coaction twisted on coefficients by the antipode square. The coefficient Hopf algebra need not be commutative and its antipode need not be invertible. For commutative coefficients the twist is identity.
