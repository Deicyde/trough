# MeasureTheory.ringHaarChar\_pi

If $R_i$ are a finite collection of locally compact topological rings, and $u_i\in R_i^\times$ then $\delta_{\prod_i R_i}((u_i)_i)=\prod_i\delta_{R_i}(u_i)$.
