## The Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every finite dimension

We prove the Hilbert–Smith conjecture in every finite dimension: every locally compact second-countable Hausdorff group acting faithfully and jointly continuously on a connected Hausdorff second-countable finite-dimensional topological manifold without boundary is a Lie group.
