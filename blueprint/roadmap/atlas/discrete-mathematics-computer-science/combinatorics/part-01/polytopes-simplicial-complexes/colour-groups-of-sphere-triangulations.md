## Triangles of a sphere triangulation

For a finite simplicial 2-sphere $X$ in
$\mathbb{R}^3$, the triangles of $X$ are its faces with exactly three vertices, that is, its
2-dimensional faces.

## Proper colourings of the triangles of a sphere triangulation

Let $X$ be a finite
simplicial 2-sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$, and let a colouring assign white or black to every finite
set of points of $\mathbb{R}^3$. The colouring is proper on $X$ if any two distinct triangles of
$X$ (faces with exactly three vertices) that have exactly two vertices in common receive different
colours.

## Colour group of a 2-coloured sphere triangulation

Let $X$ be a finite simplicial
2-sphere in $\mathbb{R}^3$, let a colouring assign white or black to every finite set of points of
$\mathbb{R}^3$, and let $c$ be one of the two colours. The colour group of $X$ for $c$ is the
abelian group generated by the vertices of $X$ with the relations $u + v + w = 0$ for each
triangle $\{u, v, w\}$ of $X$ (a face with exactly three vertices) of colour $c$; that is, the
free abelian group $\mathbb{Z}^{(V)}$ on the vertex set $V$ of $X$ modulo the subgroup generated by
the indicator functions of the triangles of $X$ of colour $c$.

## Kourovka Problem 17.35 (statement)

The statement that for every finite simplicial
2-sphere $X$ in $\mathbb{R}^3$ and every colouring of the finite sets of points of $\mathbb{R}^3$
white or black such that any two distinct triangles of $X$ (faces with exactly three vertices)
with exactly two vertices in common receive different colours, the groups $G_W$ and $G_B$ are
isomorphic. Here $G_W$ is the abelian group generated by the vertices of $X$ with the relations
$u + v + w = 0$ for each white triangle $\{u, v, w\}$ of $X$, and $G_B$ is defined in the same way
from the black triangles.

## Blackburn–McCourt theorem (Kourovka Problem 17.35)

Let $X$ be a finite simplicial 2-sphere in
$\mathbb{R}^3$, and let a colouring assign white or black to every finite set of points of
$\mathbb{R}^3$ so that any two distinct triangles of $X$ (faces with exactly three vertices) with
exactly two vertices in common receive different colours. Let $G_W$ be the abelian group generated
by the vertices of $X$ with the relations $u + v + w = 0$ for each white triangle $\{u, v, w\}$ of
$X$, and let $G_B$ be defined in the same way from the black triangles. Then $G_W$ and $G_B$ are
isomorphic.
