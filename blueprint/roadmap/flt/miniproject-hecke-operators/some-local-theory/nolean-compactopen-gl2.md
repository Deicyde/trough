# nolean-compactopen-GL2

$GL_2(\mathcal{O}_v)$ is a compact open subgroup of $GL_2(K_v)$.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof depends on

- [Submonoid.units\_isOpen](../restricted-products/submonoid-units-isopen.md)
- [Submonoid.units\_isCompact](../restricted-products/submonoid-units-iscompact.md)

## Proof

$K_v$ is known to be Hausdorff, so $M_2(K_v)$ is Hausdorff and the results follow from lemmas [Submonoid.units\_isOpen](../restricted-products/submonoid-units-isopen.md) and [Submonoid.units\_isCompact](../restricted-products/submonoid-units-iscompact.md).
