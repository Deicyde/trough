The attribute's comment: “The base change assertion.”.

> Let `R → S` be a surjective ring map and let `S'` be the base change of `S` along an arbitrary
> ring map `R → R'`, i.e. `R'`, `S'` complete `R`, `S` to a pushout square of `R`-algebras. Then
> `R' → S'` is surjective and its kernel is the ideal of `R'` generated by the image of the kernel of
> `R → S`.

```lean
public theorem surjective_and_ker_eq_map_of_surjective
    {R S R' S' : Type*} [CommRing R] [CommRing S] [CommRing R'] [CommRing S'] [Algebra R S]
    [Algebra R R'] [Algebra R S'] [Algebra S S'] [Algebra R' S'] [IsScalarTower R S S']
    [IsScalarTower R R' S'] [IsPushout R S R' S']
    (hsurj : Function.Surjective (algebraMap R S)) :
    Function.Surjective (algebraMap R' S') ∧
      RingHom.ker (algebraMap R' S') = (RingHom.ker (algebraMap R S)).map (algebraMap R R')
```

## Tag 0BR7, a lemma

> Let `k'/k` be a field extension. Then every element of `k'` has a positive power lying in `k`
> if and only if either `k'/k` is purely inseparable — which in characteristic zero means `k' = k` —
> or `k'` has positive characteristic and is algebraic over its prime field `𝐅_p`.

```lean
public theorem forall_exists_pow_mem_iff_isPurelyInseparable_or_isAlgebraic_bot
    {k k' : Type*} [Field k] [Field k'] [Algebra k k'] :
    (∀ x : k', ∃ n > 0, x ^ n ∈ (algebraMap k k').range) ↔
      IsPurelyInseparable k k' ∨
        (ringChar k' ≠ 0 ∧ Algebra.IsAlgebraic (⊥ : Subfield k') k')
```

## Tag 0BR9, a lemma

[Tag 0BR9](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BR9) in the Stacks Project.

**`satisfiesGeneratingCondition_iff_structuralCondition`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/UniversalHomeomorphism/Examples/PowerConditions.lean, line 230](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/UniversalHomeomorphism/Examples/PowerConditions.lean#L230).

> Let `K / k` be a field extension and `p` a prime. Then `K` is generated as a field extension
> of `k` by the elements `x` admitting an `n > 0` with `x ^ p ^ n ∈ k` and `p ^ n • x ∈ k` — the
> generating condition of the source — if and only if either `k = K`, or the characteristic is `p`
> and `K / k` is purely inseparable, which is the source's structural condition.
>
> The two directions are the two halves of the perfect closure: in characteristic `p` the generators
> lie in the perfect closure of `k`, which is therefore all of `K`; away from `p` the multiple
> `p ^ n • x` of a generator is `x` times a unit of `k`, so the generators lie in `k` already.

```lean
public theorem satisfiesGeneratingCondition_iff_structuralCondition
    {K : Type*} [Field K] (k : Subfield K) (p : ℕ) (hp : p.Prime) :
    Subfield.closure ((k : Set K) ∪ {x : K | ∃ n > 0, x ^ p ^ n ∈ k ∧ (p ^ n : ℕ) • x ∈ k}) = ⊤ ↔
      k = ⊤ ∨ (ringChar K = p ∧ IsPurelyInseparable k K)
```
