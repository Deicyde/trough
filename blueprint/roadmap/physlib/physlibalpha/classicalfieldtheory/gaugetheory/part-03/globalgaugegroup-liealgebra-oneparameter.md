# One-parameter subgroups of a global gauge group

> The one-parameter subgroup `t ↦ exp (t X)` of a generator `X` of a global gauge group.

## i. Overview

> By the definition of the Lie algebra `𝔤` of a global gauge group `G`, each generator
> `X ∈ 𝔤` has `exp (t X) ∈ G` for every real `t`. This gives a one-parameter subgroup
> `t ↦ exp (t X)` of `G`, a continuous homomorphism from the additive group `ℝ` to `G`. In
> physics it is the finite gauge transformation built from the infinitesimal one `X`. A
> representation of `G` is differentiated along these subgroups in
> `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.MatterField.DerivRep`.

## ii. Key results

> - `GlobalGaugeGroup.oneParameterSubgroup` : the one-parameter subgroup of a generator.
> - `GlobalGaugeGroup.oneParameterSubgroup_add` : it is a homomorphism from `ℝ`.
> - `GlobalGaugeGroup.continuous_oneParameterSubgroup` : it is continuous.

## iv. References

> * B. C. Hall, *Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations*, 2nd ed., Springer (2015).
