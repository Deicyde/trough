# IsPavingAnalytic.prod

If $s$ is $F$-analytic and $t$ is $G$-analytic, then $s \times t$ is $(F \times G)$-analytic: $\mathcal{A}(F) \times \mathcal{A}(G) \subseteq \mathcal{A}(F \times G)$.
