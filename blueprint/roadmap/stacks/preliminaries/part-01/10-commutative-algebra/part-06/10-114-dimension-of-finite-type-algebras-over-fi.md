> **Stacks 00OQ.** A polynomial algebra `k[ι]` in finitely many variables over a field is a
> regular ring; its global dimension, the supremum of the projective dimensions of its modules, is
> the number `Nat.card ι` of variables; and every localization of `k[ι]` at a maximal ideal is a
> regular local ring of dimension `Nat.card ι`.

```lean
public theorem isRegularRing_and_iSup_projectiveDimension_eq_and_isRegularLocalRing_localization
    (k : Type u) [Field k] (ι : Type v) [Finite ι] :
    IsRegularRing (MvPolynomial ι k) ∧
      (⨆ M : ModuleCat.{max u v} (MvPolynomial ι k), projectiveDimension M) =
        (Nat.card ι : WithBot ℕ∞) ∧
      ∀ (𝔪 : Ideal (MvPolynomial ι k)) [𝔪.IsMaximal] (A : Type w) [CommRing A]
          [Algebra (MvPolynomial ι k) A] [IsLocalization.AtPrime A 𝔪],
        IsRegularLocalRing A ∧ ringKrullDim A = (Nat.card ι : WithBot ℕ∞)
```

> **Stacks 00OR.** In a regular ring, every maximal chain of primes between `𝔭` and `𝔮` — a
> chain from `𝔭` to `𝔮` no two consecutive members of which admit a prime strictly between them —
> has length `height 𝔮 - height 𝔭`, stated as `height 𝔮 = length + height 𝔭`.

```lean
public theorem IsRegularRing.height_last_eq_length_add_height_head {R : Type*} [CommRing R]
    [IsRegularRing R] (s : RelSeries {(p, q) : PrimeSpectrum R × PrimeSpectrum R | p ⋖ q}) :
    s.last.asIdeal.height = s.length + s.head.asIdeal.height
```

The attribute's comment: “`dim(S_𝔪)` is written `𝔪.height`, the Krull dimension of any model of the local ring at `𝔪`; the source's "in words" clause is kept as a second conjunct, a maximal chain of primes being a `RelSeries` for the covering relation `⋖` on `PrimeSpectrum S` whose head is minimal and whose last is maximal. > **Stacks 00OS.** Let `k` be a field and let `S` be a finite type `k`-algebra which is a
> domain. Then `dim S = dim S_𝔪 = height 𝔪` for every maximal ideal `𝔪` of `S`, and every maximal
> chain of primes of `S` — one starting at a minimal prime, ending at a maximal ideal, and with all
> steps covering pairs — has length `dim S`.

```lean
public theorem ringKrullDim_eq_height_of_isMaximal_and_length_eq_ringKrullDim (k : Type*)
    [Field k] (S : Type*) [CommRing S] [IsDomain S] [Algebra k S] [Algebra.FiniteType k S] :
    (∀ (𝔪 : Ideal S) [𝔪.IsMaximal], ringKrullDim S = (𝔪.height : WithBot ℕ∞)) ∧
      ∀ s : RelSeries {(p, q) : PrimeSpectrum S × PrimeSpectrum S | p ⋖ q},
        IsMin s.head → IsMax s.last → (s.length : WithBot ℕ∞) = ringKrullDim S
```

> **Stacks 00OT.** Let `k` be a field, let `S` be a finite type `k`-algebra, let `X = Spec S`,
> and let `x ∈ X` be a point, with prime ideal `𝔭 = x.asIdeal`. Then the dimension of `X` at `x` is
> both the largest dimension of an irreducible component of `X` through `x` and the smallest of the
> dimensions `dim S_𝔪 = height 𝔪` over the maximal ideals `𝔪 ⊇ 𝔭`.

```lean
public theorem localKrullDim_eq_iSup_irreducibleComponents_and_eq_iInf_height
    (k : Type u) [Field k] {S : Type v} [CommRing S] [Algebra k S] [Algebra.FiniteType k S]
    (x : PrimeSpectrum S) :
    (TopologicalSpace.localKrullDim (PrimeSpectrum S) x =
        ⨆ Z ∈ {Z ∈ irreducibleComponents (PrimeSpectrum S) | x ∈ Z}, topologicalKrullDim Z) ∧
      (TopologicalSpace.localKrullDim (PrimeSpectrum S) x =
        ⨅ 𝔪 ∈ {𝔪 : Ideal S | 𝔪.IsMaximal ∧ x.asIdeal ≤ 𝔪}, (𝔪.height : WithBot ℕ∞))
```
