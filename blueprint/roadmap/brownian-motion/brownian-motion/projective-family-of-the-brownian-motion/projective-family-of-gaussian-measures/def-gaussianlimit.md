# gaussianLimit

We denote by $P_B$ the projective limit of the projective family of the Brownian motion given by Theorem [Kolmogorov extension theorem](../kolmogorov-extension-theorem/thm-kolmogorovextension.md). This is a probability measure on $\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{R}_+}$.
