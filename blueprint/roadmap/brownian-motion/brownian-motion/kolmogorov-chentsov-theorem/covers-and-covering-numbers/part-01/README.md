## Covering number and volume

In this section $E$ is a normed additive group with an additive Haar measure $V$. For the bounds on the covering numbers of balls, $E$ is a finite dimensional real normed space, with dimension $d$.

- [volume_le_of_isCover](lem-volume-le-of-iscover.md)

In a finite dimensional real normed space of dimension $d$, the measure of a ball scales as $V(B_r(x)) = r^d V(B_1)$ (see [MeasureTheory.Measure.addHaar\_closedBall](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathlib4_docs/Mathlib/MeasureTheory/Measure/Lebesgue/EqHaar.html#MeasureTheory.Measure.addHaar_closedBall)).
