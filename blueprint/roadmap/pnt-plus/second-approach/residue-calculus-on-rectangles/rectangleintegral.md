# RectangleIntegral

A RectangleIntegral of a function $f$ is one over a rectangle determined by $z$ and $w$ in $\mathbb{C}$. We will sometimes denote it by $\int_{z}^{w} f$. (There is also a primed version, which is $1/(2\pi i)$ times the original.)
