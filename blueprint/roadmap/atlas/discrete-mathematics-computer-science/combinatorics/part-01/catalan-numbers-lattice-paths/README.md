# Catalan numbers and lattice paths

312 results of ATLAS's vault, 214 proved and 98 stated but not proved, in this branch's topics.

The Catalan family of counting sequences and the lattice paths they count: Catalan, Fuss–Catalan, Narayana, Motzkin, Schröder, Fine and Riordan numbers, Dyck and Motzkin paths and their statistics, Delannoy numbers and walks with prescribed steps, including arithmetic properties (parity, integrality, perfect powers) of these numbers.
