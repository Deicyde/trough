# Quantum code certificates and hypergraph-product theory

> Formalizes general binary row reduction, kernel bases and rank certificates; quantum-code distance bounds from witnesses and exhaustive low-weight enumeration; arbitrary-matrix hypergraph-product dimension and X/Z cleaning bounds; and CSS orthogonality of lifted products over finite groups. Includes a Pauli expression translation modulo global phase, a Boolean encoding with proved soundness and model extension, and sound checking of RUP additions in LRAT certificates, bridged to matrix-level distance bounds. Concrete code-family datasets, large certificate replays and measurement protocols are outside this import.
