# chainingSequence_mem

Let $(C_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be nonempty finite sets of $E$ and $x \in C_k$ for some $k \in \mathbb{N}$. Then for all $i \le k$, $\bar{x}_i\in C_i$.
