## Artin's braid relators

For a natural number $n$, the set of Artin's relators for the braid
group on $n$ strands, as elements of the free group on the $n - 1$ generators
$\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{n-2}$ (no generators when $n \le 1$): the commutators
$\sigma_i \sigma_j \sigma_i^{-1} \sigma_j^{-1}$ for $0 \le i$ and $i + 2 \le j \le n - 2$, and the
words $\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i (\sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1})^{-1}$ for
$0 \le i \le n - 3$.

## The braid group $B_n$ by Artin's presentation

For a natural number $n$, the braid group
$B_n$ on $n$ strands is the group generated by $\sigma_0, \dots, \sigma_{n-2}$ subject to the
relations $\sigma_i \sigma_j = \sigma_j \sigma_i$ for $j \ge i + 2$ and
$\sigma_i \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i = \sigma_{i+1} \sigma_i \sigma_{i+1}$ for $0 \le i \le n - 3$; it is
the trivial group when $n \le 1$.

## Makanin's root-uniqueness question for braid groups

The statement that for every natural
number $n$ and every integer $k \ge 1$, any two elements $\alpha, \beta$ of the braid group $B_n$ on
$n$ strands with $\alpha^k = \beta^k$ are conjugate in $B_n$. This is Kourovka Problem 10.23
(G. S. Makanin).

## Roots in braid groups are unique up to conjugacy (González-Meneses)

For every natural number
$n$ and every integer $k \ge 1$, if two elements $\alpha, \beta$ of the braid group $B_n$ on $n$
strands satisfy $\alpha^k = \beta^k$, then $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are conjugate in $B_n$. This answers
Kourovka Problem 10.23 (G. S. Makanin) affirmatively.
