# The invariants of the Standard Model up to mass dimension four

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.Invariants`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/Invariants.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/Invariants.lean): 14 declarations, 14 with a docstring.

> The classification of the gauge and Lorentz invariants of the Standard Model of mass dimension at
> most four, on the covariant field algebra, on the local field algebra and in a realization.

## i. Overview

> This file states the classification of the Standard Model invariants of mass dimension at most
> four, in three forms.
>
> The first is on the covariant field algebra `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` of the Standard
> Model, the covariant field algebra of its field datum. An element of mass weight at most eight
> is invariant under the ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group exactly when it is a
> combination of the constant term, the Higgs mass term `H† H` and the dimension-four
> Lagrangian. This is the classification through `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.standardModelSpanLE`, once
> the field algebra `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.fieldAlgebra` is seen to be the whole of
> `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra`.
>
> The second is on the local field algebra `fieldData.LocalFieldAlgebra`, the jet algebra of
> the Standard Model, on which the gauge jets act. By the descent of invariants,
> `StandardModel.fieldData_mem_invariants_iff_exists_mem_invariants` at the identity
> realization of the local field algebra, a jet-invariant and Lorentz-invariant element is a
> covariant invariant. Reading off the weight components of a covariant element does not leave
> the covariant algebra, so an element of mass weight at most eight is invariant exactly when it
> is the image of an element of the dimension-four span.
>
> The third is for an arbitrary realization `k` of the local field algebra in an algebra `B`.
> Without a grading on `B`, the image `k x` is invariant exactly when it is the image of a
> covariant invariant. With a mass-weight algebra map on `B` compatible with `k`, the image of an
> element of mass weight at most eight is invariant exactly when it is the image of an element of
> the dimension-four span: the components of weight above eight of the covariant preimage are
> invisible in `B`. A grading is needed for this, since in a quotient of the local field algebra
> an element of weight at most eight can be identified with an invariant of higher weight.
>
> These are statements about formal expressions. The listed terms span, they are not shown to be
> independent or nonzero, and nothing is identified modulo total derivatives or the equations of
> motion.

## B. The mass-weight filtration of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model

> The mass-weight filtration of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model is the filtration of
> the local field algebra read on `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra`. One inclusion is the restriction
> of the mass-weight polynomial. The other uses that the weight components of a covariant element are
> covariant, being the coefficients of its image under the restricted mass-weight polynomial.

## C. The classification on the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.mem_massWeightSubmoduleLE_eight_and_invariant_iff`**, a lemma, [line 261](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/Invariants.lean#L261).

> The covariant invariants of the Standard Model up to mass dimension four. An element of
> the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model of mass weight at most eight is invariant under
> the ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group exactly when it lies in the span of the filtration
> at bound eight. Forwards, the invariant is a constant plus a sum of invariant elements of the
> seven nonempty sectors of mass weight at most eight, by
> `GaugeFieldData.exists_eq_smul_one_add_sum_sector_of_mem_invariants_massWeightLE`, and each
> of these lies in the span by the classification of its sector.

```lean
lemma mem_massWeightSubmoduleLE_eight_and_invariant_iff (a : fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra) :
    (a ∈ LocalCovFieldAlgebra.massWeightSubmoduleLE 8
        ∧ a ∈ (GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repValue fieldData).invariants
        ∧ a ∈ repLorentzGroup.invariants)
      ↔ a ∈ LocalCovFieldAlgebra.standardModelSpanLE 8
```

> The covariant invariants of the Standard Model up to mass dimension four, with the span
> written out. An element of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model of mass weight at
> most eight is invariant under the ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group exactly when it is a
> combination of the constant term, the Higgs mass term `H† H`
> (`ScalarSector.dotSpan`), and the dimension-four span, consisting of the gauge kinetic and
> theta terms (`GaugeSector.lorentzContractionEightSpan`), the Higgs kinetic term, quartic
> potential and box terms (`ScalarSector.lorentzContractionEightSpan`), the
> fermion kinetic terms (`FermionSector.kineticSpan`) and the Yukawa couplings
> (`yukawaSpan`).

```lean
lemma mem_massWeightSubmoduleLE_eight_and_invariant_iff_lagrangian
    (a : fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra) :
    (a ∈ LocalCovFieldAlgebra.massWeightSubmoduleLE 8
        ∧ a ∈ (GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repValue fieldData).invariants
        ∧ a ∈ repLorentzGroup.invariants)
      ↔ a ∈ (1 : Submodule ℂ fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra)
        ⊔ (ScalarSector.dotSpan 0 0
          ⊔ (GaugeSector.lorentzContractionEightSpan
              ⊔ ScalarSector.lorentzContractionEightSpan
            ⊔ (FermionSector.kineticSpan
              ⊔ LocalCovFieldAlgebra.yukawaSpan)))
```

## D. The classification on the local field algebra

> The invariants of the Standard Model up to mass dimension four, on its local field
> algebra. An element of mass weight at most eight is invariant under the gauge jets of
> `globalGaugeGroup` and under the Lorentz group exactly when it is a covariant element of the
> span of the filtration at bound eight, a combination of the constant term, the Higgs mass
> term and the dimension-four Lagrangian. There is no other hypothesis on the element.

```lean
lemma mem_massWeightSubmoduleLE_eight_and_invariant_iff_lagrangian
    (x : fieldData.LocalFieldAlgebra) :
    (x ∈ fieldData.massWeightSubmoduleLE 8 ∧ x ∈ fieldData.repJet.invariants
        ∧ x ∈ fieldData.repLorentzGroup.invariants)
      ↔ x ∈ (LocalCovFieldAlgebra.standardModelSpanLE 8).map
          fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.val.toLinearMap
```

## E. The classification in a realization

**`StandardModel.realization_mem_invariants_iff`**, a lemma, [line 384](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/Invariants.lean#L384).

> The invariants of a realization of the local field algebra of the Standard Model. The
> image `k x` is invariant under the jet gauge group and the Lorentz group exactly when it is
> the image of an element of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model invariant under the
> ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group.

```lean
lemma realization_mem_invariants_iff {B : Type} [Ring B] [Algebra ℂ B]
    {repJet : Representation ℂ globalGaugeGroup.gaugeJetGroup B}
    {repLorentz : Representation ℂ SL(2,ℂ) B}
    (k : GaugeFieldData.Realization fieldData B repJet repLorentz)
    (x : fieldData.LocalFieldAlgebra) :
    k.toAlgHom x ∈ repJet.invariants ∧ k.toAlgHom x ∈ repLorentz.invariants ↔
      k.toAlgHom x ∈ ((GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repValue fieldData).invariants
          ⊓ LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repLorentzGroup.invariants).map
        (k.toAlgHom.toLinearMap ∘ₗ fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.val.toLinearMap)
```
