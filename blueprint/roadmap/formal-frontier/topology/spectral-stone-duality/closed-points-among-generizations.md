# Closed points among generizations

In any `T₀` space, the original point
is the unique closed point of its generization subspace `nhdsKer {x}`:
[`isClosed_singleton_nhdsKer_iff`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/spectral-stone-duality/blob/3d9e22b25e636e8c1303bb66b8902beb8c5f4b1f/SpectralStoneDuality/Topology/NhdsKer.lean)
characterizes every closed singleton in that subspace. No sobriety or
spectrality is needed. See [closed generizations](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/spectral-stone-duality/blob/3d9e22b25e636e8c1303bb66b8902beb8c5f4b1f/docs/Guide.md#closed-generizations).
