# Free magma of a confluent theory

Let $\Gamma$ be a confluent theory. Then the free magma $M_{X,\Gamma}$ subject to this theory can be described as the space of reduced words in $M_X$ in normal form, where the operation $w \diamond_\Gamma w'$ on this magma is defined as the normal form reduction of $w \diamond w'$, and $\iota_{X,\Gamma}$ is the identity embedding (note that every single-letter word is already in normal form).
