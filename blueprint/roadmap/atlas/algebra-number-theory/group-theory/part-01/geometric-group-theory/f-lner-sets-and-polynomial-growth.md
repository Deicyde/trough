## Ball in the word metric

For a finite subset $S$ of a group $\Gamma$ and a natural number
$n$, the ball $B_S(n)$ of radius $n$ is the set of products of at most $n$ elements of
$S \cup S^{-1}$, the empty product $1$ included.

## Polynomial growth with respect to a finite subset

Let $S$ be a finite subset of a group
$\Gamma$, and let $B_S(n)$ be the ball of radius $n$ for $S$, the set of products of at most $n$
elements of $S \cup S^{-1}$. Then $\Gamma$ has polynomial growth with respect to $S$ if there are
natural numbers $C$ and $d$ such that for every natural number $n$ the ball $B_S(n)$ has at most
$C\,(n + 1)^d$ elements.

## Boundary of a finite subset of a group

For a group $\Gamma$ and finite subsets $S$ and $F$
of $\Gamma$, the boundary of $F$ with respect to $S$ is the set
$\partial F = \{\gamma \in \Gamma \setminus F : \gamma s \in F \text{ for some } s \in S\}$.

## Vaillant's question on Følner exhaustions of polynomial size (Kourovka 14.27)

The statement
that for every group $\Gamma$ generated by a finite subset $S$ the following holds: if there are
finite subsets $F_0 \subsetneq F_1 \subsetneq F_2 \subsetneq \cdots$ of $\Gamma$ whose union is
$\Gamma$ such that $|\partial F_k| / |F_k| \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$, where $\partial F_k$ is the
boundary of $F_k$ with respect to $S$, and such that for some real $c \ge 0$ and some natural
number $d \ge 1$ we have $|F_k| \le c\,(k + 1)^d$ for every $k$, then $\Gamma$ has polynomial
growth with respect to $S$.

The Kourovka Notebook indexes the sets from $F_1$, so its size condition $|F_k| \le c\,k^d$ reads
$|F_k| \le c\,(k + 1)^d$ here.

## Følner exhaustions of polynomial size need not give polynomial growth

There exist a group
$\Gamma$ generated by a finite subset $S$ and finite subsets
$F_0 \subsetneq F_1 \subsetneq F_2 \subsetneq \cdots$ of $\Gamma$ whose union is $\Gamma$ such that
$|\partial F_k| / |F_k| \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$, where $\partial F_k$ is the boundary of $F_k$
with respect to $S$, and such that for some real $c \ge 0$ and some natural number $d \ge 1$ we
have $|F_k| \le c\,(k + 1)^d$ for every $k$, while $\Gamma$ does not have polynomial growth with
respect to $S$. That is, the answer to Kourovka Problem 14.27 is negative.

By Bardakov, every finitely generated group of intermediate growth (such groups exist by
Grigorchuk) has such a sequence of subsets.
