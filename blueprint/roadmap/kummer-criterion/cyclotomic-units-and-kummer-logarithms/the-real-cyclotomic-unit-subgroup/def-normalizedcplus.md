# normalizedCPlus

For $2 \le a \le (p-1)/2$, the *normalised cyclotomic unit* is

$$
\xi_a \;=\; \zeta_p^{\,(1-a)/2}\,\frac{1-\zeta_p^a}{1-\zeta_p},
$$

where the exponent $(1-a)/2$ is read modulo $p$. The twist by the root of unity makes $\xi_a$ fixed by complex conjugation, so it defines a unit of $\mathcal O_{K^+}$. The normalised subgroup $C^+_{\mathrm{norm}}$ is generated by $-1$ and the units $\xi_a$.
