# a-d24

For $r>2$, define

$$
a(r) := -4\sin\!\left(\frac{\pi r^2}{2}\right)^{\!2}\int_{0}^{i\infty}\varphi\!\left(-\frac{1}{z}\right)z^{10}e^{\pi i r^2 z}\,dz.\qquad\text{(3)}
$$
