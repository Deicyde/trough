# isPavingAnalytic_of_measurableSet_generateFrom

Let $F$ be a nonempty paving and let $\sigma(F)$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by $F$. Suppose that for every $t \in F$, $t^c$ is $F$-analytic. Then every set in $\sigma(F)$ is $F$-analytic: $\sigma(F) \subseteq \mathcal{A}(F)$.
