# a-zeros-values-d24

The function $a$ maps $\mathbb{R}$ to $i\mathbb{R}$, and it has a double zero at $r=\sqrt{2k}$ for each integer $k>2$. Moreover,

$$
a(0)=\frac{113218560\,i}{\pi},\quad a(\sqrt2)=\frac{725760\,i}{\pi},\quad a'(\sqrt2)=\frac{-4437504\sqrt2\,i}{\pi},\quad a(2)=0,\quad a'(2)=\frac{-3456\,i}{\pi}.
$$
