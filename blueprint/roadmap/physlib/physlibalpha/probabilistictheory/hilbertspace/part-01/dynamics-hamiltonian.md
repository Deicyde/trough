# Hamiltonian dynamics

> The unitary evolution and Heisenberg flow of a bounded Hamiltonian, and their uniqueness.

## i. Overview

> A bounded Hamiltonian `H` generates the unitary evolution `U(t) = exp(-i t H / ℏ)` and the flow
> `αₜ(a) = U(t) a U(t)⋆`, the unique solution of `d/dt αₜ(a) = -(i / ℏ) [H, αₜ(a)]` with `α₀ = id`.
> Shifting `H` by a real multiple of the identity changes `U(t)` only by a phase and leaves the flow
> unchanged, and this is the only freedom.

## ii. Key results

> - `UnitaryOneParameterGroup.unitaryEvolution` : the unitary evolution of a bounded Hamiltonian.
> - `UnitaryOneParameterGroup.hamiltonianFlow` : the Heisenberg-picture flow.
> - `UnitaryOneParameterGroup.hamiltonianFlow_unique` : the flow solves the Heisenberg equation
>   uniquely.
> - `UnitaryOneParameterGroup.hamiltonianFlow_add_smul_one` : shifting the Hamiltonian by a constant
>   leaves the flow unchanged.

## F. Classification up to star-automorphism conjugation

> `hamiltonianFlow_map` and `hamiltonianFlow_add_smul_one` give the easy direction: `K` generates the
> `β`-conjugate of `H`'s flow whenever `K = β(H) + c • 1`. The converse needs a differential argument,
> `hamiltonianFlow_eq_iff` below.

**`UnitaryOneParameterGroup.hamiltonianFlow_map_add_smul_one`**, a lemma, [line 223](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/Dynamics/Hamiltonian.lean#L223).

> If `K = β(H) + c • 1`, `K`'s flow is `H`'s flow viewed through `β`.

```lean
lemma hamiltonianFlow_map_add_smul_one (ℏ : ℝ) (β : (H →L[ℂ] H) ≃⋆ₐ[ℂ] (H →L[ℂ] H))
    (Hm K : Observable (H →L[ℂ] H)) (c : ℝ) (h : K = β.observable Hm + c • 1) :
    hamiltonianFlow ℏ K = (hamiltonianFlow ℏ Hm).conj β
```

**`UnitaryOneParameterGroup.comm_G_of_hamiltonianFlow_eq`**, a lemma, [line 230](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/Dynamics/Hamiltonian.lean#L230).

> If `H` and `K` generate the same flow, `G ℏ H - G ℏ K` commutes with everything: equal flows

