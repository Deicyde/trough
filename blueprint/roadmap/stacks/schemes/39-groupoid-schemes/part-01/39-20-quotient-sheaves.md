> **Stacks 03C5.** Let `τ` be one of the Zariski, étale, smooth, syntomic and fppf
> topologies, let `j : R ⟶ U ×_S U` be a pre-relation of `S`-schemes, and let `f : U ⟶ M` be a
> morphism of `S`-schemes equalizing its source and its target. If `f` induces a surjection of
> `τ`-sheaves on the functors of points, and if `(t, s) : R ⟶ U ×_M U` induces one too, then `M`
> represents the quotient sheaf `U/R`: the canonical map `U/R ⟶ h_M` is an isomorphism.
>
> The source's three hypotheses are `hf`, `hU` and `hR`, in that order. Nothing is assumed about
> the functor of points of `M`: it is a `τ`-sheaf by
> `Stacks.Groupoids.isSheaf_points_baseOver`, and neither `M` nor `R` is assumed to be an object
> of the site.

```lean
theorem isIso_quotientSheafToPoints_baseOver {R U M : Over S} (j : PreRelation R U) (f : U ⟶ M)
    (hf : j.target ≫ f = j.source ≫ f)
    (hU : Presheaf.IsLocallySurjective (BigFppfOver.baseOverTopology B S hS τ)
      ((Over.post (X := B.obj hS) B.inclusion).op.whiskerLeft (yoneda.map f)))
    (hR : Presheaf.IsLocallySurjective (BigFppfOver.baseOverTopology B S hS τ)
      ((Over.post (X := B.obj hS) B.inclusion).op.whiskerLeft
        (yoneda.map (pullback.lift j.target j.source hf)))) :
    IsIso (quotientSheafToPoints (BigFppfOver.baseOverTopology B S hS τ)
      (Over.post (X := B.obj hS) B.inclusion) j f hf
      (isSheaf_points_baseOver B S hS τ M))
```

## Tag 045Y, a lemma

[Tag 045Y](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/045Y) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.Groupoids.toQuotientSheaf_apply_eq_iff_locally_related`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Spaces/QuotientSheafEquality.lean, line 134](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Spaces/QuotientSheafEquality.lean#L134).

> **Stacks 045Y.** Two `T`-valued points of `U` have the same image in the quotient sheaf
> of a pre-equivalence relation if and only if, on some covering sieve of `T`, their
> restrictions are the source and the target of a point of the relation scheme.

```lean
theorem toQuotientSheaf_apply_eq_iff_locally_related
    {S : Scheme.{u}} {C : Type v} [Category.{w} C]
    (J : GrothendieckTopology C) [HasSheafify J (Type u)]
    [J.WEqualsLocallyBijective (Type u)]
    (F : C ⥤ Over S) {R U : Over S} (j : PreRelation R U)
    (hj : IsPreEquivalenceRelation j)
    (T : C) (a b : F.obj T ⟶ U) :
    (toQuotientSheaf J F j).app (op T) a = (toQuotientSheaf J F j).app (op T) b ↔
      ∃ W : Sieve T, W ∈ J T ∧
        ∀ (X : C) (f : X ⟶ T), W.arrows f →
          ∃ r : F.obj X ⟶ R,
            (r ≫ j.source = F.map f ≫ a) ∧ (r ≫ j.target = F.map f ≫ b)
```

[Tag 07S3](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07S3) in the Stacks Project.

**`AlgebraicGeometry.isPullback_toQuotientSheaf_overTopology`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Groupoids/QuotientSheaf/BigSitePullbackSquare.lean, line 117](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Groupoids/QuotientSheaf/BigSitePullbackSquare.lean#L117).

> **Stacks 07S3.** Let `S` be a scheme and let `(r, f)` be a morphism of equivalence
> relations `j : R ⟶ U ×_S U` and `j' : R' ⟶ U' ×_S U'` over `S` whose square on source maps is
> cartesian. Then for each of the Zariski, étale, smooth, syntomic and fppf topologies the
> square formed by the two quotient maps and by `f` is a fibre product square of `τ`-sheaves:
> `U` is the fibre product of `U'` and `U/R` over `U'/R'`.
>
> The topology is quantified over `AlgebraicGeometry.CoveringTopology`, so the single statement
> is the five of the source. The two schemes of objects `U` and `U'` are the schemes presented
> by objects of the site; the two schemes of relations are arbitrary schemes over `S`.

```lean
theorem isPullback_toQuotientSheaf_overTopology (τ : CoveringTopology) (B : BigSite.{u} τ)
    (S : Scheme.{u}) (hS : S.size < cutoff τ B)
    {R R' : CategoryTheory.Over S} {U U' : CategoryTheory.Over (base B S hS)}
    (j : PreRelation R ((overToSlice τ B S hS).obj U))
    (j' : PreRelation R' ((overToSlice τ B S hS).obj U'))
    [IsEquivalenceRelation j.target j.source] [IsEquivalenceRelation j'.target j'.source]
    (r : R ⟶ R')
    (f : (overToSlice τ B S hS).obj U ⟶ (overToSlice τ B S hS).obj U')
    (hs : r ≫ j'.source = j.source ≫ f) (ht : r ≫ j'.target = j.target ≫ f)
    (hcart : IsPullback r j.source j'.source f) :
    IsPullback (toQuotientSheaf (overTopology τ B S hS) (overToSlice τ B S hS) j)
      ((overToSlice τ B S hS).op.whiskerLeft (yoneda.map f))
      (quotientSheafMap (overTopology τ B S hS) (overToSlice τ B S hS) j j' r f hs ht).hom
      (toQuotientSheaf (overTopology τ B S hS) (overToSlice τ B S hS) j')
```
