# TotallyDefiniteQuaternionAlgebra.WeightTwoAutomorphicFormOfLevel

The quaternionic modular forms of level $U$, with notation $S^D(U;R)$, are the $U$-invariants for the $D_{\mathbb{A}^\infty}^\times$-action on $S^D(R)$.
