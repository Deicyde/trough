# Antichain coefficients of monomial ideals

For any commutative semiring
`R`, arbitrary variable type `σ` and antichain `S : Set (σ →₀ ℕ)`,
[`MvPolynomial.monomialIdealCoeffMap`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/MonomialIdeal.lean)
restricts coefficients from the stock ideal generated by the monic monomials
with exponents in `S` to `S →₀ R`. It is surjective and semilinear over
`constantCoeff`, for the full polynomial-semiring action on the ideal and
the coefficient action on the target. Its coordinate and monomial laws give
the original coefficients and the corresponding single functions.
Coefficients and variables have independent universes; empty or infinite
antichains and variable types, and trivial coefficients, are allowed. The
[coordinate-axes example](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomialsTests/MonomialIdealGenerators.lean)
records the three exponents of `(xy, xz, yz)` over `ℂ`, their antichain and
monomial presentation, and the ideal's nonzeroness and properness.
For **finite** `S` and **nontrivial** `R`,
[`MvPolynomial.spanFinrank_ideal_span_monomial`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/MonomialIdeal.lean)
proves that the ideal's minimum number of polynomial generators is `S.ncard`.
[`MvPolynomial.card_le_card_of_span_eq_ideal_span_monomial`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/MonomialIdeal.lean)
bounds every finite generating set, not only sets of monomials. This is
generation over the full polynomial semiring, not coefficient-module dimension.
The coordinate-axes example consequently has minimum number of generators three.
