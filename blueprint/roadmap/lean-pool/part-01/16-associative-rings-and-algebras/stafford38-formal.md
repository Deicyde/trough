# Stafford 3.8

> Lean formalization of Stafford's Conjecture 3.8 for every characteristic-zero field and every Weyl-algebra rank, proving that each nonzero d admits F, R, and S with 1 = d\*R + F\*d\*S. The development also proves an exact Bernstein-degree fixed-source strengthening and derived localization, adjoint, and evolutionary results.
