# 203. Donovan's conjecture over fields and complete mixed-characteristic DVRs

Proves Donovan's conjecture: over each fixed algebraically closed field of characteristic *p*, blocks of finite groups with bounded defect-group order have only finitely many Morita-equivalence classes, for every prime *p*. ## Donovan's Conjecture over Algebraically Closed Fields

We prove Donovan's conjecture over every algebraically closed field *K* of characteristic *p* > 0. For each fixed *K* and bound on defect-group order, blocks of finite groups represent only finitely many *K*-linear Morita equivalence classes. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes *p* = 2.

[The manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae/preprints/Donovans-Conjecture-over-Algebraic-Closures-of-Prime-Fields-September-24-2026/main.pdf).

## Integral Donovan Finiteness over Witt Vectors

We prove integral Donovan finiteness: for every prime *p* and positive integer *M*, the blocks of all finite groups with defect groups of order at most *M* have only finitely many Morita equivalence classes over $W(\overline{\mathbb F}_p)$. The defect groups need not be abelian, and the result includes *p* = 2. The same bounded-defect finiteness holds over each fixed complete discrete valuation ring of characteristic zero with algebraically closed residue field of characteristic *p*, including ramified rings.
