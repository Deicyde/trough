# Connected cographs with distance eigenvalues approaching −2 from below

> Constructs connected cographs with a distance eigenvalue arbitrarily close to −2 from below. For each positive m, the join of a singleton with the disjoint union of a singleton and m edges has a distance eigenvalue between −2 − 1/(2m) and −2. The proof lifts a root of a cubic to an explicit eigenvector.
