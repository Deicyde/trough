The attribute's comment: “corrected: for `X ⟶ Spec A` quasi-compact and quasi-separated; without quasi-compactness the statement is false, see `not_forall_nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff`”.

> **[Stacks, Tag 0B57], corrected.** Let `f : X ⟶ Spec A` be a quasi-compact quasi-separated
> morphism, let `g : Spec B ⟶ Spec A` be flat and surjective — equivalently, a faithfully flat
> ring map `A ⟶ B` — and let `F` be a quasi-coherent `𝒪_X`-module. Then `F` is generated by
> global sections if and only if its base change along `g` is.
>
> The correction is the quasi-compactness and quasi-separatedness of `f`, which the Stacks Project
> does not assume. Without quasi-compactness the equivalence fails:
> `not_forall_nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff` gives a quasi-coherent module on a scheme
> over `Spec ℤ` which is not generated by global sections, while its base change along the
> faithfully flat `ℤ ⟶ ℤ[1/3] × ℤ[1/2]` is. That scheme is quasi-separated, so it does not show
> that quasi-separatedness of `f` is needed; it is the hypothesis of [Stacks, Tag 02KH].

```lean
theorem nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff (H : IsPullback g' f' f g) [Flat g]
    [Surjective g] [QuasiCompact f] [QuasiSeparated f] (F : X.Modules) [F.IsQuasicoherent] :
    Nonempty F.GeneratingSections ↔
      Nonempty ((Scheme.Modules.pullback g').obj F).GeneratingSections
```

**`Stacks.AlgebraicGeometry.FlatBaseChange.nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff_specMap`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/FlatBaseChange/GlobalGeneration.lean, line 346](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/FlatBaseChange/GlobalGeneration.lean#L346). The attribute's comment: “corrected, on a faithfully flat ring map: for `X ⟶ Spec A` quasi-compact and quasi-separated; without quasi-compactness the statement is false, see `not_forall_nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff_specMap`”.

> **[Stacks, Tag 0B57], corrected, read on a faithfully flat ring map.** For a faithfully
> flat ring map `φ : A ⟶ B` and a quasi-coherent module `F` on a scheme quasi-compact and
> quasi-separated over `Spec A`, global generation of `F` is equivalent to global generation of
> the base change of `F` to `X ×_{Spec A} Spec B`.
>
> The correction is the quasi-compactness and quasi-separatedness of `X ⟶ Spec A`, which the
> Stacks Project does not assume. Without quasi-compactness the equivalence fails:
> `not_forall_nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff_specMap` is the negation of this statement
> with those two hypotheses removed.

```lean
theorem nonempty_generatingSections_pullback_iff_specMap {A B : CommRingCat.{u}} (φ : A ⟶ B)
    (hφ : φ.hom.FaithfullyFlat) {X : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ Spec A) [QuasiCompact f]
    [QuasiSeparated f] (F : X.Modules) [F.IsQuasicoherent] :
    Nonempty F.GeneratingSections ↔
      Nonempty (((Scheme.Modules.pullback
        (Limits.pullback.fst f (Spec.map φ))).obj F).GeneratingSections)
```

> **Stacks Tag 0B58.** Let `k` be an infinite field, `X` a scheme of finite type over `k`, `L` a
> very ample invertible `𝒪_X`-module, `x` a `k`-rational point of `X` and `xs` finitely many points

> An invertible sheaf generated by a linear family of sections is generated by a
> subspace of dimension at most one more than the dimension of the scheme.

```lean
theorem exists_submodule_generatingSections_finrank_le_dimension_add_one
    (k : Type u) [Field k] [Infinite k] (X : Scheme.{u})
    (f : X ⟶ Spec (.of k)) [LocallyOfFiniteType f] [QuasiCompact f]
    (L : X.Modules)
    [SheafOfModules.IsInvertible X.sheaf.obj X.ringCatSheaf.property L]
    (V : Type u) [AddCommGroup V] [Module k V] :
    letI : Algebra k Γ(X, ⊤) :=
      ((Scheme.ΓSpecIso (.of k)).inv ≫ f.appTop).hom.toAlgebra
    letI : Module k Γ(L, ⊤) :=
      Module.compHom Γ(L, ⊤) (algebraMap k Γ(X, ⊤))
    ∀ (s : V →ₗ[k] Γ(L, ⊤)),
      Epi (L.freeHomEquiv.symm (fun v : V ↦ Stacks.SheafOfModules.sectionsOfTop L (s v))) →
      ∃ W : Submodule k V,
        FiniteDimensional k W ∧
        Module.finrank k W ≤ ((topologicalKrullDim X).unbotD 0).toNat + 1 ∧
        Epi (L.freeHomEquiv.symm (fun w : W ↦ Stacks.SheafOfModules.sectionsOfTop L (s w.1)))
```
