# The closed subalgebra generated by one observable

> The closed subalgebra `C(a)` generated by one observable is a commutative real Banach algebra.

## i. Overview

> The closure `C(a)` of the subalgebra generated by `1` and `a` in a JB-algebra is closed under the
> Jordan product, because the product is continuous. It is complete, and associative since the
> subalgebra generated by one element is. So `C(a)` is a commutative real Banach algebra.

## ii. Key results

> - `NormedJordanAlgebra.closedGeneratedByOne` : the closed subalgebra generated by `a`.
> - `NormedJordanAlgebra.mul_mem_closedGeneratedByOne` : it is closed under the Jordan product.
> - `NormedJordanAlgebra.assoc_of_mem_closedGeneratedByOne` : the product is associative on it.
> - `NormedJordanAlgebra.ClosedGeneratedByOne` : it as a commutative normed algebra.
> - `NormedJordanAlgebra.isComplete_closedGeneratedByOne` : it is complete.

> **The topological half of the reduction.** `C_J(a)` is closed under the Jordan product: a
