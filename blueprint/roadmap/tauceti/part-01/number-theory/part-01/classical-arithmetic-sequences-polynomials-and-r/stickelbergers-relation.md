# Stickelberger's relation

Let `K` be the `m`-th cyclotomic field, `m ≥ 2`, and `A` an
ideal of `𝓞 K` whose norm is prime to `m`. Then the principal ideal generated by `Φ(A)` is
`A ^ γ = ∏_σ (σ⁻¹ A) ^ c(σ)`, the product over `σ ∈ Gal(K/ℚ)` with `c(σ)` the representative in
`[1, m)` of the class of `σ` in `(ZMod m)ˣ`. Here `γ = ∑_{1 ≤ t < m, (t, m) = 1} t σ_t⁻¹`.

```lean
Ideal.span_stickelbergerPhi.{u_1} {K : Type u_1} [Field K] [NumberField K] {m : ℕ} [NeZero m]
  [IsCyclotomicExtension {m} ℚ K] [HasEnoughRootsOfUnity (NumberField.RingOfIntegers K) m]
  (hm : 2 ≤ m) {A : Ideal (NumberField.RingOfIntegers K)} (hA : (Ideal.absNorm A).Coprime m) :
  Ideal.span {A.stickelbergerPhi m} =
    ∏ σ, (σ⁻¹ • A) ^ (↑((IsCyclotomicExtension.Rat.galEquivZMod m K) σ)).val
```
