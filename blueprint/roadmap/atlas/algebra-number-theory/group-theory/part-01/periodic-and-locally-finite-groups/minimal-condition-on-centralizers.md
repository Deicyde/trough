## Locally finite group

A group $G$ is locally finite when for every finite subset $S$ of $G$
the subgroup generated by $S$ is finite, that is, every finitely generated subgroup of $G$ is
finite.

## Minimal condition on centralizers

For a subset $X$ of a group $G$, write $C_G(X)$ for the
centralizer of $X$ in $G$. The group $G$ satisfies the minimal condition on centralizers when there
is no sequence of subsets $X_0, X_1, X_2, \ldots$ of $G$ whose centralizers form a strictly
descending chain $C_G(X_0) > C_G(X_1) > C_G(X_2) > \cdots$, where $H > K$ means that $K$ is a
proper subgroup of $H$.

## Wilson's question on $2$-groups with the minimal condition on centralizers (Kourovka problem 11.103)

**Wilson's question on $2$-groups with the minimal condition on centralizers (Kourovka
problem 11.103).** The statement that every group $G$ in which every element has order a power of
$2$ and which satisfies the minimal condition on centralizers is locally finite.

## Every $2$-group with the minimal condition on centralizers is locally finite (Kourovka 11.103)

Every group $G$ in which every element has order a power of $2$ and which satisfies the minimal
condition on centralizers is locally finite.

Wagner proved that the Sylow $2$-subgroups of a periodic group with the minimal condition on
centralizers are locally finite; a $2$-group is periodic and is its own Sylow $2$-subgroup. The
Kourovka Archive answers 11.103 "Yes, it is".
