## Global quantum geometric Langlands at irrational level

We prove the unramified quantum geometric Langlands equivalence for every connected simple complex algebraic group *G* and every level $c\in\mathbb C\setminus\mathbb Q$, including non-real levels. For every smooth projective connected complex curve *X*, it identifies the full twisted *D*-module categories on $\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _G(X)$ and $\mathop{\mathrm{Bun}}\nolimits _{G^\vee}(X)$ at the dual shifted levels *c* and $-1/(rc)$, where *r* is the lacing number. The equivalence uses the given global forms and includes all connected components.
