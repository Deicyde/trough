The attribute's comment: “the general field case, granted `h0FH5` (`SeparablePointComplementaryCycles`): for each closed point `x`, two zero cycles supported on separable closed points with `[x] + [Z] = [Z']`. THAT HYPOTHESIS IS THE MATHEMATICAL SUBSTANCE OF THE TAG — it is the output of Steps 3-8 of the source proof, the Bertini argument resting on Tags 01VU, 0FD5, 01Q3, 0FD6 and 0FFD (with 056T, 0BUG, 0FD1 for the reduction to a geometrically integral scheme over a finite separable extension). What is proved here is the passage from one closed point to all of `A_0(X)`: a class is that of a zero cycle, finitely supported since `X` is proper, whose support points have delta-dimension zero and are therefore closed. The perfect-field case is proved outright in `Stacks.Chow.mem_closure_separablePointClasses_of_perfectField`”.

> **Stacks 0FH5.** Let `k` be a field and let `X` be a smooth projective scheme over `k`. Then
> `CH_0(X) = A_0(X)` is generated by the classes of the closed points of `X` whose residue fields
> are separable over `k`.
>
> The per-point conclusion of the Bertini argument of the source proof is carried as
> `h0FH5 : Stacks.Chow.SeparablePointComplementaryCycles k`; everything else is proved. A class in
> `A_0(X)` is the class of a zero cycle, which has finite support because `X` is proper over `k`
> and hence quasi-compact, so the cycle is a finite integral combination of the prime cycles of its
> support points; each of those has delta-dimension zero and is therefore a closed point, and `h0FH5`
> replaces its class by the difference of two classes of cycles supported on closed points with
> separable residue fields.

```lean
theorem mem_closure_separablePointClasses (k : Type u) [Field k] {X : Scheme.{u}}
    (p : X ⟶ (fieldChowSituation k).S) [Smooth p] [IsProper p]
    (hproj : IsProjectiveMorphism p) (h0FH5 : SeparablePointComplementaryCycles.{u} k)
    (α : chowGroup (fieldChowSituation k) p 0) :
    α ∈ AddSubgroup.closure (separablePointClasses k p)
```

> **Stacks Project, Tag 0FH6.** For an algebraic field extension `K/k` and a quasi-compact
> scheme `X` of finite type over `k`, the Chow group `CH_i(X_K)` is the colimit of the Chow groups
> `CH_i(X_{k'})` over the finite subextensions `K/k'/k`, along the base-change homomorphisms.

```lean
def chowColimitAddEquiv_algebraicExtensionSystem [Algebra.IsAlgebraic k K]
    (X : Over (fieldChowSituation k).S) [CompactSpace X.left] [LocallyOfFiniteType X.hom]
    [QuasiCompact X.hom] (i : ℤ) :
    ↥(colimit ((algebraicExtensionSystem k K X).chowDiagram i)) ≃+
      chowGroup (fieldChowSituation K)
        (pullback.snd X.hom (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (algebraMap k K)))) i
```
