**`DividedPowers.TateResolution.squareZeroExtensionDerivation`**, a def in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/DividedPowers/TateResolution/SquareZeroLift.lean, line 180](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/DividedPowers/TateResolution/SquareZeroLift.lean#L180).

> **The degree-minus-two derivation of Tag 09PS.** Let `q : R' → R` be a surjection of
> commutative rings whose kernel is generated by a single element `f` of square zero, let
> `(A, 𝒜, γ, d)` be a graded divided power polynomial algebra over `R[xᵢ]` with a differential
> compatible with its divided powers, and let `I` be the image under `q` of the annihilator of `f`.
> Then `A/IA` carries an `R`-linear operator `θ` which obeys the Leibniz rule, lowers degrees by
> two, and vanishes in degrees zero and one.
>
> The construction the source retains is part of the statement: `θ` is the square of an operator
> `D` lifting `d` to a divided power polynomial algebra `B` over `R'[xᵢ]` on the same variables,
> compared into `A/IA` by division by `f`.

```lean
              π (δ.dpow n b) = γ.dpow n (π b)) ∧
            (∀ b : B, π b = 0 ↔ ∃ c : B, b = algebraMap R' B f * c) ∧
            (∀ (b : B), b ∈ ℬ 0 → D b = 0) ∧
            (∀ (n : ℕ) (b : B), b ∈ ℬ (n + 1) → D b ∈ ℬ n) ∧
```

> **Rodicio's theorem, Stacks Tag 09PV.** For proper ideals `I ⊆ J` of a Noetherian local ring
> `A` with `A/J` of finite Tor dimension over `A/I`, the canonical map `I/𝔪I → J/𝔪J` is injective.
>
> Finite Tor dimension is spelled as in Stacks Tag 0652: a degree beyond which
> `Tor_i^{A/I}(A/J, N)` vanishes for every `A/I`-module `N`.
>
> This is `StacksDraft.Rodicio.injective_mapQ_maximalIdeal_of_gulliksenObstruction` with its Tag
> 09PU hypothesis discharged by `StacksDraft.Rodicio.gulliksenObstruction`.

```lean
public theorem injective_mapQ_maximalIdeal_of_finite_torDimension

    (I J : Ideal A) (hI : I ≠ ⊤) (hJ : J ≠ ⊤) (hIJ : I ≤ J)
    (hTor : ∃ b : ℕ,
```

> Let `I ⊆ J` be proper ideals of a Noetherian local ring `A`. If `A / J` has finite Tor
> dimension over `A / I` and `J` is generated by a regular sequence, then `I` is generated by a
> regular sequence and the image of `J` in `A / I` is generated by a regular sequence of `A / I`.


## Tag 09PX, a lemma

[Tag 09PX](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/09PX) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.CompleteIntersection.regularSequence_quotient_tfae_of_flat`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/DividedPowers/CompleteIntersection/RegularSequenceTFAE.lean, line 400](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/DividedPowers/CompleteIntersection/RegularSequenceTFAE.lean#L400).

> **Stacks 09PX.** Let `R → S` be a flat local homomorphism of Noetherian local rings whose
> closed fibre is a regular local ring, let `I ⊆ 𝔪_R` and let `J` be an ideal of `S` containing
> `I S`. The following are equivalent:
>
> 1. `J` is generated by a regular sequence and `S / J` has finite Tor dimension over `R / I`;
> 2. `J` is generated by a regular sequence and the `Tor` groups of `S / J` against the residue
>    field of `R` vanish in all large degrees;
> 3. `I` is generated by a regular sequence of `R` and `J / IS` is generated by a regular sequence
>    of `S / IS`.
>
> The quotient ideal `J / IS` is represented by the image of `J` under `S → S / IS`, and the residue
> field of `R` is represented as the `R / I`-module `R / 𝔪_R`.

```lean
public theorem regularSequence_quotient_tfae_of_flat
    {R S : Type u} [CommRing R] [CommRing S]
    [IsNoetherianRing R] [IsNoetherianRing S] [IsLocalRing R] [IsLocalRing S]
    [Algebra R S] [IsLocalHom (algebraMap R S)] [Module.Flat R S]
    (I : Ideal R) (J : Ideal S) (hI : I ≤ IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R)
    (hIJ : I.map (algebraMap R S) ≤ J)
    (hfiber : IsRegularLocalRing
      (S ⧸ (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R).map (algebraMap R S))) :
    List.TFAE [
      (Ideal.IsGeneratedByRegularSequence J ∧
        FiniteTorDimension (quotientModule
          (Ideal.quotientMap J (algebraMap R S) ((Ideal.map_le_iff_le_comap).mp hIJ)))),
      (Ideal.IsGeneratedByRegularSequence J ∧
        ∃ n : ℕ, ∀ p : ℕ, n < p → IsZero
          (((Tor (ModuleCat.{u} (R ⧸ I)) p).obj
            (quotientModule
              (Ideal.quotientMap J (algebraMap R S) ((Ideal.map_le_iff_le_comap).mp hIJ)))).obj
            (quotientModule
              (Ideal.quotientMap (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R) (RingHom.id R)
                (by simpa using hI))))),
      (Ideal.IsGeneratedByRegularSequence I ∧
        Ideal.IsGeneratedByRegularSequence
          (J.map (Ideal.Quotient.mk (I.map (algebraMap R S)))))]
```
