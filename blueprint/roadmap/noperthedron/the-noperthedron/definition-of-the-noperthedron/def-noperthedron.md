# noperthedron

The Noperthedron $\operatorname{\mathbf{NOP}}$ is the convex polyhedron given by the vertex set

$$
\mathcal{C}_{30} \cdot C_1 \cup \mathcal{C}_{30} \cdot C_2 \cup \mathcal{C}_{30} \cdot C_3
$$
