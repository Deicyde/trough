# TauCeti.LimitGroup.MarkedGroup

An `n`-marked group, recorded by the kernel of its marking `FreeGroup (Fin n) →* G`. The type
`MarkedGroup n` is the space `𝒢ₙ` of normal subgroups of `FreeGroup (Fin n)`.
