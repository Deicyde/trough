- [Ambient answer-reduction contract](def-answer-reduction-contract.md)

Audit against the source (2026-09-23). Oracularization's first ambient contract was vacuous: a wrapper that keeps the input verifier satisfied every field (Remark The specification and the earlier contract). This one does not admit that witness, because its complexity clause does the work: an input whose decider runs in time $2^{(\lambda n+1)^\mu}$ at degree $\mu$, with answers of that length, must give an output within a polynomial in $(\lambda n+1)^\mu+\sigma+\lambda+n$ that rejects longer answers. A constant decider fails too: always accepting breaks soundness once $(\lambda n)^{-\mu b}$ is small, and always rejecting breaks completeness. Against the source's `thm:ar` (`ld_compiler.tex`) the contract is a weakening at every point where it differs:

- it drops the hypothesis that the input decider is in timeout-counter form, and assumes the time bound only semantically, through `Verifier.Within`, at the index in question; a construction clocks the input decider itself, as the typed oracularized decider does (Lemma [The typed oracularized decider](../part-07/lem-oracle-typed-decider.md));
- its decider bound $2^{(\lambda n+1)^\mu}$ exceeds the source's $(2^{\lambda n})^\mu$, but its logarithm is still polynomial in $(\lambda n+1)^\mu$, which is all the PCP needs;
- it allows $b = 1$ and requires $a \geq 1$, both choices of the prover;
- it omits the Entanglement clause, which compression does not use;
- $\delta$ vanishes at $\sigma = 0$, but no input satisfies $\left\vert {\mathcal{D}} \right\vert \leq 0$, program encodings having positive size (`MIPRE.Cost.esize_pos`).

So the contract is neither trivially satisfiable nor, as far as this comparison shows, stronger than the source. The comparison does not replace the proof, which is the only check of the time bounds against the construction.

## Gap amplification: parallel repetition

Answer reduction leaves a shrunken gap ($1$ vs. $1 - 1/\mathrm{poly}(n)$). Direct parallel repetition (Theorem [Direct parallel repetition for entangled strategies](../../parallel-repetition/thm-direct-repetition-q.md), formalized end to end) restores soundness $\frac12$ with polynomially many repetitions, at polynomial cost in complexity and without anchoring; the anchored, entanglement-form theorem used in (JNVWY20) ((BVY17)) is not needed (Remark [Direct versus anchored repetition](../../parallel-repetition/README.md)).

- [Parallel repetition of normal form verifiers](thm-parallel-repetition.md)

Lean statement. The theorem is the structure `MIPRE.Repetition`, parametrized by $\ell$: the repeated sampler as a function of the input sampler and $(\lambda, \tau)$ with its program computable in polynomial time, the repeated decider's program as a polynomial-time function of the input programs and $(\lambda, \tau, \beta)$, the packaging as an $\ell$-level normal form verifier, the complexity clause in the vocabulary of budgets (`Verifier.Within`) at a degree affine in the input's — the repeated decider runs the input decider through the universal machine, whose overhead is polynomial — and the two value clauses at the answer bounds stated, with $\lambda n + 1$ in place of $\lambda n$ so that every quantity is meaningful at every index. An earlier statement of this theorem coupled the answer length to a hypothesis $\mathsf{TIME}_\mathcal{D}(n) \leq (\lambda n)^\tau$ and took $k(n) = (\lambda n)^{(1 + c')\tau}$ for a constant $c'$ depending on a parameter $\kappa$ left over from the transport through ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$; writing the consumer showed that the repetition count and the answer length have to be independent parameters, and the constant $c'$ is now the choice of $\tau$ in Lemma [A universal repetition exponent](lem-compress-tau.md). It is inhabited by `MIPRE.repetition` (`MIPRE/Background/Repetition/Verifier.lean`): the repeated sampler runs $k(n)$ copies of $\mathcal{S}$ through the universal machine, its CL functions the direct sums of the input's (`MIPRE.Repeat.repSampler`); the repeated decider sanitizes its input, parses the two answers as self-delimiting tuples and runs $\mathcal{D}$ on every coordinate (`MIPRE.Repeat.repDecider`); both are the s-m-n construction on a fixed core, so their programs are polynomial-time functions of the inputs. The game of the output at index $n$ is the direct repetition of $\mathsf{V}_n$ up to a relabeling of the questions into blocks and the embedding of encoded answer tuples (`valStar_repVerifier`, by `quantumValue_extendAnswers`); completeness is the tensor power carried across that relabeling, soundness is Theorem [Direct parallel repetition for entangled strategies](../../parallel-repetition/thm-direct-repetition-q.md) on the repeated game, and the complexity clause is the explicit running-time accounting of the two programs (`repSampler_timeBound`, `repDecider_timeBound`), each dominated by $c (W + 1)^m (|d| + 1)^{e (k + 1)}$ for constants produced by the accounting itself, $W$ the argument of the polynomial.

## The compression theorem

The three exterior stages are introspection, full answer reduction (with its typed oracle step), and repetition. Their ambient contracts yield gap-preserving compression, the compression hypothesis of Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md). The soundness clause is the value-form clause that (JNVWY20) state first and then strengthen to an entanglement bound (Remark The entanglement form of (JNVWY20)).

- [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md)

The assumed ambient introspection contract outputs a $5$-level verifier, and the answer-reduction contract takes $\ell$ to $\max\{\ell+2,5\}$, here $7$; these match the exterior levels of the source constructions. Then direct repetition preserves it (Theorem [Parallel repetition of normal form verifiers](thm-parallel-repetition.md)); so ${\mathsf{Compress}}$ outputs a $7$-level verifier on every input, and $7$ is consequently the level at which the recursion closes. A verifier of fewer levels is presented at that level by padding its decomposition with trivial factors, which an $\ell$-level conditionally linear function (Definition [Conditionally linear function](../part-01/def-cl-function.md)) admits. In (JNVWY20) the count is $9$, anchored repetition adding two levels (Remark [Direct versus anchored repetition](../../parallel-repetition/README.md)).

The output contract. The conditional theorem constructs a `MIPRE.GapCompression`: a record of the constant $C_0$; the compressed sampler $\lambda \mapsto \mathcal{S}^{\textsc{Compr}}_\lambda$, a valid $7$-level sampler for every $\lambda$, with a polynomial-time program computing its description from the binary representation of $\lambda$ (Lemma [The compressed sampler does not depend on the verifier](lem-compress-sampler-indep.md)); the polynomial-time map ${\mathsf{Compress}}$ from pairs of programs and $\lambda$ to the program of the compressed decider; the packaging of every output, well-formed input or not, as a $7$-level normal form verifier with that sampler and that decider; the bound $\mathrm{poly}(n, \lambda)$ on the running times of the output at index $n$, at the degree `deg` in the size of the input that Definition [Decider](../../foundations/part-03/def-decider.md) carries, the same bound on the dimension of the compressed sampler (in the paper a consequence of its running time) and on the length of the answers the compressed decider can accept (`output_rejects_long`: the paper's repeated decider accepts only after fully parsing the answers, so an answer longer than its time bound forces a timeout, the property the amended proof of Lemma [Values of the halting verifier](lem-dhalt-values.md) relies on; in the ambient model, where running times scale with the input, it is a demand on the construction, an explicit length check); and the completeness and soundness clauses for $\lambda$-bounded $7$-level inputs at $n \geq C_0$. The checked pipeline constructs an instance from the stated transformation hypotheses; it does not inhabit introspection or answer reduction, nor discharge their source-model adaptations. The halting reduction consumes an instance (Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma). Two readings are fixed there. $\mathsf{V}_N$ is taken with answers of length at most $N^\lambda$, the explicitly chosen input answer cut (also the ambient runtime coefficient), and $\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Compr}}_n$ with answers of length at most the stated running-time bound — beyond which its decider rejects, so that its value is the same with any larger alphabet (`Verifier.valStar_eq_of_rejects`) — since the exact running times of Definition [Normal form verifier in the ambient model](../../foundations/part-03/def-normal-verifier.md) are not functions of the verifier in the formalization. And “a value-$1$ PCC strategy” is a PCC synchronous strategy of value $1$ for the game on the *doubled* question set (`Verifier.HasPerfectPCC`, on `Verifier.doubledGame`: Alice's questions tagged $0$, Bob's tagged $1$, every other pair of weight zero and rejected), which is a synchronous game for every verifier, so nothing is presupposed of the decider. An earlier reading took $\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Compr}}_n$ itself as the synchronous game, which asks its decider to reject unequal answers to equal questions — a property of the decider that the paper never states, *synchronous* there being a property of strategies, and one the paper's completeness clause does not supply. The doubled game is the precise ambient completeness interface. The source's identical operators and support commutation do not by themselves put its state in the ambient normalized tracial representation. Applying source completeness requires the ambient-to-source direction of Remark [Source PCC and the required representation bridge](../../foundations/README.md) for its input and the source-to-ambient direction for its output; those are supply obligations, not definitional identifications.

**Remark (How the value chain composes, and what it avoids).** The soundness clause of Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) is obtained by applying each transformation's soundness clause contrapositively, with a margin at every step. From $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathsf{V}_N) \leq \frac12$ and $\delta_1(\varepsilon_1, n) < \frac12$: were $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathsf{V}^{(1)}_n) > 1 - \varepsilon_1$, the assumed ambient introspection contract would give $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathsf{V}_N) \geq 1 - \delta_1 > \frac12$, so $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathsf{V}^{(1)}_n) \leq 1 - \varepsilon_1$. The same step through the assumed ambient answer-reduction contract, using $\delta_2(\varepsilon_2, n) < \varepsilon_1$ (Lemma [The margin claim](lem-compress-margin.md)), gives $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathsf{V}^{(2)}_n) \leq 1 - \varepsilon_2$; and Theorem [Parallel repetition of normal form verifiers](thm-parallel-repetition.md) with Lemma [A universal repetition exponent](lem-compress-tau.md) brings that below $\frac12$. Oracularization is internal to the proposed source proof of answer reduction; it is not a separate premise of this conditional value chain.

Every step of that chain is an implication between $\mathrm{val}^*$ bounds, which is worth recording because the route of (JNVWY20) cannot take it. There the repetition step's soundness hypothesis is an *entanglement* bound, and $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathfrak{G}, 1 - \varepsilon) = \infty$ needs $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathfrak{G}) < 1 - \varepsilon$ *strictly* — a non-strict $\leq$ leaves the value possibly attained and the entanglement requirement finite. So the paper runs the whole chain in $\mathrm{Ent}$, starting it at the threshold $1 - \delta_1$ rather than at $\frac12$; the value-form route would force it to apply repetition at $\varepsilon_2/2$ instead of $\varepsilon_2$, at a cost of $2^{17}$ in the exponent of its repetition parameter. Direct repetition (Theorem [Direct parallel repetition for entangled strategies](../../parallel-repetition/thm-direct-repetition-q.md)) takes a value hypothesis and so needs no strictness and pays no such factor: this is the second place, after Remark [Direct versus anchored repetition](../../parallel-repetition/README.md), where the value-form pipeline is cheaper than the entanglement-form one rather than merely equivalent to it. It is also why no entanglement lower bound has to be tracked anywhere in this chapter.

**Remark (Relation to the abstract compression lemma).** Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) instantiates the hypotheses of Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md): take $A$ to be the set of (descriptions of) normal form verifier games with a value-$1$ PCC strategy and $B$ the set of those with $\mathrm{val}^* \leq \frac12$, with the trivially accepting and the trivially rejecting game as the distinguished elements; the complement of $B$ is semidecided by enumerating tensor-product strategies, the $\mathrm{val}^*$ half of Lemma [The value is approximable from below](../../background-results/part-05/lem-value-lower-approx.md); and a description $(\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{D}, n)$ with $n$ in binary is a succinct description of the game $\mathsf{V}_{2^n}$ played by a $\lambda$-bounded verifier. Completeness of compression is the preservation of $A$; preservation of $B$ is the soundness clause of Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) verbatim, hypothesis and conclusion being in the same value. Taking $B$ in $\mathrm{val}^*$ throughout is what keeps Theorem [Almost-synchronous strategies are near synchronous strategies](https://github.com/vidick/MIPRE-formalization/blob/5926bdff5db621f44682a101007ef2fe3ae8d970/blueprint/src/content/03_background_results.tex#L137) out of the pipeline altogether: the source transformations and direct repetition already use that value (Remark Why the pipeline is bipartite throughout). It is also the form in which the semidecision procedure is available, the rounding argument for the ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$ half not being the same one. Making this dictionary precise is a formalization task of this section. The four obligations recorded here from the referee report (R1) — the universe of the criterion's strings, compression from a *succinct* input, preservation on inputs that are not $\lambda$-bounded descriptions, and the choice of $\lambda$ along the recursion — are settled as follows, against the per-level form of the lemma (`compressibility_criterion_levels`, Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md)), whose classes $A_n, B_n$ are indexed by the level of the recursion.

1. *Strings are descriptions, never games.* If they were games, $\textsc{Compr}$ could not be polynomial-time, a game of $\mathsf{V}_{2^n}$ having $2^{\mathrm{poly}(n, \lambda)}$ questions. A string is a pair $(\lambda, \mathcal{D})$ of a parameter and a decider program, read as the verifier $(\mathcal{S}^{\textsc{Compr}}_\lambda, \mathcal{D})$ with the compressed sampler of Lemma [The compressed sampler does not depend on the verifier](lem-compress-sampler-indep.md), the length check of Definition [Normal form verifier in the ambient model](../../foundations/part-03/def-normal-verifier.md) being enforced by a wrapper around $\mathcal{D}$; so every string names a normal form verifier, and the interpretation sits outside every time bound. In Lean the decider component is the *datum* the string carries, run by the universal machine (`Verifier.ofSamplerDeciderD`): a datum that is not the encoding of any program still names a verifier, whatever the interpreter does with it, and on the encoding of a program the reading is the program's own run (`Decider.wrap_accepts_prog`). Decoding the datum to a program with a fallback, the earlier reading, would have obliged the compressor's decider of item 2 to reimplement the decoder — a tree-grammar check — in the ambient model, to reproduce a fallback nobody wanted. $A_n$ is the set of strings whose verifier is $n$-bounded and whose $n$-th game has a value-$1$ PCC strategy and whose decider rejects every answer longer than the answer bound at index $n$; $B_n$ the set of those that are $n$-bounded, reject long answers, and have $\mathrm{val}^* \leq \frac12$ at index $n$ (`Verifier.InClassA`, `InClassB`, the rejection clause being `Verifier.RejectsLong`; disjoint, since a perfect PCC strategy gives $\mathrm{val}^* = 1$). The rejection clause is Step 6 of the paper's decider $F$ — an explicit length check before $\mathcal{D}^{\textsc{Compr}}$ is run, which the amended proof of Lemma [Values of the halting verifier](lem-dhalt-values.md) spends to identify the answer alphabets of $\mathsf{V}^{\mathsf{halt}}_n$ and $\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Compr}}_n$ — made a condition on membership: the soundness direction of the compressor's obligation raises the answer bound from the one a class is read with to the one Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) takes its hypothesis at, and only a decider that rejects beyond the former keeps the value fixed under that (`Verifier.valStar_eq_of_rejects`; enlarging the bound can otherwise only raise the value). Only the length check has to go into the wrapper: the answer-length bound is a condition on membership in these classes, not on well-formedness, synchronicity is asked of no decider at all (the value-$1$ PCC strategy lives on the doubled game), and the paper's timeout counter is not needed either — a decider that fails to halt simply fails to be $n$-bounded, and its string then lies in neither class. Tying the bound $\lambda = n$ to the level is what replaces the choice of $\lambda$ along the recursion (Lemma [The parameter of the halting verifier](lem-lambda.md)): the levels $r(e) = 2^{K + 1 + \left\vert {e} \right\vert}$ at which the recursion runs are exponential in $\left\vert {e} \right\vert$, above every polynomial in $\left\vert {e} \right\vert$ that the paper's $\lambda$ has to dominate.
2. *Compression from a succinct input.* The compressor cannot read the string it is handed: its time is polynomial in the length of $(\hat x, n)$, hence polylogarithmic in $n$, and a single bit query to $\hat x$ costs up to $n$. On $(\hat x, n)$ it therefore outputs the string $(\lambda(n), \mathcal{D}_{\hat x})$, where $\lambda(n) = \mathrm{poly}(n)$ is a fixed formula and $\mathcal{D}_{\hat x}$ is the decider that, at its own index $N$, reads $x = (\lambda', \mathcal{D}')$ from $\hat x$ by bit queries (at most $2n + O(1)$ of them, rejecting otherwise), forms the verifier $\mathsf{V}'$ of $x$ *frozen at index $2n + 1$* (`Verifier.freeze`: a program that replaces the index in its input by the frozen one, so that its sampler and decider answer at every index as $\mathsf{V}'$ does at index $2n + 1$, and its game at index $2^n$ is the game of $x$ at level $2n + 1$ — `freeze_valStar`, `freeze_hasPerfectPCC`, with $\lambda$-boundedness transferred by `freeze_isBounded`; the answer-length bound is immaterial above the length the decider rejects, `Verifier.valStar_eq_of_rejects`, `hasPerfectPCC_of_le`) — runs ${\mathsf{Compress}}(\mathsf{V}', \lambda(n))$, and finally the compressed decider at index $N$. The freezing reconciles the recursion's levels $n \to 2n + 1$ with the compression theorem's $n \leftarrow 2^n$, at the cost of a logarithmic factor in $\lambda(n)$. Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) at index $n \geq C_0$ then transfers the class of $x$ at level $2n + 1$ to the class of the output at level $n$, and the output is $n$-bounded for $n$ above a universal threshold: its size is $\left\vert {\hat x} \right\vert + O(\log n) \leq n$ by the slack $2\left\vert {\hat x} \right\vert \leq n$ of the lemma, and its time at index $N$ is $\mathrm{poly}(N, n) \leq N^n$. In Lean that transfer is done once, against a specification of the output written before its construction — `MIPRE.Halting.CompressorSpec`: the parameter $\lambda(n)$ the output carries, the agreement of its decider at every index with the compressed decider of the frozen verifier, its $n$-boundedness, the growth of $\lambda(n)$ that `freeze_isBounded` consumes, and the answer-budget comparison of `exists_ansBound_le` — and `CompressorSpec.toObligations` is the class transfer, in both directions, from those fields alone. What the construction owes is exactly the fields of that structure. The program half is done: `MIPRE.Halting.haltProg` is the decider of the output — it reads $x$ from its succinct description by bit queries (`Cost.Prog.readProg`), rebuilds the description of the wrapped decider of $x$ and freezes it at $2n + 1$ in the ambient model, runs ${\mathsf{Compress}}$, and runs the result on its own input through the universal machine (`prepProg`, `prepProg_runs`) —, the output string `comprStr` is $\lambda(n)$ together with that decider with $(c, n, \lambda(n))$ hardcoded, and `comprStr_accepts` is the field `accepts_compr` verbatim, from the forward runs and the inversion `haltProg_accepts_iff`. The accounting is done too, with $\lambda(n) = 2^{2 \log n}$ (Lemma [The parameter of the halting verifier](lem-lambda.md): `isBounded_comprStr`, `lamOf_ge`, `ansBound_le_lamOf`), the compressor is a polynomial-time function (`comprPoly`), and `exists_obligations` inhabits the structure: obligation (d) is discharged.
3. *Junk inputs.* Nothing is demanded of the compressor off strings in $A_{2n+1} \cup B_{2n+1}$, every string names a verifier, and $\lambda$-boundedness is decided nowhere. What the lemma does demand is that the complement of $B_n$ be semidecidable: “not $n$-bounded” is witnessed by an index and an input on which a time bound fails — a search, so $\Sigma_1$, and this is Lemma [A violated time bound is enumerable](lem-bounded-violation-re.md) —, “accepts some answer longer than the bound” is witnessed by the tuple and a run, a search again (`Verifier.rePred_not_rejectsLong`), and “$\mathrm{val}^* > \frac12$ at index $n$” is the $\mathrm{val}^*$ half of Lemma [The value is approximable from below](../../background-results/part-05/lem-value-lower-approx.md) applied to the tabulated game, which is computable from an $n$-bounded verifier. No disjunct decides $n$-boundedness, which is $\Pi_1$; the point of taking the disjunction is that it does not have to (`Verifier.not_inClassB_iff`), and Lemma [The semidecider of the halting reduction](lem-halting-semidecider.md) is the three merged. Two things about the search for a violated bound. The budget it tests against is a *cost* and not a number of machine steps, and a step is not a unit of cost, so `Machine.runForD` below does not decide it and Lemma [Halting within a cost budget is decidable](../../computability/lem-cost-budget-decidable.md) is what does. And the index at which a bound fails ranges over all of $\mathbb{N}$, while the bound reads its input with the index encoded in binary; carrying the index as a *datum*, read back by `Data.natOf`, is what makes the encoding primitive recursive in the search witness and so keeps the enumeration from needing the primitive recursiveness of $n \mapsto \mathrm{encode}(n)$, which the development did not have when that enumeration was written — it is now `MIPRE.Cost.Data.primrec_encode_nat`, written for the tabulation of item 3 and load-bearing under it. The clause “computable from an $n$-bounded verifier” is load-bearing and not a convenience: acceptance by a decider is $\Sigma_1$, so a tabulation correct at *every* string would decide the halting problem — take two strings whose deciders differ only in how long they run before accepting. Boundedness is what makes acceptance decidable, by supplying a budget within which the decider must halt, and one budgeted run then settles it (`Verifier.accepts_iff_runForD`, on `Machine.runForD`: iterate the evaluation machine's step function $3k$ times and read off the result if the configuration reached is final, which is enough because a derivation of cost $t$ is a run of at most $3t$ steps and final configurations are fixed). Every Lean statement about the tabulation's *acceptance* carries that hypothesis for this reason — the two sampler runs are budgeted otherwise and carry none, as the paragraph after this enumerate records — and it does one more thing than supply the budget: the time clauses of $\lambda$-boundedness are guarded by $2 \leq n$, so at $n = 0, 1$ no budget exists at all, and what excludes those two indices is the size clause — every program encodes to a non-empty datum, so $2 \leq \left\vert {\mathcal{D}} \right\vert \leq \left\vert {\mathsf{V}} \right\vert \leq \lambda$ (`Verifier.IsBounded.two_le`, on `Cost.Prog.two_le_esize`). Each statement carried a second hypothesis until the doubling, and why it did is worth naming: a game description denotes a *synchronous* game by construction — Definition [Game description](../../foundations/part-02/def-game-description.md) rejects unequal answers to equal questions — whereas $\mathsf{V}_n$ does no such thing, so no description can match a verifier that is not synchronous at $n$, and $B_n$ does not ask that it be. What is tabulated is therefore not the game of $\mathsf{V}_n$ but its *doubled* one (`MIPRE.Game.doubled`, `Verifier.doubledGame`): the questions are $\{0,1\} \times {\mathcal{X}}$, Alice is asked a question tagged $0$ and Bob one tagged $1$, and every other pair — the diagonal included — carries no weight and is rejected outright. Two things follow, and both are what make the repair cheap. The diagonal carries no weight, so the description's veto is *implied by the distribution* rather than imposed on the verifier, and the doubled game is synchronous whatever $\mathsf{V}_n$ is; and since the decision predicate rejects off the tag block as well, a description matches the doubled game *totally*, at every question pair and not merely on the support, so `quantumValue_eq_of_equiv` and `SyncStrategy.isPCC_relabel` apply unchanged and no support-restricted version of either is needed. Neither value moves: $\mathrm{val}^*$ is unchanged because a strategy for the doubled game restricts to Alice's $0$-tagged and Bob's $1$-tagged measurements while one for the original ignores the tag (`quantumValue_doubled`), and a value-$1$ PCC strategy doubles to one (`SyncStrategy.double`, `isPCC_double`), the doubled support mapping *into* the original so that no commutation obligation is added. The match (`MIPRE.Halting.tab_match`) is therefore asked of every $n$-bounded verifier, synchronous or not, which is what Lemma [The semidecider of the halting reduction](lem-halting-semidecider.md) needs and what $B_n$ was never going to supply; and the separate soundness clause that used to stand beside it — that the description does not *overshoot*, forcing the diagonal tuples to reject being a lowering of the value — is deleted, the soundness branch of Theorem [Halting reduction](thm-halting.md) taking the equality of values instead. The tabulation itself is read with the answer alphabet enumerated by `Verifier.answerEquiv` and the doubled question alphabet by `Verifier.tagEquiv`, a tag bit prepended to the point's bit string, and a description matching the doubled game along those indexings has the value of $\mathsf{V}_n$ (`quantumValue_toGame_eq_valStar_doubled`), which is what carries the verdict of a procedure that runs on game descriptions back to the verifier. The same transport in ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$ is `Verifier.syncValue_syncGame_eq_one_doubled`, which is what item 1 of Theorem [Halting reduction](thm-halting.md) needs; see the end of this remark.
4. *The choice of $\lambda$.* Absorbed into item 1.

Of this dictionary the per-level lemma, the Lean statement of Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) as a hypothesis (`MIPRE.GapCompression`) and the value bookkeeping are in Lean: the quantum value is invariant under relabelings of the alphabets (`quantumValue_eq_of_equiv`, the tool for the tabulation) and under always-rejected extra answers (`quantumValue_extendAnswers`, by zero extension one way and merging of outcomes the other). Both clauses are where the doubling is actually performed, and each takes one indexing that prepends a tag bit: `Verifier.accListW` is the acceptance table indexed by two *given* indexings of the questions rather than by `bitsToIdx` twice, with the old `accList` recovered as the case of two equal ones by `rfl`, so that nothing downstream of that name changed. Prepending the tag rather than appending it is what keeps the bounds and injectivity of `bitsToIdx` applicable verbatim at length $s(n) + 1$; and the weight list still enumerates $\mathbb{F}_2^{s(n)}$ once, so the total weight is still $2^{s(n)}$ — enumerating the strings of length $s(n) + 1$ instead would compile and silently halve every probability. The entanglement form of (JNVWY20) (Remark The entanglement form of (JNVWY20)) instantiates Lemma [Recursive compression](../../computability/lem-recursive-compression.md) instead, with $f = \mathrm{Ent}(\cdot, \frac12)$; it is not used by the main theorem.

Comments. The hypothesis is an equality of values under $n$-boundedness *alone*, and for a while that was more than the tabulation delivered: a game description denotes a synchronous game by construction, so the match was asked only of a verifier synchronous at $n$ (item 3 of Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma), while membership in $B_n$ does not carry synchronicity, and the semidecider was therefore *not* obtainable from the tabulation together with a disjunction, as the plan had it. The other two were rejected for what they would have cost elsewhere, which is why the choice was not this lemma's to make. Making the wrapper of item 1 reject unequal answers to equal questions would change what a string *denotes*, and the denotation is what `accOf_iff`, the classes $A_n, B_n$ and the compression hypothesis of Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) are all stated about; all three would be reopened, and the wrapper is deliberately the length check and nothing else (item 1). **Remark (The entanglement form of (JNVWY20)).** The paper states the soundness of each transformation with an additional entanglement clause, obtained from the same proofs by tracking the Schmidt rank of the extracted strategies: $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Intro}}_n, 1 - \varepsilon) \geq \max\{\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}_{2^n}, 1 - \delta(\varepsilon, n)),\ (1 - \delta(\varepsilon, n))\, 2^{2^{\lambda n}}\}$ for introspection; $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Orac}}_n, 1 - \varepsilon) \geq \mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}_n, 1 - \delta(\varepsilon))$ for oracularization; $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}^{\mathsf{ar}}_n, 1 - \varepsilon) \geq \frac12 \mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}_n, 1 - \delta(\varepsilon, n))$ for answer reduction, the factor $\frac12$ being the Schmidt-rank doubling of the symmetrization step; the anchored repetition theorem of (BVY17); and, composed, $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Compr}}_n, \frac12) \geq \frac12 \max\{\mathrm{Ent}(\mathsf{V}_N, \frac12),\ 2^{N^\lambda - 1}\}$ for compression. Fed into the variant of Lemma [Recursive compression](../../computability/lem-recursive-compression.md) tolerating the factor $\frac12$ (harmless, since the second term grows much faster than the levels of the recursion), with $f = \mathrm{Ent}(\cdot, \frac12)$, this yields Theorem [Halting reduction](thm-halting.md) with the stronger conclusion $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathfrak{G}_\mathcal{M}, \frac12) = \infty$ for non-halting $\mathcal{M}$. The value form adopted here needs none of it; the entanglement clauses remain of interest for the corollaries of Remark [further](../../downstream-results/README.md).

One proviso on the measure: the clauses above are transcribed from (JNVWY20), where the quantity is the least Schmidt rank of a *bipartite* tensor-product strategy, whereas $\mathrm{Ent}$ of Definition [Entanglement requirement](../../foundations/part-01/def-ent.md) is the least dimension of a *synchronous* one. Identifying these measures would require a separate strategy and dimension comparison; no such comparison follows merely from the synchronization statement of Theorem [Almost-synchronous strategies are near synchronous strategies](https://github.com/vidick/MIPRE-formalization/blob/5926bdff5db621f44682a101007ef2fe3ae8d970/blueprint/src/content/03_background_results.tex#L137). These clauses are recorded in the paper's measure and are not consumed by the value-form Lean pipeline.

The paper's route, at a fixed level. The source's statement is now formalized for the verifier itself: `MIPRE.Halting.halting_paper` (`Halting/Paper/Main.lean`) says that for every machine $\mathcal{M}$ and every $\lambda \geq \Lambda_0 + 4\left\vert {\mathcal{M}} \right\vert$ (Lemma [The parameter of the halting verifier](lem-lambda.md)), the verifier $\texttt{Vpaper}\ \mathcal{M}\ \lambda = \texttt{Vhalt}\ \mathcal{M}\ \lambda$ of Lemma [The self-referential decider is well defined](lem-halt-construction.md) with the search program `semL`, at the *fixed* level $C = \max(C_0, 2)$ and with answers of length at most the compressor's bound $\mathrm{poly}(C, \lambda)$, has a value-$1$ PCC strategy if $\mathcal{M}$ halts on the empty input and value at most $\frac12$ if it does not (`halting_paper_valStar` in $\mathrm{val}^*$). The proof is the downward induction of the source along the levels $C, 2^C, 2^{2^C}, \ldots$ (`Halting/Paper/Induction.lean`): a halting machine puts every level past its halting time in the class of value-$1$ PCC verifiers (item 1 of Lemma [Values of the halting verifier](lem-dhalt-values.md)), and the completeness clause of Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md) carries the class down one level at a time (`A_step`), the search branch never firing since it would put the start level below $\frac12$; for a non-halting machine, a value above $\frac12$ at level $C$ makes the search halt at some step, every level whose $\log$ exceeds it has value $0$, and the soundness clause carries $\leq \frac12$ down to $C$ (`B_step`), a contradiction. This is the criterion's argument at a fixed level with the succinct description replaced by the hardcoded one. The game is the source's $\mathsf{V}^{\mathsf{halt}}$ at index $C$: its sampler is $\mathtt{ComputeSampler}(\lambda(\mathcal{M}))$ and its decider runs within the polynomial bound of Lemma [The parameter of the halting verifier](lem-lambda.md), which is what makes the reduction polynomial-time once read off a uniform program, the class-level statement of Definition [The class $\mathrm{MIP}^*_{1,1/2}(2,1)$](../../foundations/part-03/def-mipstar.md).

The class verifier. That program is `MIPRE.Halting.classV` (`Halting/Paper/ClassVerifier.lean`, `ClassMain.lean`), a `PolyVerifier` for a polynomial-time map $R : z \mapsto \mathcal{M}_z$. Its sampler `sampProg` is that of Lemma [The class verifiers' sampler](lem-class-sampler.md) for the compressed samplers at the level $C$: on $(z, r)$ it computes $\mathcal{M}_z$, $\lambda(z) = \Lambda_0 + 4\left\vert {\mathcal{M}_z} \right\vert$ and the compressed sampler's program $\mathtt{ComputeSampler}(\lambda(z))$, asks it its dimension $s(C)$ through the universal machine, and returns its two marginals at the first $s(C)$ bits of the seed (`sampProg_runs`); so the uniform seed of length $B \geq s(C)$ pushes forward to the compressed sampler's distribution. Its decider `decProg`, on $(z, x, y, a, b)$, computes the description of $\mathcal{D}^{\mathsf{halt}}$ (`decBuild`, the fixed tree around $\mathcal{M}_z$ and $\lambda(z)$) and its wrapper, rejects if $a$ or $b$ exceeds the cutoff $2^{K + \deg \cdot \left\vert {\lambda(z)} \right\vert}$ — a power of two, computable from $\left\vert {\lambda(z)} \right\vert$ in unary and above the compressor's answer bound for $K$ chosen from $G$ — and otherwise runs the wrapper on $(C, x, y, a, b)$ through the universal machine (`decProg_accepts`). Both run within an explicit polynomially bounded cost in their total input length (`sampProg_runs`, `decProg_runs`), so the verifier is efficient (`classV_efficient`), and its game has the value of $\mathsf{V}^{\mathsf{halt}}$ at level $C$ with the answers cut at the cutoff (`classV_value`: the game is the restriction of $\mathsf{V}^{\mathsf{halt}}$'s to the questions of length $s(C)$, `quantumValue_restrictQuestions`, with the answers extended by always-rejected ones, `quantumValue_extendAnswers`), which is $1$ or at most $\frac12$ by the previous paragraph (`classV_values`). This is the polynomial-time clause of the theorem, and $\mathrm{RE} \subseteq \mathrm{MIP}^*$ for the class of Definition [The class $\mathrm{MIP}^*_{1,1/2}(2,1)$](../../foundations/part-03/def-mipstar.md) in Theorem [$\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$](../../the-main-theorem/thm-mipstar-eq-re.md).

The proof is Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md) applied to the instantiation of Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma; the semidecidability hypothesis of the lemma is the $\mathrm{val}^*$ half of Lemma [The value is approximable from below](../../background-results/part-05/lem-value-lower-approx.md). The obligations of that instantiation are inherited here, and all four listed in Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma are formalized: the tabulation (`MIPRE.Halting.tab`, with `tab_match`, `tab_value` and `gameValue_tab_eq_one`), the semidecider on top of it (`MIPRE.Halting.exists_sem`, Lemma [The semidecider of the halting reduction](lem-halting-semidecider.md), which takes no hypotheses), the two distinguished strings `MIPRE.Halting.yYes_mem` and `yNo_mem`, and the compressor (Lemmas [The self-referential decider is well defined](lem-halt-construction.md), [The parameter of the halting verifier](lem-lambda.md) and [Values of the halting verifier](lem-dhalt-values.md): `MIPRE.Halting.comprPoly`, its specification `exists_compressorSpec`, and `exists_obligations`). The synchronization invariant that an earlier revision also required of the games produced inside ${\mathsf{Compress}}$ is no longer among them (Remark Why the pipeline is bipartite throughout).

In Lean the assembly is `MIPRE.Halting.halting_reduction` (`Halting/Reduction.lean`), which takes three hypotheses: a `MIPRE.GapCompression` (which is Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md)), a universal machine, and an inhabitant of `MIPRE.Halting.Obligations`, whose content is exactly the compressor — `compr`, `compr_spec`, and the threshold above which its guarantee holds. The other three obligations of Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma are theorems that the proof applies directly, and the fourth is now a theorem too: `MIPRE.Halting.exists_obligations` (`Halting/CompressorProgram.lean`) inhabits the structure, so `MIPRE.Halting.halting_reduction_of` takes only a `GapCompression` and a universal machine — of which `Cost.exists_efficient_universal` provides one — and is free of `sorry`. So the reduction is machine-checked from an inhabitant of the compression contract alone; supplying that inhabitant is Theorem [Ambient compression inhabitation target](thm-compression-target.md). The `#print axioms` check shows no further unproved assumption. That assembly concludes in $\mathrm{val}^*$ only, and item 1 claims more: the witness is a *PCC* strategy, so ${\mathrm{val}^{s}} = \mathrm{val}^* = 1$. The two steps that carry it there are now in Lean, neither of them part of the instantiation. First, a synchronous strategy transports along a relabeling of the alphabets (`MIPRE.syncValue_eq_of_equiv`, from `SyncStrategy.relabel`, which preserves the value and the commutation condition), so a value-$1$ PCC strategy of $\mathsf{V}_n$ — a strategy of its doubled game, which is what `HasPerfectPCC` is — gives one for a description tabulating that doubled game: `Verifier.exists_perfectPCC_syncGame_doubled`, hence `Verifier.syncValue_syncGame_eq_one_doubled`. Second, `MIPRE.syncValue` and `HaltingGameValue.gameValue` — parallel developments with parallel notions of synchronous strategy — are the same number on the same description (`HaltingGameValue.GameData.gameValue_eq_syncValue`), so the conclusion is available in the vocabulary of `halting_reduces_to_gameValue` (Section [The main theorem](../../the-main-theorem/README.md)): `Verifier.gameValue_toGame_eq_one_doubled`, which on the tabulation is `MIPRE.Halting.gameValue_tab_eq_one`. Soundness needs neither step, only ${\mathrm{val}^{s}} \leq \mathrm{val}^*$ (Lemma [Synchronous value lower-bounds the quantum value](../../foundations/part-01/lem-sync-le-valstar.md)), which is `Verifier.gameValue_toGame_le_of_valStar_le_doubled` in the same vocabulary. The soundness bridge against $\mathsf{V}_n$ itself is still there and still true; the three un-doubled completeness bridges went with the restatement of `HasPerfectPCC` on the doubled game (Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](thm-compression.md), “two readings”), a strategy of the doubled game having no reason to agree with itself across the two tags on the diagonal, which carries no weight. Both steps consume the tabulation's agreement with $\mathsf{V}_n$ in the form of the two alphabet equivalences and the matching of $\mu$ and $D$ along them, not merely the equality of $\mathrm{val}^*$ they imply — from that equality alone the synchronous half cannot be recovered, ${\mathrm{val}^{s}} \leq \mathrm{val}^*$ pointing the wrong way. That is why `MIPRE.Halting.tab_match` delivers the matching data and not only the equality of values, with `tab_value` reading that equality off it; the tabulation builds the equivalences anyway, out of `answerEquiv` and `questionEquiv`, so the stronger statement cost nothing.

**Remark (Identical measurement operators).** The conditional ambient halting construction yields a witness in `SyncStrategy`, represented by a single projective measurement family and normalized trace probabilities. Within that representation the common family is part of the definition (Definition [Synchronous strategy](../../foundations/part-01/def-sync-strategy.md)). The source instead tracks identical operators on a bipartite state through its transformations. These are distinct representations: identical operators alone do not supply either direction of Remark [Source PCC and the required representation bridge](../../foundations/README.md). The checked downstream halting and tabulation proofs start with the ambient interface and therefore do not discharge that source-to-ambient obligation. The entanglement form, with the stronger conclusion $\mathrm{Ent}(\mathfrak{G}_\mathcal{M}, \frac12) = \infty$ for non-halting $\mathcal{M}$, is discussed in Remark The entanglement form of (JNVWY20).
