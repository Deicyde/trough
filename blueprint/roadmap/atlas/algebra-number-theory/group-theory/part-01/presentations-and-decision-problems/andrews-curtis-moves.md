## Elementary Andrews–Curtis moves

Let $F_n$ be the free group on $n$ generators. A map
$f \colon F_n^n \to F_n^n$ on $n$-tuples $(u_1, \dots, u_n)$ of elements of $F_n$ is an elementary
Andrews–Curtis move if there is an index $i$ such that $f$ replaces the entry $u_i$ of every tuple
by $u_i u_j$, $u_i u_j^{-1}$, $u_j u_i$ or $u_j^{-1} u_i$ for a fixed index $j \ne i$, or by
$u_i^{-1}$, or by $w u_i w^{-1}$ for a fixed $w \in F_n$, and leaves all other entries unchanged.

## The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$

For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ let $F_n$ be the free group on $n$
generators. The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$ is the subgroup of the group of permutations of the set
$F_n^n$ of $n$-tuples $(u_1, \dots, u_n)$ of elements of $F_n$ generated by the elementary
Andrews–Curtis moves that are permutations: $u_i \mapsto u_i u_j^{\pm 1}$ and
$u_i \mapsto u_j^{\pm 1} u_i$ for indices $i \ne j$, $u_i \mapsto u_i^{-1}$, and
$u_i \mapsto w u_i w^{-1}$ for $w \in F_n$, all other entries unchanged. Viewing a tuple as the
relators of a balanced presentation $\langle x_1, \dots, x_n \mid u_1, \dots, u_n \rangle$, these
are the Nielsen transformations of the relator tuple together with conjugations of relators.

## The Andrews–Curtis group $AC_2$ is not finitely presented (Kourovka 18.89)

The Andrews–Curtis
group $AC_2$, the subgroup of the permutation group of the set $F_2^2$ of pairs of elements of the
free group $F_2$ generated by the elementary Andrews–Curtis moves, is not finitely presented.

The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) records this, citing Krstić–McCool (1997) and Roman'kov (2023),
who also shows that the group of moves on all relator tuples is isomorphic to the Andrews–Curtis
group of $F_2$.

## The Andrews–Curtis groups $AC_n$ are finitely presented for $n \ge 3$ (Kourovka 18.89)

For
every $n \ge 3$, the Andrews–Curtis group $AC_n$, the subgroup of the permutation group of the set
$F_n^n$ of $n$-tuples of elements of the free group $F_n$ generated by the elementary Andrews–Curtis
moves, is finitely presented.

The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) records this, deducing it from Kiralis–Krstić–McCool (1996),
Theorem 3.1, through an isomorphism observed by M. Ershov (letter of 2025, using Roman'kov's
Lemma 3.2).
