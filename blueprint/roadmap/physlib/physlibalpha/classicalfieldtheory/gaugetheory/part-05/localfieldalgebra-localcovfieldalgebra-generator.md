> The covariant generators of a gauge field theory sorted by kind and mass weight, and the
> products of them, which span the covariant field algebra.

## i. Overview

> The covariant field algebra `T.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` of a field datum `T` is generated by
> three kinds of covariant expressions.
>
> - Gauge. The field strength `F_μν` and its covariant derivatives `∇_l F_μν`.
> - Scalar. The bosonic matter fields `φ`, their conjugates `φ†`, and their covariant
>   derivatives.
> - Fermion. The fermionic matter fields `ψ`, their conjugates `ψ̄`, and their covariant
>   derivatives.
>
> Each generator also carries a mass weight, twice its mass dimension. The tower `∇_l F_μν` has
> weight `4 + 2 |l|`, and the tower of `n` covariant derivatives of a species of mass weight `c`,
> or of its conjugate, has weight `c + 2 n`. The set `T.weightedGenerators k w` collects the
> generators of kind `k` and weight `w`.
>
> A product of generators is recorded as a list of generators, each with its kind and weight. The
> set `T.weightedMonomials A w` holds the products whose set of kinds is `A` and whose weights add
> up to `w`. Every element of the covariant field algebra is a combination of such products. The
> algebra is not free on its generators, so this combination is not unique, and every argument
> below works with the products as a spanning set only.
>
> The gauge and Lorentz groups carry each generator into the span of the generators of the same
> kind and weight, so they preserve the span of the products of any given kinds and weight. When
> the mass weights of the fermionic species are odd and those of the bosonic species even, the
> mass weight also records the statistics: two products of weights `v` and `w` exchange up to the
> sign `(-1) ^ (v * w)`.

## ii. Key results

> - `GaugeFieldData.FieldKind` : the three kinds of covariant generators.
> - `GaugeFieldData.kindGenerators`, `GaugeFieldData.weightedGenerators` : the generators of a
>   given kind, and of a given kind and mass weight.
> - `GaugeFieldData.weightedMonomials` : the products of generators of given kinds and weight.
> - `GaugeFieldData.mem_span_weightedMonomials` : the products span the covariant field algebra.
> - `GaugeFieldData.map_mem_span_weightedMonomials` : a unital multiplicative map preserving the
>   kinds and weights of the generators preserves the span of the products of given kinds and
>   weight.
> - `GaugeFieldData.anticomm_of_mem_span_kindGenerators_fermion` : the fermionic generators
>   anticommute on their span.
> - `GaugeFieldData.mul_eq_smul_mul_of_mem_span_weightedMonomials` : combinations of products of
>   weights `v` and `w` exchange up to the sign `(-1) ^ (v * w)`.
