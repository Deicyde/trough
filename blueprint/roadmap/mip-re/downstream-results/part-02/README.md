**Remark (What is shared, what remains, and which commuting value this is).** The value throughout this section is the bipartite ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$ of Definition [Commuting-operator value](../../parallel-repetition/def-co-value-bipartite.md), in which the upper semidecider of Lemma [Upper semidecision of the commuting-operator value](../part-01/lem-valco-upper-re.md) is complete, and not the tracial ${\mathrm{val}^{\mathrm{co}}}$ of Definition [Commuting value](../../foundations/part-01/def-commuting-value.md); Remark Which commuting value this is, and why it is not the tracial one says why the two are not interchanged. The bookkeeping of Remark [Relation to the abstract compression lemma](../../structure-of-the-proof/README.md) — the value of a verifier's game under relabeling, answer padding, doubling and freezing — is done once, in a value model, and what ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$ contributes is the eight transport properties of Definition [Value models](../part-01/def-value-model.md), proved for commuting-operator strategies transported as POVMs, where merging answers is a sum of positive operators and needs no orthogonality. In Lean the tensor-product statements of Theorems [Halting reduction](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-halting.md) and [$\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$](../../the-main-theorem/thm-mipstar-eq-re.md) are the generic ones at the model $\mathrm{val}^*$.

What remains is the hypothesis. Lin proves the commuting-operator soundness of question reduction, answer reduction and parallel repetition on tracially embeddable strategies, whose correlations are dense in $C_{qc}$ (Theorem [Lin's tracial density theorem](../../parallel-repetition/thm-tracial-density.md)), through the rounding of almost-synchronous correlations for balanced games, the rigidity of the Pauli basis test and the soundness of the low-individual-degree test for synchronous strategies (Lin23, Lin25). That last soundness he cites rather than proves. The closest proved theorem is for a test with subcube pair questions in place of diagonal lines, and his proof of the version with several codewords is incomplete (`reports/lidt-co-audit.md`, Section 2). Parallel repetition in the commuting-operator model is Theorem [Direct parallel repetition for commuting-operator strategies](../../parallel-repetition/thm-direct-repetition-co.md), and its verifier-level reading is Theorem [Parallel repetition is sound in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$](thm-parallel-repetition-co.md). The commuting-operator *completeness* of compression is never needed: the nested criterion asks the compressor to preserve perfect PCC strategies, which the tensor completeness does, and the class of value $1$ is entered only through the semidecider. The hypothesis splits into the stages' soundness clauses in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, of which parallel repetition's is proved, answer reduction's is proved from the soundness of the low-individual-degree test in the commuting-operator model (Theorem [Answer reduction is sound in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, given the low-individual-degree test](../part-03/thm-ar-sound-co.md)), and introspection's from the soundness of the Pauli basis test in the commuting-operator model (Theorem [Introspection is sound in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, given the Pauli basis test](../part-04/thm-intro-sound-co.md); Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the Pauli basis test and of the low-individual-degree test](cor-mipco-from-stages.md)); and the Pauli basis test is sound there as soon as the low-individual-degree test is (Lemma [The Pauli basis test is sound in the commuting-operator model, given the seeded test](../part-04/lem-qld-sound-co.md)). So what remains is one statement: the soundness of the seeded CL test (Theorem [Quantum soundness of the seeded CL test](../../background-results/part-01/thm-lidt-cl-soundness.md)) in the commuting-operator model (Definition [The low-individual-degree test is sound in a model](../part-03/def-lidt-sound-in.md)), from which $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$ follows (Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the low-individual-degree test](../part-04/cor-mipco-from-lidt.md)). No source proves that statement for an arbitrary vector state (`reports/lidt-co-audit.md`), and Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the soundness of the low-individual-degree test in dyadic pairs](../part-05/cor-mipco-from-lidt-fin.md) replaces it by its restriction to *dyadic pairs*, the models whose two algebras are each other's commutants, carry faithful tracial states and contain unital matrix units of every size $2^n$ (Section The hypothesis in dyadic pairs). Each stage, and the Pauli basis test, was proved by generalizing the existing analysis over the projective commuting-operator model of Section The projective commuting-operator model rather than by re-proving it, with the tensor-product strategies as an instance: the operator calculus (Remark The operator calculus in a bipartite model), oracularization (Section Parallel repetition and oracularization in the model), answer reduction (Section Answer reduction in the model), introspection (Section Introspection in the model, given the Pauli basis test) and the Pauli basis test (Section The Pauli basis test in the model, given the low-individual-degree test). The low-individual-degree test is Phase 6, the one item that is new mathematics rather than a generalization: the only proof of its soundness here is the vendored finite-dimensional one, whose mechanisms use the finite dimension throughout. Phase 6 reduced the hypothesis to dyadic pairs (Section The hypothesis in dyadic pairs) and proved it there (Theorem [The low-individual-degree test is sound in dyadic pairs](../part-07/thm-lidt-sound-fin.md)), so $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$ holds (Theorem [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$](../part-07/thm-mipco-eq-core-unconditional.md)). Sizes, phases and the delicate points are in `planning/mipco-track.md`, and the audit that fixed the model is `reports/co-generalization-audit.md`.

### The projective commuting-operator model

The hypothesis of Theorem [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from co-soundness](thm-mipco-eq-core.md) was discharged by generalizing the stage analyses of compression, not by writing them a second time (`planning/mipco-track.md`, Phases 1–6). Those analyses are bipartite, vector-state and projective: a unit vector on a tensor product, the first player's operators acting on the first factor and the second's on the second, every measurement projective, and the tracial state never used (`reports/co-generalization-audit.md`). Their common generalization is a Hilbert space with a unit vector and two commuting families of projective measurements, of which a tensor-product strategy is an instance. That model reaches the whole commuting-operator value by the dilation below. The dilation is elementary: it compares no projections and uses no von Neumann algebra theory, where the finite-dimensional dilation extends an isometry to a unitary by counting dimensions.

- [Projective commuting-operator strategy](def-co-projective.md)
- [Commutation-preserving dilation](lem-co-dilation.md)
- [The commuting-operator value is attained on projective strategies](thm-co-value-projective.md)

The operator calculus of the analyses is restated over the following model, in which a tensor-product strategy and a commuting-operator strategy are both instances. The players' operators are elements of two abstract $*$-algebras rather than operators on $\mathcal{H}$, so that a product, a sum or an adjoint of one player's operators is again one of that player's and commutes with the other player's: this is what the matrix analyses get from the two factors of a tensor product. The state calculus of the matrix analyses, $\|M\psi\|$, the bound $\|Mv\| \leq K\|v\|$ and $\mathrm{Re}\langle \psi, M\psi \rangle$, and every estimate built on it — the consistency and commutation analyses, the sandwich and pasting chains, coarse-graining, the adaptation of a strategy to another game — is proved once in the model, and the matrix statements of the earlier chapters are its instances in the tensor-product model (`planning/mipco-track.md`, Phase 1(b); Remark The operator calculus in a bipartite model).

- [Bipartite model](def-bipartite-model.md)
- [The commuting-operator value is a supremum of model values](lem-co-value-model.md)

A finite ancilla is adjoined to a model without leaving it: the space becomes the $\ell^2$-sum of finitely many copies of $\mathcal{H}$, and the players' algebras become matrix algebras over theirs. This is the expanded state of the Pauli basis test (Definition [The expanded state, and an inert ancilla](../../foundations/part-02/def-expanded-state.md)) in any model, and in the tensor-product model it is that expanded state.

- [Ancilla extension of a bipartite model](def-ancilla-extension.md)
- [The ancilla extension](lem-ancilla-extension.md)

**Remark (The operator calculus in a bipartite model).** Phase 1(b) of `planning/mipco-track.md` restated the operator calculus of the analyses over the bipartite model of Definition [Bipartite model](def-bipartite-model.md): the state norm, the quadratic form and the operator bound; the first player's state norm and the cross-party deviation; Born probabilities, the value of a pair of families of POVMs and conditional failure; POVMs and projective measurements in a $*$-ring; and on these, the consistency engine, the commutation analysis, the triangle inequalities for agreement, disagreement under coarse-graining, the sandwich and pasting chains, the invariance under exchanging the players and the adaptation of a strategy to another game. Each is proved once, in the model, and the matrix statement of the earlier chapters that carries the same name is its instance in the tensor-product model, derived and not re-proved. So a bound proved there for tensor-product strategies holds verbatim for the measurements of a commuting-operator strategy in its model (Lemma [The commuting-operator value is a supremum of model values](lem-co-value-model.md)), which is what Phases 2 to 5 use to read each stage's soundness clause in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$.

### Answer reduction in the model

Phase 3 of `planning/mipco-track.md` reads answer reduction in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$. Its soundness analysis (Lemmas [Typed soundness setup](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-08/lem-ar-soundness-setup.md) to [Answer-reduction quantitative assembly](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-08/lem-ar-error-assembly.md)) works with a strategy: it restricts it to the copies of the low-individual-degree test, extracts polynomial measurements from the pieces, and decodes from them a strategy for the typed oracularized game of the input verifier. It is written once, for a projective strategy in a bipartite model, and the one fact about the low-individual-degree test it uses, the soundness of the seeded test (Theorem [Quantum soundness of the seeded CL test](../../background-results/part-01/thm-lidt-cl-soundness.md)), becomes a hypothesis on the model. That hypothesis holds in the tensor-product model, which gives back the soundness clause of Theorem [Answer reduction](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-answer-reduction.md); in the commuting-operator model it is what Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the Pauli basis test and of the low-individual-degree test](cor-mipco-from-stages.md) assumes in place of answer reduction's clause, and, since Phase 5 (Section The Pauli basis test in the model, given the low-individual-degree test), the one hypothesis of Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the low-individual-degree test](../part-04/cor-mipco-from-lidt.md). The analysis needs no dilation: its decoded measurements are coarse-grainings of the extracted projective ones, where the matrix proof dilated a POVM strategy.
