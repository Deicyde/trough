# Theorem Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test on the valid answers

In the setting of Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md), suppose in addition that the strategy's measurements for the questions $(\textsc{Pauli}, W)$ are *supported on correctly formatted answers*: for both players and both bases, the measurement has no element at an answer that is not of the form $h \in \mathbb{F}_q^M$. Then for the first player's $X$ measurement and the second player's $Z$ measurement, the two that introspection uses, item 2 holds with the sum over $h \in \mathbb{F}_q^M$ taken at the answer $h$ itself rather than over every answer the game reads as $h$, with the same local isometry, the same ancilla model and the same $\delta_{qld}$. The constants $a$, $b$ of $\delta_{qld}$ are fixed once, before the parameters, and at every $\varepsilon \geq 0$ the error is $\delta_{qld}(\varepsilon, m, d, q)$. The support hypothesis holds for the restriction to the Pauli basis test (Lemma [Restriction to the actual Pauli game](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-05/lem-intro-pauli-restriction.md)) of a strategy of the parsed introspection game whose measurements at the Pauli questions have nonzero elements only at genuine full-register answers. The Lean proves it for a projective strategy in any bipartite model in which the Pauli basis test is sound (Definition [The Pauli basis test is sound in a model](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-qld-sound-in.md)); the statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

When a measurement has no element at a malformed answer, the coarse measurement that reads every answer as cube data has at $h$ exactly the element of the answer $h$: the fibre of $h$ holds $h$ and malformed answers, which contribute nothing — including at $h = 0$, where the game's reading puts every malformed answer. So the two sums agree term by term, and item 2 of Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md) is the statement. The constants are those of the closed form of the error, Lemma [The error of the Pauli basis test](../part-03/lem-qld-error.md); the theorem is applied at $\min(\varepsilon, 1)$, which bounds the failure probability too, the value being nonnegative. For the restriction of a parsed strategy, completing the malformed outer answers at a scalar answer moves no mass onto a malformed Pauli answer, every nonzero element at a Pauli question being a genuine full-register answer.
