## Localization at a height-one prime is a DVR

In a Noetherian integrally closed domain, the localization at a
height-one prime ideal is a discrete valuation ring.

Formalized by `Localization.AtPrime.isDiscreteValuationRing_of_height_eq_one`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/DiscreteValuationRing.lean, line 45](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/RingTheory/Localization/AtPrime/DiscreteValuationRing.lean#L45). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/RingTheory/valuation-rings/discrete-valuation-rings-at-height-one-primes/index.html#is-discrete-valuation-ring-of-height-eq).

## Valuation subring at a height-one prime of a normal domain

At a height-one prime of a Noetherian integrally closed domain, there
is a principal ideal valuation subring of the fraction field whose
membership is the usual numerator-denominator criterion.

Formalized by `IsIntegrallyClosed.exists_valuationSubring_mem_iff_of_height_eq_one`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/RingTheory/Valuation/HeightOne.lean, line 50](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/RingTheory/Valuation/HeightOne.lean#L50).
