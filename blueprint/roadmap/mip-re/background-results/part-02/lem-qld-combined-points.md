# Combined $XZ$ point measurements

Let $|\hat\psi\rangle$ be as in Lemma [Expanded point measurements](lem-qld-expanded-points.md), and suppose both players' measurements are projective, as they are for a strategy of a bipartite model (Definition [Projective strategy in a bipartite model](../../downstream-results/part-02/def-model-proj-strategy.md)). Put

$$
\delta_Q(\varepsilon) \;=\; 2\sqrt{57676416\,\varepsilon} \;+\; \sqrt{86\,\varepsilon} \;+\; 86\,\varepsilon
\;=\; \mathrm{poly}(\varepsilon)\;.
$$

Then for every pair $(x, z) \in \mathbb{F}_q^m \times \mathbb{F}_q^m$ and each player there is a *projective* measurement $\{\widehat{Q}^{x, z}_{a, b}\}_{a, b \in \mathbb{F}_q}$, acting on that player's expanded space enlarged by one ancilla register, two copies of $\mathbb{F}_q \times \mathbb{F}_q$, held in the basis state $|0, 0\rangle$ of the first copy, which jointly returns the $X$-value at $x$ and the $Z$-value at $z$ and satisfies, on average over the verifier's content:

1. (**Self-consistency**) $\widehat{Q}^{x, z}_{a, b} \otimes {I} \;\simeq_{2 \delta_Q}\; {I} \otimes \widehat{Q}^{x, z}_{a, b}$;

2. (**Consistency with the ordered products**)

    $$
    \begin{gathered}
    \widehat{Q}^{x, z}_{a, b} \otimes {I} \;\simeq_{4 \delta_Q + 115352832 \varepsilon}\;
    {I} \otimes \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, Z), z}_b \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, X), x}_a\;, \\
    \widehat{Q}^{x, z}_{a, b} \otimes {I} \;\simeq_{4 \delta_Q + 461411328 \varepsilon}\;
    {I} \otimes \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, X), x}_a \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, Z), z}_b\;.
    \end{gathered}
    $$

Moreover $\widehat{Q}^{x, z}$ compresses, along the isometry that adjoins $|0, 0\rangle$, to the **sandwich** $\widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b} = \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, Z), z}_b \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, X), x}_a \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, Z), z}_b$. The conclusions depend on the content only through $(x, z)$, whose marginal is uniform, so “on average over the content” is “on average over uniform $(x, z)$”. The marginals of $\widehat{Q}^{x,z}$ are not claimed here; they are obtained in Lemma [Pairs of lines](lem-qld-pairs-of-lines.md). The Lean proves it for a strategy in any bipartite model, on the ancilla extension of its register model by that register on each side (Definition [Ancilla extension of a bipartite model](../../downstream-results/part-02/def-ancilla-extension.md)), and states the estimates also for the sandwich itself on the register model, as agreements; the statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

The sandwich, its Naimark dilation, and one five-link chain.

**The sandwich is a POVM, exactly.** With $\{\widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Point}, Z), z}_b\}$ projective,

$$
\sum_{a, b} \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b
\;=\; \sum_b \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b \Bigl(\sum_a \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a\Bigr)
\widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b
\;=\; \sum_b \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b \;=\; {I}\;,
$$

and each term is $(\widehat{M}^{X, x}_a \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b)^\dagger (\widehat{M}^{X, x}_a \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b)$, hence positive. No approximate commutation is used here, and no orthonormalization is needed afterwards: the family $(a, b) \mapsto \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b$ is a Kraus family of $\{\widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b}\}$, and the Halmos unitary of its Naimark column dilates it to a *projective* measurement on one extra register, two copies of $\mathbb{F}_q \times \mathbb{F}_q$, which compresses to the sandwich at $|0, 0\rangle$ (as in Lemma [Projective dilation against a fixed state, in a star-ordered ring](../../downstream-results/part-03/lem-kraus-dilation.md)); one reference state serves every $(x, z)$ at once. Projectivity of $\widehat{M}$ itself is two closure properties of projective measurements — products and coarse-grainings — applied to the convolution of Definition [The expanded state, and an inert ancilla](../../foundations/part-02/def-expanded-state.md).

**Parseval moves the commutation input to the elements.** What Lemma [Expanded point measurements](lem-qld-expanded-points.md) bounds is the commutator of the *observables* $\widehat{W}^{r}(u)$, one pair per probe; what the sandwich needs is the commutator of the measurement *elements*, one pair per outcome. The elements are the two-variable Fourier transform of the observables against the characters of the trace form (Definition [Generalized Pauli observables in characteristic two](../../foundations/part-02/def-generalized-pauli.md)), so by Parseval the sum over outcomes of the one is the average over probes of the other — and the content already averages over the probes uniformly and independently of everything else it carries, so that average is free.

**The chain.** Write $P_b = \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b \otimes \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b$ and $\Delta = \sum_a ({I} - \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a) \otimes \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{a, b} \langle\hat\psi| \widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b} \otimes \widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b}
|\hat\psi\rangle
&\approx \sum_{a, b} \langle\hat\psi| \widehat{M}^{Z}_b \widehat{M}^{X}_a \otimes
\widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b} |\hat\psi\rangle
\approx \sum_{a, b} \langle\hat\psi| \widehat{M}^{Z}_b \widehat{M}^{X}_a \otimes
\widehat{M}^{Z}_b \widehat{M}^{X}_a |\hat\psi\rangle \\
&\approx \sum_{a, b} \langle\hat\psi| \widehat{M}^{Z}_b \otimes
\widehat{M}^{Z}_b \widehat{M}^{X}_a |\hat\psi\rangle
= \sum_b \langle\hat\psi| P_b |\hat\psi\rangle
\;=\; 1 - \tfrac12 \sum_b \delta^Z_b\;,
\end{aligned}
$$

where $\delta^Z_b$ is the $Z$-consistency defect. The first two steps are the two players' commutators: each difference is $\widehat{M}^Z_b$ times a commutator, the front factor is a contraction, and one Cauchy–Schwarz over *all* outcome pairs pays for it — against $\sum_{a,b} \left\| {\widehat{R}_{a,b} |\hat\psi\rangle} \right\|^2 \leq 1$ for the first and $\sum_{a,b} \left\| {\widehat{M}^X_a \widehat{M}^Z_b |\hat\psi\rangle} \right\|^2 = 1$ for the second. The third step is the one place the outcome sum must *not* be broken up: the whole double sum is the single product $(\sum_b P_b) \Delta$, both factors are projections, and Cauchy–Schwarz gives $\sqrt{\langle\hat\psi| \Delta |\hat\psi\rangle} = \sqrt{\tfrac12 \sum_a \delta^X_a}$. Breaking it up instead would cost a factor $q$, which is exactly the defect the paper's round-11 note repairs. Both parties' commutators are available because the game is symmetric in the players (`MIPRE.QLD.accepts_symm`).

**Transport.** The compression identity of the dilation turns every Born-rule expectation on the extended state into one on $|\hat\psi\rangle$, so the chain's bound is the dilated pair's self-consistency with no further loss, and the same expansion against $\widehat{R}^{x, z}_{a, b} \otimes {I}$ gives item 2 after one triangle inequality replacing the sandwich by each ordered product — the difference being $\widehat{M}^Z_b$ times a commutator again.
