**`Stacks.Divisors.Eta.derivedEta_isQuasicoherent_and_affineComparison`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/EtaDerivedBerthelotOgus.lean, line 151](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/EtaDerivedBerthelotOgus.lean#L151).

> **[Stacks, Tag 0GTV].** Let `X` be a scheme and let `D ⊆ X` be an effective Cartier divisor
> with ideal sheaf `I ⊆ 𝒪_X`. The functor `Lη_I : D(𝒪_X) ⥤ D(𝒪_X)` of [Stacks, Tag 0F8Q] sends
> `D_QCoh(𝒪_X)` into itself: if every cohomology sheaf `H^n(K)` of `K` is quasi-coherent, so is
> every cohomology sheaf of `Lη_I K`.
> Moreover, if `X = Spec(A)` is affine and `D = V(f)` — the ideal of `D` in `A = Γ(X, 𝒪_X)` is
> generated by `f` — then the functor `Lη_f` on `D(A)` of [Stacks, Tag 0F7R] and the functor `Lη_I`
> correspond via the equivalence `(-)^~ : D(A) ≌ D_QCoh(𝒪_X)` of [Stacks, Tag 06Z0]:
> `(-)^~ ⋙ Lη_I ≅ Lη_f ⋙ (-)^~`.
>
> The functor `Lη_f` is defined because `f` is a nonzerodivisor, which follows from the
> hypotheses (`Stacks.Divisors.Eta.mem_nonZeroDivisors_of_ideal_top_eq_span`).

```lean
theorem derivedEta_isQuasicoherent_and_affineComparison :
    (∀ (X : Scheme.{u}) (D : X.IdealSheafData) [Stacks.Divisors.IsEffectiveCartierDivisor D]
        (K : DerivedCategory X.Modules),
        (∀ n : ℤ, ((DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor X.Modules n).obj K).IsQuasicoherent) →
          ∀ n : ℤ, ((DerivedCategory.homologyFunctor X.Modules n).obj
            ((derivedEtaData D).functor.obj K)).IsQuasicoherent) ∧
      ∀ (R : CommRingCat.{u}) (D : (Spec R).IdealSheafData)
        [Stacks.Divisors.IsEffectiveCartierDivisor D] (f : GammaTop R)
        (hf : D.ideal ⟨⊤, isAffineOpen_top (Spec R)⟩ = Ideal.span {f}),
        Nonempty (derivedTildeGamma R ⋙ (derivedEtaData D).functor ≅
          Stacks.Eta.DerivedDescent.moduleDerivedEta f
              (mem_nonZeroDivisors_of_ideal_top_eq_span R D hf) ⋙
            derivedTildeGamma R)
```
