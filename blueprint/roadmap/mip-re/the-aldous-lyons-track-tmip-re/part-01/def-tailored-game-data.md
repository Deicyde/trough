# Tailored game descriptions, standalone; II:1243, II:1279

A *tailored game description* lists the number of vertices, the readable and linear lengths at each vertex, the question weights, and the controlled linear constraints as entries $(x, y, \gamma^R, c)$, read as $c \in L_{xy}(\gamma^R)$. It denotes a synchronous game in the sense of Definition [Game description](../../foundations/part-02/def-game-description.md): questions are vertices, answers bit vectors of the maximal length that vanish beyond the vertex's length, the weights are normalized as there, and acceptance is the canonical decider's, unequal answers at a loop losing. ZPC strategies are Definition [Z-aligned permutation strategies commuting along edges; II:1043–1057, II:1279–1283](def-zpc-strategy.md)'s, the generators beyond a vertex's length acting as the identity. These definitions are written on top of Mathlib alone, in `MIPRE/TailoredGameValue.lean`, beside the standalone statement of Theorem [Halting reduces to the game value; Theorem 12.2 of (JNVWY20), synchronous form](../../the-main-theorem/thm-main.md), whose game value they reuse.
