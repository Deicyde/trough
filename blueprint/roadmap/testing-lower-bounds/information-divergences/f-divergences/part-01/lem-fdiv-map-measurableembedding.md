# fDiv_map_measurableEmbedding

Let $\mu$ and $\nu$ be two measures on $\mathcal X$ and let $g : \mathcal X \to \mathcal Y$ be a measurable embedding. Then $D_f(g_* \mu, g_* \nu) = D_f(\mu, \nu)$.
