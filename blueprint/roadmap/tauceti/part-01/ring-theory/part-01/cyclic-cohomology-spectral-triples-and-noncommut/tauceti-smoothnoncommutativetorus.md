# TauCeti.SmoothNoncommutativeTorus

The **smooth noncommutative torus** `𝒜_θ`, the algebra of rapidly decreasing Fourier series
`∑ a_(m,n) U₁ ^ m U₂ ^ n` with `U₂ U₁ = exp(2πiθ) U₁ U₂`, represented by its coefficients.
