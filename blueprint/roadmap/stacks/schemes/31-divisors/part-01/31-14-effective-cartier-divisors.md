## Tag 01WS, a lemma

[Tag 01WS](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01WS) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.CartierAffineLocalConverse.isEffectiveCartierDivisor_iff_isRegular`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/CartierAffineLocalConverse/Basic.lean, line 546](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/CartierAffineLocalConverse/Basic.lean#L546). References: EGA IV (21.2.12), “partial”; EGA IV (21.6.5), “partial”.

> **Stacks Project, Tag 01WS.** A closed subscheme `D ⊆ X` is an effective Cartier divisor if
> and only if every point of `D` has an affine open neighbourhood `U` in `X` on which `D` is cut
> out by a nonzerodivisor `f ∈ Γ(X, U)`.
>
> The forward direction is `Stacks.CartierAffineLocal.exists_affineOpen_isRegular`, which proves
> the stronger statement with the quantifier over all of `X`; the reverse direction is
> `isEffectiveCartierDivisor_of_affineLocal`.
>
> The right-hand side renders the source's `D ∩ U = Spec(A/fA)` as the equation
> `I.ideal U = Ideal.span {f}` between ideals of `A = Γ(X, U)`: `I.ideal U` *is* the ideal cutting
> `D` out on `U`, so the two say the same thing. The scheme-level packaging as an isomorphism
> `D ×_X U ≅ Spec (A ⧸ (f))` compatible with the closed immersions is not produced here; see the
> module docstring.

```lean
theorem isEffectiveCartierDivisor_iff_isRegular (I : X.IdealSheafData) :
    Stacks.Divisors.IsEffectiveCartierDivisor I ↔
      ∀ x ∈ I.supportSet, ∃ V : X.affineOpens, x ∈ (V : Set X) ∧
        ∃ f : Γ(X, V.1), IsRegular f ∧ I.ideal V = Ideal.span {f}
```

**`Stacks.Divisors.isEffectiveCartierDivisor_iff_affineLocal`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/EffectiveCartierAffineLocal.lean, line 524](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/EffectiveCartierAffineLocal.lean#L524).

> A closed subscheme is an effective Cartier divisor if and only if it is locally, on affine
> neighbourhoods of its support, the zero scheme of a nonzerodivisor.

```lean
theorem isEffectiveCartierDivisor_iff_affineLocal :
    IsEffectiveCartierDivisor I ↔
      ∀ x ∈ I.supportSet,
        ∃ (U : X.Opens) (hU : IsAffineOpen U),
          x ∈ (U : Set X) ∧
            ∃ f : Γ(X, U), IsRegular f ∧
              ∃ e : pullback I.subschemeι U.ι ≅
                  Spec (.of (Γ(X, U) ⧸ Ideal.span {f})),
                pullback.snd I.subschemeι U.ι ≫ hU.isoSpec.hom =
                  e.hom ≫ Spec.map
                    (CommRingCat.ofHom (Ideal.Quotient.mk (Ideal.span {f})))
```

**`Stacks.Divisors.isEffectiveCartierDivisor_of_forall_mem_support`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/IdealSheafSections.lean, line 374](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Divisors/IdealSheafSections.lean#L374). The attribute's comment: “(2) implies (1)”. References: EGA IV (21.2.12), “partial”.

> **Stacks Project, Tag 01WS, the direction (2) ⇒ (1).** A closed subscheme which near every
> point of its support is cut out, on an affine open, by a single nonzerodivisor is an effective
> Cartier divisor.
>
> The hypothesis is clause (2) of the tag: the points of `I.support` are the points of `D`, by
> `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.range_subschemeι`, and `I.ideal U = (f)` says that
> `U ∩ D = Spec(Γ(X, U)/(f))`, by
> `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.IdealSheafData.ker_subschemeι_app`.
>
> Near such a point the generator gives a rank-one dual basis of the ideal sheaf
> (`Stacks.Divisors.dualBasisOneOfPrincipal`), and off the support the ideal is the unit ideal,
> generated by the nonzerodivisor `1`; the resulting dual bases cover `X`, so
> `Stacks.Divisors.isEffectiveCartierDivisor_of_forall_exists_dualBasisOne` applies.
>
> The converse direction (1) ⇒ (2) is not proved: it needs that an invertible sheaf of modules is
> locally free of rank one, which is the descent step
> `StacksFrontier.Algebra.Category.ModuleCat.Sheaf.Invertible` records as missing.

```lean
theorem isEffectiveCartierDivisor_of_forall_mem_support (I : X.IdealSheafData)
    (h : ∀ x ∈ I.support, ∃ (U : X.affineOpens) (f : Γ(X, (U : X.Opens))),
      x ∈ (U : Set X) ∧ I.ideal U = Ideal.span {f} ∧
        f ∈ nonZeroDivisors Γ(X, (U : X.Opens))) :
    IsEffectiveCartierDivisor I
```
