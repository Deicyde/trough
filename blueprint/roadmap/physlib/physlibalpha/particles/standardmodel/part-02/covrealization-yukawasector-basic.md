# The Yukawa sector's mass-weight submodules

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.CovRealization.YukawaSector.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/YukawaSector/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/YukawaSector/Basic.lean): 9 declarations, 9 with a docstring.

## i. Overview

> The mixed Higgs-fermion sector — the `{higgs, fermion}` two-class sector of
> `Sectors.lean` — is the home of the Yukawa couplings. Since the Higgs sector is
> bosonic, its algebra commutes with the fermion algebra
> (`commute_of_mem_higgsAlgebra_of_mem_fermionAlgebra`), which feeds the abstract
> two-class machinery `sectorMassWeight_pair_le` to bound each weight-`w` piece of the
> sector by a join of products of the two sectors' own mass-weight submodules
> (`sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le`). Combined with the explicit low-weight tables
> for the Higgs sector (vanishing at odd weight) and the fermion sector (vanishing at
> weight `1`, `2` and `4`), this pins the sector down explicitly up to weight eight: it
> vanishes below weight five and at weight six, and at weights five, seven and eight it
> sits inside the expected Yukawa-type products, the last of these being the weight of
> the Yukawa term `H ψ ψ` itself.

## ii. Key results

> - `commute_of_mem_higgsAlgebra_of_mem_fermionAlgebra` : the Higgs sector is bosonic: every element
>   of the algebra generated by the Higgs towers commutes with every element of the algebra generated
>   by the fermion towers
> - `sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le` : the Yukawa-sector decomposition: the weight-`w` piece of the
>   `{higgs, fermion}` sector is contained in the join, over the splittings of `w` into two non-zero
>   parts, of the products of the Higgs-sector and fermion-sector mass-weight submodules

## B. The Yukawa sector at a fixed mass weight

**`StandardModel.CovAlgebraRealization.sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le`**, a lemma, [line 137](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L137).

> **The Yukawa-sector decomposition**: the weight-`w` piece of the `\{higgs,
> fermion}` sector is contained in the join, over the splittings of `w` into two
> non-zero parts, of the products of the Higgs-sector and fermion-sector mass-weight
> submodules.

```lean
lemma sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le (w : ℕ) :
    h.sectorMassWeight {GeneratorClass.higgs, GeneratorClass.fermion} w
      ≤ ⨆ (p : ℕ × ℕ) (_ : p.1 + p.2 = w) (_ : p.1 ≠ 0) (_ : p.2 ≠ 0),
        h.isHiggsSector.massWeightSubmodule p.1 * h.isFermionSector.massWeightSubmodule p.2
```

**`StandardModel.CovAlgebraRealization.sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_eq_bot_of_lt_five`**, a lemma, [line 151](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L151).

> **Below weight five, the Yukawa sector vanishes**: no splitting of a total weight
> under five into two non-zero parts survives — the Higgs part is either odd (hence
> zero) or equal to two, forcing the fermion part to be one or two (hence also
> zero).

```lean
lemma sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_eq_bot_of_lt_five {w : ℕ} (hw : w < 5) :
    h.sectorMassWeight {GeneratorClass.higgs, GeneratorClass.fermion} w = ⊥
```
