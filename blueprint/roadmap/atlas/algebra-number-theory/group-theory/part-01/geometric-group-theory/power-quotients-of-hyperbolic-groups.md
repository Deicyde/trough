## Power subgroup

For a group $G$ and a natural number $n$, $G^n$ is the subgroup of $G$
generated by the $n$-th powers $g^n$ of all elements $g \in G$.

## Kourovka Problem 11.74(a) (infinite power quotients of hyperbolic groups)

For every
word-hyperbolic group $G$ that is not virtually cyclic (non-elementary), there is an integer
$n \ge 1$ such that the subgroup $G^n$ generated by the $n$-th powers of the elements of $G$ has
infinite index in $G$, i.e. the quotient $G / G^n$ is infinite.

The problem is due to M. Gromov and A. Yu. Olshanskii.

## Kourovka Problem 11.74(b) (trivial intersection of power subgroups)

For every
word-hyperbolic group $G$ that is not virtually cyclic (non-elementary), the only element of $G$
lying in the subgroup $G^n$ generated by the $n$-th powers for every integer $n \ge 1$ is the
identity, i.e. $\bigcap_{n \ge 1} G^n = 1$.

The problem is due to A. Yu. Olshanskii.

## Non-elementary hyperbolic groups have infinite power quotients

For every word-hyperbolic group
$G$ that is not virtually cyclic, there is an integer $n \ge 1$ such that the subgroup $G^n$
generated by the $n$-th powers of the elements of $G$ has infinite index in $G$, i.e. the quotient
$G / G^n$ is infinite.

This answers Kourovka Problem 11.74(a) ("Yes, it is"); the proof is due to Ivanov and Olshanskii.

## Power subgroups of a non-elementary hyperbolic group intersect trivially

For every
word-hyperbolic group $G$ that is not virtually cyclic, the only element of $G$ lying in the
subgroup $G^n$ generated by the $n$-th powers for every integer $n \ge 1$ is the identity, i.e.
$\bigcap_{n \ge 1} G^n = 1$.

This answers Kourovka Problem 11.74(b) ("Yes, it is"); the proof is due to Ivanov and Olshanskii.
