# Erdős problems

The 1,018 problem files in [FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/tree/5258ab9ac6db5eb0047aa4100ea0f995c5466da6/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems) that state a research or textbook statement, at commit [5258ab9a](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/tree/5258ab9ac6db5eb0047aa4100ea0f995c5466da6), one article each, in sections by hundreds of problem number.

Their docstrings and Lean statements are quoted from [Formal Conjectures](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures) (Google DeepMind) at that commit, under its Apache-2.0 license.

Formal Conjectures' [README for this folder](https://github.com/google-deepmind/formal-conjectures/blob/5258ab9ac6db5eb0047aa4100ea0f995c5466da6/FormalConjectures/ErdosProblems/README.md) asks contributors to keep each docstring as close as possible to the problem's text on [erdosproblems.com](https://www.erdosproblems.com), and to copy it from the site's LaTeX. Each article links its problem's page there.
