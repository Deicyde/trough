# "Poincaré duality"

If $\mu=\bigcup_{n\geq1}\mu_n$ and $M':=\operatorname{Hom}(M,\mu)$ is the dual of $M$ then for $0\leq i\leq 2$ the cup product pairing $H^i(G_K,M)\times H^{2-i}(G_K,M')\to H^2(G_K,\mu)=\mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z}$ is perfect.
