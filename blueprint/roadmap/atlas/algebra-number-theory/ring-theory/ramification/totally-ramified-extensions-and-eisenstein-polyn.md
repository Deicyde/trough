# Totally ramified extensions and Eisenstein polynomials

## A generating uniformizer gives total ramification

Let $A \subseteq B$ be an extension of
discrete valuation rings with $B$ finite over $A$ and the inclusion a local homomorphism, and let
$K \subseteq L$ be their fraction fields, with the maps $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$
agreeing. If a uniformizer $\pi$ of $B$ satisfies $B = A[\pi]$, then the ramification index of the
maximal ideal of $B$ over $A$ equals $[L : K]$.

The proof shows that the inertia degree $f$ of the maximal ideal of $B$ over $A$ is $1$ and applies
the identity $[L : K] = ef$, where $e$ is the ramification index.

## An Eisenstein generating uniformizer gives total ramification

Let $A \subseteq B$ be an
extension of discrete valuation rings with $B$ finite over $A$ and the inclusion a local
homomorphism, and let $K \subseteq L$ be their fraction fields, with the maps $A \to B \to L$ and
$A \to K \to L$ agreeing. If a uniformizer $\pi$ of $B$ satisfies $B = A[\pi]$ and its minimal
polynomial over $A$ is Eisenstein at the maximal ideal of $A$, then the ramification index of the
maximal ideal of $B$ over $A$ equals $[L : K]$.

The Eisenstein hypothesis is not used: a generating uniformizer already suffices.

## Totally ramified extensions are generated by an Eisenstein uniformizer

Let $A \subseteq B$
be an extension of discrete valuation rings with $B$ finite over $A$ and the inclusion a local
homomorphism, and let $K \subseteq L$ be their fraction fields, with $L$ finite over $K$ and the
maps $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$ agreeing. If the ramification index of the maximal ideal of
$B$ over $A$ equals $[L : K]$, then every uniformizer $\pi$ of $B$ satisfies $B = A[\pi]$, and the
minimal polynomial of $\pi$ over $A$ is Eisenstein at the maximal ideal of $A$.

## Eisenstein criterion for total ramification of discrete valuation rings

Let $A \subseteq B$
be an extension of discrete valuation rings with $B$ finite over $A$ and the inclusion a local
homomorphism, and let $K \subseteq L$ be their fraction fields, with $L$ finite over $K$ and the
maps $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$ agreeing. For a uniformizer $\pi$ of $B$, the ramification
index of the maximal ideal of $B$ over $A$ equals $[L : K]$ if and only if $B = A[\pi]$ and the
minimal polynomial of $\pi$ over $A$ is Eisenstein at the maximal ideal of $A$.

## The constant coefficient lies in the ideal generated by the adjoined root

Let $A$ be a
commutative ring, let $f \in A[X]$ be a polynomial, and let $\alpha$ be the image of $X$ in
$A[X]/(f)$. Then the image in $A[X]/(f)$ of the constant coefficient $f(0)$ of $f$ lies in the
principal ideal $(\alpha)$.

## The constant term of an Eisenstein polynomial generates the maximal ideal of a DVR

Let
$A$ be a discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, and let $f \in A[X]$ be a
polynomial of positive degree that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$. Then
$\mathfrak m$ is the principal ideal generated by the constant coefficient $f(0)$ of $f$.

## The maximal ideal of a DVR maps into the ideal of an Eisenstein root

Let $A$ be a
discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $f \in A[X]$ be a polynomial of
positive degree that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$, and let $\alpha$ be the image of
$X$ in $A[X]/(f)$. Then the ideal of $A[X]/(f)$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak m$ is
contained in the principal ideal $(\alpha)$.

## The adjoined root of an Eisenstein polynomial is nonzero

Let $A$ be a local integral
domain with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, let $f \in A[X]$ be a monic polynomial of positive degree
that is Eisenstein with respect to $\mathfrak m$, and let $\alpha$ be the image of $X$ in
$A[X]/(f)$. Then $\alpha \neq 0$.

## The maximal ideal of an Eisenstein extension of a DVR is generated by the root
