# IsGaussian.memLp_id

A Gaussian measure $\mu$ has finite moments of all orders. In particular, there is a well defined mean $m_\mu := \mu[\mathrm{id}]$, and for all $L \in F^*$, $\mu[L] = L(m_\mu)$.
