## Interior regularity of planar Mumford–Shah minimizers

We prove the interior regularity assertion of the planar Mumford–Shah conjecture for reduced absolute minimizers with bounded fidelity data. Every interior point of the closed discontinuity set has a neighborhood consisting of a $C^{1,\alpha}$ arc, an arc ending at that point, or three such arcs meeting at 120 degrees. Only finitely many global connected components meet any relatively compact open set.
