# htpy_formal_sol

A homotopy of formal solutions of $\mathcal{R}$ is a smooth family of sections $\mathcal{F} : ℝ × M → J^1(M, N)$ such that each $m ↦ \mathcal{F}(t, m)$ is a formal solution.
