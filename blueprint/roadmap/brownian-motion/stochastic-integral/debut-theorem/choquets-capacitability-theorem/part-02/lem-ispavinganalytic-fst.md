# IsPavingAnalytic.fst

Let $F$ be a set of sets of $\mathcal{X}$ and $K$ be a compact system of $\mathcal{K}$. If a set $s$ of $\mathcal{X} \times \mathcal{K}$ is $F \times K$-analytic, then its projection on $\mathcal{X}$ is $F$-analytic.
