## Quasivariety generated by a finite group

For a group $K$, a group $X$ lies in the class
$Q(K)$ when $X$ embeds in a direct power of $K$: there are a set $I$ and an injective
homomorphism $X \to K^I$.

For a finite group $K$, the only case used, $Q(K)$ is the quasivariety generated by $K$, since
ultrapowers of a finite group are isomorphic to it.

## Join of the quasivarieties generated by two finite groups

For groups $G$ and $H$, a group
$X$ lies in the class $Q(G, H)$ when $X$ embeds in the product of a direct power of $G$ and a
direct power of $H$: there are sets $I$ and $J$ and an injective homomorphism $X \to G^I \times
H^J$.

For finite groups $G$ and $H$, $Q(G, H)$ is the join of the quasivarieties generated by $G$ and by
$H$, that is, the quasivariety generated by both.

## Kourovka Problem 10.9 (Budkin): quasivarieties generated by finite $p$-groups form a sublattice

**Kourovka Problem 10.9 (Budkin): quasivarieties generated by finite $p$-groups form a
sublattice.** For every prime $p$ and all finite $p$-groups $G$ and $H$, there is a finite
$p$-group $K$ such that a group lies in the quasivariety generated by $K$ exactly when it lies
in both the quasivariety generated by $G$ and the quasivariety generated by $H$, and there is a
finite $p$-group $K'$ such that a group lies in the quasivariety generated by $K'$ exactly when
it lies in the join of the quasivarieties generated by $G$ and by $H$. Equivalently, the set
$L_p$ of quasivarieties generated by a single finite $p$-group is a sublattice of the lattice of
all quasivarieties of groups.

## Quasivarieties generated by finite $p$-groups need not form a sublattice

For some prime $p$
there are finite $p$-groups $G$ and $H$ such that no finite $p$-group generates the intersection
of the quasivarieties generated by $G$ and by $H$, or no finite $p$-group generates their join.
This answers Kourovka Problem 10.9 (Budkin) in the negative.

Since the join is always generated by the finite $p$-group $G \times H$, it is the intersection that
fails to be generated by a finite $p$-group.
