# FG-definition

Define two (quasi) modular forms as

$$
\begin{aligned}
F(z) &= (E_2(z) E_4(z) - E_6(z))^2\qquad\text{(5)}\\
G(z) &= H_2(z)^{3} (2 H_{2}(z)^{2} + 5 H_{2}(z) H_{4}(z) + 5 H_{4}(z)^{2}).\qquad\text{(6)}\end{aligned}
$$
