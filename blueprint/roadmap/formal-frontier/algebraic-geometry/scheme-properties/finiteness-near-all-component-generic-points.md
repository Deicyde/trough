# Finiteness near all component generic points

A locally finite type, quasi-compact morphism with finite ordinary fibres over every component generic point becomes finite on the whole preimage of a dense open containing all those points whenever their canonical subspace topology is discrete, in particular when the target has finitely many irreducible components.
