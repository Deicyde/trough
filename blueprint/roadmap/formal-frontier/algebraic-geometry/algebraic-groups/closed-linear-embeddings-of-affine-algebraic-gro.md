# Closed linear embeddings of affine algebraic groups

Every affine group scheme locally of finite type over a field K admits a group-scheme morphism into GL_n over K for some natural number n whose underlying scheme morphism is a closed immersion. Nonreduced group schemes are included, with no representation or basis supplied.
