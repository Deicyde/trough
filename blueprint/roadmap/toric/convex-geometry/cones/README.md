## Dual Cones and Faces

- [Dual cone](1-2-3-dual-cone.md)

Given $m \ne 0$ in $M$, we get the hyperplane

$$
H_m = \{u \in N | \langle m, u\rangle = 0\} \subseteq N
$$

and the closed half-space

$$
H_m^+ = \{u \in N | \langle m, u\rangle \ge 0\} \subseteq N.
$$
