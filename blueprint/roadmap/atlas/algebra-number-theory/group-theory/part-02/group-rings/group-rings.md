## Augmentation right ideal of a subset

Let $G$ be a group, $S \subseteq G$ a subset and
$\mathbb{Z}G$ the integral group ring of $G$. This is the right ideal of $\mathbb{Z}G$ generated by
the elements $g - 1$ with $g \in S$, regarded as a right $\mathbb{Z}G$-submodule of
$\mathbb{Z}G$.
