# The group `Int L` of inner automorphisms of a Lie algebra `L`

the subgroup of the general
linear group of `L` generated by the automorphisms `exp (ad x)` with `ad x` nilpotent. Its elements
preserve the bracket (`TauCeti.map_lie_of_mem_intLieAlgebra`).

```lean
TauCeti.intLieAlgebra.{u_1, u_2} (K : Type u_1) (L : Type u_2) [CommRing K] [LieRing L]
  [LieAlgebra K L] [LieAlgebra ℚ L] : Subgroup (LinearMap.GeneralLinearGroup K L)
```
