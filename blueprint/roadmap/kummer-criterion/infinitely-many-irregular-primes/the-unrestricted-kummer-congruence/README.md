# The unrestricted Kummer congruence

The infinitude argument needs the Kummer congruence $B_m/m \equiv B_n/n \pmod p$ for *all* positive even indices $m \equiv n \pmod{p-1}$ away from the boundary $(p-1)\mid n$ — with no upper bound on the indices and no coprimality restrictions. It is proved here by the elementary Voronoi route.
