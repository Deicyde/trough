# Combinatorics

5,678 results of ATLAS's vault, 3,392 proved and 2,286 stated but not proved, in this subject's topics.

Combinatorics studies finite and discrete configurations through counting, extremal structure, and constructive arguments.

[Additive combinatorics](part-01/additive-combinatorics/README.md) is the largest field, with about one article in seven. It
runs from [zero-sum problems](part-01/additive-combinatorics/zero-sum-subset-sums/README.md) in finite abelian groups and
[sumsets and inverse theorems](part-01/additive-combinatorics/sumsets-inverse-theorems/README.md), from the
Cauchy–Davenport–Chowla theorem to the polynomial Freiman–Ruzsa theorem over $\mathbb{F}_2$ of Gowers, Green, Manners
and Tao, to [Sidon sets and difference sets](part-01/additive-combinatorics/sidon-sets-difference-sets/README.md) and
[progressions in dense sets](part-01/additive-combinatorics/progressions-dense-sets/README.md), with Szemerédi's theorem.
[Ramsey theory](part-01/ramsey-theory/README.md) has the theorems of Ramsey, van der Waerden and Schur, the density and
infinitary Hales–Jewett theorems, and Hindman's finite sums theorem.

[Combinatorics on words](part-01/combinatorics-on-words/README.md), nearly as large, studies
[morphic and Sturmian words](part-01/combinatorics-on-words/morphic-sturmian-words/README.md) such as the Fibonacci and
Thue–Morse words, [automatic sequences](part-01/combinatorics-on-words/automatic-sequences/README.md) with the theorems of
Cobham and Christol, and [finite words](part-01/combinatorics-on-words/finite-words/README.md), where Witt's formula counts
Lyndon words of given content. [Permutations](part-01/permutations/README.md) are studied through pattern avoidance and
statistics, and [Boolean functions](part-02/boolean-functions/README.md) include the
[Kahn–Kalai–Linial inequality](part-02/boolean-functions/kahn-kalai-linial-inequality/README.md): some variable of a Boolean
function on $n$ bits has influence at least of order $\operatorname{Var}(f) \log n / n$.

Eight enumerative fields hold over a third of the articles between them:
[Stirling, Bell and Eulerian numbers](part-01/stirling-bell-eulerian/README.md),
[integer partitions](part-01/integer-partitions/README.md), [binomial coefficients](part-01/binomial-coefficients/README.md) and their
sums, [Catalan numbers and lattice paths](part-01/catalan-numbers-lattice-paths/README.md),
[integer compositions](part-01/integer-compositions/README.md), [Riordan arrays](part-01/riordan-arrays-transforms/README.md),
[parking functions](part-01/parking-functions/README.md) and [set partitions](part-01/set-partitions/README.md).
