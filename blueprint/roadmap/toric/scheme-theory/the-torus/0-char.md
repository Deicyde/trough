# Characters of a group scheme

For a group scheme $G$ over $S$, the **character lattice** of $G$ is

$$
X(G) := \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathsf{GrpSch}_S}(G, {\mathbb G}_m).
$$

An element of $X(G)$ is called a **character**.
