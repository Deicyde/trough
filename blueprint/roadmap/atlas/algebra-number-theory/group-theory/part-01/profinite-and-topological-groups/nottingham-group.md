## Nottingham condition on automorphisms of power series

Let $R$ be a commutative ring. A
ring automorphism $\varphi$ of the power series ring $R[[x]]$ satisfies the Nottingham condition
when $\varphi(x) - x$ lies in the ideal generated by $x^2$, that is, when
$\varphi(x) = x(1 + a_1 x + a_2 x^2 + \cdots)$ for some $a_1, a_2, \ldots \in R$.

For a prime $p$ and $R = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$, every ring automorphism of $R[[x]]$ is
$R$-linear and preserves the maximal ideal, so it is the substitution $x \mapsto \varphi(x)$; the
automorphisms satisfying the Nottingham condition then form the Nottingham group $N(R)$.

## Kourovka Problem 12.24 (free subgroups of the Nottingham group)

For every prime $p$ there
is an injective group homomorphism from the free group $F_2$ on two generators to the group of
ring automorphisms of $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})[[x]]$ whose image consists of automorphisms
$\varphi$ with $\varphi(x) - x \in (x^2)$, that is, of elements of the Nottingham group
$N(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$.

## The Nottingham group contains a free subgroup of rank 2

For every prime $p$ there is an
injective group homomorphism $f$ from the free group $F_2$ on two generators to the group of ring
automorphisms of the power series ring $(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})[[x]]$ such that, for every
$g \in F_2$, the automorphism $\varphi = f(g)$ satisfies $\varphi(x) - x \in (x^2)$; that is, the
Nottingham group $N(\mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z})$ contains a copy of $F_2$.

The Kourovka Archive answers 12.24 "Yes, it does" (R. Camina, 1997). Camina embeds, for every
prime $p$, every countably based pro-$p$ group in $N(\mathbb{F}_p)$, where
$\mathbb{F}_p = \mathbb{Z}/p\mathbb{Z}$; the free pro-$p$ group of rank $2$ contains $F_2$, since
free groups are residually $p$.
