# Glimm–Jaffe field configurations

The module `Physlib.QFT.Euclidean.GlimmJaffe.FieldConfiguration`, [Physlib/QFT/Euclidean/GlimmJaffe/FieldConfiguration.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/Physlib/QFT/Euclidean/GlimmJaffe/FieldConfiguration.lean): 5 declarations, 5 with a docstring.

> Real scalar field configurations, their complex pairing and their time translation.

## i. Overview

> A field configuration of a real scalar Euclidean quantum field is a tempered distribution `ω`,
> so the basic observable is its evaluation `ω f` on a real Schwartz test function `f`.
> Time translation acts by duality on complex-linear functionals of complex Schwartz tests,
> which include the complex-linear extensions of field configurations.
>
> The parameter `d` counts spatial dimensions. Spacetime is Physlib's
> `TimeAndSpace d = Time × Space d`, so the source's total dimension is `d + 1`.
> Field configurations carry the weak dual topology and the cylinder sigma algebra.
> Their pairing with complex Schwartz functions is extended complex linearly.

## ii. Key results

> * `FieldConfiguration`: real tempered distributions with the cylinder sigma algebra.
> * `FieldConfiguration.pairing`: the complex-linear extension of a field configuration.
> * `timeTranslation`: time translation of a field by duality.

## iv. References

> * Glimm and Jaffe, *Quantum Physics*. [ref: glimm1987quantum]
