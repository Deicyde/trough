# MeasurableSet.isPavingAnalytic_fst

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a measurable space and $s$ be a measurable set of $\mathcal{X} \times \mathbb{R}$. Then the projection of $s$ on $\mathcal{X}$ is $M_{\mathcal{X}}$-analytic.
