# Sigma-compactness of a fiber bundle

For a fiber bundle with nonempty R₁ base and nonempty model, the original total space is sigma-compact if and only if the base and model are both sigma-compact.

*Quoted from the highlights in [formalization.yaml](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/vector-bundles/blob/e3fb9de334c8b60bdb707b3e48b2094b2202c725/formalization.yaml) of [FormalFrontier/vector-bundles](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/vector-bundles) at commit [e3fb9de3](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/vector-bundles/tree/e3fb9de334c8b60bdb707b3e48b2094b2202c725), Apache-2.0.*
