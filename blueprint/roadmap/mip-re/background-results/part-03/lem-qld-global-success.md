# All padded subtests succeed

In the setting of Lemma [Admissible padded setup](lem-qld-global-setup.md), if the strategy wins the Pauli basis test with probability at least $1 - \varepsilon$, $\varepsilon \geq 0$, then the padded strategy fails the seeded test at $(q, 4m, d, 1)$ with probability at most

$$
5\, m^2 \delta_P(\varepsilon, md/q + 1/q) + 4\, \delta_Q(\varepsilon) + \frac{md + 1}{q}\;,
$$

with $\delta_P$ the error of Lemma [Pairs of lines](../part-02/lem-qld-pairs-of-lines.md) and $\delta_Q$ that of Lemma [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-combined-points.md); that is, $\delta' = C(\delta_{\mathrm{combine}} + \delta_Q + md/q)$ for a universal $C$, with $\delta_{\mathrm{combine}} = m^2 \delta_P$ as in Lemma [Padded line POVM](lem-qld-padded-lines.md). The bound covers the line–point, identical-point and identical-line subtests under their actual seeded distributions; line equality uses polynomial separation. The Lean proves it for a strategy in any bipartite model, the padded strategy's value being computed on its register model; the statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

The failure probability is the average over the verifier's samples of the conditional failure at the two questions the sample generates, and the sample splits into the ordered pair of types and the ambient triple (point, seed, raw direction), uniform on its own. The two cross pairs are always accepted. Each other pair is bounded by an agreement defect of two families of POVMs indexed by the ambient triple, averaged uniformly: a line type against a point by the agreement of the line measurement read at the sample's point with the opposite party's padded point measurement, which is exactly the padded consistency quantity of Lemma [Padded line POVM](lem-qld-padded-lines.md) once the strategy's average over raw directions is composed with the sample's; two points by the agreement of the two padded point measurements, which is the self-consistency item of Lemma [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-combined-points.md) read through the compressions; and two identical lines by polynomial separation — two distinct outcomes of degree at most $md + 1$ are told apart by reading both at a uniformly random parameter of the line, up to $(md + 1)/q$, and the parameter of the sample's point is uniform on the line once the raw direction is fixed and the line is not degenerate, because translating the point along the line is a bijection of the sample space that fixes the line and shifts the parameter. The evaluated disagreement of the two line measurements is then bounded through the agreement triangle (the POVM version of the paper's `fact:triangle-for-simeq`, with $11\delta$ in place of $9\delta$) by the line–point and point–point defects. A degenerate diagonal direction has probability at most $1/q$ and is paid for in full. Collecting the nine terms with weight $1/9$ gives the constants stated.
