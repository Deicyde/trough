> `Spec (R ⧸ I)` is the closed subspace of `Spec R` cut out by the quasi-coherent sheaf of
> ideals `I^~`: there is a unique isomorphism of locally ringed spaces from `Spec (R ⧸ I)` to that
> closed subspace commuting with the two morphisms to `Spec R`.

```lean
theorem existsUnique_iso_tildeSubspace :
    ∃! t : (Spec (CommRingCat.of (↑R ⧸ I))).toLocallyRingedSpace ≅ tildeSubspace I,
      t.hom ≫ tildeSubspaceInclusion I =
        (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (Ideal.Quotient.mk I))).toLRSHom
```

> A closed immersion of locally ringed spaces into `Spec R` is, over `Spec R`, the spectrum of
> the quotient by a unique ideal of `R`.

```lean
theorem existsUnique_ideal_iso_quotientSpec {Z : LocallyRingedSpace.{u}}
    (i : Z ⟶ (Spec R).toLocallyRingedSpace) [IsClosedImmersion i.toShHom] :
    ∃! I : Ideal ↑R, ∃ e : Z ≅ (Spec (CommRingCat.of (↑R ⧸ I))).toLocallyRingedSpace,
      i = e.hom ≫ (Spec.map (CommRingCat.ofHom (Ideal.Quotient.mk I))).toLRSHom
```
