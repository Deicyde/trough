# Gaussian splitting and convex interaction for rigid diffusions

> Formalizes the convex interaction theorem of Chafaï and Fathi and their Euclidean formulation of Cheng and Zhou's Gaussian splitting theorem for normalized C² Gibbs potentials at curvature equality. The proofs connect the canonical spectral gap to an affine first eigenspace and an independent Gaussian factor. On a connected complete smooth weighted Riemannian manifold attaining the positive Bakry–Émery curvature bound, it constructs a Gaussian product isometry and identifies the smooth first eigenfunctions in the Gaussian coordinates. The first two moments of the Gaussian projection give a lower bound on the quadratic transport cost to equilibrium. A separate Hilbert-space development constructs the Friedrichs resolvent and its strongly continuous contraction semigroup. The closed energy inherits energy-decreasing maps from the operator core. A variational comparison criterion gives fixed points of these maps on resolvent vectors. On a smooth weighted Riemannian manifold with finite weighted volume, the negative diffusion generator on smooth compactly supported functions has a symmetric nonnegative dense core in L². Its Friedrichs resolvent and semigroup preserve almost-everywhere nonnegativity. On a complete connected manifold the constant one has zero closed energy. The semigroup fixes it, preserves functions between zero and one almost everywhere, and preserves the integral against the weighted volume.
