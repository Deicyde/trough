# b-zeros-values-d24

The function $b$ maps $\mathbb{R}$ to $i\mathbb{R}$, and it has a double zero at $r=\sqrt{2k}$ for each integer $k>2$. Moreover,

$$
b(0)=b(\sqrt2)=b(2)=0,\quad b'(\sqrt2)=928\,i\pi\sqrt2,\quad b'(2)=-8\pi i.
$$
