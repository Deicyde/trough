## Free Burnside group

For natural numbers $m$ and $n$, the free Burnside group $B(m, n)$ is
the quotient of the free group $F_m$ on $m$ generators by the normal subgroup generated by all
$n$-th powers $w^n$ with $w \in F_m$.

## Mazurov's fixed-point question for free Burnside groups (Kourovka 17.70)

The assertion
that for all primes $q$ and $p$ such that the free Burnside group $B(q, p)$ of rank $q$ and
exponent $p$ is infinite, the automorphism of $B(q, p)$ induced by the cyclic permutation
$x_i \mapsto x_{i+1}$ (indices mod $q$) of its free generators $x_0, \dots, x_{q-1}$ fixes some
nontrivial element of $B(q, p)$.

## Mazurov's fixed-point question for free Burnside groups has a negative answer

There exist
primes $q$ and $p$ such that the free Burnside group $B(q, p)$ of rank $q$ and exponent $p$ is
infinite and the automorphism of $B(q, p)$ induced by the cyclic permutation $x_i \mapsto x_{i+1}$
(indices mod $q$) of its free generators $x_0, \dots, x_{q-1}$ fixes no nontrivial element of
$B(q, p)$.
