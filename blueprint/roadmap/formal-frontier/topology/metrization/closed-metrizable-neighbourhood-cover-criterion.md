# Closed metrizable neighbourhood-cover criterion

A space is metrizable if it has a locally finite family of closed subsets whose
interiors cover the space and each of which is metrizable in its original
subtype topology. No ambient separation assumption is required.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/metrization/blob/9c7cf0d82ab3c69a759c43e2ab6a246d537b7235/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/metrization](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/metrization) at commit [9c7cf0d8](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/metrization/tree/9c7cf0d82ab3c69a759c43e2ab6a246d537b7235), Apache-2.0.*
