# The one-kind sectors of the Standard Model

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.Sectors`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/LocalCovFieldAlgebra/Sectors.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/LocalCovFieldAlgebra/Sectors.lean): 13 declarations, 13 with a docstring.

> The pure gauge, Higgs and fermion sectors of the Standard Model as its three sector algebras.

## i. Overview

> The sectors of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model are the generic sectors
> `fieldData.sector A` of its field datum, and their weight pieces are
> `fieldData.sectorMassWeight A w`. The Higgs is the only bosonic species, so the scalar kind is
> the Higgs kind.
>
> The Standard Model sector files work with three algebras: `GaugeSector.gaugeAlgebra`, generated
> by the field-strength towers, `ScalarSector.higgsAlgebra`, generated by the Higgs towers, and
> `FermionSector.fermionAlgebra`, generated by the fermion towers. Each is generated by covariant
> generators of one kind, and contains all of them. So at a non-zero weight `w` the weight-`w`
> piece of the sector of that kind is the weight-`w` piece of the algebra. At weight zero the
> algebras also contain the constants, which the generic decomposition files under the sector
> of `∅`.

## ii. Key results

> - `sectorMassWeight_gauge_eq`, `sectorMassWeight_higgs_eq`, `sectorMassWeight_fermion_eq` : at
>   a non-zero weight the one-kind sectors are the weight pieces of the three sector algebras.

> The tower `covF`, indexed by functionals on `GaugeAlgebra`, at the transport of a
> functional on the Lie algebra of the global gauge group, is the tower `fieldStrength`,
> indexed by functionals on that Lie algebra.

```lean
lemma covF_comp_gaugeAlgebraEquiv {n : ℕ} (l : Fin n → (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3))
    (μ ν : Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3) (φ : Module.Dual ℝ globalGaugeGroup.lieSubalgebra) :
    LocalCovFieldAlgebra.covF l μ ν
        (φ ∘ₗ (gaugeAlgebraEquiv : GaugeAlgebra →ₗ[ℝ] globalGaugeGroup.lieSubalgebra))
      = fieldStrength l μ ν φ
```
