# Selected laws

In this project we study the 4694 laws (up to symmetry and relabeling) of total order at most $4$.

Selected laws of interest are listed below, as well as in [this file](https://github.com/teorth/equational_theories/blob/main/equational_theories/Equations/Basic.lean). As will be discussed in Chapter [General implications](../general-implications/README.md), every equational law comes with a dual.
