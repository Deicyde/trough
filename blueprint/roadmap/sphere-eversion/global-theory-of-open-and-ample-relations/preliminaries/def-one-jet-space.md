# one_jet_space

Let $M$ and $N$ be smooth manifolds. Denote by $p_1$ and $p_2$ the projections of $M × N$ to $M$ and $N$ respectively.

The space $J^1(M, N)$ of $1$-jets of maps from $M$ to $N$ is $Hom(p_1^*TM, p_2^*TN)$
