# The typed answer-reduced sampler

For a PCP decider and parameters $(\lambda,\mu,\sigma)$, and an $(\ell+1)$-level input sampler $S$, there is a typed sampler with $\max\{\ell+1,3\}$ levels on the types of Definition [The typed answer-reduced game](def-ar-typed-game.md) whose typed CL functions are that game's, with the PCP parameters $\mathrm{pcpparams}(n,T,Q,\sigma)$, $Q=(\lambda n+1)^\mu$, $T=2^{(Q+5)(\mu+1)}$, and with the seed selectors reading the high bits of the seed. Its program is computed from the program of $S$ and $(\lambda,\mu,\sigma)$ alone. If the PCP's parameters are polynomial in $(\log n,\log T,Q,\sigma)$ and $S$ runs within $Q(|d|+1)^\mu$ at $n$, it runs within $(C(W+1)^M)^{\mu+1}(|d|+1)^{E(\mu+1)}$ for universal $C,M,E$ and any $W$ above $Q$, $\lambda$, $\sigma$, $n$ and $|S|$.

## Proof

The program first runs a routine computing the PCP parameters at the index: $\lambda$ and $n$ in unary ($\lambda$ replaced by $0$ at the index $0$), $Q$ by a power loop, $\mu$ in unary and $T=2^{(Q+5)(\mu+1)}$, the parameters by the PCP decider's parameter program, and the copies' offsets, lengths, selector widths and seeds in unary; every loop is as long as one of $\lambda$, $n$, $\mu$, $Q$, $k$, $m$, $s$, and the bit length of $T$ is the product of one quantity below $W$ and one below $\mu+1$. A query at a type is then split: the bits of the PCP part are answered outright, and the others make up a query to the typed oracularized sampler, answered through the universal machine, below its own level; beyond it the marginal is asked at its last level and a linear map or factor query has a zero oracle half. A PCP query is answered by the copy its type names, on the three runs of $k$-bit blocks: the seed on the seed run, the selected direction on the direction run and the line's base point on the point run, computed by the introspection sampler's Shoup programs. Each of the three kinds of answer is the Shoup bits of the presentation's, run by run. The forwarded query is at most linearly larger than the typed query, so the oracularized sampler's time on it, which is polynomial in $W$ times the query's size to the power $e(\mu+1)$, raises only constants to the power $\mu+1$; the rest is polynomial-time work on data of dominated size. Bounds of the shape $(c(W+1)^m X^e)^{\mu+1}$ are closed under sums, products, fixed polynomials and such calls, which is the whole accounting.
