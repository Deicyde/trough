# Anisotropic Carleson bounds and almost-everywhere Fourier convergence

> Weak (2,2) bounds for maximally modulated anisotropically homogeneous smooth multipliers on Euclidean space, following the Lacey–Thiele time-frequency method, and almost-everywhere convergence of the Fourier series of periodic L² functions.
