# The typed answer-reduced game

The typed answer-reduced game at one index has the types (role, PCP type), fifty-four of them, on the complete type graph with loops. Its CL function at a type is the product of two parts on disjoint coordinates, both padded to $\max\{\ell+1,3\}$ levels:

- the oracularized sampler's function of the role;
- the PCP presentation of the PCP type, downsized to $\mathbb{F}_2$ along the Shoup polynomial basis of $\mathbb{F}_q$, whose coordinates are the bits the PCP verifier reads.

The coordinates of the PCP part are numbered in three runs: the concatenated point register of the sixth copy, its concatenated direction register, and the six seeds; each copy of the test is then a contiguous run of the first two with its own seed. A question carries the oracularized question in the first part and the downsized PCP question in the second, the latter as the Shoup bits of its coordinates in that order. An answer is written as its field elements' bit blocks, serialized, and parsed back with the count and block length its type prescribes; an answer of the right format parses back from its encoding. The predicate rejects a failed parse and otherwise applies Definition [The answer-reduced decision predicate](def-ar-decider-predicate.md) to the decoded questions. Its game check runs the PCP verifier on the input question pair and the view read off the $\mathrm{Point}_6$ question and answer.
