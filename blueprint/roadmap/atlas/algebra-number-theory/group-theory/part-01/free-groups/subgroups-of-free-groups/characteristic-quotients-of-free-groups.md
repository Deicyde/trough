## Kourovka Problem 17.62 (two-generated characteristic quotients of free groups)

The
statement that for every natural number $n \ge 3$ the free group $F_n$ on $n$ generators has a
characteristic subgroup $C \ne F_n$ such that the quotient $F_n / C$ is generated by two elements,
hence by fewer than $n$ elements. This is the positive answer to Problem 17.62 (J. Conrad) in
every rank where two generators are fewer than $n$.

## Free groups of rank at least 3 have proper two-generated characteristic quotients

For every
natural number $n \ge 3$, the free group $F_n$ on $n$ generators has a characteristic subgroup
$C \ne F_n$ such that the quotient $F_n / C$ is generated by two elements; since $2 < n$, the
quotient is generated by fewer than $n$ elements.

Resolved true: Coulon and Fournier-Facio (Theorem 1.1) prove that for all $n \ge 2$ the free group
$F_n$ admits continuum many pairwise non-isomorphic 2-generated infinite simple characteristic
quotients; for $n \ge 3$ such a quotient $F_n / C$ is generated by fewer than $n$ elements, and
$C$ is proper since the quotient is infinite. The Kourovka Notebook (21st issue) answers 17.62
"Yes, it is possible".
