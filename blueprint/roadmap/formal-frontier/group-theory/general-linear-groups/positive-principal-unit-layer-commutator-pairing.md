# Positive principal-unit layer commutator pairings

For a left ideal I in any ring R and positive degrees a, b, the bracket xy - yx descends to an integer-bilinear pairing of successive ideal layers in degree a+b, and its restriction along the injective principal-unit symbols is the pairing represented by the ordered commutator of original-ring units, without two-sidedness, Jacobson containment or symbol surjectivity.
