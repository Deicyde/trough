# The Chebotarev density theorem: the case of abelian extensions

Now let $L/K$ be an arbitrary finite abelian extension with Galois group $G$, and fix $\sigma \in G$. The goal is to show that the primes of $K$ with Frobenius $\sigma$ still have Dirichlet density $1/|G|$, by reducing to the cyclotomic case already treated. (Cf. Theorem 7.2.2, Step 2, of <https://www.math.ucla.edu/~sharifi/algnum.pdf>; cyclic extensions already suffice for the later reduction to the general case.)
