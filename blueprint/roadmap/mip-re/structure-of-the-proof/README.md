# Structure of the proof

This chapter records the main transformations of (JNVWY20) at headline level: for each, what it does, what it relies on, and one theorem statement so that the dependency graph reflects the architecture. The conditional assembly, its parameter arithmetic and the ambient introspection construction for seven-level inputs are formalized. Supplying answer reduction remains a main task of the project. These soundness analyses use the bipartite framework. The paper factors these transformations through *typed* verifiers and detypes at specified boundaries (Remark Typed verifiers and the exterior transformation boundaries); the ambient Lean hypotheses also require the runtime adaptation of Remark Runtime normalization obligation.

Following Lin (Lin25), every soundness statement below is in *value form*: a transformation preserves the property “value at most $1 - \varepsilon$” up to a quantitative loss, and compression preserves “value at most $\frac12$”. The three inner transformations, repetition and compression all carry that property in $\mathrm{val}^*$, the bipartite tensor-product value: it is the class the compressibility criterion is instantiated with and the form Definition [The computable class](../foundations/part-03/def-mipstar-computable.md) requires (Remark Relation to the abstract compression lemma), and no passage between the two values occurs anywhere on the path. The synchronous value is not used, and neither is the almost-synchronous transport of Theorem [Almost-synchronous strategies are near synchronous strategies](https://github.com/vidick/MIPRE-formalization/blob/5926bdff5db621f44682a101007ef2fe3ae8d970/blueprint/src/content/03_background_results.tex#L137); see Remark [The synchronization invariant](../foundations/README.md) for why that is the better arrangement and Remark Why the pipeline is bipartite throughout for the evidence. No entanglement lower bound is tracked. Remark [Direct versus anchored repetition](../parallel-repetition/README.md) explains why none is needed, Section [The abstract compression lemmas](../computability/README.md) states the abstract lemma (Lin's compressibility criterion) that turns value-form compression into the halting reduction, and the entanglement clauses of (JNVWY20) are recorded for reference in Remark The entanglement form of (JNVWY20).

## Conditionally linear functions and samplers

Question distributions must be structured enough that players can sample them *on their own* in a certifiable way (introspection), yet expressive enough to implement all the verifiers in the pipeline. The right class is that of conditionally linear distributions: linear maps applied in stages, where each stage may depend on the output of the previous ones.
