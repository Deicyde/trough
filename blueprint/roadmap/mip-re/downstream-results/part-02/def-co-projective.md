# Projective commuting-operator strategy

A commuting-operator strategy $\mathscr{S} = (\mathcal{H}, \psi, E, F)$ is *projective* if every $E^x_a$ and every $F^y_b$ is idempotent. Being positive, they are then orthogonal projections, and every measurement of the strategy is projective.
