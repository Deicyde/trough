# semiRingOfSets_disjoint_of_union

Suppose that $C$ is a semi-ring of sets, and let $A_k \in C$, $k \in \{1, ..., n\}$ . Then there exists a family of pairwise disjoint sets $B_j \in C$, $j \in \{1, ..., m\}$, such that $\bigcup_{k=1}^n A_k = \bigcup_{j=1}^m B_j$ .
