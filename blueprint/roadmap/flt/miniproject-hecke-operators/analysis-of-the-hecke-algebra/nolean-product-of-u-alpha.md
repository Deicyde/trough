# nolean-product-of-U-alpha

If $v\in S$ and $\begin{pmatrix}1&*\\0&1\end{pmatrix}\subseteq\Gamma_v\subseteq\begin{pmatrix}*&*\\0&*\end{pmatrix}$ then $U_{\alpha,v}U_{\beta,v}=U_{\alpha\beta,v}$.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

Follows easily from the explicit double coset decomposition proved above.
