**`AlgebraicGeometry.curvesSmoothLocus_property_iff_isSmooth_schemeDeformation`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Quot/CurvesSmoothLocusUnobstructed.lean, line 83](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Quot/CurvesSmoothLocusUnobstructed.lean#L83).

> Let `k` be a field, let `X` be a proper scheme of dimension at most one over `k`, and let `Λ`
> be a Cohen ring for `k`: a complete discrete valuation ring whose maximal ideal is generated by a
> prime number `p` (`IsCohenRing`), with a surjection `Λ → k`, so that `k` is its residue field.
> Then the following are equivalent:
>
> 1. the classifying morphism `Spec(k) → Curves` of `X` factors through the open substack where
>    `Curves → Spec(ℤ)` is smooth;
> 2. the deformation category `Def_X` of `X` over `𝒞_Λ` is unobstructed.
>
> The stack `Curves` is read on its small model `AlgebraicGeometry.curvesAlgebraicStack`, the
> presentation at which it is an object of the 2-category of algebraic stacks; the classifying
> morphism of `X` is the object `AlgebraicGeometry.curvesSmallPoint` over `Spec(k)`, and it factors
> through an open substack exactly when it is an object of that substack. The open substack where
> `Curves → Spec(ℤ)` is smooth is the maximal one of Stacks 0DZR,
> `AlgebraicGeometry.curvesSmoothLocus`. Unobstructedness of `Def_X` is smoothness of its
> projection to `𝒞_Λ` (Stacks 06HG).

```lean
theorem curvesSmoothLocus_property_iff_isSmooth_schemeDeformation
    {k Λ : Type u} [Field k] [CommRing Λ] [IsDomain Λ] [IsCohenRing Λ] [Algebra Λ k]
    (hsurj : Function.Surjective (algebraMap Λ k))
    (X : Over (Spec (.of k))) [IsProper X.hom] (hk : B.Contains (Spec (.of k)))
    (hdim : topologicalKrullDim X.left ≤ 1) :
    (curvesSmoothLocus.{u, w} B hZ).property (curvesSmallPoint B hZ X hk hdim) ↔
      IsSmooth (baseProjection (schemeDeformationCategoryProjection (Λ := Λ) X))
```

> **Stacks 0DZX.** `Curves^{lci+} = Curves^{lci} ∩ Curves^{+}`, the strictly full subcategory of
> families of curves with local complete intersection structure morphism and singular locus finite
> over the base, is an open substack of `Curves`: its inclusion is representable by algebraic
> spaces and an open immersion.

```lean
theorem curvesLciPlus_isOpenSubstack :
```

> **Stacks 0DZX, part (2).** The classifying morphism of a family of curves `X ⟶ S` factors
> through `Curves^{lci+}` exactly when `X ⟶ S` is a local complete intersection morphism and its
> singular locus, with any (equivalently, some) closed subspace structure, is finite over `S`.

```lean
theorem factors_curvesLciPlus_iff (U : BigFppfOver B integerBase hZ)
    (y : BigFppfOver B U.left.1 U.left.2 ⥤ CurveFamily B hZ)
    (hy : y ⋙ curvesProjectionOver B hZ = Over.map U.hom) :
    let X := y.obj (Over.mk (𝟙 U.left))
    ((∃ z : BigFppfOver B U.left.1 U.left.2 ⥤
        (IsLciPlusFamily B hZ).FullSubcategory,
        z ⋙ (IsLciPlusFamily B hZ).ι = y) ↔
      IsLciFamily B hZ X ∧
        ∀ Z : Subfunctor X.total.obj, Space.IsClosedSubspace B integerBase hZ Z →
          Set.range (Space.pointsMap B integerBase hZ Z.toFunctor Z.ι) =
            {x : Space.points B integerBase hZ X.total.obj |
              ¬ Space.IsSmoothAt B integerBase hZ X.toBase.hom x} →
          Space.IsFinite B integerBase hZ (Z.ι ≫ X.toBase.hom)) ∧
    ((∃ z : BigFppfOver B U.left.1 U.left.2 ⥤
        (IsLciPlusFamily B hZ).FullSubcategory,
        z ⋙ (IsLciPlusFamily B hZ).ι = y) ↔
      IsLciFamily B hZ X ∧
        ∃ Z : Subfunctor X.total.obj, Space.IsClosedSubspace B integerBase hZ Z ∧
          Set.range (Space.pointsMap B integerBase hZ Z.toFunctor Z.ι) =
            {x : Space.points B integerBase hZ X.total.obj |
```
