# mem_discontinuousSquareIntegrable

If $M$ is a purely discontinuous martingale in the sense of Definition [IsPurelyDiscontinuous](../part-01/def-ispurelydiscontinuous.md), then it belongs to $\mathcal{M}^{2,d}$.

## Depends on

- [IsPurelyDiscontinuous](../part-01/def-ispurelydiscontinuous.md)
- [discontinuousSquareIntegrable](def-discontinuoussquareintegrable.md)

## Proof

By definition of the orthogonal projection.
