# Hurwitz.left_ideal_princ

All left ideals of $\mathcal{O}$ are principal.

## Depends on

- [Hurwitz.norm](hurwitz-norm.md)

## Proof depends on

## Proof

If the ideal is 0, use 0. Otherwise, choose a nonzero element of smallest norm.
