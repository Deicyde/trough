# Grothendieck ∞-groupoids, globular sums and the degree-zero comparison

> Grothendieck's globular ∞-groupoids in the form Maltsiniotis and Ara gave to the definition of Pursuing Stacks. The globe category, globular sets, coglobular objects and the coglobular space of disks with its hemisphere inclusions; tables of dimensions and globular sums, which exist in every category with pushouts; globular extensions, globular functors, admissible pairs, contractibility and pseudo-coherators; ∞-groupoids of type C, the presheaves sending globular sums to globular products, among which the terminal and the representable presheaves. Liftings of admissible pairs give identities, inverses and composites of cells, so that the homotopy relation on the cells of an ∞-groupoid of type C is an equivalence relation; weak equivalences contain the identities and are closed under composition. For a supplied globular functor from a globular extension C into the disks, the fundamental ∞-groupoid of a space sends globular sums to globular products, an ∞-groupoid of type C when C is a pseudo-coherator, and its 0-cells modulo homotopy are the path components of the space. It detects bijections on path components.
