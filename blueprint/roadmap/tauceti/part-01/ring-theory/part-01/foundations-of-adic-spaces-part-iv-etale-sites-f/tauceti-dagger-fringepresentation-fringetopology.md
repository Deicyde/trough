# TauCeti.Dagger.FringePresentation.fringeTopology

The fringe topology of a presentation, generated by its stagewise continuous
ultrametric seminorms. The stages have their quotient Gauss topologies.

```lean

  [NormedField K] [IsUltrametricDist K] [CommRing A] [Algebra K A]
```
