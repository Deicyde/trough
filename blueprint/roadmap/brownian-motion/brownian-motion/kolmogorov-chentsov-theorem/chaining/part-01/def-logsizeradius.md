# logSizeRadius

Let $V$ be a finite subset of a metric space and let $t \in V$ and $a > 1$, $c > 0$. Let the *log-size radius* of $t$ in $V$, denoted by $r_{V,t}$, be the smallest positive integer $r$ such that $\vert B_V(t, r c) \vert \le a^{r}$.
