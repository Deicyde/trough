# The classification of gauge invariants of a gauge field

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.AdjointFamily.Classification`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily/Classification.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily/Classification.lean): 23 declarations, 21 with a docstring.

> A gauge-invariant polynomial in the symbols of a gauge field, and in further generators fixed
> by the pure jets, is a polynomial in the covariant derivatives of the field strength and those
> generators.

## i. Overview

> Let `A` be a family of gauge-field symbols in an algebra `B` that transforms as a gauge field
> under a multiplicative action of the jet gauge group, and whose symbols commute with each
> other. The classification `invariant_mem_adjoin_fieldStrength` says that a gauge-invariant
> element of the subalgebra generated by the symbols and a set `S` of elements fixed by the pure
> jets lies in the subalgebra generated by the covariant derivatives of the field strength and
> `S`. The elements of `S` need only commute with the symbols. No algebraic independence of
> the symbols is assumed, and nothing about the rest of `B`.
>
> By the generation theorem the symbols may be traded for the symmetrized derivatives and the
> covariant field-strength tower. Pure jets fix the tower and act on the symmetrized derivatives
> by translations, and every translation is realized by a pure jet, since the pure jets are free
> coordinates. The ring-theoretic extraction principle `mem_of_translationInvariant` then strips
> the symmetrized symbols order by order, by a Vandermonde argument.

## ii. Key results

> - `GaugeBoson.exists_translation_of_support` : every top-order translation is realized by a
>   pure jet.
> - `GaugeBoson.mem_of_translationInvariant` : the ring-theoretic extraction principle.
> - `GaugeBoson.invariant_mem_adjoin_fieldStrength` : the classification of invariants.

## B. Translation invariance in a ring

> The extraction principle behind the classification is pure ring theory: if a family of ring
> endomorphisms fixes a subalgebra `R` pointwise and translates finitely many central
> elements `y i` by arbitrary prescribable scalars, then an element of the subalgebra
> generated by `R` and the `y i` that is invariant under the whole family lies in `R`. The
> proof chooses any polynomial representation and kills its top coefficient by evaluating the
> invariance at enough shifts.

## C. The classification of invariants

> The goal is the classification theorem: a gauge-invariant element of the subalgebra
> generated by the gauge-field symbols together with a set `S` of elements fixed by the pure
> jets lies in the subalgebra generated by the covariant field-strength tower together with
> `S`, assuming only that the gauge-field symbols are central (bosonic), with no
> algebraic-independence hypothesis.
>
> The strategy, by downward induction on the top symmetrized order `m + 1` present in `x`:
>
> * By the generation theorem, `x` is a polynomial in the symmetrized symbols of order at most
>   `m + 1`, the covariant tower, and `S`.
> * By freeness of the symmetrized Maurer–Cartan data, there are pure jets whose data are
>   supported at exactly order `m + 1`. Triangularity places them in the deep truncation
>   kernel `truncationKer m`, so they fix every generator of order at most `m`, the covariant
>   tower and `S`, and act on the order-`m + 1` symmetrized symbols by pure translations with
>   arbitrary prescribable scalars.
> * The extraction theorem `mem_of_translationInvariant` then places `x` in the subalgebra of
>   order at most `m`.

### C.1. The order-`m` subalgebra

> The subalgebra generated by the symmetrized symbols of order at most `m`, the covariant
> tower and `S`. It is fixed by the pure jets trivial to order `m`, and its elements commute
