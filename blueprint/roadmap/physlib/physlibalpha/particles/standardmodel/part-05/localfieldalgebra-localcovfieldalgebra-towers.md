# The covariant towers of the Standard Model

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.Towers`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/LocalCovFieldAlgebra/Towers.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/LocalCovFieldAlgebra/Towers.lean): 47 declarations, 47 with a docstring.

> The thirteen covariant towers of the Standard Model in the notation of the classification of
> invariants, their commutation across sectors, and the algebra they generate.

## i. Overview

> The covariant fields of the Standard Model, and every polynomial expression in them, live in
> the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model `StandardModel.fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra`,
> which carries the global gauge action, the Lorentz action and the mass-weight grading. This file
> names its thirteen covariant towers in the notation of the classification of invariants, records
> that towers of different sectors commute, and defines the field algebra they generate.
>
> The thirteen towers are the field strength, the Higgs field and its conjugate, and the five
> fermion species in three generations with their conjugates, each with all of its covariant
> derivatives. Section A names them. Section B records the symmetry of the field-strength tower
> at a dual vector vanishing on brackets. Section C states the commutation of towers of
> different sectors, which is what the classification of invariants is written in terms of, and
> section D defines the field algebra.

## ii. Key results

> - `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.covF`, `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.covH` and their companions : the thirteen
>   covariant towers.
> - `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.F_comm_H` and its companions : the towers of different sectors
>   commute.
> - `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.fieldAlgebra` : the algebra generated by all the covariant towers.

## B. The field strength at a dual vector vanishing on brackets

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.covF_comp_perm_of_map_lie`**, a lemma, [line 157](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/LocalCovFieldAlgebra/Towers.lean#L157).

> At a dual vector vanishing on every bracket, such as the hypercharge coordinate, the
> field-strength tower is symmetric in its derivative slots, at every order. In the covariant field
> algebra of the Standard Model those covariant derivatives are ordinary ones. The components along
> the non-abelian factors have no such symmetry.

```lean
lemma covF_comp_perm_of_map_lie {φ : Module.Dual ℝ GaugeAlgebra}
    (hφ : ∀ x y : GaugeAlgebra, φ ⁅x, y⁆ = 0) {n : ℕ} (σ : Equiv.Perm (Fin n))
    (l : Fin n → (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3)) (μ ν : Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3) :
    covF (l ∘ σ) μ ν φ = covF l μ ν φ
```

## C. The cross-sector commutation rules

> A bosonic tower is central (`fieldStrength_commute`, `higgsField_commute`,
> `conjHiggsField_commute`), so it commutes with every tower of another sector. The rules are
> named as the classification of invariants uses them.
