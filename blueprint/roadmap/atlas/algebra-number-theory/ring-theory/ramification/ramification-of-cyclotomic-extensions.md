# Ramification of cyclotomic extensions

5 results, all proved, quoted from [Deicyde/atlas-lean](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean) at commit [035e1808](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/tree/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce); [its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/RingTheory/ramification/ramification-of-cyclotomic-extensions/index.html).

## Without primitive $p$-th roots of unity, every $p$-th root of unity is trivial

Let $p$ be a
prime and let $L$ be a field containing no primitive $p$-th root of unity. Then every $\eta \in L$
with $\eta^p = 1$ equals $1$.

The order of $\eta$ divides the prime $p$, so it is $1$ or $p$, and $p$ is excluded.

## No primitive p-th root in an unramified DVR extension

For a complete DVR base whose maximal ideal is generated by `p`, a
primitive `p`-th root cannot appear in an index-one DVR extension: if the residue characteristic is `p`,
`(p)` is the maximal ideal, the ramification index is `1`, and the base field
has no primitive `p`-th root, then neither does the extension.

The proof is the valuation argument: a primitive root `b ∈ B` reduces to `1`
in the residue field (characteristic `p`), so each factor `1 - b ^ (k + 1)`
lies in the maximal ideal, while their product is exactly `p`; hence
`p ∈ 𝔪_B ^ (p - 1)`, which forces `p ∈ 𝔪_B ^ 2`, contradicting
ramification index `1` (for `p = 2` the argument is replaced by the
observation that `-1` would be a primitive square root in the base field).

## Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$ and no primitive $p$-th root of unity, an index-one extension has no nontrivial $p$-th roots of unity

**Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$ and no primitive $p$-th root of unity, an
index-one extension has no nontrivial $p$-th roots of unity.** Let $A$ be a discrete valuation
ring, complete with respect to its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_A$, with fraction field $K$ and
finite residue field of characteristic a prime $p$, and suppose $\mathfrak m_A = (p)$. Let $L$ be a
finite extension of $K$, and let $B$ be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field $L$ and an
$A$-algebra structure such that the maps $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$ agree. Suppose the
ramification index of the maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_B$ of $B$ over $\mathfrak m_A$ (the largest
$n$ with $\mathfrak m_A B \subseteq \mathfrak m_B^n$) is $1$. If $K$ contains no primitive $p$-th
root of unity, then every $\eta \in L$ with $\eta^p = 1$ equals $1$.

## Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$, the $p$-th roots of unity in an index-one extension come from the base

**Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$, the $p$-th roots of unity in an index-one
extension come from the base.** Let $A$ be a discrete valuation ring, complete with respect to its
maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_A$, with fraction field $K$ and finite residue field of characteristic a
prime $p$, and suppose $\mathfrak m_A = (p)$. Let $L$ be a finite extension of $K$, and let $B$ be
a discrete valuation ring with fraction field $L$ and an $A$-algebra structure such that the maps
$A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$ agree. Suppose the ramification index of the maximal ideal
$\mathfrak m_B$ of $B$ over $\mathfrak m_A$ (the largest $n$ with
$\mathfrak m_A B \subseteq \mathfrak m_B^n$) is $1$. Then every $\eta \in L$ with $\eta^p = 1$ is
the image of an element of $K$.

## Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$ and no nontrivial $p$-th roots of unity, cyclotomic extensions of order divisible by $p$ ramify

**Over a complete base with maximal ideal $(p)$ and no nontrivial $p$-th roots of unity,
cyclotomic extensions of order divisible by $p$ ramify.** Let $A$ be a discrete valuation ring,
complete with respect to its maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_A$, with fraction field $K$ and finite
residue field of characteristic a prime $p$, and suppose $\mathfrak m_A = (p)$. Suppose $1$ is the
only $p$-th root of unity in $K$. Let $m > 0$ be divisible by $p$, let $L$ be an $m$-th cyclotomic
extension of $K$ that is finite over $K$, and let $B$ be a discrete valuation ring with fraction
field $L$ and an $A$-algebra structure such that the maps $A \to B \to L$ and $A \to K \to L$
agree. Then the ramification index of the maximal ideal $\mathfrak m_B$ of $B$ over
$\mathfrak m_A$ (the largest $n$ with $\mathfrak m_A B \subseteq \mathfrak m_B^n$) is not $1$.
