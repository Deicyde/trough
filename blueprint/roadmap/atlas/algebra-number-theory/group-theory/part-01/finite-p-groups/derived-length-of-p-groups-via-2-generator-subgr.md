## Derived length of $p$-groups whose $2$-generator subgroups have cyclic derived subgroups (Kourovka Problem 17.46)

**Derived length of $p$-groups whose $2$-generator subgroups have cyclic derived subgroups
(Kourovka Problem 17.46).** Let $p$ be a prime and $G$ a finite $p$-group such that for all
$x, y \in G$ the derived subgroup $[H, H]$ of $H = \langle x, y \rangle$ is cyclic. Then $G$ has
derived length at most $4$, that is, the fourth term $G^{(4)}$ of the derived series of $G$ is
trivial.

## Metacyclic group

A group $G$ is metacyclic if it has a cyclic normal subgroup $N$ such
that the quotient $G/N$ is cyclic.

## Kourovka Problem 15.33, with the bound $2$

The statement that every finite $2$-group $G$
such that for all $x, y \in G$ the subgroup $\langle x, y \rangle$ generated by $x$ and $y$ is
metacyclic has derived length at most $2$, that is, its second derived subgroup $G''$ is

## Finite $2$-groups with metacyclic $2$-generator subgroups are metabelian (Kourovka Problem 15.33)

**Finite $2$-groups with metacyclic $2$-generator subgroups are metabelian (Kourovka
Problem 15.33).** Let $G$ be a finite $2$-group such that for all $x, y \in G$ the subgroup
$\langle x, y \rangle$ generated by $x$ and $y$ is metacyclic, that is, has a cyclic normal
subgroup with cyclic quotient. Then $G$ is metabelian: its second derived subgroup $G''$ is
trivial.
