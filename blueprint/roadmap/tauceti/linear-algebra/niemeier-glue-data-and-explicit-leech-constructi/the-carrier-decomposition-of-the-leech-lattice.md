# The carrier decomposition of the Leech lattice

a vector lies in the Leech lattice
exactly when it or its difference with the odd shift lies in the Golay Construction B
lattice.

```lean
TauCeti.mem_leechLattice_carrier_iff (x : Fin 24 → ℚ) :
  x ∈ TauCeti.leechLattice.carrier ↔
    x ∈ TauCeti.BinaryGolay.constructionBLattice.carrier ∨
      x - TauCeti.leechShift ∈ TauCeti.BinaryGolay.constructionBLattice.carrier
```
