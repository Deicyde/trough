# serre-der

For $k \in \mathbb{R}$, define the weight $k$ Serre derivative $\partial_{k}$ of a modular form $F$ as

$$
\partial_{k}F := F' - \frac{k}{12} E_2 F.\qquad\text{(25)}
$$
