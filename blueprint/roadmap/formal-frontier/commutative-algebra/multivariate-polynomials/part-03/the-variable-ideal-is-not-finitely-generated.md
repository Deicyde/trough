# The variable ideal is not finitely generated

For a nontrivial
commutative semiring and infinitely many variables,
[`MvPolynomial.idealOfVars_not_fg`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/IdealOfVars.lean)
proves non-finite generation. A finite union of polynomial supports misses
some variable; evaluation separating that variable gives a contradiction.
Mathlib supplies the complementary *finite*-variable ideal theorem. This
library does not prove the motivating complete short exact sequence.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
