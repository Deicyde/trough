## Composition factor of a group (via subnormal subgroups)

For groups $T$ and $Q$, $T$ is a
composition factor of $Q$ when some subnormal subgroup $H$ of $Q$ maps onto $T$, that is, there is a
surjective group homomorphism $H \to T$. Here $H$ is subnormal in $Q$ when there is a finite chain
of subgroups $H = H_0 \le H_1 \le \dots \le H_k = Q$ in which each $H_j$ is normal in $H_{j+1}$.

For simple $T$ and finite $Q$ this is the same as $T$ occurring as a factor of a composition series
of $Q$ (refine the kernel and the subnormal chain).

## Upper composition factors

For groups $T$ and $G$, $T$ is an upper composition factor of $G$
when it is a composition factor of some finite quotient of $G$: there are a finite group $Q$ and a
surjective group homomorphism $G \to Q$ such that $T$ is a composition factor of $Q$, that is, $T$
is a homomorphic image of a subnormal subgroup of $Q$.

## Kourovka Problem 11.57, 63-generator form (statement)

The statement that for every family
$(T_i)_{i \in I}$ of finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group $G$ generated by $63$
elements such that a finite simple group $Q$ is an upper composition factor of $G$ if and only if
$Q$ is isomorphic to $T_i$ for some $i \in I$.

## Segal's theorem on upper composition factors, 63 generators (Kourovka Problem 11.57)

For every
family $(T_i)_{i \in I}$ of finite non-abelian simple groups there is a group $G$ generated by $63$
elements such that a finite simple group $Q$ is an upper composition factor of $G$ (a composition
factor of some finite quotient of $G$) if and only if $Q$ is isomorphic to $T_i$ for some $i \in I$.
In particular, no cyclic group of prime order is an upper composition factor of $G$.
