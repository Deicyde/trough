> **Stacks 051W**. Let `R → S` be a ring map and let `0 → M₁ → M₂ → M₃ → 0` be a short exact

> `U(R → S, M₁) ∩ U(R → S, M₃) ⊆ U(R → S, M₂)`: an extension of free modules is free and an
