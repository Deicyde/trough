# Axis degree for expanded line measurements

Without loss of generality the strategy answers every question in the format its type prescribes: replacing each answer of the wrong format by a fixed legal one keeps the measurements projective, does not decrease the success probability, and leaves every reading the rules and the conclusions use — point values, trace probes, Pauli encodings, bits — unchanged. For such a strategy and $d \geq 1$, the expanded line measurement of Lemma [Expanded line measurements](lem-qld-expanded-lines.md) at an axis-parallel line is supported on polynomials of degree at most $d$: it has no element on any other outcome. The same holds for the padded line measurement of Lemma [Padded line POVM](../part-03/lem-qld-padded-lines.md) at an axis-parallel padded line, so its outcomes are legal answers to the axis-parallel line questions of the seeded test at $(q,4m,d,1)$. The original strategy's axis-line answers have degree at most $d$ by their legal format. Expansion convolves that outcome with the restriction of a multilinear Pauli encoding. The latter has degree at most one on an axis line, so addition preserves the bound for $d\geq1$. The Lean proves it for a strategy in any bipartite model, the expanded and padded measurements living in the players' algebras of its register model; the legalized strategy is the one the assembly of Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md) runs the chain on.

## Proof

Legalizing (`legalizeStrat`) relabels each measurement along the map that keeps a well-formatted answer and sends any other to a default legal answer reading as $0$. A relabelling of a projective measurement is projective. The decider rejects an answer of the wrong format before running any rule, so every accepted answer pair is left alone and the accepted mass can only grow (`povmValue_le_legalizeStrat`, by data processing on the Born distribution). Every reading of Lemma [Win implications](lem-qld-win.md)'s rules sends ill-formatted answers to $0$, as the defaults do, so the point-probe, hatted point and Pauli readings of the two strategies coincide.

For a legally supported strategy the strategy's factor of the expanded axis-line measurement is supported on `apoly` answers, of degree at most $d$, and the ancilla's factor on restrictions of the multilinear encoding to the axis line, of degree at most $1$ (`natDegree_lineRestrict_single_le`); their convolution is supported on degree at most $d$ for $d \geq 1$ (`hatLinePOVM_aline_eq_zero_of_not_degLE`). The padded line measurement is an average of sandwiches of the two sides' axis-line elements, coarse-grained by the combining map, whose degree bound `degLE_padCombine_aline` contraposes: an outcome of degree above $d$ can only come from a pair with a factor of degree above $d$, and that factor vanishes.
