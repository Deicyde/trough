# Final assembly

This chapter combines the minus criterion of Chapter [The minus class-number criterion](../the-minus-class-number-criterion/README.md) with the cyclotomic-unit results of Chapter [Cyclotomic units and Kummer logarithms](../cyclotomic-units-and-kummer-logarithms/README.md). The bridge is the implication $p \mid h^+ \Rightarrow p \mid h^-$: if $p \nmid h^-$, the minus criterion clears every Bernoulli numerator in Kummer's range, the Kummer logarithm determinant is then nonzero, $C^+$ is $p$-saturated, and the prime-conductor index theorem forces $p \nmid h^+$.
