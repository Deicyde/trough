**`generate_iff_generateStalks`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/GeneratedByGlobalSections.lean, line 359](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/GeneratedByGlobalSections.lean#L359). References: EGA 0 (5.1.1), “parenthetical”.

> **Stacks Tag 01AN, Lemma 17.4.2.** Let `(X, O_X)` be a ringed space, `F` a sheaf of
> `O_X`-modules, `I` a set, and `s_i ∈ Γ(X, F)` global sections. Then the sections generate
> `F` (Definition 2) if and only if for every point `x` the germs `s_{i,x}` generate the
> `O_{X,x}`-module `F_x` (Definition 3 at the stalk).
>
> PROOF: a finite chain of iff's. With `φ := assocMap O_X F s` and `ψ := (toSheaf O_X).map φ`:
>   Epi φ  ↔ Epi ψ                                   -- toSheaf_preservesEpi + toSheaf_reflectsEpi
>         ↔ Sheaf.IsLocallySurjective ψ              -- Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_iff_epi'
>         ↔ Presheaf.IsLocallySurjective (sheafToPresheaf.map ψ)
>                                     -- Sheaf.isLocallySurjective_sheafToPresheaf_map_iff
>         ↔ ∀ x, Surjective ((stalkFunctor _ x).map (sheafToPresheaf.map ψ))
>                                                    -- locally_surjective_iff_surjective_on_stalks
>                                                    -- (defeq to TopCat version)
>         ≡ GenerateStalks                           -- defeq: matches `stalkMapAb`.

```lean
theorem generate_iff_generateStalks (s : I → F.sections) :
    Generate O_X F s ↔ GenerateStalks O_X F s
```

> **Stacks 01AR.** Let `G` be the smallest subsheaf of `F` containing local sections
> `s i ∈ F(U i)`. At a point `x`, the set of germs of local sections of `G`, viewed in the
> stalk of `F`, is the `R_x`-submodule spanned by the germs `(s i)_x` for which `x ∈ U i`.

```lean
theorem generated_subsheaf_stalk_eq_span {ι : Type*}
    (U : ι → (Opens X)ᵒᵖ) (s : ∀ i, F.val.obj (U i))
    (G : F.Submodule) (hcontains : ∀ i, s i ∈ G.obj (U i))
    (hminimal : ∀ G' : F.Submodule,
      (∀ i, s i ∈ G'.obj (U i)) → G ≤ G')
    (x : X) :
    {t : (show X.Presheaf Ab from F.val.presheaf).stalk x |
      ∃ (V : Opens X) (hxV : x ∈ V) (a : F.val.obj (op V)),
        a ∈ G.obj (op V) ∧
          (show X.Presheaf Ab from F.val.presheaf).germ V x hxV a = t} =
      (Submodule.span ((show X.Presheaf RingCat from R.obj).stalk x)
        {t | ∃ (i : ι) (hxi : x ∈ unop (U i)),
          (show X.Presheaf Ab from F.val.presheaf).germ
            (unop (U i)) x hxi (s i) = t} :
        Submodule ((show X.Presheaf RingCat from R.obj).stalk x)
          ((show X.Presheaf Ab from F.val.presheaf).stalk x))
```
