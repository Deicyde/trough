# A global padded polynomial PVM

Suppose that the seeded CL test is sound (Definition [The low-individual-degree test is sound in a model](../../downstream-results/part-03/def-lidt-sound-in.md)) in every ancilla extension of the strategy's model by a unit vector. Then, for every padding size beyond a threshold, the test's soundness in the padded model of Definition [The models of stages 4 and 5](def-qld-physical-model.md), applied at $(q,4m,d,1)$, one codeword, to a projective dilation of the padded strategy of Lemma [Admissible padded setup](lem-qld-global-setup.md) on the two padding registers, supplies projective global polynomial measurements $G_A$, $G_B$ in the padded model's two algebras, with both point-consistency estimates and the full-polynomial consistency. Their common error is $\delta_{\mathrm{ld}} := \delta_{\mathrm{sim}}(q, 4m, d, 1, \delta_{\mathrm{GS}})$, the error of Lemma [Absorbing the simultaneous reduction's error](../part-01/lem-lidt-ldc-error.md) at one codeword, where $\delta_{\mathrm{GS}} = 5 m^2 \delta_P(\varepsilon, md/q + 1/q) + 4 \delta_Q(\varepsilon) + (md+1)/q$ is the bound of Lemma [All padded subtests succeed](lem-qld-global-success.md); it has the form $A(md)^A((\delta_{\mathrm{GS}})^b+q^{-b}+2^{-bmd})$ for universal $A,b$. The point-consistency estimates hold against the padded point measurements of the *original* strategy — the expanded sandwich combinations, carried to the padded model by the inert embedding of the register model — so no dilation appears in the conclusion, and no further dilation of the projective outputs is required. The conclusion is packaged as the structure `GlobalPair`, which the rest of stage 4 is stated on. The same holds for the strategy with the players exchanged, in the model with the players exchanged, the test being symmetric in the players, and the two hold at any common padding size beyond both thresholds. The Lean proves it for a legal projective strategy in any bipartite model; the statement above is its tensor-product instance, where the hypothesis holds by Lemma [The low-individual-degree test is sound in the tensor-product model](../../downstream-results/part-03/lem-lidt-sound-in-tensor.md).

## Proof

Lemma [All padded subtests succeed](lem-qld-global-success.md) is the value hypothesis of the seeded test for the padded strategy at $(q, 4m, d, 1)$. Each player's family of POVMs is dilated to a projective family on that player's padding register, both at one common size beyond the two thresholds (Lemma [Projective dilation against a fixed state, in a star-ordered ring](../../downstream-results/part-03/lem-kraus-dilation.md)), with reference state $t_0$ at the zero answer: the dilated families are a projective strategy of the padded model whose reference entries are the padded strategy's, and since a Born probability of the padded model is that of the reference entries, it has the padded strategy's value. The padded model is an ancilla extension of the strategy's model up to associativity, so the test is sound in it (Definition [The models of stages 4 and 5](def-qld-physical-model.md)); applied at one codeword, it returns projective low-degree measurements in the padded model's algebras and the three consistency estimates against the dilation's own point measurements, a tuple of one value being read as that value. In each inconsistency the dilation's point measurement may then be replaced by the padded point measurement carried along the inert embedding: the two have the same reference entry, and the number does not change. Expectations of operators carried along that embedding are their expectations on the expanded state, so the conclusions of Lemmas [Expanded point measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-expanded-points.md) and [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-combined-points.md) transfer to the padded model.
