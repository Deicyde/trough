# Integral closure in arbitrarily many variables

For every integrally closed
commutative domain `R` and arbitrary variable type `ι`,
[`MvPolynomial.isIntegrallyClosed`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/IntegrallyClosed.lean)
makes the ordinary polynomial ring `MvPolynomial ι R` integrally closed.
The coefficient and variable universes are independent, and the variables may
be empty or infinite. No Noetherian or unique-factorization hypothesis is
imposed on `R`.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
