## Tag 00WV, a definition

[Tag 00WV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00WV) in the Stacks Project.

**`StacksContinuousFunctor.IsContinuous`**, a structure in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous/ContinuousDefs.lean, line 55](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous/ContinuousDefs.lean#L55).

> **Stacks 00WV.** A functor `u : C ⥤ D` between sites given by pretopologies `J` and `K`
> is *continuous* if for every covering `S` of an object `V` in `J` the family obtained by
> pushing `S` forward covers `u V`, and `u` turns the fibre products of a covering arrow with
> an arbitrary arrow into fibre products in `D`.
>
> **Warning: `cover_pushforward` below is NOT clause (1) of Tag 00WV, and this structure is
> strictly stronger than the source's notion.**  Tag 00WV asks that the *image family*
> `{u Vᵢ → u V}_{i ∈ I}`, with the same index set, be a covering.  `Presieve.functorPushforward`
> is instead the sieve *generated by* that family, so it also contains every arrow factoring
> through a member of it.  Coverings of a `Pretopology` are not required to be downward closed,
> so the two conditions are incomparable in general — see the discussion in
> `StacksFrontier.CategoryTheory.Sites.Hypercovering.Basic`, which states the faithful clause as
> `MapsCoveringsToCoverings`.
>
> How much stronger: Stacks' Definition 00WV is satisfied by the identity functor of *every*
> site, trivially.  This structure is not — `𝟭` of the two-element lattice site under the
> trivial pretopology fails `cover_pushforward`, because the downward closure of `{𝟙 ⊤}` also
> contains `⊥ ⟶ ⊤` and so is not the singleton presieve.  That is a machine-checked
> counterexample, not a suspicion.  So the attribute above over-claims: what is formalized here
> implies Tag 00WV's clause (2) faithfully (`pullback_iso`) but replaces its clause (1) by a
> condition the source's own examples do not satisfy.
>
> The attribute is left in place rather than moved, because repairing the field would change a
> definition that around ninety files in this tree construct, and `MapsCoveringsToCoverings`
> already carries the faithful clause for the consumers that need it.  Anything relying on this
> structure *as* Tag 00WV should be read against this warning first.

```lean

    Prop
```

**`CategoryTheory.Functor.sheafPullbackSheafificationCompatibility`**, a def in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Pullback.lean, line 56](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Pullback.lean#L56).

> **Stacks 00WY**.
> Sheafification commutes with the presheaf pullback `u_p` along a continuous functor
> `u : C ⥤ D`: the composite of `u_p = u.op.lan` with sheafification on `D` is naturally
> isomorphic to the composite of sheafification on `C` with the sheaf pullback
> `f⁻¹ = u.sheafPullback`. Both composites are left adjoint to the same functor
> `Sheaf K A ⥤ Cᵒᵖ ⥤ A`, namely restriction along `u` followed by the inclusion of sheaves
> into presheaves, so the isomorphism is uniqueness of left adjoints.

```lean
noncomputable def sheafPullbackSheafificationCompatibility
    {C : Type u₁} [Category.{v₁} C] {D : Type u₂} [Category.{v₂} D] (A : Type u₃)
    [Category.{v₃} A] (J : GrothendieckTopology C) (K : GrothendieckTopology D) (u : C ⥤ D)
    [u.IsContinuous J K] [HasWeakSheafify J A] [HasWeakSheafify K A]
    [(u.sheafPushforwardContinuous A J K).IsRightAdjoint]
    [∀ F : Cᵒᵖ ⥤ A, u.op.HasLeftKanExtension F] :
    u.op.lan ⋙ presheafToSheaf K A ≅ presheafToSheaf J A ⋙ u.sheafPullback A J K
```

> **Definition 3 (Quasi-continuous functor).** Here sites are presented by
> pretopologies `PC` (on `C`) and `PD` (on `D`). The first clause is the relaxation
> of the pretopological continuity condition: the image family need only be
> *tautologically equivalent* to a covering family of `D`, rather than itself being
> one. The second clause says `u` preserves the fibre products appearing in
> covering families, expressed via the pullback comparison morphism being an
> isomorphism.

```lean
def IsQuasiContinuous {C : Type u} [Category.{v} C] {D : Type u} [Category.{v} D]
    [HasPullbacks C] [HasPullbacks D] (u : C ⥤ D)
    (PC : Pretopology C) (PD : Pretopology D) : Prop
```

References: SGA 4 III (1.6), “iii) ⇒ i)”.

> **Background Lemma A.** A quasi-continuous functor `u : C ⥤ D` is continuous, so
> restriction of sheaves of sets along `u` is defined and has a left adjoint `u_s`.
>
> For a `PD`-sheaf `G` and a covering presieve `R ∈ PC X`, repackage `R` as an `ArrowFamily 𝒱`;
> quasi-continuity supplies a `PD`-covering tautologically equivalent to `u(𝒱)`, so `G` is a sheaf
> for `u(𝒱)` by `isSheafFor_iff_tautologicallyEquivalent`, and preservation of the relevant fibre
> products carries that back to `u.op ⋙ G.obj` and `𝒱` via
> `isSheafFor_image_of_pullbackComparison`.
