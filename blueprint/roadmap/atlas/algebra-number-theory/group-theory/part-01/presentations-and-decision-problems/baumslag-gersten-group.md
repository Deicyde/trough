## Baumslag's group

Baumslag's group is the one-relator group
$B = \langle a, b \mid (bab^{-1})\, a\, (bab^{-1})^{-1} = a^2 \rangle$, the quotient of the free
group on the two generators $a$ and $b$ by the normal subgroup generated by
$(bab^{-1})\, a\, (bab^{-1})^{-1} a^{-2}$.

## Letter functions for Baumslag's group

To each letter of the free group on $a, b$ attach a
function $\mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$: $a$ gives $f(x) = 2x$, $a^{-1}$ gives $x/2$, $b$ gives
$g(x) = 2^x$ and $b^{-1}$ gives $\log_2 x$.

On $(0, \infty)$ the function $\log_2 x$ is the inverse of $g$, which is all that matters for germs
at $+\infty$.

## Word functions for Baumslag's group

To an element $w$ of the free group on $a, b$ with
reduced word $l_1 l_2 \cdots l_n$ attach the composite
$\varphi_w = \varphi_{l_1} \circ \varphi_{l_2} \circ \cdots \circ \varphi_{l_n}$, where
$\varphi_l$ is the function attached to the letter $l$; the first letter is applied last, and
$\varphi_1$ is the identity. With this order products of germs are composites, and $f(x) = 2x$
and $g(x) = 2^x$ satisfy the defining relation of Baumslag's group as germs at $+\infty$.

## Faithfulness of Baumslag's germ representation (Kourovka 17.99)

Let $B$ be Baumslag's
group $\langle a, b \mid (bab^{-1})\, a\, (bab^{-1})^{-1} = a^2 \rangle$, let $f(x) = 2x$ and
$g(x) = 2^x$, and for $w$ in the free group on $a, b$ let $\varphi_w$ be its word function, the
composite of $f$, $g$ and their inverses along the reduced word of $w$. The representation
$a \mapsto f$, $b \mapsto g$ of $B$ by germs at $+\infty$ is faithful when every $w$ with
$\varphi_w(x) = x$ for all sufficiently large real $x$ is trivial in $B$.

This is Olshanskii's question whether the representation of $B$ in the group of germs at $+\infty$
of increasing continuous functions on $(0, \infty)$ is faithful.

## Baumslag's germ representation is not faithful

Let $B$ be Baumslag's group
$\langle a, b \mid (bab^{-1})\, a\, (bab^{-1})^{-1} = a^2 \rangle$, let $f(x) = 2x$ and
$g(x) = 2^x$, and for $w$ in the free group on $a, b$ with reduced word $l_1 l_2 \cdots l_k$ let
$\varphi_w = \varphi_{l_1} \circ \varphi_{l_2} \circ \cdots \circ \varphi_{l_k}$, where
$\varphi_a = f$, $\varphi_{a^{-1}}(x) = x/2$, $\varphi_b = g$ and $\varphi_{b^{-1}}(x) = \log_2 x$.
The representation $a \mapsto f$, $b \mapsto g$ of $B$ by germs at $+\infty$ is not faithful: there
is an element $w$ of the free group on $a, b$ such that $\varphi_w(x) = x$ for all sufficiently
large real $x$, but the image of $w$ in $B$ is not trivial.
