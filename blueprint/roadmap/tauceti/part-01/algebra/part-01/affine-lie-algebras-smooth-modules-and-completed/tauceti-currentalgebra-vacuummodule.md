# TauCeti.CurrentAlgebra.VacuumModule

The **vacuum module** `V = U / I₀` of the residue central extension of `Φ` at the central
value `a`: the module induced from the character of `L[[t]] ⊕ R K` which kills the nonnegative
currents and sends `K` to `a`. It is the quotient of the left regular module of
`U = U(ĝ) / (ι K - a)` by the left ideal `I₀` generated by the currents of order at least `0`.
