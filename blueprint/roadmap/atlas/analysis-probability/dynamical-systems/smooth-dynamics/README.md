# Smooth dynamics

28 results of ATLAS's vault, 22 proved and 6 stated but not proved, in this branch's topics.

Smooth dynamics studies differentiable maps and flows: hyperbolicity, stable manifolds and shadowing, Lyapunov exponents and entropy, Hamiltonian perturbation theory, and stability of flows given by differential equations.
