# Tau Ceti

[Tau Ceti](https://taucetiproject.org/), read at commit [a6027b1d](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/a6027b1d24fcb935c53bd5b50d11b40991cda418) (2026-11-11), with its roadmaps at [9f7c032a](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/9f7c032adaf1b838e8183d63ff3cfa886d061cb3): 3,608 articles on 4,865 of its declarations, in parts by the library's top-level areas. 2,019 are the declarations a roadmap's status names as a result or a notable definition. A model writes the statuses and names at most five results in each, so the other 2,846 are results Tau Ceti's own docstrings name: a theorem whose docstring opens with a bold title naming a result after a person ("Burnside's pᵃqᵇ theorem", "Schur-Weyl duality", "Hille–Yosida"), a title such as "The cellular approximation theorem", or a short title pinned to a textbook theorem; a definition that carries such a theorem as data; and the four theorems Tau Ceti's website shows. The forms of one result in one area share an article. The 6 names the statuses give that Tau Ceti does not have at this commit have no article. A roadmap's plan, its suggested signatures and its frontier say what Tau Ceti may add, so they are left out, and so is the statuses' prose: a model writes it, and Tau Ceti does not check it.

Each of the 186 roadmaps whose status names a declaration has a section, in the part where most of its articles are. It holds what the status names and the other named results near them: those in a module where no other roadmap's status names a declaration, or else in a folder of modules below the areas where this status names at least 2 declarations and no other names any. The other named results are in 138 sections by area, a module's first two levels under `TauCeti`; an area with fewer than 3 of them joins its part's *Other results*, and a part with fewer than 10 articles joins *Other Areas*.

Each article's text is its declaration's docstring and signature, under Tau Ceti's Apache-2.0 license; a docstring that opens with a bold title, or with bold words its first sentence goes on from, gives the article its title. Dependencies come from Tau Ceti's declarations (`lean/tauceti/TauCetiDeps.lean`): under *Depends on*, the articles that a statement reaches through the statements of declarations not mapped here, and under *Proof depends on*, those a proof reaches through any chain of Tau Ceti declarations not mapped here. A theorem or definition that reaches `sorry`, itself or through a declaration mapped here, says so, and one stated from a proposition Tau Ceti defines says what it assumes.
