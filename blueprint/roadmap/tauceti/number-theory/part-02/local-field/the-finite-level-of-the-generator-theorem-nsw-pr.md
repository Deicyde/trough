# The finite level of the generator theorem (NSW, proof of (7.4.1))

For a finite
extension `K` of `ℚ_p`, every finite quotient of `G_K` through which the commutator subgroup of
wild inertia dies is generated by `[K : ℚ_p] + 2` elements. The generators form a tuple, in which
repetitions are allowed: the quotient may have fewer than `[K : ℚ_p] + 2` elements.

```lean
TauCeti.exists_generating_tuple_quotient (p : ℕ) [Fact (Nat.Prime p)] (K : Type) [Field K]
  [ValuativeRel K] [TopologicalSpace K] [IsNonarchimedeanLocalField K] [Algebra ℚ_[p] K]
  [Module.Finite ℚ_[p] K] [ValuativeExtension ℚ_[p] K]
  (U : OpenNormalSubgroup (Field.absoluteGaloisGroup K))
  (hU : ⁅TauCeti.wildInertiaSubgroup K, TauCeti.wildInertiaSubgroup K⁆ ≤ ↑U.toOpenSubgroup) :
  ∃ x, Subgroup.closure (Set.range x) = ⊤
```
