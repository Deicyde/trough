# Composition Algebras over Commutative Rings

> Develops conic algebras, Cayley--Dickson doubling, and finitely generated projective composition algebras over an arbitrary commutative ring, following Garibaldi, Petersson and Racine. Composition algebras are alternative over every base. Over a nontrivial base, if the underlying module has constant rank, that rank is 1, 2, 4 or 8. Over LG rings, a class containing all fields and semilocal rings, composition algebras are classified by their norms, and reduced composition algebras are shown to be split. Along the way the project proves Witt cancellation for quadratic forms over LG rings by the elementary Dias--Baeza--Knebusch route, Artin's theorem that a ring is alternative if and only if its two-generated subrings are associative, freeness of constant-rank projective modules over LG rings, and the classification of composition algebras over the reals and over finite fields. These main results do not assume that 2 is invertible.
