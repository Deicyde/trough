# VerticalIntegral

Let $f$ be a function from $\mathbb{C}$ to $\mathbb{C}$, and let $\sigma$ be a real number. Then we define

$$
\int_{(\sigma)}f(s)ds =
\int_{\sigma-i\infty}^{\sigma+i\infty}f(s)ds.
$$
