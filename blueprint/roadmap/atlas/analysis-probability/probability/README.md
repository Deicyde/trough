# Probability

2,690 results of ATLAS's vault, 2,604 proved and 86 stated but not proved, in this subject's topics.

Probability studies random phenomena through distributions, expectation, independence, concentration, and stochastic processes.

The subject starts from limit theorems for sums of independent variables.
[Laws of large numbers](part-01/laws-of-large-numbers/README.md) give almost-sure convergence, with the
[strong law](part-01/laws-of-large-numbers/strong-law-of-large-numbers.md) and Kolmogorov's three-series theorem, and
[central limit theorems](part-01/central-limit-theorems/README.md) give convergence in law, with the
[Berry–Esseen theorem](part-01/central-limit-theorems/berry-esseen-theorem.md) bounding the error by
$C\rho/(\sigma^3\sqrt{n})$. [Markov chains](part-01/markov-chains/README.md) include the convergence theorem for finite
irreducible aperiodic chains, and [stochastic calculus](part-01/stochastic-calculus/README.md) has the
[Itô integral](part-01/stochastic-calculus/it-integral.md) and its isometry.

[Percolation](part-01/percolation/README.md) is the largest field, with about an eighth of the articles. For Bernoulli bond
percolation on $\mathbb{Z}^d$ with $d \ge 2$ the critical probability lies strictly between $0$ and $1$
([phase transition](part-01/percolation/phase-transition/README.md)); below it, the probability that the origin is connected
to distance $n$ decays exponentially in $n$ ([sharpness](part-01/percolation/sharpness/README.md)); and on $\mathbb{Z}^2$ at
$p = 1/2$ the [Russo–Seymour–Welsh theorem](part-01/percolation/crossings/README.md) keeps the probability of crossing a
rectangle of fixed aspect ratio away from $0$ and $1$. [Correlation inequalities](part-01/correlation-inequalities/README.md)
supply the [Harris–FKG and van den Berg–Kesten inequalities](part-01/correlation-inequalities/harris-fkg-bk/README.md) and
[Reimer's inequality](part-01/correlation-inequalities/reimer-inequality/README.md), and
[high-dimensional probability](part-01/high-dimensional-probability/README.md) has bounds on the
[KLS constant](part-01/high-dimensional-probability/kannan-lov-sz-simonovits-conjecture.md) of log-concave laws.

[Distributions](part-01/distributions/README.md), the second largest field, works out the moments, mass functions and tails of
named laws. About a sixth of the subject is statistics: [hypothesis testing](part-02/hypothesis-testing/README.md),
[linear models and multivariate analysis](part-02/linear-models-multivariate/README.md),
[statistical estimation](part-01/statistical-estimation/README.md), [Bayesian statistics](part-02/bayesian-statistics/README.md)
around the Dirichlet process, and [nonparametric and survival methods](part-02/nonparametric-survival/README.md) such as the
Kaplan–Meier estimator and the Cox proportional hazards model.
