# Minimax risk

The minimax risk of $(P, y, \ell')$ is $\mathcal R^* = \inf_{\hat{y} : \mathcal X \rightsquigarrow \mathcal Z} \sup_{\theta \in \Theta} r^P_\theta(\hat{y})$ .
