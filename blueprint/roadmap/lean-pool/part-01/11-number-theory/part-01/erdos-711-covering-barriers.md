# Cauchy–Schwarz covering barriers for Erdős problem 711

> Proves two lower bounds for the divisor-multiplicity sums in the three-path reflection method. For sufficiently large N, the results hold for every finite cover of (N/2, N] and give exponential corrections with exponents two thirds and one half, respectively.
