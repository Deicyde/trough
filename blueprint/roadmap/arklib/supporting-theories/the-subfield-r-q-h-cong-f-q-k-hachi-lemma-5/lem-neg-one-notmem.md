# $-1 \notin \langle q\rangle$

For $q \equiv 5 \pmod 8$ and $\alpha \ge 1$, no power of $q$ equals $-1$ in $\mathbb{Z}/2^{\alpha+1}$ (every power is $\equiv 1 \pmod 4$, while $-1 \equiv 3$).
