It could seem more natural to take $[0, 1] × E$ as the source of a homotopy but this would be less convenient for formalization and wouldn't change anything since any map from $ℝ × E$ can be restricted to $[0, 1] × E$ and every map from $[0, 1] × E$ could be extended.
