# formal_sol_loc

A formal solution of a differential relation $\mathcal{R}$ is a map $\mathcal{F} = (f, φ) \!: E → F × \operatorname{Hom}(E, F)$ such that, for every $x$, $(x, f(x), φ(x))$ is in $\mathcal{R}$.
