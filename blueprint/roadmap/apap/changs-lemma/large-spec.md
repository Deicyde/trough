# Large spectrum

Let $G$ be a finite abelian group and $f:G\to\mathbb{C}$. Let $\eta\in \mathbb{R}$. The $\eta$-large spectrum is defined to be

$$
\Delta_\eta(f) = \{ \gamma\in\widehat{G} : \lvert \widehat{f}(\gamma)\rvert \geq \eta\lVert f\rVert_1\}.
$$
