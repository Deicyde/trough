# Detyped conditionally linear functions

For $\ell\geq1$, concatenate the two graph levels with the selected type's $\ell$ levels on an independent binary seed block. On an unselected branch use the zero CL function. The result has $\ell+2$ levels and dimension $4|T|+s(n)$. Conditionally on $E_G$, the contents are exactly the typed distribution generated by one shared seed; the two question contents need not be independent.

## Depends on

- [Graph rejection sampling](lem-detype-graph.md)
- [Closure of CL functions](lem-cl-closure.md)

## Proof

Embed the graph and content presentations into the two summands of the ambient register and concatenate them. The zero fallback consumes the whole content register at its first level and uses empty factors thereafter, so exactness also holds on rejected branches. On an ordered edge each player's graph output is the fixed view of its own type. Reindexing the valid graph seeds by ordered edges proves the joint conditional law with one common content seed. The positive-level hypothesis covers the three-level typed sampler used by introspection.
