> **Henselization and quasi-finite ring maps** (Stacks 05WP). Let `R → S` be of finite type,
> let `q` be a prime of `S` lying over `p`, and assume `R → S` is quasi-finite at `q`. Let `Rh` and
> `Sh` be henselizations of `R_p` and `S_q`, let `Rh → Sh` be the local ring map over `R_p → S_q`
> of Lemma 04GS, and let `T = Rh ⊗_{R_p} S_q`, mapping to `Sh` through the square. Then the ideal
> `𝔪_{S_q} T` generated by `q` is prime, `Sh` is the localization of `T` at it, and `Sh` is finite
> over `Rh`.

```lean
theorem isLocalization_and_finite_of_quasiFiniteAt
    [Algebra.FiniteType R S] [Algebra.QuasiFiniteAt R q] :
    ∃ _ : ((maximalIdeal Sq).map (algebraMap Sq T)).IsPrime,
      IsLocalization.AtPrime Sh ((maximalIdeal Sq).map (algebraMap Sq T)) ∧
        Module.Finite Rh Sh
```

> **Henselization is compatible with quotients** (Stacks 05WQ), with the base and the
> henselization in independent universes. Let `(R, 𝔪, κ)` be a local ring, let `Rh` be a
> henselization of `R` and let `I ⊆ 𝔪` be an ideal. Then `Rh/I Rh` is a henselization of `R/I`,
> with respect to the residue field of `R/I`.
>
> `Rh/I Rh` is henselian because a quotient of a henselian local ring by a proper ideal is
> (`Stacks.Henselian.henselianLocalRing_quotient`); it is ind-étale over `R/I` by
> `IsLocalRing.IsHenselization.indEtale_quotient`; and its residue field is that of `R/I` because
> the two quotient maps `R → R/I` and `Rh → Rh/I Rh` are surjective local maps, hence
> isomorphisms on residue fields, and the third side of the square is the identification carried by
> the henselization.

```lean
theorem quotient_of_le_maximalIdeal [IsHenselization R (ResidueField R) Rh]
    {I : Ideal R} (hI : I ≤ maximalIdeal R) :
    letI : IsLocalRing (R ⧸ I) := isLocalRing_quotient_of_le_maximalIdeal I hI
    letI : IsLocalRing (Rh ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R Rh)) :=
      isLocalRing_quotient_of_le_maximalIdeal (I.map (algebraMap R Rh))
        ((Ideal.map_mono hI).trans (map_maximalIdeal (R := R) (L := ResidueField R)).le)
    IsHenselization (R ⧸ I) (ResidueField (R ⧸ I)) (Rh ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R Rh))
```

> **Strict henselization is compatible with quotients** (Stacks 05WS). Let `(R, 𝔪, κ)` be a
> local ring and let `R → Rsh` be a strict henselization of `R`, that is: `Rsh` is a henselian local
> ring whose residue field is a separable algebraic closure `κ^{sep}` of `κ`, it is a filtered
> colimit of étale `R`-algebras, and `𝔪 Rsh` is its maximal ideal. Let `I ⊆ 𝔪` be an ideal. Then
> `Rsh/I Rsh` is a strict henselization of `R/I`, for the induced `R/I`-algebra structure: it is a
> henselian local ring, it is a filtered colimit of étale `R/I`-algebras, the ideal generated by the
> image of `𝔪/I` is its maximal ideal, and its residue field is again a separable algebraic closure
> of `κ`.

```lean

    [CommRing Rsh] [IsLocalRing R] [Algebra R Rsh] [HenselianLocalRing Rsh]
    [IsLocalHom (algebraMap R Rsh)] [IsSepClosure (ResidueField R) (ResidueField Rsh)]
    (hind : (CommAlgCat.etale.{u} R).ind.{u} (CommAlgCat.of R Rsh))
    (hmax : (maximalIdeal R).map (algebraMap R Rsh) = maximalIdeal Rsh)
    (I : Ideal R) (hI : I ≤ maximalIdeal R) :

      (maximalIdeal (R ⧸ I)).map (algebraMap (R ⧸ I) (Rsh ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R Rsh))) =
        maximalIdeal (Rsh ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R Rsh)) ∧
      IsSepClosure (ResidueField (R ⧸ I))
        (ResidueField (Rsh ⧸ I.map (algebraMap R Rsh)))
```
