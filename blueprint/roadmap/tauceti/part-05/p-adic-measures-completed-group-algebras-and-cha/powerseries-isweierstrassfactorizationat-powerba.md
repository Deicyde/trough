# PowerSeries.IsWeierstrassFactorizationAt.powerBasis

For a Weierstrass factorization `g = f * h`, the power basis of `A⟦X⟧ ⧸ (g)` over `A`
generated by the class of `X`: the classes of `1, X, …, X ^ (deg f - 1)` form a basis. It is the
monic power basis of `A[X] ⧸ (f)` transported along `algEquivQuotient`.

```lean
PowerSeries.IsWeierstrassFactorizationAt.powerBasis.{u_1} {A : Type u_1} [CommRing A] {I : Ideal A}
  {g : PowerSeries A} {f : Polynomial A} {h : PowerSeries A}
  (H : g.IsWeierstrassFactorizationAt f h I) [IsAdicComplete I A] :
  PowerBasis A (PowerSeries A ⧸ Ideal.span {g})
```
