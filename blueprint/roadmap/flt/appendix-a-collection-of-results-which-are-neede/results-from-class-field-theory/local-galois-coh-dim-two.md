# "the dimension is 2"

If $M$ is torsion then $H^i(G_K,M)=0$ if $i>2$.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

This follows from Proposition 15 in section 5.3 of \[Serre 2002\].
