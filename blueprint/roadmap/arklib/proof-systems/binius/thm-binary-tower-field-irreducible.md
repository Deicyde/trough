# Irreducible defining polynomial

The defining polynomial $X_{\iota-1}^{2}+X_{\iota-2} \cdot X_{\iota-1}+1$ of $\mathcal{T}_{\iota}$ is irreducible over $\mathcal{T}_{\iota-1}$ for all $\iota > 0$.
