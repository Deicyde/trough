## Tag 07LA, a lemma

[Tag 07LA](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/07LA) in the Stacks Project.

**`Crystalline.id_completedTensorCompletedForms_homotopic_zero`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Crystalline/Cosimplicial/CompletedForms.lean, line 534](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Crystalline/Cosimplicial/CompletedForms.lean#L534).

> **Stacks Project, Tag 07LA.** With the notation of the equations (07JF) for `D(n)` and
> (07L0) for `Ω_{D(n)}`, given any cosimplicial module `M_*` over `D(*)` and any `i > 0`, the
> cosimplicial module
>
> `M_0 ⊗̂_{D(0)} Ω^i_{D(0)} → M_1 ⊗̂_{D(1)} Ω^i_{D(1)} → M_2 ⊗̂_{D(2)} Ω^i_{D(2)} → ⋯`
>
> is homotopic to zero, where `Ω^i_{D(n)}` is the `p`-adic completion of the `i`-th exterior power
> of `Ω_{D(n)}` and `⊗̂` is the `p`-adic completion of the tensor product.
>
> By Tag 07L9 the identity and the zero endomorphism of `Ω_{D(*)}` are homotopic.  Applying the
> functor `∧^i` keeps them homotopic, and for `i > 0` turns the zero endomorphism into the zero
> endomorphism, so the identity of `∧^i Ω_{D(*)}` is homotopic to zero; the `p`-adic completion
> functor then gives the same for `Ω^i_{D(*)}`, tensoring with `M_*` gives it for
> `M_* ⊗_{D(*)} Ω^i_{D(*)}`, and a final `p`-adic completion finishes the proof.

```lean
public theorem id_completedTensorCompletedForms_homotopic_zero
    (E : CosimplicialEnvelope s f hf) (M : CosimplicialModule E.ring) {i : ℕ} (hi : 0 < i) :
    Homotopic (𝟙 ((pAdicCosimplicialIdeal E.ring s.p).completion
      (M ⊗ (pAdicCosimplicialIdeal E.ring s.p).completion
        ((exteriorPowerFunctor E.ring i).functor.obj E.differentials)))) 0
```
