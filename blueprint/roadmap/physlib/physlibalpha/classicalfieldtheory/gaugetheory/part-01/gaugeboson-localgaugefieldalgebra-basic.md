# The local gauge field algebra of a gauge theory

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra/Basic.lean): 6 declarations, 4 with a docstring.

> The jet algebra of the gauge bosons, generated by the component functions `∂_s A_μ^φ`.

## i. Overview

> The gauge bosons of a gauge theory with Lie algebra `G.lieSubalgebra` are jointly one bosonic field
> valued in `Lorentz.CoVector ⊗[ℝ] G.lieSubalgebra`, the target space `GaugeBoson G` of
> `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.Basic`. Its *local gauge field
> algebra* `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra G`, the jet algebra in which the gauge-boson part of a
> Lagrangian lives, is the free commutative algebra on the component functions `∂_s A_μ^φ`. No
> basis of the gauge algebra is chosen. The adjoint index is carried by an abstract covector
> `φ : Module.Dual ℝ G.lieSubalgebra` throughout, following the dual-family formulation of the
> adjoint families in the directory
> `PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily`.
> For the Standard Model, `G.lieSubalgebra` is `StandardModel.GaugeAlgebra`.
>
> The files of this directory are the following.
> 1. This file gives the jet algebra with its generators, over the target space and the
>    field-derivative space of `GaugeBoson.Basic`.
> 2. `LorentzAction` gives the action of the Lorentz group.
> 3. `GaugeAction` gives the action of the jet gauge group.
> 4. `JetDeriv` gives the formal total derivative.
> 5. `MassDim` and `MassWeightPoly` give the mass-dimension grading.
> 6. `GaugeField` gives the gauge-field symbols and their laws.
> 7. `FieldStrength` gives the field strength and its covariant derivatives.

## ii. Key results

> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra` : the local gauge field algebra, the jet algebra of the gauge
>   bosons.
> - `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.ofComponent`, `LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra.ofA` : the generators.

## A. The jet algebra

**`LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra`**, an abbrev, [line 78](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/LocalGaugeFieldAlgebra/Basic.lean#L78).

> The local gauge field algebra, the jet algebra of the gauge bosons. It is the free
> commutative algebra on the component functions `∂_s A_μ^φ` of the gauge-boson field,
> realized as the symmetric algebra on the field-derivative space. The commutativity of the
> product is the Bose statistics of the gauge fields.
