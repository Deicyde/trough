# local inv

Let $G$ be a finite cyclic group of order $n$ generated by an element $\mathrm{gen}$. Then the map

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathrm{inv}_G : H^2(G,{\mathbb Z}) & \to {\mathbb Z}/n{\mathbb Z} \\
\sigma & \mapsto \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} \sigma(\mathrm{gen}^i,\mathrm{gen})
\end{aligned}
$$

is called the *local invariant* of $G$.
