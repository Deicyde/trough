# pSaturated

A subgroup $H\le E$ is *$p$-saturated* in $E$ when

$$
H\cap E^p\subseteq H^p,
$$

that is, when every element of $H$ that is a $p$th power in $E$ is already the $p$th power of an element of $H$.
