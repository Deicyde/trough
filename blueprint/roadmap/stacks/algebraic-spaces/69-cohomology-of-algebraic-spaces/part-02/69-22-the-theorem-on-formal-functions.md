> morphism of algebraic spaces and `F` a coherent `𝒪_X`-module.  Then for every `p ≥ 0` the
> canonical map `Hᵖ(X, F) ⟶ lim_n Hᵖ(X, F / Iⁿ F)` is bijective, i.e.
> `Hᵖ(X, F) = lim_n Hᵖ(X, F / Iⁿ F)`.
>
