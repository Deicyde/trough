## Integrable processes

Integrable -$>$ content on the ring of sets generated by predictable rectangles. Can be extended to a measure on the predictable $\sigma$-algebra if the process is of class DL and ... (see Metivier, Semimartingales, 13.3).

Let $X : T \to \Omega \to E$ be a stochastic process such that $X_t$ is integrable for all $t$ . For $s < t$ and $A \in \mathcal{F}_s$, let

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_X((s, t] \times A) = \mathbb{E}[\mathbb{1}_A (X_t - X_s)]
\: .
\end{aligned}
$$

Set $\mu_X(\{0\} \times A) = 0$ . Then $\mu_X$ is a content on the predictable rectangles, which can be extended to the ring of sets it generates.
