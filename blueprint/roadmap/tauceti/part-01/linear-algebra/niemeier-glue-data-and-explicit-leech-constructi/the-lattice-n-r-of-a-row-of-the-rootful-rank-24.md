# The lattice `N_r` of a row of the rootful rank-24 table

the even overlattice of the
orthogonal sum `R_r` of the component root lattices glued along the glue subgroup `H_r`, which is
generated by the decoded Conway--Sloane glue words of the row.

```lean
TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType.lattice (r : TauCeti.RootfulNiemeierType) :
  TauCeti.IntegralLattice ((i : Fin r.components.length) → Fin (r.componentType i).rank → ℚ)
```
