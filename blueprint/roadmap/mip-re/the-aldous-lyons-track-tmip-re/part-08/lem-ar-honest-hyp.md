# The honest PCPs' hypotheses at the parameters

Let the circuit of a seed be the window describer's description of the output indicator at the constant parameters $(\ell, \Delta)$, through the windows $\ell + 1, \ell + 1, o + 1$, at $(n, T, Q, \sigma')$, padded to $s$ gates. If $E_1 \geq 2E$ and $c_0$ is the constant of Lemma [The output indicator at constant parameters](lem-lstar-const.md), then for every input tailored verifier within the input budget of answer reduction at $n$, with $|\mathsf{V}| \leq \sigma$ and $\lambda, \mu \geq 1$, the hypotheses of Definition [The honest answers](../part-06/def-ar-honest-answers.md) hold at the description time $T$.

## Proof

The input's lengths are at most $2^Q = 2^\ell$. On readable answers, of at most the readable lengths, its processor runs within $2^Q 2^{(Q + 4)\mu}$ steps, so it outputs at most that many constraints, and $2^\Delta$ holds them. The describer's validity holds: $Q$ and $2 \log n$ are below $T$, the output indicator's program is at most $\sigma'$ long, and the questions have at most $Q$ bits; the windows fit $T$, and $r \geq K + 2$ since $4T \leq 2^r$. On accepted inputs the honest strings have lengths $2^\ell + 2^o$ and $2^\ell$, so the output indicator accepts them within $2^{E(\mu + 1)(Q + o + 6)} \leq T$.
