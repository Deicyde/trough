# StrongZeroFree

There is a constant $c>0$, so that $\zeta(s)$ does not vanish in the region $\sigma>1-\frac{c}{\log t}$, and moreover,

$$
-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(\sigma+it) \ll (\log t)^2
$$

there.

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof
