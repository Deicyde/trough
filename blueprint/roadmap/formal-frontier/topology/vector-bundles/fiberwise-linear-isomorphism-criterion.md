# Fiberwise linear isomorphism criterion

Over a nontrivially normed field, a family of fiberwise algebraic linear equivalences between two vector bundles on the same topological base induces a base-preserving homeomorphism of their original total spaces whenever its total-space map is continuous, the source normed model is finite-dimensional and the target normed model has the module topology.
