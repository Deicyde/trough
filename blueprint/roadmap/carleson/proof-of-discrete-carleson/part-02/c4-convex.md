# C4 convex

For each $k,n,j$, the collection ${\mathfrak C}_4(k,n,j)$ is convex.

## Depends on

- [C3 convex](c3-convex.md)

## Proof

The proof is entirely analogous to [C2 convex](../part-01/c2-convex.md), substituting ${\mathfrak C}_4$ for ${\mathfrak C}_2$, ${\mathfrak C}_3$ for ${\mathfrak C}_1$ and ${\mathfrak p}'\le{\mathfrak p}''$ for ${\mathfrak p}\le{\mathfrak p}'$.
