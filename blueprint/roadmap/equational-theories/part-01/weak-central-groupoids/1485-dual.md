# 1485 equivalent to 2162

[E1485](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?1485) is equivalent to the dual law

$$
x = ((y \diamond z) \diamond x) \diamond (x \diamond y)\qquad\text{(2)}
$$

(equation 2162).

## Depends on

- [Equation 1485](../selected-laws/part-02/eq1485.md)

## Proof

It suffices to prove that [(1)](README.md) implies (2). Write $w = y \diamond z$, then from [(1)](README.md) we can write $z = z_1 \diamond z_2$ with $z_1 = z \diamond z$ and $z_2 = z \diamond (z \diamond z)$, and then by [(1)](README.md)

$$
y = (z_2 \diamond y) \diamond (y \diamond (z_1 \diamond z_2)) = (z_2 \diamond y) \diamond w.
$$

From another application of [(1)](README.md) we have

$$
x = (w \diamond x) \diamond (x \diamond ((z_2 \diamond y) \diamond w)) = ((y \diamond z) \diamond x) \diamond (x \diamond y)
$$

as required.
