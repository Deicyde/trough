# Hairy-ball theorem for the metric two-sphere

Every continuous map V from the unit sphere in EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3) to EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3) satisfying ⟨p, V(p)⟩ = 0 at every sphere point p vanishes somewhere.
