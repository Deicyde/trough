# Dilatations of categories and commutative rings

> Constructs categorical dilatations from sieves of permitted numerators and denominator morphisms, and proves their universal property for Sigma-regular functors satisfying the sieve containment condition. Develops restriction and union of centers, dual codilatations, and ordinary localization as a special case. The composition isomorphism retains the explicit Sigma-regularity hypothesis left open in the original paper. For commutative rings, an exponent-profile center recovers the ring dilatation; the naive single-index comparison is refuted over Z[X]. A finite category example refutes a general intermediate-category characterization. Independent ring modules provide fraction arithmetic, a universal property, reindexing, and the unique isomorphism for two-stage dilatations.
