# The main theorem

The statement below is the formalization target of the whole project. It matches, definition for definition, the Lean file `MIPRE/HaltingGameValue.lean`, which states it on top of Mathlib alone (theorem `halting_reduces_to_gameValue`): Turing machines are Mathlib's `Nat.Partrec.Code`, games are game descriptions (Definition [Game description](../foundations/part-02/def-game-description.md)), and the value is the synchronous value (Definition [Synchronous value](../foundations/part-01/def-sync-value.md)).

- [Halting reduces to the game value; Theorem 12.2 of (JNVWY20), synchronous form](thm-main.md)

*Status.* The theorem is proved in Lean, with no hypothesis: `HaltingGameValue.halting_reduces_to_gameValue` (`MIPRE/MainTheorem.lean`) proves the proposition `HaltingGameValue.HaltingReducesToGameValue`, which `MIPRE/HaltingGameValue.lean` states on top of Mathlib alone. The proof cannot live in that file, which imports nothing else; it is the conditional `MIPRE.Halting.halting_reduces_to_gameValue_of` at the compression of Theorem [Ambient compression inhabitation target](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-compression-target.md), built from the supplied introspection, answer-reduction and repetition stages (Corollary [Compression, and $\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$, from answer reduction alone](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/cor-compression-from-answer-reduction.md)), and it depends on the axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` only.

Theorem [Halting reduces to the game value; Theorem 12.2 of (JNVWY20), synchronous form](thm-main.md) reads the computable explicit-game conclusion of Theorem [Halting reduction](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-halting.md) in the synchronous value. Its construction tabulates a normal form verifier at a fixed index. The source's polynomial-time reduction constructs sampler and decider programs; passing to the explicit finite table establishes computability, with no polynomial bound on enumeration asserted (Section [Departures from the paper](../introduction/README.md)). A game description rejects unequal answers to equal questions by fiat (Definition [Game description](../foundations/part-02/def-game-description.md)), which costs nothing here: against a tracial state a projective family is perfectly consistent, so the terms the fiat deletes carry no weight in ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$, which is unchanged, and it can only lower $\mathrm{val}^*$ — the safe direction for the soundness clause, while the completeness witness is synchronous and survives. The two passages this needs from Theorem [Halting reduction](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-halting.md), whose Lean assembly concludes in $\mathrm{val}^*$, are formalized: the transport of a value-$1$ PCC strategy along the relabelings of the tabulation (`MIPRE.Verifier.gameValue_toGame_eq_one_doubled`, through `MIPRE.syncValue_eq_of_equiv`) for completeness, and `MIPRE.Verifier.gameValue_toGame_le_of_valStar_le_doubled` for soundness; both are stated directly in `HaltingGameValue.gameValue`, by Remark The standalone restatement's identification of the two vocabularies, and both are read against the *doubled* game of $\mathsf{V}_n$, which is how the tabulation matches it without asking the verifier to be synchronous (Remark [Relation to the abstract compression lemma](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md), item 3). On the tabulation the completeness bridge is `MIPRE.Halting.gameValue_tab_eq_one`. The four obligations of Theorem [Halting reduction](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-halting.md) are all discharged (`MIPRE.Halting.exists_obligations`), so nothing but Theorem [Ambient compression inhabitation target](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-compression-target.md) stands between the conditional and the unconditional statement. The record of the tabulation's agreement with $\mathsf{V}_n$ in the form the completeness bridge consumes is `MIPRE.Halting.tab_match` (Remark [Relation to the abstract compression lemma](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md)), and `MIPRE.Halting.halting_reduction_both_of` is the one map carrying both readings of the value at once, which is what Corollary [Halting reduces to the quantum value](cor-main-quantum.md) asks.

**Remark (The standalone restatement).** The Lean main statement is deliberately self-contained: the file `MIPRE/HaltingGameValue.lean` imports nothing from the rest of this development, re-declaring the synchronous game, the synchronous strategy and the value on top of Mathlib alone, so that Theorem [Halting reduces to the game value; Theorem 12.2 of (JNVWY20), synchronous form](thm-main.md) can be read without any of the vocabulary built in Section [Foundations](../foundations/README.md). In that reduced vocabulary `gameValue` is the synchronous value of Definition [Synchronous value](../foundations/part-01/def-sync-value.md) and `equivTuple` exhibits the canonical Gödel numbering asserted in Definition [Game description](../foundations/part-02/def-game-description.md) as an explicit equivalence between game descriptions and a tuple of integers and lists, which is what supplies the `Encodable` instance the computability clause quantifies over. The duplication is the price of a statement that stands alone, and keeping the two vocabularies in step is a maintenance obligation rather than a mathematical one: the definitions match one for one, and this is the list of the ones that do. The correspondence is not left to inspection where it is used: `GameData.syncGame` and `GameData.game` read a description in the vocabulary of Section [Foundations](../foundations/README.md) with the same distribution and predicate, and `GameData.gameValue_eq_syncValue` proves the two synchronous values equal — the two structures of synchronous strategy, one carrying `POVM`s with a projectivity certificate and the other a `ProjectiveMeasurement`, repackage into each other with the same dimension and the same operators, so the two suprema are over the same set of numbers. `GameData.gameValue_le_quantumValue` is the comparison ${\mathrm{val}^{s}} \leq \mathrm{val}^*$ (Lemma [Synchronous value lower-bounds the quantum value](../foundations/part-01/lem-sync-le-valstar.md)) in that vocabulary.

- [Halting reduces to the quantum value](cor-main-quantum.md)

Both forms come from the two items of Theorem [Halting reduction](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-halting.md) directly: the value-$1$ witness is an ambient synchronous PCC strategy, so it gives value $1$ in both senses; and the soundness conclusion is $\mathrm{val}^*(\mathfrak{G}_\mathcal{M}) \leq \frac12$, which gives ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}(\mathfrak{G}_\mathcal{M}) \leq \frac12$ by Lemma [Synchronous value lower-bounds the quantum value](../foundations/part-01/lem-sync-le-valstar.md). The quantum form is the one Definition [The computable class](../foundations/part-03/def-mipstar-computable.md) asks for, and it is the primary one: no transport from ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$ to $\mathrm{val}^*$ is involved anywhere, the pipeline's soundness being carried in $\mathrm{val}^*$ at every step (Remark [Why the pipeline is bipartite throughout](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md)). The synchronous reading of Theorem [Halting reduces to the game value; Theorem 12.2 of (JNVWY20), synchronous form](thm-main.md) is the derived one, obtained from this corollary by the free comparison ${\mathrm{val}^{s}} \leq \mathrm{val}^*$.
