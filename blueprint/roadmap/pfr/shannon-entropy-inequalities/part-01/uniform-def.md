# Uniform distribution

If $H$ is a subset of $S$, an $S$-random variable $X$ is said to be uniformly distributed on $H$ if $\mathbb{P}[X = s] = 1/|H|$ for $s \in X$ and $\mathbb{P}[X=s] = 0$ otherwise.
