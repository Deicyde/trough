# elemStochIntegralLinear

Let $E$ and $F$ be normed real vector spaces. We call *linear elementary stochastic integral* the general elementary stochastic integral of Definition [Elementary stochastic integral](def-elemstochintegralbilin.md) with $V$ being valued in $L(E, F)$-valued and $B(L, x) = L(x)$ the evaluation map. We denote it by $V \bullet_L X$, or just $V \bullet X$ when $L$ is clear from the context.
