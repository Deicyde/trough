# concreteKummerLogMatrix

The *Kummer logarithm matrix* $M$ is the $r \times r$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_p$ whose $a$th column consists of the coordinates, in the Dwork basis of the relevant fixed subalgebra, of the completed $p$-adic logarithm of the generator $\varepsilon_a$, reduced modulo $p$.
