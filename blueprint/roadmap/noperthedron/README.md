# Noperthedron

[jcreedcmu/Noperthedron](https://github.com/jcreedcmu/Noperthedron) says in its README:

> This project formally proves that the Noperthedron of ["A convex polyhedron without Rupert's property"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.18475) by Jakob Steininger & Sergey Yurkevich (`[SY25]`) does not "[fit through itself](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_Rupert%27s_cube)".

Its blueprint, by David Renshaw and Jason Reed, read at commit [0143f65b](https://github.com/jcreedcmu/Noperthedron/tree/0143f65be7f59b3ac09b99debb9c37944a0a2f4b) (2026-11-24): one article for each of its 66 nodes whose Lean declarations it names and marks formalized, or in Mathlib. Its other 1 node has no article, and a section of only those is left out: the map records what the project contains, not what it plans. A statement for which the blueprint marks no proof `\leanok` says so. Dependencies are the blueprint's `\uses`: a statement's under *Depends on*, a proof's under *Proof depends on*.

The text is the blueprint's, under its Apache-2.0 license: each page has a section's prose, remarks and examples in the blueprint's order, with each node's statement and proof where the node stands.
