# Binary observables

An *observable* in a star-algebra is a self-adjoint element squaring to $1$. Binary projective measurements and observables are two views of the same data: $\{P_0, P_1\}$ gives the observable $P_0 - P_1$, and an observable $O$ gives the measurement $\{\tfrac12(1 + O), \tfrac12(1 - O)\}$.
