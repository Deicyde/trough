## The braid group $B_4$

The braid group on four strands, given by Artin's presentation: the
group generated by $\sigma_1, \sigma_2, \sigma_3$ subject to the relations
$\sigma_1 \sigma_2 \sigma_1 = \sigma_2 \sigma_1 \sigma_2$, $\sigma_2 \sigma_3 \sigma_2 =
\sigma_3 \sigma_2 \sigma_3$ and $\sigma_1 \sigma_3 = \sigma_3 \sigma_1$.

## Bardakov's question on embedding $B_4$ in $\operatorname{Aut}(F_2)$

The statement that
there is an injective group homomorphism from the braid group $B_4$ on four strands to the
automorphism group $\operatorname{Aut}(F_2)$ of the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$. Kourovka
Problem 17.14 (V. G. Bardakov) asks whether, for $n \ge 4$, the braid group $B_n$ embeds in the
automorphism group of the free group $F_{n-2}$ of rank $n - 2$; this is the case $n = 4$.

## Bardakov–Bellingeri: $B_4$ does not embed in $\operatorname{Aut}(F_2)$

There is no injective
group homomorphism from the braid group $B_4$ on four strands to the automorphism group
$\operatorname{Aut}(F_2)$ of the free group $F_2$ of rank $2$. Kourovka Problem 17.14
(V. G. Bardakov) asks whether, for $n \ge 4$, the braid group $B_n$ embeds in the automorphism
group of the free group $F_{n-2}$ of rank $n - 2$; this answers the case $n = 4$ negatively.
