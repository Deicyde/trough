# Classical graph colouring through lists, orientations and spectra

> Proves Kőnig's edge-colouring theorem for finite bipartite simple graphs by reusing Lean Pool's Vizing partial-colouring and Kempe-chain infrastructure. A stable-matching theorem for strict preferences and incomplete acceptability lists supplies kernels for Galvin's orientation of the line graph. This gives list edge colouring from lists of size at least the maximum degree and the Dinitz array theorem. Gallai–Hasse–Roy–Vitaver relates graph colourings to directed paths (with a bridge to Lean Pool's digraph simple-path API), while Wilf's and Hoffman's eigenvalue bounds control the chromatic and independence numbers. Two interface corollaries turn the spectral colouring bounds into directed-path bounds. Existing Vizing, matching-cover, poset and equitable-colouring results are reused or left in their established Lean Pool projects rather than copied.
