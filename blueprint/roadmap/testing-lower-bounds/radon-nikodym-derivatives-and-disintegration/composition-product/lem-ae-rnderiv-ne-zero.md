# ae_rnDeriv_ne_zero

Let $\mu, \nu \in \mathcal M(\mathcal X)$ be two $\sigma$-finite measures and let $p$ be a predicate on $\mathcal X$. If $p$ is true $\mu$-almost surely, then for $\nu$-almost all $x$, either $\frac{d\mu}{d\nu}(x) = 0$ or $p(x)$.
