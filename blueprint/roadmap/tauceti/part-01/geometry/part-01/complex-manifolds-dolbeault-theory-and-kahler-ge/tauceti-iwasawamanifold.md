# TauCeti.IwasawaManifold

The **Iwasawa manifold** `H(ℤ[i]) \ H(ℂ)`: the orbit space of the action of the deck group
`H(ℤ[i])` on `ℂ³` by `(a, b, c) • (x, y, z) = (a + x, b + y, c + z + a y)`. It is a compact
complex manifold modelled on `ℂ³`.
