## The higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture

For every fixed integer *d* ≥ 3, we prove that every set of *n* ≥ 2 distinct points in ℝ$^{d}$ determines at least $c_d n^{2/d}$ distinct distances, where $c_d\gt 0$ depends only on *d*. This resolves the higher-dimensional Erdős distinct-distances conjecture positively.
