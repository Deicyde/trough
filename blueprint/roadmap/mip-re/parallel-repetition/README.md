# Parallel repetition

This chapter states the parallel repetition theorem used for gap amplification in the compression procedure (Section [Gap amplification: parallel repetition](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md)): *direct* (unanchored) parallel repetition in value form, for entangled and for commuting-operator strategies, together with Lin's tracial density theorem. Both theorems are imported from external Lean developments vendored into this repository, and the chapter records their statements in the vocabulary of Section [Foundations](../foundations/README.md) together with the bridges connecting the two; both are formalized end to end, the entangled one through Lemma [Mixed states and POVMs do not change the quantum value](lem-povm-value-eq.md).

The *anchored*, entanglement-preserving theorem of Bavarian, Vidick and Yuen (BVY17), which (JNVWY20) uses for this step, is not stated here. Anchoring is what the entanglement form forces: (MNY)'s recursive compression lemma needs a measure $f$ with $f(\textsc{Compr}(\hat x, n)) \geq \max\{f(x), n\}$, so every transformation has to preserve $\mathrm{Ent}$, and only the anchored theorem does. The compressibility criterion of (Lin25) used here (Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md)) carries no measure at all, so value decay suffices and the repeated game may be the game as it is. Remark Direct versus anchored repetition records the comparison, and Remark [The entanglement form of (JNVWY20)](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md) what the entanglement clauses of (JNVWY20) would buy.

## Direct parallel repetition

The $n$-fold direct repetition of a game asks $n$ independent question pairs at once and accepts if all $n$ answer pairs are accepted. Two recent Lean developments prove that the value of the repeated game decays exponentially in $n$, for every game, with no anchoring and at a rate depending only on the gap $1 - \mathrm{val}(\mathfrak{G})$ and on the size of the answer alphabets: one for tensor-product (entangled) strategies, by OpenAI (OpenAI26), and one for commuting-operator strategies (Vid26), the latter building on the former and on Lin's tracial density theorem (Lin23). Both are vendored into this repository (`MIPRE/Background/Repetition/`, with provenance recorded in the `README.md` of each directory) and connected to the definitions of Section [Foundations](../foundations/README.md) by explicit bridges; the statements below are the bridged forms. The commuting-operator theorem is recorded now, although only the entangled one enters $\mathrm{MIP}^* = \mathrm{RE}$, because it is the form needed for the commuting-operator counterpart $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$ (Lin25) (Remark [further](../downstream-results/README.md)).

**Remark (Direct versus anchored repetition).** Theorems [Direct parallel repetition for entangled strategies](thm-direct-repetition-q.md) and [Direct parallel repetition for commuting-operator strategies](thm-direct-repetition-co.md) are statements about values, while the anchored theorem of (BVY17) is a statement about entanglement requirements: the anchored proof extracts from a good strategy for the repeated game a strategy for the original game of *no larger dimension*, whereas the proofs of the direct theorems round through auxiliary resources (an embezzling state in (OpenAI26), a tracial approximation in (Vid26)) and give no control of the dimension. Lin's proof of $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$ (Lin25) shows that the entanglement clauses of (JNVWY20) are dispensable: with completeness and soundness of compression stated in value form (perfect strategies are preserved, and $\mathrm{val}^*$ at most $\frac12$ is preserved, a ${\mathrm{val}^{s}}$ hypothesis side being converted by the free comparison Lemma [Synchronous value lower-bounds the quantum value](../foundations/part-01/lem-sync-le-valstar.md)), the halting reduction follows from the $\mathrm{val}^*$ half of the enumeration of Lemma [The value is approximable from below](../background-results/part-05/lem-value-lower-approx.md) and Kleene's recursion theorem alone (Lemma [Compressibility criterion](../computability/lem-compressible-criterion.md)), and gap amplification then needs only a value statement — exactly the theorems of this section, applied to the game as it is, without anchoring. The pipeline of Section [Structure of the proof](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md) is stated in that form (Section [Departures from the paper](../introduction/README.md)): Theorem [Parallel repetition of normal form verifiers](../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-parallel-repetition.md) rests on Theorem [Direct parallel repetition for entangled strategies](thm-direct-repetition-q.md), and the anchored theorem with its toolkit — the entropy inequalities, Uhlmann's theorem, the quantum Raz lemma and Holenstein conditioning that its proof needs — is not on the path to the main theorem and is no longer stated in this blueprint; the entanglement clauses are recorded in Remark [The entanglement form of (JNVWY20)](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md). The active pipeline carries bipartite soundness throughout (Remark [Why the pipeline is bipartite throughout](../structure-of-the-proof/README.md)); its completeness witnesses use the separately specified synchronous PCC interface.
