# Decomposition of an orthogonal transformation as a product of reflections

The orthogonal group of `F` is generated by reflections.

```lean
LinearIsometryEquiv.reflections_generate.{u_3} {F : Type u_3} [NormedAddCommGroup F]
  [InnerProductSpace ℝ F] [FiniteDimensional ℝ F] :
  Subgroup.closure (Set.range fun v => (ℝ ∙ v)ᗮ.reflection) = ⊤
```
