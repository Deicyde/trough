# ZHat.e_def

The collection $(e_N)_N$ is an element of $\widehat{\mathbb{Z}}$.

## Depends on

- [ZHat.e](zhat-e.md)

## Proof

This boils down to checking that $D!+(D+1)!+\cdots+(N-1)!$ is a multiple of $D$.
