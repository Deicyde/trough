## Subgroup generated by a symmetric set

Let $G$ be a group and let $X \subseteq G$
satisfy $X^{-1} = X$, where $X^{-1} = \{x^{-1} : x \in X\}$. Then the subgroup of $G$
generated by $X$ equals $\bigcup_{n \in \mathbb{N}} X^n$, where $X^0 = \{1\}$ and
$X^{n+1} = X^n X = \{yx : y \in X^n,\ x \in X\}$.
