# fDiv_mono_fun

If $f \le g$, then $D_f(\mu, \nu) \le D_g(\mu, \nu)$.

## Depends on

- [f-divergence](../part-01/def-fdiv.md)

## Proof

Monotonicity of the integral and of $f \mapsto f'(\infty)$.
