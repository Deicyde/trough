## The Blockwise Alperin Weight Conjecture

We prove the numerical blockwise Alperin weight conjecture for every finite group and every prime *p*. For each *p*-block *B*, the number of irreducible Brauer characters in *B* equals the number of conjugacy classes of *B*-weights.
