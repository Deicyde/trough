# 040. Bloch’s conjecture for complex surfaces

1 manuscript, with no Lean, quoted from [openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math) at commit [4eaee875](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae).

Proves Bloch's conjecture: for every smooth connected projective complex surface *S* with $p_g(S)=0$, the Albanese map $\mathrm{CH}_0(S)^0\to\mathrm{Alb}(S)(\mathbb C)$ on integral degree-zero zero-cycles is an isomorphism. This combines the new $p_g=q=0$ theorem with the classical theorem of Bloch, Kas, and Lieberman.

## Bloch’s conjecture for surfaces with p_g=0

We prove Bloch's conjecture for smooth connected projective complex surfaces with $p_g=0$: the Albanese homomorphism on integral degree-zero zero-cycles is an isomorphism.
