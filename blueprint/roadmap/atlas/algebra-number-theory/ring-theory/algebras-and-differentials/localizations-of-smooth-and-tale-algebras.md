## Localizations of étale algebras are integrally closed domains

integrally closed.

## Local rings with principal regular maximal ideal are domains

A Noetherian local ring whose maximal ideal is generated by a single
regular element is a domain.

Formalized by `IsLocalRing.isDomain_of_maximalIdeal_eq_span`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Algebra/StandardSmooth/DVR.lean, line 76](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Algebra/StandardSmooth/DVR.lean#L76). ## Smooth curves have discrete valuation rings as local rings

The localization at a maximal ideal of a standard smooth algebra of
relative dimension `1` over a field is a discrete valuation ring.

Formalized by `Algebra.IsStandardSmoothOfRelativeDimension.isDiscreteValuationRing_localization_atPrime`, a theorem declared in [MathlibExt/Algebra/StandardSmooth/DVR.lean, line 128](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Algebra/StandardSmooth/DVR.lean#L128).
