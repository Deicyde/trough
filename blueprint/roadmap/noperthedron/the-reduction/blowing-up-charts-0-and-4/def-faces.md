# faces

For a face index $f \in \{0, \dots, 5\}$ and $(s_0, s_1) \in [-1,1]^2$, $c_f(s_0, s_1)$ is the point of face $f$ of $[-1,1]^3$ with free coordinates $(s_0, s_1)$. A point of a blown-up chart has coordinates $X = (u, v, s_0, s_1, \rho)$ and Cayley vector $r = \rho\, c_f(s_0, s_1)$; a point of charts 1–3 has coordinates $X = (u, v, r_0, r_1, r_2)$.
