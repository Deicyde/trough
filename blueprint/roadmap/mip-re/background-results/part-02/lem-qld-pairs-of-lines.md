# Pairs of lines

Let $|\hat\psi\rangle$ and $\{\widehat{Q}^{x,z}_{a,b}\}$ be as in Lemma [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](lem-qld-combined-points.md). Fix a *line presentation* on each side — a line type, axis-parallel or diagonal, so that each content presents a line $\ell_W$ with a base point and a direction on side $W$, and the sampled point $u_W$ lies on it. For a pair of degree-$md$ polynomials $(f_X, f_Z)$ on the two lines put

$$
T^{\ell_X, \ell_Z}_{f_X, f_Z}
\;=\; \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Line}, X), \ell_X}_{f_X}\,
\widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Line}, Z), \ell_Z}_{f_Z}\,
\widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Line}, X), \ell_X}_{f_X}\;,
$$

which is a POVM with outcomes pairs of degree-$md$ polynomials. Then, on average over the verifier's content,

$$
\widehat{Q}^{x,z}_{a,b} \otimes {I}
\;\simeq_{2 \delta_P}\; {I} \otimes T^{\ell_X, \ell_Z}_{[\mathrm{eval}_x(\cdot) = a,\,
\mathrm{eval}_z(\cdot) = b]}\;,\qquad\text{(3)}
$$

with $\delta_P = \mathrm{poly}(\varepsilon, \varepsilon_c)$ where $\varepsilon_c$ is the average collision probability of the $X$-side evaluation map: $md/q$ for an axis-parallel line and $md/q + 1/q$ for a diagonal one. The Lean proves it for a strategy in any bipartite model, on the extension of its register model that $\widehat{Q}$ acts on; from the game it states the estimate in agreement form, for the sandwich $\widehat{R}^{x,z}$ to which $\widehat{Q}^{x,z}$ compresses, on the register model. The statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

This is the pasting lemma (Lemma [Pasting, the two-measurement case](../../foundations/part-02/lem-pasting-updated.md)) at $G_1 = \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Line}, Z), \ell_Z}$, $G_2 = \widehat{M}^{(\mathtt{Line}, X), \ell_X}$ and $A = \widehat{Q}^{x,z}$, and what has to be supplied is its four hypotheses.

**The two marginal consistencies.** Lemma [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](lem-qld-combined-points.md) gives the joint measurement against the *ordered product* of the two point measurements, in either order; the marginal step of Lemma [Cool closeness fact, and the marginal step](../../foundations/part-02/lem-cool-closeness-fact.md) turns each of those into the corresponding marginal against a single point measurement, at ten times the input. Replacing that point measurement by the line measurement evaluated at the point is one triangle inequality through Bob's own point measurement — whose two legs are item 1 of Lemma [Expanded point measurements](lem-qld-expanded-points.md) (Alice's copy against Bob's) and item 2 of Lemma [Expanded line measurements](lem-qld-expanded-lines.md) (Alice's point against Bob's line).

**The fine-grained self-consistency of the $X$-line measurement** is item 1 of Lemma [Expanded line measurements](lem-qld-expanded-lines.md) verbatim. The joint measurement lives on each party's space enlarged by the dilation register and the line measurements do not, so both inputs are transported by the fact that an operator with an inert ancilla has the same cross-party deviation on the extended state as the operator itself.

**The collision term** needs the point to be uniform on its line once the line is fixed. The content determines both, so the factorization is produced by *shifting*: for a fixed $t \in \mathbb{F}_q$ the map that replaces $u_W$ by $u_W + t \cdot w_W$ is a bijection of contents, so averaging over contents and over (content, shift) pairs give the same thing; the line's question, base point and direction are invariant under it; and the point's parameter moves by exactly $t$. Two distinct degree-$md$ polynomials then agree at at most $md$ of the $q$ parameters, which is the univariate case of Lemma [Schwartz–Zippel](../part-05/lem-schwartz-zippel.md).
