# 086. An $L^{3}$ bound for the trilinear Hilbert transform

1 manuscript, with no Lean, quoted from [openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math) at commit [4eaee875](https://github.com/openai/math/tree/4eaee87548250b2682affda7d05d947c05554bae).

Proves that the principal-value trilinear Hilbert transform with shifts $x-t$, $x-2t$, $x-3t$ is bounded from $L^3(\mathbb R)^3$ to $L^1(\mathbb R)$. This resolves the $L^{3}$ exponent case of the standard conjecture for slopes 1, 2, 3.

## An $L^{3}$ bound for the trilinear Hilbert transform

We prove that the trilinear Hilbert transform with fixed slopes 1, 2, 3 maps $L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)\times L^3(\mathbb R)$ to $L^1(\mathbb R)$, resolving this case of the trilinear Hilbert transform conjecture.
