# TauCeti.index_square_le_of_closure_eq_top

In a commutative group generated by a finite set `S`, the subgroup of squares has index at
most `2 ^ S.card`. The quotient by the squares is an elementary abelian `2`-group spanned by
the image of `S`.

```lean
TauCeti.index_square_le_of_closure_eq_top.{u_1} {G : Type u_1} [CommGroup G] {S : Finset G}
  (hS : Subgroup.closure ↑S = ⊤) : (Subgroup.square G).index ≤ 2 ^ S.card
```
