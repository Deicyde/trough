# Convolution support

Over a no-zero-divisor semiring and a magma with
two unique products, a product of input support cardinalities greater than
one forces the convolution support cardinality to be greater than one.
Coefficients need not commute, and no monoid or nontriviality assumption is
imposed. See
[`MonoidAlgebra.one_lt_card_support_coeff_mul`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/group-rings/blob/8e90e6563300656410a098a0fea83332930218b5/GroupRings/MonoidAlgebra/SupportUniqueProds.lean)
and the [precise support bound](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/group-rings/blob/8e90e6563300656410a098a0fea83332930218b5/docs/HeadlineResults.md#convolution-support).
