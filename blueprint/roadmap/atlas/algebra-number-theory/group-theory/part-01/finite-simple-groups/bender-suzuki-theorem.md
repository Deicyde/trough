## Definitions

## Strongly embedded subgroups

A subgroup $M$ of a group $X$ is strongly embedded in $X$
if $M \ne X$, the order of $M$ is even, and for every $g \in X$ outside $M$ the order of
$M \cap g M g^{-1}$ is odd. Here an infinite group is assigned order $0$, so an infinite $M$
counts as having even order and an infinite intersection never has odd order.

## The group $\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$

For a natural number $n \ge 1$, $\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$ is
the projective special linear group of $2 \times 2$ matrices over the Galois field
$\mathrm{GF}(2^n)$. For $n = 0$ the same construction is taken over $\mathrm{GF}(2)$ instead.

## Generators of the Suzuki group

Let $n$ be a natural number, $q = 2^{2n+1}$,
$\theta = 2^{n+1}$ and $F = \mathrm{GF}(q)$. The generating set of $\mathrm{Sz}(q)$ is the set of
$4 \times 4$ matrices over $F$ of the following three kinds: for $a, b \in F$, the root element
with rows $(1, a, b, a^{2+\theta} + ab + b^{\theta})$, $(0, 1, a^{\theta}, a^{1+\theta} + b)$,
$(0, 0, 1, a)$ and $(0, 0, 0, 1)$; for nonzero $x \in F$, the torus element with diagonal entries
$x^{1+2^n}$, $x^{2^n}$, $x^{-2^n}$, $x^{-(1+2^n)}$ and zeros elsewhere; and the Weyl element, the
matrix with ones on the antidiagonal and zeros elsewhere.

The map $z \mapsto z^{\theta}$ is the Tits automorphism of $F$: its square is $z \mapsto z^2$.

## The Suzuki group $\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$

For a natural number $n$, the Suzuki group
$\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$ is the subgroup of $\mathrm{GL}(4, \mathrm{GF}(2^{2n+1}))$ generated by
the matrices in the generating set of the Suzuki group (all of which are invertible).

## The group $\mathrm{PSU}(3, 2^n)$

Let $n \ge 1$, let $q = 2^n$ and let $J$ be the
$3 \times 3$ matrix with ones on the antidiagonal and zeros elsewhere. Then $\mathrm{PSU}(3, q)$
is the image in the projective general linear group $\mathrm{PGL}(3, \mathrm{GF}(q^2))$ of the
group of matrices $A \in \mathrm{GL}(3, \mathrm{GF}(q^2))$ with $\det A = 1$ and
$\bar{A}^{\mathsf{T}} J A = J$, where $\bar{A}$ is obtained from $A$ by applying $z \mapsto z^q$ to
every entry. For $n = 0$ the same construction is taken over $\mathrm{GF}(2)$ instead, with
$z \mapsto z^q$ the identity.

The map $z \mapsto z^q$ is the involution of $\mathrm{GF}(q^2)$ whose fixed field is
$\mathrm{GF}(q)$, so these are the isometries of determinant one of the Hermitian form with Gram
matrix $J$, taken modulo scalars.

## Simple Bender groups

A group $G$ is a simple Bender group if it is isomorphic to
$\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$, to the Suzuki group $\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$ for some
$n \ge 1$, or to $\mathrm{PSU}(3, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$.

## Bender–Suzuki theorem

Let $X$ be a finite simple group that has a strongly embedded
subgroup $M$. Then $X$ is a simple Bender group: $X$ is isomorphic to $\mathrm{PSL}(2, 2^n)$ for
some $n \ge 2$, to the Suzuki group $\mathrm{Sz}(2^{2n+1})$ for some $n \ge 1$, or to
$\mathrm{PSU}(3, 2^n)$ for some $n \ge 2$.
