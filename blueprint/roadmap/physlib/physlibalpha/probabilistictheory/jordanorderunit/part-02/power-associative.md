# Power-associativity

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.JordanOrderUnit.Power.Associative`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/JordanOrderUnit/Power/Associative.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/JordanOrderUnit/Power/Associative.lean): 8 declarations, 8 with a docstring.

> Jordan algebras are power-associative: `aᵐ ∘ aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ`.

## i. Overview

> The subalgebra generated by one element of a Jordan algebra is associative: `aᵐ ∘ aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ`.
> Polarizing the Jordan identity shows that `L_a` commutes with every `L_{aⁿ}`, and the power law
> follows by induction.

## ii. Key results

> - `JordanAlgebra.linearized_mul_sq` : the polarized Jordan identity.
> - `JordanAlgebra.commute_mulLeft_pow` : `L_a` commutes with `L_{aⁿ}`.
> - `JordanAlgebra.pow_add` : `aᵐ ∘ aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ`.

## iv. References

> - K. McCrimmon, *A Taste of Jordan Algebras*, Springer, 2004, Ch. II.

> A polarized form of the Jordan identity. This is Basic Identity (2.1.2) in McCrimmon's
> *A Taste of Jordan Algebras*. It is the algebraic engine behind the recursion for multiplication
> operators of powers.

```lean
lemma linearized_mul_sq (a y z : E) :
    (a * a * y) * z =
      -(2 : ℝ) • (a * (y * (a * z))) + (a * a) * (y * z) +
        (2 : ℝ) • ((a * y) * (a * z))
```
