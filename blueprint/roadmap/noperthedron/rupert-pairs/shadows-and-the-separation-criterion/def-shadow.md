# shadow

The *shadow* of a $3 \times 3$ matrix $R$ is $\mathrm{Sh}(R) = \pi(R\,\operatorname{\mathbf{P}})$. A pair of matrices $(R_o, R_i)$ is a *Rupert pair* if $\overline{\mathrm{Sh}(R_i)} \subseteq \operatorname{int} \mathrm{Sh}(R_o)$.
