> **Stacks 00R9.** Let `k` be a field and `S` a finite type `k`-algebra which is Cohen–Macaulay
> (every local ring `S_𝔭` satisfies `depth_{𝔪}(S_𝔭) = dim S_𝔭`) and equidimensional of dimension
> `d`. Let `f = f₁, …, f_i` be elements of `S` cutting out a nonempty closed subset
> `V(f₁, …, f_i)` of `Spec S` of dimension at most `d - i`. Then that dimension is exactly `d - i`,
> and `f₁, …, f_i` is a regular sequence in `S_𝔮` for every prime `𝔮 ⊇ (f₁, …, f_i)`.

```lean
public theorem ringKrullDim_quotient_add_length_eq_and_isRegular

The attribute's comment: “`V(f₁, …, f_i)` is the subspace `PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus ↑(Ideal.ofList f)` of `Spec S` and openness is for its subspace topology. `S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮` is the quotient of `Localization.AtPrime 𝔮` by the ideal generated by `𝔭 = 𝔮.under R`, which is the fibre `κ(𝔭) ⊗_R S_𝔮` of tag 00PK by `Ideal.Fiber.ringEquivQuotient` but carries the canonical `S`-algebra structure. Cohen-Macaulayness of the fibre (Definition 00NC) is spelled out over its primes at `Localization.AtPrime` and equidimensionality of dimension `d` as Definition 0058 plus `ringKrullDim = d`, as in tags 00ND and 00R9; the hypothesis is guarded by the fibre being nonzero, where the source's condition on its components has content. See the module docstring.”.

> **Stacks 00RA.** Let `R → S` be a finite type ring map and let `d` be such that every
> nonempty fibre `S ⊗_R κ(𝔭)` is Cohen–Macaulay (each of its local rings `A` satisfies
> `depth_{𝔪_A}(A) = dim A`) and equidimensional of dimension `d`. Let `f = f₁, …, f_i` be elements
> of `S`. Then the set of points `𝔮` of `V(f₁, …, f_i)` at which `f₁, …, f_i` is a regular sequence
> in `S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮`, where `𝔭 = R ∩ 𝔮`, is open in `V(f₁, …, f_i)`.

```lean
public theorem isOpen_setOf_isRegular_fiber_of_isMaximalCohenMacaulay_of_equidimensional
    {R S : Type*} [CommRing R] [CommRing S] [Algebra R S] [Algebra.FiniteType R S] (d : ℕ)
    (hfib : ∀ (p : Ideal R) [p.IsPrime], Nontrivial (p.Fiber S) →
      (∀ (Q : Ideal (p.Fiber S)) [Q.IsPrime],
          Module.IsMaximalCohenMacaulay (Localization.AtPrime Q) (Localization.AtPrime Q)) ∧
        TopologicalSpace.Equidimensional (PrimeSpectrum (p.Fiber S)) ∧
        ringKrullDim (p.Fiber S) = (d : WithBot ℕ∞))
    (f : List S) :
    IsOpen {q : ↥(PrimeSpectrum.zeroLocus (Ideal.ofList f : Set S)) |
      RingTheory.Sequence.IsRegular
        (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.1.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal)))
        (f.map (algebraMap S (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal ⧸
          (q.1.asIdeal.under R).map (algebraMap R (Localization.AtPrime q.1.asIdeal)))))}
```

> **Stacks 00RB.** Let `R` be Noetherian, let `R → S` be a flat ring map of finite type all of
> whose nonempty fibres `S ⊗_R κ(𝔭)` are Cohen–Macaulay (each of their local rings `A` satisfies
> `depth_{𝔪_A}(A) = dim A`) of dimension `d`, and let `0 → F_{e+1} →[φ e] F_e → ⋯ → F_1 →[φ 0] F_0`
> be a finite complex of finite free `S`-modules. Then the set of primes `𝔮` of `S` at which the
> fibre complex `F̄_{•,𝔮} = F_{•,𝔮} ⊗_R κ(𝔭) = (S_𝔮/𝔭S_𝔮) ⊗_S F_•`, where `𝔭 = R ∩ 𝔮`, is exact
> at `F̄_{e+1}, …, F̄_1` is open in `Spec S`.
>
> The fibre hypothesis is not used: the conclusion holds for every flat ring map of finite type over
> a Noetherian ring, `isOpen_setOf_exact_baseChange_fiber_of_flat`.
