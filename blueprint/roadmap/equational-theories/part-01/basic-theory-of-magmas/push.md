# Pushforward

Let $w \simeq w'$ be a law with some alphabet $X$, $G$ be a magma, and $\pi: X \to Y$ be a function. If $G \models w \simeq w'$, then $G \models \pi_*(w) \simeq \pi_*(w')$. In particular, if $\pi$ is a bijection, the statements $G \models w \simeq w'$ and $G \models \pi_*(w) \simeq \pi_*(w')$ are equivalent.
