# The intermediate fields of a tamely ramified Kummer extension

Let `L` be generated over
the fraction field `K` of a discrete valuation ring `A` by an `n`-th root `α` of a uniformizer of
`A`, with `n` invertible in the residue field of `A`. Then every intermediate field `M` of `L / K`
is generated by `α ^ [L : M]`, an `[M : K]`-th root of the uniformizer, where
`[L : M] · [M : K] = n`.

```lean

  {A : Type u_1} [CommRing A] [IsDomain A] [IsDiscreteValuationRing A] {π : A} {n : ℕ}
  {K : Type u_2} {L : Type u_3} [Field K] [Algebra A K] [IsFractionRing A K] [Field L] [Algebra K L]
  [Algebra A L] [IsScalarTower A K L] {α : L} (hπ : Irreducible π) (hn : IsUnit ↑n)
  (hα : α ^ n = (algebraMap A L) π) (hgen : K⟮α⟯ = ⊤) (M : IntermediateField K L) :
  M = K⟮α ^ Module.finrank (↥M) L⟯
```
