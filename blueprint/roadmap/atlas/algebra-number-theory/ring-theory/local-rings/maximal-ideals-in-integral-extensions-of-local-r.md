## Kernel of reducing polynomial coefficients modulo an ideal

Let $A$ be a commutative
ring and $I$ an ideal of $A$. The kernel of the ring homomorphism $A[X] \to (A/I)[X]$ that
reduces every coefficient modulo $I$ is the ideal of $A[X]$ generated by the constant
polynomials $a$ with $a \in I$.

## Maximal ideals of a monogenic algebra over a local ring come from residual irreducible factors

**Maximal ideals of a monogenic algebra over a local ring come from residual irreducible
factors.** Let $A$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, and for
$f \in A[X]$ write $\bar{f}$ for its image in $(A/\mathfrak{m})[X]$. Let $g \in A[X]$ and suppose
that $A[X]/(g)$ is integral over $A$. Then for every maximal ideal $M$ of $A[X]/(g)$ there is a
polynomial $q \in A[X]$ such that $\bar{q}$ is irreducible in $(A/\mathfrak{m})[X]$, $\bar{q}$
divides $\bar{g}$, and $M$ is the ideal of $A[X]/(g)$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak{m}$
and the class of $q$.

## Residual irreducible factors give maximal ideals of a monogenic algebra

Let $A$ be a
commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, and for $f \in A[X]$ write $\bar{f}$ for
its image in $(A/\mathfrak{m})[X]$. Let $g, q \in A[X]$ be such that $\bar{q}$ is irreducible in
$(A/\mathfrak{m})[X]$ and $\bar{q}$ divides $\bar{g}$. Then the ideal of $A[X]/(g)$ generated by
the image of $\mathfrak{m}$ and the class of $q$ is maximal.

No integrality assumption on $A[X]/(g)$ over $A$ is needed.

## Maximal ideals of an integral algebra over a local ring contain the base maximal ideal

Let $A$ be a commutative local ring with maximal ideal $\mathfrak{m}$, and let $B$ be a
commutative $A$-algebra that is integral over $A$. Then every maximal ideal $M$ of $B$ contains
the ideal of $B$ generated by the image of $\mathfrak{m}$.
