# HLineq

For any $p>0$ and $t\in\mathbb{R}$,

$$
3+p^{2it}+p^{-2it}+2p^{it}+2p^{-it} \ge 0.
$$

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

This follows immediately from the identity

$$
|1+p^{it}+p^{-it}|^2=1+p^{2it}+p^{-2it}+2p^{it}+2p^{-it}+2.
$$
