# The modular functions of level `N` fixed by `SL(2, ℤ/Nℤ)` are `ℚ(ζ_N)(j)`

The functions
in `F_N` fixed by the action of `SL(2, ℤ/Nℤ)` on the function field of `X(N)` form the subfield
generated by `j`, pulled back from `X(1)`, and the constants in `ℚ(ζ_N)`.

```lean
TauCeti.modularFunctionField_inf_fixedField_eq_closure {N : ℕ} [NeZero N] :
  TauCeti.modularFunctionField N ⊓
      (IntermediateField.fixedField (TauCeti.modularFunctionActionZMod N).range.unop).toSubfield =
    Subfield.closure
      (insert ((Subgroup.compactifiedQuotientComap ⋯) TauCeti.ModularGroup.jMeromorphicFunction)
        (⇑(algebraMap ℂ
              (TauCeti.RiemannSurface.MeromorphicFunction
                (Subgroup.map sl2zToPSL2R (CongruenceSubgroup.Gamma N)).CompactifiedQuotient)) ''
          ↑(Subfield.closure {z | z ^ N = 1})))
```
