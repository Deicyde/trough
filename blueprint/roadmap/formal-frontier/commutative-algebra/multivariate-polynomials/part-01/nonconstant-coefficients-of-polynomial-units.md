# Nonconstant coefficients of polynomial units

Over any commutative semiring
`R`, every coefficient at a nonzero exponent of a unit in `MvPolynomial σ R`
is additively invertible, by
[`MvPolynomial.isAddUnit_coeff_of_isUnit`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/UnitCoefficients.lean).
The variable type is arbitrary, including empty and infinite types.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
