# Sylvester's theorem

For all positive natural $n, k$ such that $n \ge 2k$, at least one of the numbers $n, n - 1, \dots, n - k + 1$ has a prime divisor $p$ greater than $k$, or, equivalently the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ always has a prime factor $p > k$.
