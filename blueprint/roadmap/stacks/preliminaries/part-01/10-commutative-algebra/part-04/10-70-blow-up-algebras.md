> The affine blowup algebra `R[I/a]` of an ideal `I` of a ring `R` and an element `a : R`,
> defined as the `R`-subalgebra of a localization `S` of `R` away from `a` generated by the
> fractions `y/a` with `y ∈ I`. For `a ∈ I` its elements are exactly the fractions `x/aⁿ` with
> `x ∈ Iⁿ`.

```lean
public def affineBlowupAlgebra (I : Ideal R) (a : R) (S : Type*) [CommRing S] [Algebra R S]
    [IsLocalization.Away a S] : Subalgebra R S
```

**`Ideal.range_eq_affineBlowup_and_ker_eq_saturation`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Blowup/Affine.lean, line 279](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Blowup/Affine.lean#L279).

> **Stacks 0G8S.** Let `R` be a ring, let `I = (a, bᵢ : i ∈ ι)` be an ideal of
> `R` generated by `a` together with a family `b : ι → R`, and let `Rₐ` be the
> localization of `R` at `a`.  Let `φ : R[xᵢ : i ∈ ι] → Rₐ` be the `R`-algebra map
> with `φ xᵢ = bᵢ/a`.  Then the range of `φ` is the affine blowup algebra
> `R[I/a] = {x/aⁿ : x ∈ Iⁿ}`, and `φ f = 0` if and only if `aⁿ f` lies in the ideal
> of relations `(a xᵢ - bᵢ)` for some `n`; that is, `φ` induces a surjection
> `R[xᵢ]/(a xᵢ - bᵢ) → R[I/a]` whose kernel is the `a`-power torsion of the
> source.

```lean
public theorem Ideal.range_eq_affineBlowup_and_ker_eq_saturation
    {R : Type*} [CommRing R] {ι : Type*} {Rₐ : Type*} [CommRing Rₐ] [Algebra R Rₐ]
    {a : R} {b : ι → R} {I : Ideal R} [IsLocalization.Away a Rₐ]
    (hI : I = Ideal.span (insert a (Set.range b)))
    (φ : MvPolynomial ι R →ₐ[R] Rₐ)
    (hφ : ∀ i, φ (X i) * algebraMap R Rₐ a = algebraMap R Rₐ (b i)) :
    Set.range φ =
        {y : Rₐ | ∃ n : ℕ, ∃ x ∈ I ^ n, y * algebraMap R Rₐ a ^ n = algebraMap R Rₐ x} ∧
      ∀ f : MvPolynomial ι R, φ f = 0 ↔
        ∃ n : ℕ, a ^ n • f ∈ Ideal.span (Set.range fun i ↦ C a * X i - C (b i))
```

## Tag 052M, a lemma

[Tag 052M](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/052M) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.AffineBlowup.stacks052M_main`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/AffineBlowup/Valuation.lean, line 743](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/AffineBlowup/Valuation.lean#L743).

> **Theorem 1 (Stacks Tag 052M, main case `𝔪 ≠ 0`).**
> Let `(R, 𝔪)` be a local domain with fraction field `K`, and assume `𝔪 ≠ 0`.
> Let `A` be a valuation ring of `K` which dominates `R`. Then the family `𝒮` of
> admissible affine blowup algebras of Definition 7:
>
> 1. is **nonempty** and **directed** under inclusion, and moreover for any two
>    *admissible pairs* `(I, a)`, `(J, b)` (Definition 7'), the explicit displayed
>    product pair `(I*J, a*b)` is again an admissible pair, and its associated
>    subring `affineBlowupImage (I*J) (a*b)` contains both
>    `affineBlowupImage I a` and `affineBlowupImage J b`; and
> 2. has **directed union equal to `A`**: an element `x ∈ K` lies in `A` iff it
>    lies in some member of `𝒮`.
>
> Assembled from the four conjunct lemmas `stacks052M_C1_nonempty`,
> `stacks052M_C2_directed`, `stacks052M_C3_refinement`, `stacks052M_C4_union`.

```lean
theorem stacks052M_main
    [IsDomain R] [IsLocalRing R]
    (hm : IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal R ≠ ⊥)
    (A : ValuationSubring K) (hdom : Dominates R A) :
    -- (1a) nonemptiness
    (∃ B, IsAdmissibleBlowup R A B) ∧
    -- (1b) abstract directedness under inclusion
    DirectedOn (· ≤ ·) {B | IsAdmissibleBlowup R A B} ∧
    -- (1c) the explicit common refinement `R[IJ/(ab)]` of two admissible pairs:
    -- the displayed product pair `(I*J, a*b)` is itself an admissible pair, and
    -- its subring contains both factors.
    (∀ (I J : Ideal R) (a b : R) (ha : a ∈ I) (hb : b ∈ J),
        IsAdmissiblePair R A I a ha →
        IsAdmissiblePair R A J b hb →
        ∃ (hab : a * b ∈ I * J),
          IsAdmissiblePair R A (I * J) (a * b) hab ∧
          (affineBlowupImage I a ha : Subring K) ≤ affineBlowupImage (I * J) (a * b) hab ∧
          (affineBlowupImage J b hb : Subring K) ≤ affineBlowupImage (I * J) (a * b) hab) ∧
    -- (2) the directed union equals A
    (∀ x : K, x ∈ A ↔ ∃ B, IsAdmissibleBlowup R A B ∧ x ∈ B)
```
