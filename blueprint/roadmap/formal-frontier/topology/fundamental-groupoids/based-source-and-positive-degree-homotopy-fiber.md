# Based-source and positive-degree homotopy-fiber exactness

For every original based source (Z,z0), topological E,B in independent Z,E,B universes, f : C(E,B), e0 : E, b : B and h : f e0 = b, let F be the ordinary homotopy fiber, p its projection, J the forward-loop inclusion and Ωf the loop map; on whole based hom-sets [Z,-] relative to {z0}, transported by ordinary ULift, range p_* = f_*⁻¹{c_B}, range J_* = p_*⁻¹{c_E} and range (Ωf)_* = J_*⁻¹{c_F} for constant classes c_X without geometric hypotheses, while the pointed complex S = homotopyFiberSequence f b e0 h, functorial under strict based squares with lifted cubical quotients of B,E,F in degrees 3n,3n+1,3n+2, satisfies range (S.d (n+2) (n+1)).toFun = (S.d (n+1) n).toFun ⁻¹' {(S.X n).point} for every natural n, without asserting terminal π₀B surjectivity, and every nonempty cube index with decidable equality has a natural connecting group homomorphism whose kernel is the range of based postcomposition by f.
