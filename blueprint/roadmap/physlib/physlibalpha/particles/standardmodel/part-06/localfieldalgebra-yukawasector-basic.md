# The Yukawa sector's mass-weight submodules

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.YukawaSector.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean): 9 declarations, 9 with a docstring.

> The Yukawa sector of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model, as a join of products.

## i. Overview

> Everything below is stated about the covariant field algebra `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` of the
> Standard Model.
>
> The mixed Higgs-fermion sector — the `{scalar, fermion}` two-class sector of
> `Sectors.lean` — is the home of the Yukawa couplings. Since the Higgs sector is
> bosonic, its algebra commutes with the fermion algebra
> (`commute_of_mem_higgsAlgebra_of_mem_fermionAlgebra`), which feeds the abstract
> two-class machinery `sectorMassWeight_pair_le` to bound each weight-`w` piece of the
> sector by a join of products of the two sectors' own mass-weight submodules
> (`sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le`). Combined with the explicit low-weight tables
> for the Higgs sector (vanishing at odd weight) and the fermion sector (vanishing at
> weight `1`, `2` and `4`), this pins the sector down explicitly up to weight eight: it
> vanishes below weight five and at weight six, and at weights five, seven and eight it
> sits inside the expected Yukawa-type products, the last of these being the weight of
> the Yukawa term `H ψ ψ` itself.

## ii. Key results

> - `commute_of_mem_higgsAlgebra_of_mem_fermionAlgebra` : the algebra generated by the Higgs towers
>   commutes with the algebra generated by the fermion towers.
> - `sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le` : the Yukawa sector at weight `w` lies in the join, over the
>   splittings of `w` into two non-zero parts, of the products of the Higgs-sector and
>   fermion-sector mass-weight submodules.
> - `sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_five`, `sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_seven`,
>   `sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_eight` : the Yukawa sector at weights five, seven and eight.

## A. Cross-algebra commutation

> The Higgs sector is bosonic, so its algebra commutes with the fermion algebra
> elementwise. This is the algebraic input to the whole Yukawa-sector decomposition.

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.fermionMassWeight_mul_higgsMassWeight_le`**, a lemma, [line 126](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L126).

> The fermion-sector and Higgs-sector mass-weight submodules commute past each
> other, in the order needed by `sectorMassWeight_pair_le`.

```lean
lemma fermionMassWeight_mul_higgsMassWeight_le (a b : ℕ) :
    FermionSector.massWeightSubmodule a * ScalarSector.massWeightSubmodule b
      ≤ ScalarSector.massWeightSubmodule b * FermionSector.massWeightSubmodule a
```

## B. The Yukawa sector at a fixed mass weight

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le`**, a lemma, [line 145](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L145).

> The Yukawa-sector decomposition. The weight-`w` piece of the `\{scalar,
> fermion}` sector is contained in the join, over the splittings of `w` into two
> non-zero parts, of the products of the Higgs-sector and fermion-sector mass-weight
> submodules.

```lean
lemma sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_le (w : ℕ) :
    fieldData.sectorMassWeight {FieldKind.scalar, FieldKind.fermion} w
      ≤ ⨆ (p : ℕ × ℕ) (_ : p.1 + p.2 = w) (_ : p.1 ≠ 0) (_ : p.2 ≠ 0),
        ScalarSector.massWeightSubmodule p.1 * FermionSector.massWeightSubmodule p.2
```

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_eq_bot_of_lt_five`**, a lemma, [line 160](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L160).

> Below weight five, the Yukawa sector vanishes. No splitting of a total weight
> under five into two non-zero parts survives, since the Higgs part is either odd (hence
> zero) or equal to two, forcing the fermion part to be one or two (hence also
> zero).

```lean
lemma sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_eq_bot_of_lt_five {w : ℕ} (hw : w < 5) :
    fieldData.sectorMassWeight {FieldKind.scalar, FieldKind.fermion} w = ⊥
```

**`StandardModel.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.sectorMassWeight_higgs_fermion_six`**, a lemma, [line 180](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/YukawaSector/Basic.lean#L180).
