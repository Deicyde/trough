> ### The full gauge group
>
> The full gauge group is SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1); it projects onto each of its three factors, and an element is determined by its three projections. [\[1\]](basic.md) Its star operation commutes with the three projections. [\[2\]](basic.md) Sending a phase a to the diagonal matrix diag(a, ā) gives a homomorphism from U(1) into SU(2); an element of SU(2) with vanishing upper-right entry is of this form. The electromagnetic U(1), generated by Q = T₃ + Y, embeds in the gauge group as u ↦ (1, diag(u³, ū³), u), fixing a nonzero Higgs vacuum of the form (0, v). Its projections onto SU(3), SU(2) and U(1) are computed explicitly. [\[3\]](basic.md) The unbroken gauge group SU(3) × U(1)\_EM, color together with electromagnetism, is a group in its own right, included in the gauge group as (h, u) ↦ (h, diag(u³, ū³), u), with its projections computed explicitly. > ### Central subgroups and quotients
>
> The sixth roots of unity embed in the gauge group as a central subgroup, built factor by factor; membership in it has an explicit description, and it is normal. [\[4\]](basic.md) The quotient of the gauge group by that subgroup is a group, with its projection from the full group. [\[5\]](basic.md) The second roots of unity form a central subgroup contained in the sixth-roots subgroup, with its own quotient. [\[6\]](basic.md) So do the third roots of unity. [\[7\]](basic.md) …
>
> The choice of quotient, which the physics known so far does not settle \{1}, is itself a parameter, ranging over the sixth, second and third roots of unity and the trivial quotient; for each choice there is a gauge group and a central subgroup, normal and contained in the sixth-roots subgroup. [\[8\]](basic.md) A single quotient map out of the full gauge group serves every choice: it is computed on each central embedding, and its kernel is exactly the chosen subgroup. [\[9\]](basic.md)
