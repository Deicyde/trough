# The fermion sector

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.FermionSector.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/FermionSector/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/FermionSector/Basic.lean): 51 declarations, 51 with a docstring.

> The ten fermion towers of the Standard Model, their laws, and the submodules they span.

## i. Overview

> The fermion sector of the Standard Model's covariant field algebra `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra`
> consists of the three families of each of the ten fermion species, the five species and their
> conjugates, indexed by ordered tuples of covariant-derivative directions. This file names the ten
> towers (`d`, `bard`, `u`, `baru`, `Q`, `barQ`, `L`, `barL`, `e`, `bare`) and the gauge, Lorentz and
> mass-weight actions on `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` (`repGauge`, `repLorentz`,
> `massWeightPoly`), and records the laws they obey. Each family transforms under the global gauge
> group through the dual of the species' gauge representation (the conjugate representation for the
> barred fields), under the Lorentz group as the covariant derivatives of the species' Lorentz
> representation, and each tower is a `massWeightPoly`-eigenvector of weight `3 + 2 * n` (mass
> dimension `3/2 + n`). Any two fermionic towers anticommute, by
> `GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.covFermion_anticomm_covFermion` and its companions.
>
> Each law is the corresponding law of the covariant towers of `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra`, so
> nothing new is proved here beyond the assembly.

## ii. Key results

> - `repGauge_d`, ..., `repGauge_bare` : gauge transformation of the towers through the dual of the
>   species representations (conjugate for the barred fields).
> - `repLorentz_d`, ..., `repLorentz_bare` : the Lorentz transformation of the towers.
> - `massWeight_d`, ..., `massWeight_bare` : the mass weights `3 + 2 * n`.
> - `fermionAlgebra` : the algebra generated by the ten fermion families and all their covariant
>   derivatives.
> - `derivSubmodule` : the submodule of `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` generated by the fermion
>   symbols carrying exactly `n` covariant derivatives.
> - `anticomm_of_mem_derivSubmodule` : any two elements of the derivative submodules anticommute.
