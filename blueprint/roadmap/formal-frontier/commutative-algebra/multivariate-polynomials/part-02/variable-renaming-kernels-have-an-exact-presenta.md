# Variable-renaming kernels have an exact presentation and finite-generation criterion

For any commutative ring `R` and any map `f : σ → τ`, with all three types in
independent universes,
[`MvPolynomial.ker_rename_eq_span_X_sub_X`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/RenameKernel.lean)
identifies the kernel of `MvPolynomial.rename (R := R) f` with the ideal
generated by `X i - X j` for pairs satisfying `f i = f j`. Moreover,
[`MvPolynomial.ker_rename_fg_iff`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/RenameKernel.lean)
says that this ideal is finitely generated exactly when `R` is subsingleton
or the set `{i : σ | ∃ j : σ, i ≠ j ∧ f i = f j}` is finite. This counts
**both endpoints** of every collision, not just chosen representatives;
finiteness of individual fibres is not enough. Finite generation is as an
ideal of the **source polynomial ring** `MvPolynomial σ R`, not as an
`R`-module. No surjectivity or finite-variable hypothesis is required. See
the [applications](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomialsTests/RenameKernelCriterion.lean).

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
