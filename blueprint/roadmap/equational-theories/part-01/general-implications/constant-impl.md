# Laws implied by the constant law

If $w, w'$ each have order at least one, then the law $w \simeq w'$ is implied by the constant law ([E46](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?46) $({x \diamond y = z \diamond w})$). If exactly one of $w, w'$ has order zero, and the law $w \simeq w'$ is not implied by the constant law.
