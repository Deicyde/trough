# The generation theorem for the gauge-field symbols

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.AdjointFamily.Generation`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily/Generation.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily/Generation.lean): 25 declarations, 19 with a docstring.

> The symbols of a gauge field generate the same algebra as its symmetrized derivatives together
> with the covariant derivatives of its field strength.

## i. Overview

> The derivative symbols `∂_s A_μ` of a gauge field are not all independent modulo the
> covariant objects. The antisymmetric parts of the derivatives assemble into the field
> strength and its covariant derivatives, and what remains is the symmetrized derivative
>
>   `sym(∂_s A)_μ := (1/|s|) ∑_{ν ∈ s} ∂_{s − ν} A_ν`,
>
> with the direction of the field averaged into the derivative multiset. The generation theorem
> `symbolAdjoin_eq_symFieldAdjoin` says that the algebra generated by the symbols `∂_s A_μ` of
> order at most `n` is generated by the symmetrized symbols of order at most `n + 1` together
> with the covariant derivatives of the field strength of order less than `n`.
>
> Everything here holds for any family of symbols in any algebra `B`. No commutativity and no
> gauge action are used. The classification of gauge invariants built on this theorem is in
> `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GaugeBoson.AdjointFamily.Classification`.

## ii. Key results

> - `GaugeBoson.symmetrizedDeriv` : the symmetrized derivative symbols.
> - `GaugeBoson.symbolsLE`, `GaugeBoson.symSymbolsLE`, `GaugeBoson.towerLT` : the generating
>   sets of the three towers, filtered by order.
> - `GaugeBoson.symbolAdjoin_eq_symFieldAdjoin` : the generation theorem.

## C. The generation theorem

> The chain of lemmas below leads to
>
>   `adjoin({ ∂_p A }) = adjoin({ sym(∂_p A) } ∪ { 𝒟_q F })`.
>
> With the derivative symbols as primitives no Leibniz hypothesis is needed: the covariant
> derivative of a family shifts the derivative index and adds a bracket convolution, both of
> which stay inside the symbol subalgebras by construction.

**`GaugeBoson.bracketFam_mem_adjoin_symbols`**, a lemma, [line 236](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GaugeBoson/AdjointFamily/Generation.lean#L236).

> The bracket of two component families whose components are order-`n` symbol
> polynomials is again an order-`n` symbol polynomial, componentwise.

> The generation theorem, by strong induction on the order. The derivative symbols of
> order at most `n` and the symmetrized symbols together with the covariant field-strength
> tower generate the same subalgebra,
>
> `adjoin({ ∂_p A : |p| ≤ n }) = adjoin({ sym(∂_p A) : |p| ≤ n + 1 } ∪ { 𝒟_q F : |q| < n })`.

```lean
lemma symbolAdjoin_eq_symFieldAdjoin (n : ℕ) :
    Algebra.adjoin ℂ (symbolsLE A n) =
      Algebra.adjoin ℂ (symSymbolsLE A (n + 1) ∪ towerLT A n)
```
