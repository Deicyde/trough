# Diagonal equivalence for non-antipodal pairs

In any real normed vector
space, [`Metric.Sphere.diagonalHomotopyEquiv`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/sphere-topology/blob/672d46f90cf51cdf3f933248b85c842990ead729/SphereTopology/Homotopy/Sphere.lean)
has the diagonal as its forward map and first projection as its inverse.
Normalized affine interpolation stays in the non-antipodal-pair subspace and
fixes the diagonal. No finite-dimensionality or nonemptiness hypothesis is needed.
