## Kourovka Problem 18.52 (generation by two elements of prime-power order)

The statement
that every finite simple group $G$ is generated by two elements $x, y \in G$ whose orders are prime
powers, that is, each of the form $p^k$ with $p$ prime and $k \ge 1$ (the primes and exponents may
differ between $x$ and $y$ and depend on the group). The problem is due to J. Krempa.

## Finite simple groups are generated by two elements of prime-power order

Every finite simple
group $G$ is generated by two elements $x, y \in G$ whose orders are prime powers, that is, each
of the form $p^k$ with $p$ prime and $k \ge 1$, where the prime and the exponent may differ
between $x$ and $y$. This answers Kourovka Problem 18.52 positively.

The Kourovka Archive answers 18.52 "Yes, it is" (mod CFSG): by C. S. H. King (2017) every finite
(non-abelian) simple group is generated by an involution and an element of prime order; a cyclic
group of prime order is generated by one element of prime order.
