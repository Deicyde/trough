# The simple quotient of `M(ε q^m)`

At a generic parameter, the quotient of `M(ε q^m)` by
the submodule generated by `v_(m + 1)` is isomorphic to the signed ladder module `L_m^ε`, by the
module map sending `v₀` to the class of `v₀`.

```lean
TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule.ladderModuleEquivQuotient {q : ℂ} {m : ℕ} {ε : ℤˣ} [Fact (q ≠ 0)]
  (hq : TauCeti.IsGenericQuantumParameter q) :
  TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule q m ε ≃ₗ[TauCeti.UqSl2 q]
    TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule q (↑↑ε * q ^ m) ⧸
      TauCeti.UqSl2 q ∙ TauCeti.UqSl2.VermaModule.vec q (↑↑ε * q ^ m) (m + 1)
```
