The Lean proof uses no lower semidecider, no real-closed-field decision and no NPA levels. The witness scenario has one question alphabet and one answer alphabet shared by the two players. ## Separation of quantum and commuting values

- [Separation](thm-separation.md)

The recursion theorem used in Lean is the two-argument form of Mathlib's `Nat.Partrec.Code.fixed_point`, for partial recursive codes. No running-time bound is needed, so the efficient form of Lemma [Efficient Kleene recursion theorem](../../computability/lem-kleene.md) is not used.

Proof route. The correspondence with nodes $1.8.8$–$1.8.11$ is at the level of the conclusion. The game here is not the paper's $\mathfrak{G}^{\textsc{Sep}} = \mathsf{V}^{\textsc{Sep}}_{C_0}$: it is the halting-reduction game of a machine that runs the commuting-value semidecider on its own game. It is explicit in the same sense as the paper's. It is obtained from self-reference, uniformly from the codes of $g$ and of the semidecider, and neither proof exhibits a value-$1$ commuting-operator strategy. The paper's proof needs, in the case ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}} = 1$, that $\mathrm{val}^* \leq \frac12$ at a level of its recursion where no soundness is otherwise known, and gets it from divergence of a Schmidt-rank entanglement requirement (Remark The paper's construction and its premises). Here that case is the non-halting case of the halting reduction, whose soundness is already the value-form compression of the main theorem. The paper observes, in a footnote to its halting theorem, that the gap $\mathrm{val}^* < {\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$ holds for infinitely many non-halting machines (AuditSync26); the fixed point picks one of them out. `scripts/ledger-sync.py`, which walks the ledger's declared dependencies, still lists the Schmidt-rank clauses of stage $1.5$ among the imports reached from this theorem, as expected.

**Remark (The paper's construction and its premises).** The paper's proof builds $\mathfrak{G}^{\textsc{Sep}}$ as the compressed verifier at a fixed level $C_0 \geq 2$ of a self-referential family: its decider runs the upper semidecider on its own succinct description, accepts early if the semidecider halts, and otherwise compresses (AuditSync26). Formalizing *that* game, rather than the one of Theorem [Separation](thm-separation.md), needs the *bipartite Schmidt-rank entanglement variant* of compression, together with its effective bounded self-reference construction. Write $\mathrm{Ent}_{\mathrm{bi}}(\mathfrak{G},\nu)$ for the least Schmidt rank of a finite-dimensional tensor-product strategy attaining value at least $\nu$, or $\infty$ if there is none. This is the paper's measure; it is distinct from the synchronous dimension requirement $\mathrm{Ent}$ of Definition [Entanglement requirement](../../foundations/part-01/def-ent.md). The extra compression clauses must force $\mathrm{Ent}_{\mathrm{bi}}$ to diverge along the recursive levels, and the construction must identify the game at $C_0$ with the game supplied to the upper semidecider, with all specialization and timeout bounds justified.

These are premises of the paper's construction, not of Theorem [Separation](thm-separation.md), and none of them is formalized. The value-form Theorem [Gap-preserving compression from ambient stage contracts](../../structure-of-the-proof/part-09/thm-compression.md) and its current Lean interface do not supply the Schmidt-rank clauses. If the source completeness steps are implemented through the synchronous PCC interface, the representation transfers of Remark [Source PCC and the required representation bridge](../../foundations/README.md) must also be supplied. An implementation through the ambient verifier interfaces additionally requires the runtime and answer-cut normalization of Remark [The runtime-contract bridge remains an obligation](../../foundations/README.md); the source construction's absolute bounds cannot simply be read as relative cost coefficients. `planning/explicit-separation.md` records the cost.

**Remark (Which commuting value this is, and why it is not the tracial one).** Theorem [Separation](thm-separation.md) is stated for the *bipartite* commuting-operator value ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$ (Definition [Commuting-operator value](../../parallel-repetition/def-co-value-bipartite.md)), not for the tracial ${\mathrm{val}^{\mathrm{co}}}$ (Definition [Commuting value](../../foundations/part-01/def-commuting-value.md)) that this blueprint otherwise carries. That is not a slip, and the distinction is forced by the proof rather than cosmetic.

The proof turns on the semidecider of Lemma [Upper semidecision of the commuting-operator value](lem-valco-upper-re.md), which is complete for the event ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}} < 1$. For the tracial value, Lemma [Tracial strategies are commuting-operator strategies](../../parallel-repetition/lem-tracial-le-co.md) gives only ${\mathrm{val}^{\mathrm{co}}} \leq {\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, so the same procedure is a sound semidecider for ${\mathrm{val}^{\mathrm{co}}} < 1$ and not a complete one, and the fixed point would conclude nothing. The semidecision event is that the commuting value of the very game being defined is below $1$. No search for a proof of non-halting, and no preservation theorem for arbitrary commuting-operator strategies, is used, and the argument constructs no explicit perfect commuting-operator strategy: the correlation of payoff $1$ is obtained by compactness.

No synchronous decision predicate is asserted: $\mathfrak{G}^{\textsc{Sep}}$ is whatever game the halting reduction produces. Even for synchronous games, Lemma [Tracial strategies are commuting-operator strategies](../../parallel-repetition/lem-tracial-le-co.md) only gives ${\mathrm{val}^{\mathrm{co}}}\leq{\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, with the reverse comparison false in general. Thus this route provides neither a synchronous perfect correlation nor a tracial value-$1$ witness. Any such formulation would require additional representation and value-transport results.

**Remark (An independent Lean statement of the same results, proved).** The Lean 4 project `lukasliehr/MIPRE` states, in its own vocabulary and independently of this formalization, three terminal propositions: a game with $\mathrm{val}^* \leq \frac12$ and commuting value $1$; the strict inclusion $C_{qa} \subsetneq C_{qc}$ in some finite square scenario; and a square game with $\mathrm{val}^* < {\omega^{\mathrm{co}}} = 1$. Its games live on `EuclideanSpace`, its tensor-product strategies carry POVMs rather than projective measurements on both sides, and its values are suprema of payoffs over correlation sets. In September 2026 its statement vocabulary was vendored into this repository and all three propositions were proved from Theorem [Separation](thm-separation.md), sorry-free and from the three standard axioms (the repository at commit `d4cbb2d`, 2026-10-05, and `reports/liehr-tsirelson-bridge.md`): the commuting-operator strategies of the two developments are the same data, so the two $C_{qc}$ coincide and the two commuting values agree; a POVM tensor-product strategy has the correlation of a projective one by Naimark dilation on both sides, so the vendored $C_{qa}$ is contained in this one and the vendored finite-dimensional value is at most $\mathrm{val}^*$; and the two payoffs agree on every correlation. The vendored tree was removed on 2026-10-05, since the upstream project carries no licence and the check is not part of the formalization's results. It was a check on the statements of this chapter rather than a new result: a separation stated without reference to this repository's definitions follows from them.

## The commuting-operator class

Lin proved that the commuting-operator analogue of $\mathrm{MIP}^*$ is $\mathrm{coRE}$ (Lin25), by the compression architecture of this blueprint with the soundness of every stage carried in the commuting-operator value. This section states his theorem in the vocabulary of Section [Foundations](../../foundations/README.md), proves it first from one hypothesis, the commuting-operator soundness of gap compression (Definition [Soundness of compression in a value model](def-compression-co-sound.md)), and then proves the hypothesis; the inclusion $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} \subseteq \mathrm{coRE}$ needs none. Nothing else is proved twice: the halting reduction, its classes, semideciders and class transfer, and the classes $\mathrm{MIP}^*$ and $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}}$ themselves are written once against a *value model* (Definition [Value models](def-value-model.md)), an interface that $\mathrm{val}^*$ and ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$ both satisfy, and the two theorems differ only in the direction in which the value is semidecidable. The plan that discharged the hypothesis is `planning/mipco-track.md`; its Phases 1 to 5, the rest of this section, reduce it to the soundness of the low-individual-degree test in the commuting-operator model (Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the low-individual-degree test](../part-04/cor-mipco-from-lidt.md)), and Phase 6 to its soundness in dyadic pairs (Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the soundness of the low-individual-degree test in dyadic pairs](../part-05/cor-mipco-from-lidt-fin.md)), which is proved (Theorem [The low-individual-degree test is sound in dyadic pairs](../part-07/thm-lidt-sound-fin.md)); so his theorem holds here without hypothesis (Theorem [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$](../part-07/thm-mipco-eq-core-unconditional.md)). The companion proof repository has no $\mathrm{coRE}$ chapter: the paper of record for this section is Lin's, and its nodes carry no ledger correspondence.
