# TauCeti.UqSl2.LadderModule

The underlying complex vector space `ℂ^(m + 1)` of the signed ladder module `L_m^ε` of
`UqSl2 q`. Its `UqSl2 q`-module structure, defined for nonzero `q`, is
`LadderModule.instModule`.
