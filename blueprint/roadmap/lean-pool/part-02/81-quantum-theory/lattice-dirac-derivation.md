# Free Dirac dynamics and covariant spinors from a reciprocal C19 lattice action

> Derives the linearized free Dirac equation for a reciprocal two-field action on the C19 cubic stencil at an exact zero-energy vacuum. The cumulative V3.4 certificate keeps that action, geometry, coefficients, vacuum, and native mass unchanged while extending the V3.2 free-fermion result through a complete free-spinor observable layer. It constructs positive-frequency and antiparticle covariant spinors, proves their two-plus-two sector decomposition and covariant spin sums, establishes arbitrary nonzero-momentum helicity projectors with eigenvalues plus or minus one half, proves the non-forward Gordon identity for native-derived on-shell spinors, and decomposes the free resolvent into its spectral projectors and pole numerators. The project continues to distinguish the exact finite-C19 scalar spectrum from the continuum Clifford factorization. Its scope is the free linearized theory, including the inherited free transport results.
