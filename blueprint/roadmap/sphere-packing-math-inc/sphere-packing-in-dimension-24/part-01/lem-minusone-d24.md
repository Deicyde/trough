# minusone-d24

The function $r\mapsto b(r)$ analytically continues to a holomorphic function on a neighborhood of $\mathbb{R}$. Its restriction to $\mathbb{R}$ is a Schwartz function and a radial eigenfunction of the Fourier transform in $\mathbb{R}^{24}$ with eigenvalue $-1$.
