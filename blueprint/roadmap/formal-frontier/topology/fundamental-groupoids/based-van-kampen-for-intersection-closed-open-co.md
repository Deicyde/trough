# Based van Kampen for intersection-closed open covers

For an arbitrarily indexed, binary-intersection-closed open cover of X by path-connected neighborhoods of a common point x, homomorphisms from the local fundamental groups to any group, compatible along every inclusion, determine a unique homomorphism from π₁(X,x), and the inclusion cocone is a colimit in GrpCat at the space universe, with independent space, index and target-group universes.
