## Tag 01AN, a lemma

[Tag 01AN](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01AN) in the Stacks Project.

**`AlgebraicGeometry.generates_iff_stalkwise_generates`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/GlobalGeneration/Stalks.lean, line 323](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/GlobalGeneration/Stalks.lean#L323).

> **Global sections generate a sheaf of modules iff their germs generate every stalk.** Let
> `O_X` be a sheaf of rings on a topological space `X`, `F` a sheaf of `O_X`-modules and
> `s : I → Γ(X, F)` a family of global sections. The sections generate `F`, that is, the morphism
> `⨁_{i ∈ I} O_X ⟶ F` they determine (`F.freeHomEquiv.symm s`) is an epimorphism of sheaves of
> `O_X`-modules, if and only if for every `x : X` the germs `s_{i,x}` span the stalk `F_x` as a
> module over `O_{X,x}`.

```lean
public theorem generates_iff_stalkwise_generates (s : I → F.sections) :
    Epi (F.freeHomEquiv.symm s) ↔
      ∀ x : X,
        Submodule.span (R := (TopCat.Presheaf.stalk O_X.obj x : RingCat))
          (Set.range fun i ↦
            (TopCat.Presheaf.Γgerm F.val.presheaf x).hom
              ((s i).eval (Opposite.op ⊤))) = ⊤
```

**`SheafOfModules.Submodule.isLeast_generatedBy`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/Submodule.lean, line 82](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Sheaf/Submodule.lean#L82). The attribute's comment: “On any site with a sheaf of rings, which covers a ringed space `(X, O_X)` with its opens; existence and uniqueness of the smallest sub-`O`-module sheaf containing the given local sections.”.

> Given local sections `s i ∈ F(U i)` of a sheaf of modules `F`, there is a unique smallest
> submodule sheaf of `F` whose sections over each `U i` contain `s i`: the submodule sheaf
> `generatedBy U s` is the least element of the set of such submodule sheaves, and any least
> element of that set equals it.

```lean
theorem isLeast_generatedBy :
    IsLeast {G : F.Submodule | ∀ i, s i ∈ G.obj (U i)} (generatedBy U s) ∧
      ∀ G : F.Submodule, IsLeast {G : F.Submodule | ∀ i, s i ∈ G.obj (U i)} G →
        G = generatedBy U s
```
