## Golod group (Kourovka 12.102)

Let $k$ be a field, $N$ a non-unital associative
$k$-algebra and $y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$. The Golod group of $y$ is the subgroup of the group of
units of the unitization $k \oplus N$ generated by the units that are equal to $1 + y_i$ for some
$i$. When every $1 + y_i$ is invertible (for example when $N$ is nil), this is the subgroup
$\langle 1 + y_1, \dots, 1 + y_r \rangle$ of the adjoint group $1 + N$.

## Residual finiteness of proper quotients of Golod groups (Kourovka 12.102)

This is the
statement that the following holds for every field $k$ of prime characteristic $p$, every
non-unital associative $k$-algebra $N$, every $r \ge 2$ and all $y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$ such that
$N$ is generated by $y_1, \dots, y_r$ as a non-unital $k$-algebra, every element of $N$ is
nilpotent, and for every natural number $n$ some product of $n$ elements of $N$ is nonzero: if
$\Gamma$ is the Golod group of $y$, then for every nontrivial normal subgroup $K$ of $\Gamma$ and
every $x \in \Gamma$ with $x \notin K$ there is a normal subgroup $M$ of finite index in $\Gamma$
with $K \le M$ and $x \notin M$. In other words, every proper factor group $\Gamma / K$ of a
Golod group $\Gamma$ is residually finite. This is Shunkov's question, Kourovka Problem 12.102.

## Not every proper quotient of a Golod group is residually finite

The statement that every
proper factor group of a Golod group is residually finite is false: there exist a field $k$ of
prime characteristic $p$, an integer $r \ge 2$, a non-unital associative $k$-algebra $N$ and
elements $y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$ such that $N$ is generated by $y_1, \dots, y_r$ as a non-unital
$k$-algebra, every element of $N$ is nilpotent and for every natural number $n$ some product of
$n$ elements of $N$ is nonzero, together with a nontrivial normal subgroup $K$ of the Golod group
$\Gamma$ of $y$ and an element $x \in \Gamma$ with $x \notin K$, such that every normal subgroup of
finite index in $\Gamma$ that contains $K$ also contains $x$. This answers Kourovka Problem 12.102
(V. P. Shunkov) in the negative (L. Hammoudi).

## Existence of a Golod group with trivial centre

Kourovka 11.101 (A. V. Timofeenko), in the strong form proved: some Golod group has trivial
centre (the problem asks for finite centre). A Golod group (9.76) is
`⟨1 + x₁ + I, …, 1 + x_r + I⟩ ≤ 1 + F/I` for `r ≥ 2`, `F` the free associative algebra without
constant term on `x₁, …, x_r` over a field of characteristic `p > 0` and `F/I` a non-nilpotent
nil-algebra; equivalently `N = F/I` is a non-unital algebra generated by `y₁, …, y_r` that is nil
but not nilpotent (some product of `n` elements is nonzero for every `n`).

## There is a Golod group with trivial centre

There exist a field $k$ of prime characteristic
$p$, an integer $r \ge 2$, a non-unital associative $k$-algebra $N$ and elements
$y_1, \dots, y_r \in N$ such that $N$ is generated by $y_1, \dots, y_r$ as a non-unital
$k$-algebra, every element of $N$ is nilpotent, for every natural number $n$ some product of $n$
elements of $N$ is nonzero, and the Golod group of $y$ has trivial centre. Since a trivial centre
is finite, this answers Kourovka Problem 11.101 (A. V. Timofeenko) positively (V. A. Sereda and
A. I. Sozutov).
