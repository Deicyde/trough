# Chevalley's theorem: the coinvariant algebra of a reflection group is the regular representation

If a finite group `G` acts faithfully on a finite-dimensional vector space `V`
over a field of characteristic zero and is generated by pseudoreflections, then the coinvariant
algebra of the polynomial functions `Sym(V*)`, as an ungraded representation, is equivalent to the
regular representation `K[G]`.

```lean
Representation.nonempty_equiv_coinvariantAlgebra_dual_leftRegular_of_pseudoreflectionSubgroup_eq_top.{u_1,
    u_2, u_3}
  {K : Type u_1} {G : Type u_2} {V : Type u_3} [Field K] [Group G] [AddCommGroup V]
  [_root_.Module K V] [FiniteDimensional K V] (ρ : Representation K G V) [Finite G] [CharZero K]
  (hG : ρ.pseudoreflectionSubgroup = ⊤) (hρ : Function.Injective ⇑ρ) :
  Nonempty (ρ.dual.coinvariantAlgebra.Equiv (Representation.leftRegular K G))
```
