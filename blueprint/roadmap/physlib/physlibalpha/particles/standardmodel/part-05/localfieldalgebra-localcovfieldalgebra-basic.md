# The covariant field algebra of the Standard Model

> The covariant field algebra of the Standard Model, its gauge and Lorentz actions, its thirteen
> covariant towers and their mass weights.

## i. Overview

> The covariant field algebra of the Standard Model is the covariant field algebra
> `fieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra` of its field datum, the subalgebra of the jet algebra
> generated by the covariant towers, the covariant derivatives of the field strength and of
> the twelve matter species. It is the algebra in which every covariant expression of the
> Standard Model lives, and nothing else.
>
> This file gives it the structure in which the classification of invariants is written. The
> generic theory supplies the restricted jet and Lorentz actions; here the ordinary gauge
> group is read as `GaugeGroupI` through `gaugeGroupEquiv`, and the mass-weight polynomial of
> the jet algebra is restricted to the covariant field algebra, every covariant generator being
> an eigenvector of it. The thirteen covariant towers are the generic towers of the datum at the
> Standard Model species, and their laws are the generic laws at those species: the gauge laws
> in the contragredient of each species representation, the Lorentz laws in each species'
> Lorentz representation, the statistics, and the mass weights `2 * (2 + n)` for the field
> strength, `2 * (1 + n)` for the Higgs and `3 + 2 * n` for the fermions.
>
> Its sectors, and the algebras generated by the towers of one kind, are in
> [`Sectors.lean`](localfieldalgebra-localcovfieldalgebra-sectors.md).

## ii. Key results

## A. The gauge and Lorentz actions

> The ordinary gauge group of the datum acts on the covariant field algebra through
> `GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repValue`, the jet action at the constant jets; the global
> gauge group `GaugeGroupI` acts through `gaugeGroupEquiv`. The Lorentz action is the generic
> restricted one, under the compatibility of the Standard Model species.
