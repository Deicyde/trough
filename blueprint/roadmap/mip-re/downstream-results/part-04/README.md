### The Pauli basis test in the model, given the low-individual-degree test

Phase 5 of `planning/mipco-track.md` reads the Pauli basis test in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$. Its soundness analysis (Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../../background-results/part-01/thm-qld.md), organized as in Remark [How the soundness proof of Theorem Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test is organized](../../background-results/README.md)) works with a strategy: it unpacks the subtests into consistency and commutation estimates, adjoins EPR registers, pads the strategy and runs the seeded CL test on the padded strategy, extracts exact Pauli operators from the joint measurement that test supplies, and rotates them onto honest EPR pairs by a swap isometry. It is written once, for a projective strategy in a bipartite model, and the one fact about the low-individual-degree test it uses, the soundness of the seeded test (Theorem [Quantum soundness of the seeded CL test](../../background-results/part-01/thm-lidt-cl-soundness.md)), becomes a hypothesis on the model: the seeded test is sound (Definition [The low-individual-degree test is sound in a model](../part-03/def-lidt-sound-in.md)) in every extension of the model by a unit vector, where the padded strategy lives. The value model enters through a second hypothesis, that it dominates the POVM strategies of every such extension in every unit state, the ancilla model of the conclusion being one of them. The conclusion is the soundness of the Pauli basis test in the model (Definition [The Pauli basis test is sound in a model](../part-03/def-qld-sound-in.md)), which is what Section Introspection in the model, given the Pauli basis test assumed. In the tensor-product model both hypotheses hold (Lemma [The Pauli basis test is sound in the tensor-product model](lem-qld-sound-in-tensor.md)), and that is the form the main theorem now uses. In the commuting-operator model the first is the hypothesis of answer reduction (Theorem [Answer reduction is sound in ${\omega^{\mathrm{co}}}$, given the low-individual-degree test](../part-03/thm-ar-sound-co.md)), carried to the extensions, and the second is Lemma [Domination and approximation in the two models](../part-03/lem-povm-domination.md); so the Pauli basis test adds no hypothesis (Lemma [The Pauli basis test is sound in the commuting-operator model, given the seeded test](lem-qld-sound-co.md)), and $\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$ rests on the soundness of the seeded test in the commuting-operator model alone (Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the low-individual-degree test](cor-mipco-from-lidt.md)).

Three things change with the setting, and they are the nodes below. The matrix analysis read one state along several assignments of its registers to the players by reindexing explicit vectors; here every such state is an ancilla extension of the model, only the finite registers are regrouped, and a regrouping is an isomorphism or an embedding of models, or a reading of one extension along another cut. The hypothesis on the seeded test is moved between models: along isomorphisms, to the model with the players exchanged, and to the extensions of tensor-product and of commuting-operator models, the latter because an extension of a commuting-operator model is again one, the commutant of a matrix amplification being the amplified commutant. And the state calculus of the analysis' last stages, with Parseval for its coarse-grainings and the action of scalar matrices on a register, is restated in a model. The strategy is projective throughout, as the interface asks, so the analysis dilates no strategy except the padded one of stage 4, by the Halmos dilation of Lemma [Projective dilation against a fixed state, in a star-ordered ring](../part-03/lem-kraus-dilation.md), and never descends from a dilation back to a POVM.

### The hypothesis in dyadic pairs

The hypothesis of Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the co-soundness of the low-individual-degree test](cor-mipco-from-lidt.md) is Theorem [Quantum soundness of the seeded CL test](../../background-results/part-01/thm-lidt-cl-soundness.md) in the model of every commuting-operator strategy, with an arbitrary vector state. It is Phase 6 of `planning/mipco-track.md`, the one step of the track that is not a generalization of an analysis already formalized: the only proof of the seeded test's soundness here is the vendored finite-dimensional one, whose mechanisms use the finite dimension throughout. No transfer principle supplies it, since the correlations that approximate those of commuting-operator strategies by density are those of tracially embeddable strategies, which are still infinite-dimensional, and approximation by finite-dimensional strategies is what fails. No source proves it either (`reports/lidt-co-audit.md`).

Phase 6 therefore replaces it by its restriction to *finite pairs*: models whose two players' algebras are each other's commutants and both carry a faithful tracial state, with an arbitrary vector state (Definition [Finite pairs](def-finite-pair.md)); and, within those, to *dyadic pairs*, whose two algebras also contain unital matrix units of every size $2^n$ (Definition [Dyadic pairs](../part-05/def-dyadic-pair.md)). The two hypotheses are incomparable. What the restriction buys is a trace on both algebras, which is what a port of the vendored proof needs where it uses the dimension: the symmetrization of the two players, the semidefinite step, and orthonormalization, which in a finite von Neumann algebra is de la Salle's theorem; and the matrix units exclude abelian projections (Theorem [Dyadic matrix units exclude abelian projections](../part-05/thm-no-abelian-dyadic.md)), which makes that theorem unconditional in the class (Corollary [Orthonormalization in a dyadic pair](../part-05/cor-orthonormalization-dyadic-pair.md)). The port is complete (Theorem [The low-individual-degree test is sound in dyadic pairs](../part-07/thm-lidt-sound-fin.md)), with the doubling that supplies its symmetric model (Definition [The doubled model](../part-05/def-doubled-model.md)) and the semidefinite step (Theorem [The summed semidefinite form, in a commutant with a vector trace](../part-06/thm-summed-sdp.md)). This subsection proves that the restriction suffices (Corollary [$\mathrm{MIP}^{\mathrm{co}} = \mathrm{coRE}$, from the soundness of the low-individual-degree test in dyadic pairs](../part-05/cor-mipco-from-lidt-fin.md)). Answer reduction and the Pauli basis test use the hypothesis only in the model of one near-optimal projective strategy and in its ancilla extensions, and by Lin's tracial density (Theorem [Lin's tracial density theorem](../../parallel-repetition/thm-tracial-density.md)), followed by an amplification by an algebra with dyadic matrix units (Lemma [Amplification by an algebra with dyadic matrix units](../part-05/lem-amplification-dyadic.md)), the near-optimal strategies can be taken in dyadic pairs, a class closed under ancilla extensions.
