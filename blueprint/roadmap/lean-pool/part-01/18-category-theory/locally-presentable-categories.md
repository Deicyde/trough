# Locally presentable categories and Gabriel–Ulmer duality

> Structure theorems for locally presentable and accessible categories, built on Mathlib's carriers. A locally presentable category is complete; it is locally κ-presentable exactly when it is a full reflective subcategory of a presheaf category on a small category, with κ-accessible inclusion, and exactly when it is a small-orthogonality class of presheaves for maps between κ-presentable presheaves. A category is locally presentable exactly when it is accessible and complete. Between locally presentable categories, a functor is a right adjoint exactly when it preserves limits and is accessible, and a left adjoint exactly when it preserves colimits. A category is finitely accessible exactly when it is Ind A for a small A. Gabriel–Ulmer duality on objects and morphisms is proved; every locally κ-presentable category is the category of κ-small-limit-preserving functors from a small theory with κ-small limits, the theory is recovered as the opposite of the κ-presentable models, and the κ-accessible right adjoints between categories of models are the restrictions along morphisms of theories. Algebras of finite-product theories form a locally finitely presentable category with sifted colimits computed pointwise, the colimit of the initial chain of an endofunctor that preserves it is an initial algebra, models of theories with D-limits form a reflective locally presentable subcategory, and generation under D-limits is detected by fully faithful D-functors.
