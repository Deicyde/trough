# Nearly quadratic additive gaps for edge-clique partitions of split graphs

> Constructs connected split graphs of every sufficiently large order with a nearly quadratic difference between the integral and fractional edge-clique partition numbers. The strongest construction remains connected after deletion of any set of fewer than one fifth of the vertices. The development includes host assignments that transfer residual triangle-packings to clique partitions, and quantitative product constructions.
