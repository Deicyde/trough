# The square

> The square state space: its observables, its four facets and vertex values of composites.

## i. Overview

> The square is the simplest state space that is not a simplex: it has four pure states, the
> vertices, and the two diagonals of the square have the same midpoint. Its observables are the affine
> functions on the square, recorded by their values at the four vertices `0, 1, 2, 3`, with the
> diagonal relation `v 0 + v 3 = v 1 + v 2`. An observable is nonnegative when all four values are.
>
> The nonnegative observables are generated by the four facets: the affine functions that vanish on
> one side of the square. An observable of the composite of `E` with the square is recorded by its
> four vertex values in `E`, which again satisfy the diagonal relation.

## ii. Key results

> - `Square` : the observables of the square, an order-unit space.
> - `Square.facet` : the four facets.
> - `Square.exists_eq_sum_facet` : every nonnegative observable is a nonnegative combination of
>   facets.
> - `Square.vals` : the vertex values of an observable of `E ⊗ Square`.
> - `Square.vals_injective` : vertex values determine the observable.
