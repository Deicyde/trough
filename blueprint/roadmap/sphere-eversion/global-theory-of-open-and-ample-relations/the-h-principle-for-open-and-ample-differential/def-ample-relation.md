# ample_relation

A relation $\mathcal{R}$ is ample if, for every $σ = (x, y, φ)$ in $\mathcal{R}$ and every $(λ, v)$, the slice $\mathcal{R}_{σ, λ, v}$ is ample in $T_yY$.
