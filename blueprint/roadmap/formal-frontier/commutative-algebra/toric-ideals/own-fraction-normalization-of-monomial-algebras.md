# Own-fraction normalization of monomial algebras

For a monomial algebra in arbitrarily many variables over an integrally closed commutative coefficient domain, its normalization in its own fraction ring has canonical polynomial-fraction-field image generated by monomials whose exponents are differences of submonoid elements and have a positive multiple in the submonoid.
