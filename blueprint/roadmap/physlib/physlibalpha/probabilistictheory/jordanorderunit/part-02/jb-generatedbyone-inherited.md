> The closed subalgebra `C(a)` generated by one observable is itself an ordered JB-algebra.

## i. Overview

> The closed subalgebra `C(a)` generated by one observable inherits the order, the unit, square
> positivity and the JB norm of the ambient JB-algebra, so it is itself a JB-algebra and an
> Archimedean order-unit space.

## ii. Key results

> - `JBAlgebra.ClosedGeneratedByOne.instJBAlgebra` : `C(a)` is a JB-algebra.
> - `JBAlgebra.ClosedGeneratedByOne.instIsJBOrderUnit` : `C(a)` is an ordered JB-algebra.
> - `JBAlgebra.ClosedGeneratedByOne.instNormedJordanAlgebra` : `C(a)` is a normed Jordan algebra.
> - `JBAlgebra.ClosedGeneratedByOne.instArchimedeanOrderUnitSpace` : `C(a)` is an Archimedean
>   order-unit space.
