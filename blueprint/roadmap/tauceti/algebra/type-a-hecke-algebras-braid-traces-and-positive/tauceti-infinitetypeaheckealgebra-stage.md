# TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra.stage

The stage of the first `n` strands: the subalgebra generated by the generators
`T 0, …, T (n - 2)` that cross two of the strands `0, …, n - 1`. It is the image of the type-A
Hecke algebra on `n` strands.

```lean

  Subalgebra R (TauCeti.InfiniteTypeAHeckeAlgebra R q)
```
