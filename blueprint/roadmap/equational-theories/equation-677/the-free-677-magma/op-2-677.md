# Additional property

If $x,y \in M_X$, then

$$
x \diamond ((y \diamond x) \diamond y) = (x, (y \diamond x) \diamond y).
$$

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

Write $z := y \diamond x$, $u = z \diamond y$, $v = x \diamond u$. Our task is to show that $v = (x,u)$.

From [Properties of operation](op-prop.md) we know that $y$ is upper bounded by one of $z,x$, $z$ is upper bounded by one of $u,y$, and $x$ is upper bounded by one of $u,v$. So out of $x,y,z,u,v$, the only elements that can be maximal in this set are $u$ and $v$. If $v$ is maximal, then by [Properties of operation](op-prop.md) we have $v = (x,u)$ as required, hence we may assume for contradiction that $u$ is maximal. From [Properties of operation](op-prop.md), this implies that $u = (z,y)$ and $u = (u_L, (x \diamond u_L) \diamond x)$, hence $u_L = z$ and $y = (x \diamond z) \diamond x$.

From [Properties of operation](op-prop.md), $x$ is upper bounded by one of $x \diamond z$ and $z$, and $x \diamond z$ is upper bounded by one of $y$ and $x$. We also recall that $y$ was upper bounded by one of $z,x$. We conclude that out of $x,y,z,x \diamond z$, the only one that can be maximal is $z$. In particular $z$ is not bounded by $x$, hence by [Properties of operation](op-prop.md) $z = (y,x)$. $z$ is also not bounded by $x \diamond z$, hence by [Properties of operation](op-prop.md) $x \diamond z = z_L = y$, hence $y = y \diamond x = z$, contradicting the fact that $y$ was not maximal. This gives the required contradiction.
