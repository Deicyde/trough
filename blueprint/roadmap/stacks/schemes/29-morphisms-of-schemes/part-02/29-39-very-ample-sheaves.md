> **Stacks Tag 01VN** (EGA II, Proposition 4.6.2). Let `f : X ⟶ S` be a morphism of schemes and
> `L` an invertible `𝒪_X`-module. If `f` is quasi-compact and `L` is very ample relative to `f`,
> then `L` is ample relative to `f`.
>
> Relative very ampleness is [Stacks, Tag 01VM] at the named twist of the projective bundle:
> an immersion `i : X ⟶ 𝐏(E)` over `S` into the projective bundle of a quasi-coherent
> `𝒪_S`-module `E`, with `L ≅ i^* 𝒪_{𝐏(E)}(1)`. Relative ampleness is [Stacks, Tag 01VH]:
> quasi-compactness of `f` together with ampleness of `L|_{f⁻¹V}` for every affine open `V ⊆ S`.

```lean

References: EGA II (4.4.5).

> **Stacks Tag 01VR.** Let `f : X ⟶ S` be a quasi-compact morphism of schemes and `L` an
> invertible `𝒪_X`-module. The following are equivalent:
>
> 1. `L` is relatively very ample on `X/S`;
> 2. there is an open covering `S = ⋃ V_j` such that `L|_{f⁻¹(V_j)}` is relatively very ample on
>    `f⁻¹(V_j)/V_j` for all `j`;
> 3. there are a quasi-coherent graded `𝒪_S`-algebra `A` generated in degree one and a map of graded
>    algebras `ψ : f^* A ⟶ ⨁_{n ≥ 0} L^{⊗ n}` such that `f^* A_1 ⟶ L` is surjective and the
>    associated morphism `r_{L, ψ} : X ⟶ Proj_S(A)` is an immersion;
> 4. `f` is quasi-separated, the canonical map `f^* f_* L ⟶ L` is surjective and the associated
>    morphism `r_{L, ψ} : X ⟶ 𝐏(f_* L)` is an immersion.

```lean
theorem tfae_isRelativelyVeryAmple (f : X ⟶ S) [QuasiCompact f] (L : X.Modules)
    [IsInvertibleModule X L] :
    List.TFAE [IsRelativelyVeryAmple f L, IsRelativelyVeryAmpleLocallyOnBase f L,
      HasImmersionToRelativeProj f L, IsImmersionDirectImageBundle f L]
```

## Tag 0B3F, a lemma

[Tag 0B3F](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0B3F) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.Tag01VMVeryAmple.isRelativelyVeryAmple_pullback_fst`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeVeryAmpleTwist/BaseChangeTwist.lean, line 113](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/RelativeVeryAmpleTwist/BaseChangeTwist.lean#L113). References: EGA II (4.4.10); SGA 1 VIII (5.8), “Very ample clause”.

> **Stacks Tag 0B3F: relative very ampleness is preserved by base change.** Let `f : X ⟶ S` be
> a morphism of schemes, `L` an invertible `𝒪_X`-module which is very ample relative to `f`, and
> `g : S' ⟶ S` a morphism of schemes. Then the pullback of `L` to `S' ×_S X` is very ample relative
> to the projection `S' ×_S X ⟶ S'`.
>
> The proof is the source's. Relative very ampleness gives a quasi-coherent `𝒪_S`-module `E`, an
> immersion `i : X ⟶ 𝐏(E)` over `S` and an isomorphism `L ≅ i^* 𝒪_{𝐏(E)}(1)`. The module `g^* E` is
> quasi-coherent (Tag 01BG), its projective bundle is the base change of `𝐏(E)` and the comparison
> morphism `b` pulls the twist back to the twist (Tag 01O3,
> `Stacks.Tag01VMVeryAmple.projectiveBundleTwistBaseChange`), so the morphism `i'` induced by
> `S' ×_S X ⟶ X ⟶ 𝐏(E)` and `S' ×_S X ⟶ S'` is a base change of `i`, hence an immersion
> (Tag 01JY), it lies over `S'`, and the pullback of `L` is `i'^* 𝒪_{𝐏(g^* E)}(1)`.

```lean
theorem isRelativelyVeryAmple_pullback_fst {X S' : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ S) (g : S' ⟶ S)
    (L : X.Modules) [IsInvertibleModule X L] (hL : IsRelativelyVeryAmple f L) :
    IsRelativelyVeryAmple (pullback.fst g f)
      ((Scheme.Modules.pullback (pullback.snd g f)).obj L)
```
