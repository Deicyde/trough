# Descent and effective descent in rigid categories

> For a ring object in a rigidly compactly generated, braided closed tensor-triangulated category, descent type, effective descent type, nil-purity of the unit fiber and the existence of a pure monomorphism from the unit into its thick tensor ideal are equivalent. The development proves Brown representability and gives tools for compact generation, homotopy limits of towers and pro-constancy.
