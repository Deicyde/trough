# The fundamental group of the circle is ℤ

The complex unit circle has π₁(Circle,1) ≃* Multiplicative ℤ with its counterclockwise generator sent to +1, while for every nonzero real period T the equivalence π₁(AddCircle T,0) ≃* Multiplicative ℤ sends the projected real segment from 0 to Tn to n for every integer n.
