# bernstein

The *Bernstein coefficients* of a polynomial of degree at most $2$ in each of five variables are obtained by applying the transformation $(a_0, a_1, a_2) \mapsto (b_0, b_1, b_2)$ along each variable in turn. There are $3^5 = 243$ of them.
