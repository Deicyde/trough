# Brauer duality for the symplectic group on two strands

Over an integral domain containing
a unit `t` with `t ^ m ≠ 1` for `0 < m ≤ 4`, the operators on `K^{2n} ⊗ K^{2n}` commuting with
the diagonal action of the symplectic group form the algebra generated by the two Brauer diagrams
on two strands other than the identity: the crossing `TauCeti.symplecticCrossing` and the cap-cup
diagram `TauCeti.symplecticCupCap`.

```lean
TauCeti.adjoin_symplecticCrossing_symplecticCupCap_eq_centralizer.{u_1} {K : Type u_1} [CommRing K]
  {n : ℕ} [IsDomain K] (ht : ∃ t, ∀ (m : ℕ), 0 < m → m ≤ 4 → t ^ m ≠ 1) :
  K[TauCeti.symplecticCrossing K n, TauCeti.symplecticCupCap K n] =
    Subalgebra.centralizer K (Set.range ⇑((TauCeti.stdSymplecticRep K n).tensorPower 2))
```
