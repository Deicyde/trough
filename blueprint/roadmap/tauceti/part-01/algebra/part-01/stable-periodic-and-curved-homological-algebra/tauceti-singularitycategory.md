# TauCeti.SingularityCategory

The **singularity category** `Dᵇ(C) / Perf(C)` of an abelian category: the Verdier quotient
of the bounded derived category by the thick subcategory generated by the projective objects.

```lean
TauCeti.SingularityCategory.{w, v, u} (C : Type u) [CategoryTheory.Category.{v, u} C]
  [CategoryTheory.Abelian C] [HasDerivedCategory C] : Type (max u v)
```
