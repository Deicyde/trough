# charFun_map_eq_charFunDual_smul

Let $\mu$ be a measure on $F$ and let $L \in F^*$. Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\widehat{L_*\mu}(x) &= \hat{\mu}(x \cdot L) \: .
\end{aligned}
$$
