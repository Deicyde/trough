# Integrality of polynomial variable renaming

Over any commutative ring `R`,
[`MvPolynomial.isIntegral_rename_iff`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/RenameIntegral.lean)
states that renaming between the full polynomial rings is integral exactly
when the variable map is surjective or the coefficient ring is subsingleton.
See the [integrality section](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#integrality-of-polynomial-variable-renaming)
for the precise map, generality and applications.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
