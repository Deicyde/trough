# MarkovProcess

> A Lean formalization of continuous-path Markov processes associated with conservative Feller transition semigroups on nonempty locally compact Polish spaces. A uniform Kolmogorov displacement-moment bound yields a unique measurable kernel from each starting point to continuous-path space, with the prescribed finite-dimensional distributions. The library also develops strong Markov properties, generators and resolvents, stopping and killing, and Brownian motion as an example.
