## Theorem 1

## Number fields of degree $n$

For a natural number $n$, the type of number fields of degree
$n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$: an element is a type $K$ with a field structure making $K$ a number field
(a field of characteristic zero that is finite-dimensional over $\mathbb{Q}$) such that
$\dim_{\mathbb{Q}} K = n$.

## Jacobian determinant in the quotient ring

$E_1, \dots, E_r \in \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]$, the image in the quotient ring
$\mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]/(E_1, \dots, E_r)$ of the Jacobian determinant

## The open subscheme where the Jacobian does not vanish

For polynomials
$E_1, \dots, E_r \in \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]$, the open subscheme of
$\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]/(E_1, \dots, E_r)$ where the Jacobian
determinant $\det(\partial E_i / \partial x_j)$ does not vanish, that is, the basic open set of

## Couveignes: number fields have small models

There is a positive integer $Q$ such that for
every natural number $n \ge Q$ and every number field $K$ of degree $n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, with
discriminant $d_K$ and root discriminant $\delta_K = |d_K|^{1/n}$, there are natural numbers $r$
and $d$ with $r \le Q \log n$, $d \le Q \log n$ and $\binom{d + r}{r} \le Q n \log n$, and
polynomials $E_1, \dots, E_r \in \mathbb{Z}[x_1, \dots, x_r]$ of total degree at most $d$, all of
whose coefficients have absolute value at most $(n \delta_K)^{Q \log n}$, with the following
property. Let $X$ be the open subscheme of
$\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Q}[x_1, \dots, x_r]/(E_1, \dots, E_r)$ where
$\det(\partial E_i / \partial x_j) \ne 0$. Then $X$ has an irreducible component $Z$ which, with
its subscheme structure as the scheme-theoretic closure of the open set of points of $X$ lying on
no other irreducible component, is isomorphic to $\operatorname{Spec} K$.

## Isomorphism of number fields with bounded discriminant

For natural numbers $n$ and $H$,
two number fields $K$ and $L$ of degree $n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, each with discriminant of absolute
value at most $H$, are related when there exists a ring isomorphism from $K$ to $L$.

## Couveignes: counting number fields with bounded discriminant

There is a positive integer
$Q$ such that for all natural numbers $n \ge Q$ and $H \ge 1$, there are only finitely many
isomorphism classes of number fields of degree $n$ over $\mathbb{Q}$ whose discriminant has
absolute value at most $H$, and their number is at most $n^{Q n (\log n)^3} H^{Q (\log n)^3}$.
