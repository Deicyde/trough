## The standard 23-cycle in $M_{23}$

The permutation of $\{0, 1, \dots, 22\}$ that sends
$i$ to $i + 1$ for $i < 22$ and sends $22$ to $0$, that is, the 23-cycle
$(0\ 1\ 2\ \cdots\ 22)$. It is the first of the two standard generators of the Mathieu group
$M_{23}$.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/GroupTheory/finite-simple-groups/mathieu-group-m/index.html#m23-cycle).

## The standard product of four 5-cycles in $M_{23}$

The permutation of the points
$\{0, 1, \dots, 22\}$ given by the product of the four disjoint 5-cycles
$(2\ 16\ 9\ 6\ 8)(3\ 12\ 13\ 18\ 4)(7\ 17\ 10\ 11\ 22)(14\ 19\ 21\ 20\ 15)$, where a cycle
$(a_1\ a_2\ \cdots\ a_5)$ sends $a_k$ to $a_{k+1}$ for $k < 5$ and sends $a_5$ to $a_1$. It
fixes the three remaining points $0$, $1$ and $5$, and it is the second of the two standard
generators of the Mathieu group $M_{23}$.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/GroupTheory/finite-simple-groups/mathieu-group-m/index.html#m23-five-cycles).

## The Mathieu group $M_{23}$

The subgroup of the symmetric group on $\{0, 1, \dots, 22\}$
generated by the standard 23-cycle $(0\ 1\ 2\ \cdots\ 22)$ and the standard product of four
disjoint 5-cycles $(2\ 16\ 9\ 6\ 8)(3\ 12\ 13\ 18\ 4)(7\ 17\ 10\ 11\ 22)(14\ 19\ 21\ 20\ 15)$.

Formalized by `MathieuGroup.M23`, a definition declared in [MathlibExt/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Mathieu.lean, line 37](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/a3690dd2ad41e1ad4d871b062779ed7b0452800d/MathlibExt/GroupTheory/SpecificGroups/Mathieu.lean#L37). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/GroupTheory/finite-simple-groups/mathieu-group-m/index.html#m23).

## Subgroups containing $M_{23}$

A subgroup $H$ of the symmetric group on
$\{0, 1, \dots, 22\}$ contains the Mathieu group $M_{23}$ if and only if $H$ contains both the
standard 23-cycle and the standard product of four 5-cycles that generate $M_{23}$.
