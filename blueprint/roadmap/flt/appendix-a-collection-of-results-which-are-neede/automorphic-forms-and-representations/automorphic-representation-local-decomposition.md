# automorphic\_representation\_local\_decomposition

An irreducible admissible $(G(\mathbb{A}_N^f)\times U_\infty,\mathfrak{g})$-module is a restricted tensor product of irreducible representations $\pi_v$ of $G(N_v)$ as $v$ runs through the finite places of $N$, tensored with a tensor product of irreducible $(\mathfrak{g}_v,U_{\infty,v})$-modules as $v$ runs through the infinite places of $N$. The representations $\pi_v$ are unramified for all but finitely many $v$.
