## Markov property, set-integral form

Let $S$ be a nonempty standard Borel space, $\mu$ a
finite measure on $S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$ the path measure on
$S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the Markov chain with initial measure $\mu$ and transition kernel $\kappa$.
Let $Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and measurable for the product
$\sigma$-algebra, let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\theta_n$ be the shift
$(\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}$, and let $\mathcal{F}_n$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by
the coordinates $\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n$. For $x \in S$, let $E_x[Y]$ be the integral of $Y$
against the path measure of the chain started at $x$. Then for every set $A \in \mathcal{F}_n$,

$$\int_A Y(\theta_n \omega) \, dP_\mu(\omega) = \int_A E_{\omega_n}[Y] \, dP_\mu(\omega).$$

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/markov-chains/markov-and-strong-markov-properties/index.html#markov-set-integral-shift).

## Markov property

Let $S$ be a nonempty standard Borel space, $\mu$ a finite measure on
$S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$ the path measure on $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the
Markov chain with initial measure $\mu$ and transition kernel $\kappa$. Let
$Y : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$ be bounded and measurable for the product $\sigma$-algebra,
let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $\theta_n$ be the shift $(\theta_n \omega)_k = \omega_{n+k}$, and let
$\mathcal{F}_n$ be the $\sigma$-algebra generated by the coordinates $\omega_0, \dots, \omega_n$.
For $x \in S$, let $E_x[Y]$ be the integral of $Y$ against the path measure of the chain started
at $x$. Then for $P_\mu$-almost every $\omega$,

$$E_{P_\mu}[Y \circ \theta_n \mid \mathcal{F}_n](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/blueprint/roadmap/Probability/markov-chains/markov-and-strong-markov-properties/\omega) = E_{\omega_n}[Y].$$

In other words, conditioning on the trajectory up to time $n$ depends only on the state at
time $n$.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/markov-chains/markov-and-strong-markov-properties/index.html#markov-property-full).

## Path shifted by a random time

For a type $S$ and a function
$T : (\mathbb{N} \to S) \to \mathbb{N}$ (typically a stopping time), the shift by $T$ sends a path
$\omega : \mathbb{N} \to S$ to the path $k \mapsto \omega(T(\omega) + k)$.

Formalized by `MathlibExt.Probability.StrongMarkovChain.stoppedPathShift`, a definition declared in [MathlibExt/Probability/StrongMarkovChain.lean, line 59](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/Probability/StrongMarkovChain.lean#L59). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/Probability/markov-chains/markov-and-strong-markov-properties/index.html#stopped-path-shift).

## The shift by a random time agrees with the fixed shift on its level set

Let $S$ be a
type, let $T : (\mathbb{N} \to S) \to \mathbb{N}$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$, and let
$\omega : \mathbb{N} \to S$ be a path with $T(\omega) = n$. Then the path $\omega$ shifted by $T$,
namely $k \mapsto \omega(T(\omega) + k)$, equals the path $\omega$ shifted by $n$, namely
$k \mapsto \omega(n + k)$.

## A.e. equality from a.e. equality on the fibers of a natural-valued function

Let $\mu$
be a measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a function whose
fibers $\{T = n\}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, are all measurable, and let $f, g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$.
If for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ we have $f = g$ almost everywhere with respect to the restriction
of $\mu$ to $\{T = n\}$, then $f = g$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.

## A.e. equality from a.e. equality on the finite fibers

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a
measurable space $\Omega$, let $\tau : \Omega \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$ satisfy
$\tau \ne \infty$ $\mu$-almost everywhere and have every fiber $\{\tau = n\}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$,
measurable, and let $f, g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$. If for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ we have
$f = g$ almost everywhere with respect to the restriction of $\mu$ to $\{\tau = n\}$, then
$f = g$ $\mu$-almost everywhere.

## Strong Markov property for a.s.-finite stopping times

Let $S$ be a nonempty standard
Borel space, $\mu$ a finite measure on $S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$ the law
on the path space $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the Markov chain with initial distribution $\mu$ and
transition kernel $\kappa$. For $x \in S$ write $E_x$ for the expectation under the chain started
from $x$, and for a path $\omega$ and $n \in \mathbb{N}$ write $\theta_n \omega$ for the shifted
path $k \mapsto \omega(n + k)$. Let $T : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{N} \cup \{\infty\}$ be a
stopping time for the path filtration, with $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{F}_T$, such that
$T \ne \infty$ $P_\mu$-almost surely, and let $Y_n : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$, for
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, be measurable functions with $|Y_n(\omega)| \le C$ for a single constant $C$
and all $n$ and $\omega$. Then the conditional expectation under $P_\mu$ given $\mathcal{F}_T$ of
$\omega \mapsto Y_{T(\omega)}(\theta_{T(\omega)} \omega)$ equals
$\omega \mapsto E_{\omega(T(\omega))}[Y_{T(\omega)}]$ $P_\mu$-almost surely.

On the null event $\{T = \infty\}$ both sides are evaluated at the same unspecified finite time,
which does not affect the almost-sure equality.

## Strong Markov property for everywhere-finite stopping times

Let $S$ be a nonempty
standard Borel space, $\mu$ a finite measure on $S$, $\kappa$ a Markov kernel on $S$, and $P_\mu$
the law on the path space $S^{\mathbb{N}}$ of the Markov chain with initial distribution $\mu$ and
transition kernel $\kappa$; for $x \in S$ write $E_x$ for the expectation under the chain started
from $x$. Let $T : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for the path filtration, with
$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal{F}_T$, and let $Y_n : S^{\mathbb{N}} \to \mathbb{R}$, for
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, be measurable functions with $|Y_n(\omega)| \le C$ for a single constant $C$
and all $n$ and $\omega$. Write $\theta_T \omega$ for the path $\omega$ shifted by $T$, namely
$k \mapsto \omega(T(\omega) + k)$. Then the conditional expectation under $P_\mu$ given
$\mathcal{F}_T$ of $\omega \mapsto Y_{T(\omega)}(\theta_T \omega)$ equals
$\omega \mapsto E_{\omega(T(\omega))}[Y_{T(\omega)}]$ $P_\mu$-almost surely.

This is the special case of the strong Markov property for a.s.-finite stopping times in which
the time is finite everywhere.
