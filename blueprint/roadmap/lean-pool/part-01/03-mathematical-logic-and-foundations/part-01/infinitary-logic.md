# Infinitary logic and countable model theory

> Formalizes the syntax and semantics of infinitary logic and a broad collection of its classical theorems: Scott sentences and ranks, Karp's theorem, model existence, the sharp Morley--Hanf bound, Craig, Lyndon, and Malitz interpolation, López--Escobar definability, undefinability of well-ordering, the Silver--Burgess and Morley counting dichotomies, and an honest coded-fragment interface whose HF compactness theorem is derived from first-order compactness.
