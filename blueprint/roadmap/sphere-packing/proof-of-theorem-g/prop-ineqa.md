# ineqA

Consider the function $A:(0,\infty)\to\mathbb{C}$ defined as

$$
A(t):=-t^2\phi_0(i/t)-\frac{36}{\pi^2}\,\psi_I(it).\qquad\text{(1)}
$$

Then

$$
A(t) < 0\qquad\text{(2)}
$$

for all $t > 0$.
