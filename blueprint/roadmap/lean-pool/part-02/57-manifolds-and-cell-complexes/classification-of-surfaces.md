# Classification of Compact Surfaces

> Formalizes the classification of compact connected Hausdorff topological 2-manifolds with boundary as the sphere, an orientable normal-form quotient, or a non-orientable normal-form quotient, using triangulation and finite cyclic polygonal presentations.
