# Polynomial closed points and algebraic-closure orbits

For independently
universed fields `K` and `L` with their given `Algebra K L` and
`IsAlgClosure K L`,
[`Polynomial.algAutOrbitEquivMaximalSpectrum`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/PolynomialClosedPoints.lean)
identifies the existing quotient of `L` by its `K`-algebra automorphism orbits
with `MaximalSpectrum K[X]`. The ideal of the orbit of `a` is the kernel of
`aeval a`, equivalently the ideal generated by `minpoly K a`. No perfectness
or separability is assumed. This is a set equivalence; over an imperfect
field, a singleton orbit need not be a rational point.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
