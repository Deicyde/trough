# Strong Markov property of i.i.d. sequences

## Post-stopping-time sequence

Given maps $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ from a set $\Omega$ to a set
$S$ and a map $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$, the post-stopping-time sequence is the sequence
$(Y_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ of maps from $\Omega$ to $S$ given by
$Y_k(\omega) = X_{T(\omega)+k+1}(\omega)$. When $T$ is a stopping time, this is the sequence of
observations immediately after time $T$.

## Shifted terms of an identically distributed sequence

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a
measurable space $\Omega$ and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable
space $S$ such that $X_i$ and $X_j$ are identically distributed under $\mu$ for all $i, j$. Then
for all $n, k \in \mathbb{N}$ the map $X_{n+k+1}$ has the same distribution under $\mu$ as
$X_0$.

## A stopping-time event is independent of a later observation

Let $\mu$ be a probability
measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a filtration
on $\Omega$, and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable space
$S$ that are mutually independent under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the σ-algebra
$\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for all
$n, k \in \mathbb{N}$ and every measurable set $A \subseteq S$,
$\mu(\{T = n\} \cap X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A)) = \mu(\{T = n\})\,\mu(X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A))$.

## Level sets of a stopping time are measurable

Let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a
filtration on a measurable space $\Omega$ and let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time
for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$ the set $\{\omega : T(\omega) = n\}$ is
measurable in the σ-algebra of $\Omega$.

## The post-stopping-time sequence is a.e. measurable

Let $\mu$ be a measure on a measurable
space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a filtration on $\Omega$, let
$X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable space $S$ that are each
$\mu$-almost-everywhere measurable, and let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for
$(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ the map
$\omega \mapsto X_{T(\omega)+k+1}(\omega)$ is $\mu$-almost-everywhere measurable.

## Strong Markov property for i.i.d. sequences: the law after a stopping time

Let $\mu$ be a
probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a
filtration on $\Omega$, and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a
measurable space $S$ that are mutually independent and identically distributed under $\mu$.
Suppose that for every $n$ the σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated
by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let $T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for
$(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$ the map

## A stopping-time event is independent of finitely many later observations

Let $\mu$ be a
probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a
filtration on $\Omega$, and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a
measurable space $S$ that are mutually independent under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the
σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every
$n \in \mathbb{N}$, every finite set $F \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ and all sets $A_k \subseteq S$
($k \in \mathbb{N}$) such that $A_k$ is measurable for every $k \in F$,
$\mu(\{T = n\} \cap \bigcap_{k \in F} X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A_k)) =
\mu(\{T = n\}) \prod_{k \in F} \mu(X_{n+k+1}^{-1}(A_k))$.

## Preimage measures of a post-stopping-time variable

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on
a measurable space $\Omega$, let $(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a filtration on $\Omega$,
and let $X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable space $S$ that are
mutually independent and identically distributed under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the
σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then for every
$k \in \mathbb{N}$ and every measurable set $A \subseteq S$,
$\mu(\{\omega : X_{T(\omega)+k+1}(\omega) \in A\}) = \mu(X_0^{-1}(A))$.

## Strong Markov property for i.i.d. sequences: the post-stopping-time sequence is i.i.d

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on a measurable space $\Omega$, let
$(\mathcal{F}_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ be a filtration on $\Omega$, and let
$X_0, X_1, X_2, \dots$ be measurable maps from $\Omega$ to a measurable space $S$ that are
mutually independent and identically distributed under $\mu$. Suppose that for every $n$ the
σ-algebra $\mathcal{F}_n$ is contained in the σ-algebra generated by $X_0, \dots, X_n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb{N}$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal{F}_n)$. Then the
post-stopping-time sequence $Y_k(\omega) = X_{T(\omega)+k+1}(\omega)$, $k \in \mathbb{N}$, is
again i.i.d. with the law of $X_0$: each $Y_k$ has the same distribution under $\mu$ as $X_0$,
and $(Y_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}}$ is mutually independent under $\mu$.

## Strong Markov property (independence from the past)

Let $X_0, X_1, \dots$ be measurable
random variables on a probability space $(\Omega, \mu)$ with values in a measurable space $S$,
mutually independent and all with the same distribution. Let $(\mathcal F_n)_{n \ge 0}$ be a
filtration with $\mathcal F_n \subseteq \sigma(X_0, \dots, X_n)$ for every $n$, and let
$T : \Omega \to \mathbb N$ be a stopping time for $(\mathcal F_n)$, finite everywhere. Then the
stopped σ-algebra $\mathcal F_T$ is independent of the σ-algebra generated by the
post-stopping-time sequence $(X_{T+k+1})_{k \ge 0}$.
