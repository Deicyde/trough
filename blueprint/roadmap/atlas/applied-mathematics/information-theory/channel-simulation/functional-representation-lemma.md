## Sigma-algebra of the auxiliary coordinate

For measurable spaces $X$, $Y$ and $S$, this is
the sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$ generated by the projection $((x, y), s) \mapsto s$
onto $S$, regarded as a sub-sigma-algebra of the product sigma-algebra on
$(X \times Y) \times S$.

## Sigma-algebra of the input and auxiliary coordinates

For measurable spaces $X$, $Y$ and
$S$, this is the sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$ generated by the map
$((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, s)$ into $X \times S$ with its product sigma-algebra, regarded as a
sub-sigma-algebra of the product sigma-algebra on $(X \times Y) \times S$.

## Conditional entropy of the output given the auxiliary variable

For a probability measure
$\nu$ on $(X \times Y) \times S$, $H(Y \mid S)$ is the conditional Shannon entropy under $\nu$, in
bits and with values in $[0, \infty]$, of the coordinate $((x, y), s) \mapsto y$ given the
sigma-algebra generated by the coordinate $((x, y), s) \mapsto s$.

## Functional representation of a joint law

Let $\mu$ be a probability measure on
$X \times Y$ and $\nu$ a probability measure on $(X \times Y) \times S$. Then $\nu$ is a functional
representation of $\mu$ when all of the following hold: the image of $\nu$ under
$((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, y)$ is $\mu$; the mutual information of the image of $\nu$ under
$((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, s)$ is zero; the conditional entropy $H(Y \mid X, S)$ of $\nu$ is zero;
and there is a measurable map $g : X \times S \to Y$ with $g(x, s) = y$ for $\nu$-almost every
$((x, y), s)$.

Formalized by `InformationTheory.IsGeneralRepresentation`, a definition declared in [MathlibExt/InformationTheory/FunctionalRepresentation.lean, line 57](https://github.com/Deicyde/atlas-lean/blob/035e1808ca0c8983682b74153a02628d616d77ce/MathlibExt/InformationTheory/FunctionalRepresentation.lean#L57). [Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/InformationTheory/channel-simulation/functional-representation-lemma/index.html#is-general-representation).

## A functional representation has the prescribed joint law

Let $\mu$ be a probability
measure on $X \times Y$ and let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $(X \times Y) \times S$ that is a
functional representation of $\mu$. Then the image of $\nu$ under the projection
$((x, y), s) \mapsto (x, y)$ is $\mu$.

## A functional representation has a measurable recovery map

Let $\mu$ be a probability
measure on $X \times Y$ and let $\nu$ be a probability measure on $(X \times Y) \times S$ that is a
functional representation of $\mu$. Then there is a measurable map $g : X \times S \to Y$ such that
$g(x, s) = y$ for $\nu$-almost every $((x, y), s)$.
