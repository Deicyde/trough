# E2-transform-general

$$
(cz + d)^{-2} E_2\left(\frac{az + b}{cx + d}\right) = E_2(z) - \frac{6ic}{\pi (cz + d)}, \quad \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d\end{pmatrix} \in \mathrm{SL}_{2}(\mathbb{Z}).\qquad\text{(7)}
$$

## Depends on

- [E2-transform-S](lemma-e2-transform-s.md)
- [E2](def-e2.md)

## Proof

Use the fact that $\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ is generated by $S$ and $T$. Then (7) follows from [(6)](lemma-e2-transform-s.md) and $E_2|_T = E_2$.
