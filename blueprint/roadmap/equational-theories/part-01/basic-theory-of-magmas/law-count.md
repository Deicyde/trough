# Counting laws up to relabeling

Up to relabeling, the number of laws $w \simeq w'$ of total order $n$ is $C_{n+1} B_{n+2}$.

## Depends on

- [Pushforward](push.md)

## Proof

Follows from the properties of Catalan and Bell numbers.
