## Ideals below the different

Let $A$ be an integrally closed domain with fraction field
$K$, let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension, and let $B$ be the integral closure of $A$ in $L$;
assume that $B$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$ and is torsion-free as an
$A$-module. Write $B^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB) \subseteq A\}$ for the trace
dual of $B$ and $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ for the different ideal of $B$ over $A$. Then an ideal $I$
of $B$ satisfies $I \subseteq \mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ if and only if $I B^\vee \subseteq B$, where
the product is taken in the fractional ideals of $B$ in $L$.

[Its page on the ATLAS site](https://deicyde.github.io/atlas-lean/roadmap/RingTheory/ramification/different-and-discriminant-ideals/index.html#le-different-ideal-iff-mul-dual-le-one).

## The trace dual commutes with localization

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction
field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension, and let $B$ be the integral closure of $A$
in $L$, a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$. Let $S \subseteq A$ be a multiplicative set of
nonzerodivisors, let $A_S \subseteq K$ be the localization of $A$ at $S$ inside $K$, and let
$B_S$ be a localization of $B$ at the image of $S$ that is also an $A_S$-algebra, with a map
$B_S \to L$ such that all composite maps from $A$, $A_S$, $B$, $B_S$ and $K$ into $L$ agree.
Assume that $B_S$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$, torsion-free over $B$, and the
integral closure of $A_S$ in $L$. Write
$B^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB) \subseteq A\}$ and
$B_S^\vee = \{x \in L : \operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(xB_S) \subseteq A_S\}$ for the trace duals. Then
the fractional ideal of $B_S$ generated by $B^\vee$ is $B_S^\vee$.

## The different commutes with localization

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain with fraction
field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension, and let $B$ be the integral closure of $A$
in $L$, a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$ that is torsion-free as an $A$-module. Let

## Discriminant ideal of a Dedekind extension

Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind domains, with
$B$ an $A$-algebra that is finite and torsion-free as an $A$-module. The discriminant ideal
$\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}$ is the ideal of $A$ generated by the norms $N_{B/A}(x)$ of the elements $x$
of the different ideal $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ of $B$ over $A$.

## Tower formula for discriminant ideals

Let $A$, $B$, $C$ be Dedekind domains with
algebra maps $A \to B \to C$ whose composite is the algebra map $A \to C$, such that $B$ is
finite and torsion-free over $A$, $C$ is finite and torsion-free over $A$ and over $B$, and the
fraction field of $C$ is separable over the fraction field of $A$. Write $\mathfrak{d}_{B/A}$,
$\mathfrak{d}_{C/A}$ and $\mathfrak{d}_{C/B}$ for the discriminant ideals of $B$ over $A$, of
$C$ over $A$ and of $C$ over $B$, let $n = [\operatorname{Frac} C : \operatorname{Frac} B]$,
and for an ideal $I$ of $B$ let $N_{B/A}(I)$ be the ideal of $A$ generated by the norms of the
elements of $I$. Then
$\mathfrak{d}_{C/A} = \mathfrak{d}_{B/A}^{\,n} \, N_{B/A}(\mathfrak{d}_{C/B})$.

## The norm of the different commutes with localization

Let $A$ be a Dedekind domain
with fraction field $K$, let $L/K$ be a finite separable extension, and let $B$ be the
integral closure of $A$ in $L$, a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$ that is finite and
torsion-free as an $A$-module. Let $S \subseteq A$ be a multiplicative set of nonzerodivisors,
let $A_S \subseteq K$ be the localization of $A$ at $S$ inside $K$, and let $B_S$ be a
localization of $B$ at the image of $S$ that is also an $A$-algebra and an $A_S$-algebra, with
a map $B_S \to L$, such that all composite maps among $A$, $A_S$, $B$, $B_S$, $K$ and $L$
agree. Assume that $B_S$ is a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$, finite and torsion-free
over $A_S$, torsion-free over $B$, and the integral closure of $A_S$ in $L$. For an extension
$R \to T$ of Dedekind domains, write $\mathfrak{D}_{T/R}$ for its different ideal and, for an
ideal $I$ of $T$, write $N_{T/R}(I)$ for the ideal of $R$ generated by the norms of the
elements of $I$. Then the ideal of $A_S$ generated by the image of
$N_{B/A}(\mathfrak{D}_{B/A})$ is $N_{B_S/A_S}(\mathfrak{D}_{B_S/A_S})$.

## Element different

Let $A$ be a commutative ring, $B$ a commutative $A$-algebra, $L$ a
field that is a $B$-algebra, and $K$ a field with $L$ a $K$-algebra. For $\alpha \in B$, let
$f \in A[X]$ be the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $A$ (taken to be $0$ when $\alpha$ is not
integral over $A$). The element different $\delta_{B/A}(\alpha) \in B$ is $f'(\alpha)$ when the
image of $\alpha$ in $L$ generates $L$ as a $K$-algebra, and $0$ otherwise.

## The different of a monogenic extension

Let $A$ be an integrally closed domain with fraction
field $K$, let $L$ be a finite separable extension of $K$, and let $B$ be the integral closure of
$A$ in $L$, assumed to be a Dedekind domain with fraction field $L$ that is torsion-free as an
$A$-module, with compatible algebra maps $A \to K \to L$ and $A \to B \to L$. If $\alpha \in B$
generates $B$ as an $A$-algebra, that is $B = A[\alpha]$, and $f$ is the minimal polynomial of
$\alpha$ over $A$, then the different ideal $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$ is the principal ideal
$(f'(\alpha))$ of $B$.

## Only finitely many primes of the extension ramify

Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind domains,
where $B$ is an $A$-algebra that is finitely generated and torsion-free as an $A$-module and the
field of fractions of $B$ is separable over the field of fractions of $A$. Then only finitely many
nonzero prime ideals $\mathfrak P$ of $B$ are ramified over $A$. Here $\mathfrak P$ is unramified
over $A$ when the localization $B_{\mathfrak P}$ is formally unramified over $A$.

## Only finitely many primes of the base ramify

Let $A$ and $B$ be Dedekind domains, where
$B$ is an $A$-algebra that is finitely generated and torsion-free as an $A$-module and the field of
fractions of $B$ is separable over the field of fractions of $A$. Then only finitely many nonzero
prime ideals $\mathfrak p$ of $A$ are ramified in $B$. Here $\mathfrak p$ is unramified in $B$ when
every prime ideal $\mathfrak P$ of $B$ lying over $\mathfrak p$ is unramified over $A$, that is, the
localization $B_{\mathfrak P}$ is formally unramified over $A$.
