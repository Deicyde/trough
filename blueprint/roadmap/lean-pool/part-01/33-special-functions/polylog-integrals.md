# Polylogarithms and sixteen log-trigonometric integral identities

> Formalizes the sixteen A-series identities from rainrzk's PolyLog Integrals tutorial, together with reusable real and complex polylogarithm series, absolute convergence on the closed unit disk, differentiation, duplication, Euler reflection and Landen identities, logarithmic moments, Fubini kernels and radial integration machinery. The formulas involve Catalan's constant, zeta(3), Li_4(1/2) and Im Li_3((1+i)/2). These are classical identities, not new informal results. A004 uses the corrected factor pi^2/24; A014 uses a proved expression entirely inside the unit disk, replacing the tutorial's invalid series expansion. A015 uses radial differentiation instead of a half-disc contour integral. The formalization was materially completed on 2026-10-01 at 00:01:57 UTC. The 2022 informal tutorial is older; the eligibility date is that of the completed Lean proofs.
