# dimension-def

If $A\subseteq \mathbb{Z}^{d}$ then by $\dim(A)$ we mean the dimension of the span of $A-A$ over the reals – equivalently, the smallest $d'$ such that $A$ lies in a coset of a subgroup isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}^{d'}$.
