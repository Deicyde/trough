# Reservoir and book calculus for greedy Ramsey arguments

> Reservoirs track a clique and its pool of common neighbours. The winning-pivot theorem preserves a Pascal threshold for partially built two-colour clique spines. The development also proves greedy clique bounds from local non-adjacency budgets, books from triangle and edge density, book forcing, and anchored gluing for homogeneous block quotients. Starting from empty spines recovers Pascal upper bounds, including the Erdős–Szekeres binomial bound. A five-cycle gives the matching lower bound for R(3, 3) = 6. The Ramsey interface uses the two-colour Ramsey property in FiniteRamsey.
