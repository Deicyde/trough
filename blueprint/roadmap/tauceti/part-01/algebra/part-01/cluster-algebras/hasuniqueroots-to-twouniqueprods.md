# HasUniqueRoots.to_twoUniqueProds

A cancellative commutative monoid with unique roots has two unique products.

```lean
HasUniqueRoots.to_twoUniqueProds.{u_1} {M : Type u_1} [CancelCommMonoid M] [HasUniqueRoots M] :
  TwoUniqueProds M
```
