# The padded multi-codeword game adapter

Let $1\leq r\leq K$, $d\geq1$, $m\mid q$ and $(K+m)\mid q$, and split the points of $\mathbb{F}_q^{K+m}$ as $(x,y)$ with the $K$ combining coordinates first. A projective strategy of failure at most $\varepsilon$ at $(q,m,d,r)$ induces, on the same state and spaces, one at $(q,K+m,d,1)$ with failure at most $9(K+1)\varepsilon$. At $(x,y)$ its point answer is $\sum_{j=1}^r x_jb_j$, where $(b_1,\ldots,b_r)$ is the original answer at $y$. Axis answers have degree at most $d$, diagonal answers at most $md+1\leq(K+m)d$.

Both players share one seed offset. A combined question whose seed lies in block $i$ (counting from $0$) is sent to the original question with seed block $\max(i-K,0)$ and that offset, its line projected to the original coordinates and rebased; a line whose projection is a single point is sent to that point. Line answers are the original ones reparametrized along the combined line, multiplied by the combining coordinates and summed. All measurements are deterministic coarse-grainings once the offset is fixed, and the offset is chosen by averaging the complete game failure, not arbitrarily.

## Proof

On the two questions of one sample, the decider checks the formats, then equality for equal types, nothing for the two cross type pairs, and otherwise equality of the two answers' values at the sample's point. With a shared offset every pair of simulated questions is the pair of questions of a *single* original sample, whose point, seed and direction are the original parts of the combined ones and whose types are the combined ones, collapsed to the point type where the projected line is a point. Acceptance then transfers subtest by subtest: equal combined types have equal images, and on a line-versus-point pair the combined answer's value at the point is $\sum_j x_jb_j$ with $b$ the original answer's value at $y$.

The cost is the push-forward of the question distribution, averaged over the offset. The original sample, point, seed and offset determine the combined sample up to the three preimages of each type, the $q^{2K}$ combining coordinates of the point and the direction, and at most $(K+1)q/(K+m)$ combined seeds, the offset being determined; comparing the sizes of the two sample spaces, this is at most $9(K+1)$ times the original mass per offset.

This is not the paper's route, which draws the seeds independently for each player from the conditional seed law of the projected diagonal distribution and loses $27M(\varepsilon+d/q)$. Sharing the offset makes the simulated pair a single original sample, so the same-type line subtests need no chain of consistency links and the $d/q$ term does not arise.
