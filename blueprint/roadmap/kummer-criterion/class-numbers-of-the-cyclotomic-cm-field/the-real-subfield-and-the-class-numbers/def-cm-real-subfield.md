# cm-real-subfield

For a number field $K$, let $K^+$ denote its maximal real subfield, the largest subfield of $K$ all of whose complex embeddings are real. When $K$ is a CM field, $K^+$ is the fixed field of complex conjugation, and $[K : K^+] = 2$. For $K = \mathbb{Q}(\zeta_p)$ this is the usual maximal real subfield $\mathbb{Q}(\zeta_p + \zeta_p^{-1})$.
