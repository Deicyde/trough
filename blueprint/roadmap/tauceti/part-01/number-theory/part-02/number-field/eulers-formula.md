# Euler's formula

the trace dual of a monogenic order `ℤ[x]` is generated by the inverse
of the derivative of the minimal polynomial of `x`, evaluated at `x`.

```lean
TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder.traceDual_eq_spanSingleton_of_toSubalgebra_eq_adjoin.{u}
  {K : Type u} [Field K] [NumberField K] {O : TauCeti.GlobalNumberFields.NumberFieldOrder K} {x : K}
  (hx : O.toSubalgebra = ℤ[x]) :
  O.traceDual =
    (FractionalIdeal.spanSingleton (nonZeroDivisors ↥O.toSubalgebra))
      ((Polynomial.aeval x) (Polynomial.derivative (minpoly ℚ x)))⁻¹
```
