# The completed tensor product `(B, B⁺) ⊗̂_A (C, C⁺)` of Huber pairs along adic structure maps

Its ring is the separated completed tensor ring `CompletedTensor A B C`, and its plus ring
is the closure of the image of the integral closure of the subring of `B ⊗[A] C` generated by
`B⁺ ⊗ 1` and `1 ⊗ C⁺` (`TauCeti.Huber.Pair.completedTensor.plus_eq`).
