## A dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev inequality for subgaussian log-concave measures

We prove that every centered log-concave probability measure with a Lebesgue density on ℝ$^{n}$ and linear subgaussian parameter *a* satisfies $\mathop{\mathrm{Ent}}\nolimits _\mu(f^2)\le Ca^2\int|Df|^2\,d\mu$ for compactly supported smooth *f*, with one universal constant *C*. This resolves positively the dimension-free logarithmic Sobolev conjecture for subgaussian log-concave measures.
