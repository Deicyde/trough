# leech-distance-spectrum

Let $S$ be a periodic sphere packing in $\mathbb{R}^{24}$. We say that $S$ has the *Leech distance spectrum* if, for any distinct centers $x,y$ of $S$, the squared distance $\|x-y\|^2$ is of the form $2k$ with $k\ge 2$.
