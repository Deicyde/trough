# Binomial generation of toric ideals

Over any commutative ring, the toric ideal of
`a : ι → M` is generated by the binomials `X^u - X^v` with `∑ i, u i • a i = ∑ i, v i • a i`.

```lean
TauCeti.toricIdeal_eq_span.{u_1, u_2, u_4} {ι : Type u_1} {M : Type u_2} [AddCommMonoid M]
  (R : Type u_4) [CommRing R] (a : ι → M) :
  TauCeti.toricIdeal R a =
    Ideal.span
      {p |
        ∃ u v,
          (Finsupp.linearCombination ℕ a) u = (Finsupp.linearCombination ℕ a) v ∧
            p = (MvPolynomial.monomial u) 1 - (MvPolynomial.monomial v) 1}
```
