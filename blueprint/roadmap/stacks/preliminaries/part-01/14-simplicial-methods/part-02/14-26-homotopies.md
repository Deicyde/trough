> **Stacks 019K** (part 1).
> Given simplicial objects `U`, `V` of a category `C` for which the product
> `U × Δ[1] = U.copower Δ[1]` of [Stacks, Tag 017C] exists, a morphism `h : U × Δ[1] ⟶ V` is a
> *homotopy from `a` to `b`* for `a, b : U ⟶ V` when `a = e₀ ≫ h` and `b = e₁ ≫ h`, where
> `e₀, e₁ : U ⟶ U × Δ[1]` come from the two vertices `Δ[0] ⟶ Δ[1]` and the identification
> `U × Δ[0] = U`. In degree `m` these two vertex inclusions are the inclusions of the summands of
> `∐_{α ∈ Δ[1]_m} U_m` indexed by the constant `m`-simplices `SSet.stdSimplex.const 1 0 m` and
> `SSet.stdSimplex.const 1 1 m` of `Δ[1]`, which is how the two conditions are spelled here;
> equivalently, `h_{m, m + 1} = a_m` and `h_{m, 0} = b_m` in the notation of [Stacks, Tag 019L].

```lean
public def IsHomotopy (h : U.copower Δ[1] ⟶ V) (a b : U ⟶ V) : Prop
```

> **Stacks 019M**.
> Functors preserve homotopies of simplicial objects. Let `F : C ⥤ D` be any functor between any two
> categories, let `U`, `V` be simplicial objects of `C`, and let `a, b : U ⟶ V`. If
> `k : U × Δ[1] ⟶ V` is a homotopy from `a` to `b` in the sense of [Stacks, Tag 019K], then there is
> a homotopy `l : F(U) × Δ[1] ⟶ F(V)` from `F(a)` to `F(b)` whose component at each simplex
> `α ∈ Δ[1]_m` is `F` applied to the component of `k` at `α`; in the notation of [Stacks, Tag 019L],
> `l` is the family `{F(h_{m, i})}`. No compatibility of `F` with the coproducts making up the two
> products with `Δ[1]` is required.

```lean
public theorem IsHomotopy.exists_whiskerRight {U V : SimplicialObject C}
    [∀ m, HasCoproduct fun _ : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m => U.obj m] (F : C ⥤ D)
    [∀ m, HasCoproduct fun _ : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m => (U ⋙ F).obj m]
    {a b : U ⟶ V} {k : U.copower Δ[1] ⟶ V} (hk : IsHomotopy k a b) :
    ∃ l : (U ⋙ F).copower Δ[1] ⟶ V ⋙ F,
      (∀ (m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ) (α : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m),
          Sigma.ι (fun _ : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m => (U ⋙ F).obj m) α ≫ l.app m =
            F.map (Sigma.ι (fun _ : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m => U.obj m) α ≫ k.app m)) ∧
        IsHomotopy l (Functor.whiskerRight a F) (Functor.whiskerRight b F)
```

> **Stacks 08RJ**.
> Let `C` be any category. Being homotopic — the equivalence relation on morphisms of simplicial
> objects of `C` generated by the existence of a simplicial homotopy — is a congruence: if `a` and
> `a'` are homotopic then so are `b ∘ a` and `b ∘ a'` for any `b : V ⟶ W`, and so are `a ∘ c` and
> `a' ∘ c` for any `c : X ⟶ U`. Hence `CategoryTheory.Quotient` of this relation is the source's
> `hSimp(C)`, the category whose objects are the simplicial objects of `C` and whose morphisms are
> the homotopy classes of morphisms of simplicial objects, and `CategoryTheory.Quotient.functor` is
> the canonical functor `Simp(C) ⟶ hSimp(C)`, essentially surjective and surjective on sets of
> morphisms.

```lean
public theorem congruence_homotopic :
    Congruence fun (U V : SimplicialObject C) (a a' : U ⟶ V) ↦
      Relation.EqvGen (fun a a' : U ⟶ V ↦ Nonempty (Homotopy a a')) a a'
```

The attribute's comment: “the first sentence; being homotopy equivalent, the second sentence, is the composite `∃ a : U ⟶ V, homotopyEquivalences C a` and gets no name, as in Mathlib for complexes, where it is `Nonempty (HomotopyEquiv K L)`. Stated as a `MorphismProperty`, the shape of the sibling `HomologicalComplex.homotopyEquivalences`; being homotopic is `Relation.EqvGen fun f g => Nonempty (Homotopy f g)` as in 019S”.

> **Stacks 019N** (the first sentence).
> Let `C` be any category. A morphism `a : U ⟶ V` of simplicial objects of `C` is a *homotopy
> equivalence* if there is a morphism `b : V ⟶ U` such that `a ≫ b` is homotopic to `𝟙 U` and
> `b ≫ a` is homotopic to `𝟙 V`, where two morphisms are *homotopic* when they are related by the
> equivalence relation generated by the existence of a simplicial homotopy ([Stacks, Tag 019M]). The
> simplicial objects `U` and `V` are *homotopy equivalent* when some `a : U ⟶ V` is a homotopy
> equivalence, that is, when `∃ a : U ⟶ V, homotopyEquivalences C a`.

```lean
public def homotopyEquivalences : MorphismProperty (SimplicialObject C)
```

The attribute's comment: “the cylinder is any object with the universal property of `U × Δ[1]`, in place of a chosen construction, so that the source's finite coproducts on `C` are weakened to the coproducts making it up; the maps of [Stacks, Tag 017E] are pinned by their characterizing equations instead of being reconstructed; and the two homotopies are directed, as the source's proof produces them, which is stronger than the source's symmetric `are homotopic`”.

> The projection from the cylinder `U × Δ[1]` is a homotopy equivalence, with either vertex
> inclusion as a homotopy inverse. Let `U` be a simplicial object of a category `C`, and let `X`
> be a cylinder on `U`: a simplicial object together with morphisms `ι m α : U_m ⟶ X_m`, one for
> each `m`-simplex `α` of `Δ[1]`, which exhibit `X_m` as the coproduct `∐_{α ∈ Δ[1]_m} U_m` and
> satisfy `ι m α ≫ X(φ) = U(φ) ≫ ι m' (Δ[1](φ) α)`; this is the product `U × Δ[1]` of
> [Stacks, Tag 017C]. Let `π : X ⟶ U` be the projection, the identity on each summand, and let
> `e₀, e₁ : U ⟶ X` be the two vertex inclusions, in degree `m` the inclusions of the summands
> indexed by the constant `m`-simplices `SSet.stdSimplex.const 1 0 m` and
> `SSet.stdSimplex.const 1 1 m` of `Δ[1]`. Then `π ∘ e₀ = π ∘ e₁ = id_U`, the composite `e₀ ∘ π`
> admits a homotopy to `id_X`, and `id_X` admits a homotopy to `e₁ ∘ π`. In particular `π` is a
> homotopy equivalence ([Stacks, Tag 019N]), so `U × Δ[1]` is homotopy equivalent to `U`.

```lean
public theorem homotopyEquivalence_proj_cylinder
    (ι : ∀ m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ, (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m → (U.obj m ⟶ X.obj m))
    (hX : ∀ m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ,
      IsColimit (Cofan.mk (f := fun _ : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m => U.obj m) (X.obj m) (ι m)))
    (hι : ∀ {m m' : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ} (φ : m ⟶ m') (α : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m),
      ι m α ≫ X.map φ = U.map φ ≫ ι m' ((Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).map φ α))
    (π : X ⟶ U) (e₀ e₁ : U ⟶ X)
    (hπ : ∀ (m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ) (α : (Δ[1] : SSet.{w}).obj m),
      ι m α ≫ π.app m = 𝟙 (U.obj m))
    (he₀ : ∀ m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ, e₀.app m = ι m (stdSimplex.const 1 0 m))
    (he₁ : ∀ m : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ, e₁.app m = ι m (stdSimplex.const 1 1 m)) :
    (e₀ ≫ π = 𝟙 U ∧ e₁ ≫ π = 𝟙 U) ∧
      Nonempty (Homotopy (π ≫ e₀) (𝟙 X)) ∧ Nonempty (Homotopy (𝟙 X) (π ≫ e₁))
```

The products are pinned degreewise by limit fans, as in [Stacks, Tag 016Q], instead of by `HasProduct` in `SimplicialObject C`, and `f = ∏ a_t`, `g = ∏ b_t` by their characterizing equations. Part (1) is stated in the stronger form that the product of homotopy equivalences is one, the source's `homotopy equivalent` following by choice; part (3) is stated with the weaker hypothesis that only finitely many coordinates differ. > Products of homotopies and of homotopy equivalences of simplicial objects. Let `X t`, `Y t`
> (`t : T`) be families of simplicial objects of a category `C`, and let `p t : P ⟶ X t` and
> `q t : Q ⟶ Y t` exhibit `P` and `Q` as the products `∏_t X t` and `∏_t Y t`, that is, degreewise
> products in the sense of [Stacks, Tag 016Q]. Let `a t, b t : X t ⟶ Y t` be morphisms and let
> `f, g : P ⟶ Q` be their products, pinned by `f ≫ q t = p t ≫ a t` and `g ≫ q t = p t ≫ b t`. Then:
> * if there is a homotopy from `a t` to `b t` for every `t`, there is a homotopy from `f` to `g`
>   (part (2), no finiteness needed);
> * if `a t` and `b t` are homotopic for every `t` and `a t = b t` for all but finitely many `t`,
>   then `f` and `g` are homotopic (part (3));
> * if `T` is finite and every `a t` is a homotopy equivalence, then `f` is a homotopy
>   equivalence, so `∏_t X t` and `∏_t Y t` are homotopy equivalent (part (1)).

```lean
public theorem nonempty_homotopy_and_homotopyEquivalences_of_isLimit
    {T : Type w} {X Y : T → SimplicialObject C} {P Q : SimplicialObject C}
    (p : ∀ t, P ⟶ X t) (q : ∀ t, Q ⟶ Y t)
    (hP : ∀ n : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ, IsLimit (Fan.mk (P.obj n) fun t => (p t).app n))
    (hQ : ∀ n : SimplexCategoryᵒᵖ, IsLimit (Fan.mk (Q.obj n) fun t => (q t).app n))
    {a b : ∀ t, X t ⟶ Y t} {f g : P ⟶ Q}
    (hf : ∀ t, f ≫ q t = p t ≫ a t) (hg : ∀ t, g ≫ q t = p t ≫ b t) :
    ((∀ t, Nonempty (Homotopy (a t) (b t))) → Nonempty (Homotopy f g)) ∧
      ({t | a t ≠ b t}.Finite →
        (∀ t, Relation.EqvGen (fun u v : X t ⟶ Y t => Nonempty (Homotopy u v)) (a t) (b t)) →
          Relation.EqvGen (fun u v : P ⟶ Q => Nonempty (Homotopy u v)) f g) ∧
      (Finite T → (∀ t, homotopyEquivalences C (a t)) → homotopyEquivalences C f)
```
