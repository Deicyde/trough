# varphi-quasimodular-d24

There exist meromorphic modular forms $\varphi_1,\varphi_2$ on $\Gamma_1=\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})$ such that

$$
z^8\,\varphi\!\left(-\frac{1}{z}\right)=\varphi(z)+\frac{\varphi_1(z)}{z}+\frac{\varphi_2(z)}{z^2}.\qquad\text{(2)}
$$
