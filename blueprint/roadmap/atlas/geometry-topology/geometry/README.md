# Geometry

1,010 results of ATLAS's vault, 830 proved and 180 stated but not proved, in this subject's topics.

Geometry studies shapes, spaces, transformations, curvature, and the invariants preserved by geometric equivalence.

[Discrete geometry](discrete-geometry/README.md), the largest field with about a third of the subject's articles,
studies point configurations, lattice points, polyhedra and extremal shapes. Its largest topic,
[continuous Turán numbers](discrete-geometry/part-01/continuous-tur-n-numbers-of-planar-patterns.md), concerns
the supremum of the areas of open subsets of $[0, n]^2$ that avoid a planar pattern $P$, which for finite $P$ agrees
up to constant factors with the 0-1 matrix extremal function of $P$. The field also has
[lattice points in dilated regions](discrete-geometry/part-01/lattice-points-in-dilated-regions.md), the
[moving sofa](discrete-geometry/part-01/moving-sofa-problem.md) constant, between $2.2195$ and $2.37$, the
[Dehn invariant](discrete-geometry/part-01/dehn-invariant.md),
[distinct and unit distances](discrete-geometry/part-01/distinct-and-unit-distances.md), Monsky's theorem and
the Sylvester–Gallai theorem.

[Packings and coverings](packings-and-coverings/README.md), a fifth of the subject, has the
[Kepler conjecture](packings-and-coverings/kepler-conjecture.md) that the supremum of the densities of unit
sphere packings in $\mathbb{R}^3$ is $\pi/\sqrt{18}$, and covering problems such as
[Moser's worm problem](packings-and-coverings/moser-s-worm-problem.md),
[Lebesgue's universal covering problem](packings-and-coverings/lebesgue-s-universal-covering-problem.md) and
[Hadwiger's covering problem](packings-and-coverings/hadwiger-covering-and-illumination-problem.md), with every
convex body in $\mathbb{R}^3$ covered by $14$ translates of its interior. [Convex geometry](convex-geometry/README.md)
includes the
[Brunn–Minkowski and Prékopa–Leindler inequalities](convex-geometry/brunn-minkowski-and-pr-kopa-leindler-inequalitie.md)
and [Hadwiger's theorem](convex-geometry/hadwiger-s-theorem-on-valuations.md) that the continuous
isometry-invariant valuations on convex bodies in $\mathbb{R}^n$ form a space of dimension $n + 1$.

[Euclidean geometry](euclidean-geometry/README.md) runs from
[triangle geometry](euclidean-geometry/triangle-geometry/README.md) to
[isometries and crystallographic groups](euclidean-geometry/isometries-and-symmetry/README.md), with the 17 wallpaper
groups and the 230 space groups. On the smooth side, [Riemannian and Finsler geometry](riemannian-geometry/README.md)
has the [Hopf–Rinow theorem](riemannian-geometry/hopf-rinow-theorem.md), the
[sphere theorem](riemannian-geometry/sphere-theorem.md) for compact simply connected strictly quarter-pinched
manifolds and [Mostow rigidity](riemannian-geometry/mostow-rigidity-theorem.md) for lattices in
$\mathrm{PO}(n, 1)$ with $n \ge 3$, and [curves and surfaces](curves-and-surfaces/README.md) include the
[Fáry–Milnor theorem](curves-and-surfaces/f-ry-milnor-theorem.md) that a knot of total curvature at most $4\pi$
is unknotted.
