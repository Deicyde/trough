# The presented answer-reduced game, and meeting it

The *presented answer-reduced game* is the game that the typed data of Definition [The answer-reduced game as typed tailored data; defn:combi_ans_red](../part-06/def-ar-tailored-game.md) presents on the detyped game of a family of CL functions on the typed questions, with answers bounded by $B$. A tailored verifier meets it at $n$ along a bijection of the detyped questions with its questions at $n$ in the sense of Definition [A tailored verifier meeting a tailored game](def-tailored-meets.md).
