# TauCeti.MaxDualNumber

The dual numbers `K[ε]` with the maximum norm `‖a + b ε‖ = max ‖a‖ ‖b‖` of the two
coefficients, in place of the `ℓ¹` norm Mathlib puts on `DualNumber K`.
