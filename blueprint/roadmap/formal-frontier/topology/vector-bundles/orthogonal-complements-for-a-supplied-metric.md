# Orthogonal complements for a supplied metric

For a vector bundle over an RCLike scalar field with finite-dimensional normed model and a supplied continuous Hermitian metric, every intrinsic vector bundle of fiber submodules with a normed model and a topologically embedded total inclusion has an orthogonal-complement vector bundle in the original fiber subtype and ambient-induced total topologies, and addition identifies their fiberwise product with its equal-base product topology homeomorphically with the original ambient total space.
