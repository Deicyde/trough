# Basic properties of $SM$

$SM$ is a 1729 magma, the squaring operation $S: SM \to SM$ is just the doubling map $Sa = 2a$, and the double squaring map $S^2: SM \to SM$ is constant: $S^2 a = 0$ for all $a \in SM$.
