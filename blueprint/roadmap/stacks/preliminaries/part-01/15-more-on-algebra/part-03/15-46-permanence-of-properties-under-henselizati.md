**`henselization_faithfullyFlat_and_quotient_basis`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Henselian/PermanenceOfProperties/FlatnessAndReducedness.lean, line 322](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Henselian/PermanenceOfProperties/FlatnessAndReducedness.lean#L322). The attribute's comment: “The identification `_hRsh_res` of `ResidueField Rsh` with `κsep` is carried, for faithfulness to the source statement, but is not needed by the proof: the basis comes from ind-étaleness of `R → Rsh` together with `[IsSepClosed (ResidueField Rsh)]`. Hence the leading underscore on that binder.”.

> **Stacks 07QM.** Let `Rh` and `Rsh` be respectively a henselization and a strict
> henselization of a local ring `R`, with residue field of `Rsh` identified with a separable
> closure `κsep` of the residue field of `R`. Then `R → Rh → Rsh` is faithfully flat, the maximal
> ideals are generated by those below them, reduction modulo every power of the maximal ideal is
> unchanged by henselization, and the corresponding reduction of `Rsh` has a basis obtained by
> reducing one fixed family of elements of `Rsh`.

```lean
public theorem henselization_faithfullyFlat_and_quotient_basis.{u, v, w, t}
    {R : Type u} {Rh : Type w} {Rsh : Type t} {κsep : Type v}
    [CommRing R] [IsLocalRing R]
    [CommRing Rh] [Algebra R Rh] [HenselianLocalRing Rh]
    [IsLocalHom (algebraMap R Rh)]
    [CommRing Rsh] [Algebra R Rsh] [Algebra Rh Rsh]
    [IsScalarTower R Rh Rsh] [HenselianLocalRing Rsh]
    [IsLocalHom (algebraMap Rh Rsh)]
    [Field κsep] [Algebra (ResidueField R) κsep]
    [IsSepClosure (ResidueField R) κsep] [IsSepClosed (ResidueField Rsh)]
    (hRh_ind : (CommAlgCat.etale.{w} R).ind.{w} (CommAlgCat.of R Rh))
    (hRh_res : Function.Bijective (ResidueField.map (algebraMap R Rh)))
    (hRsh_ind : (CommAlgCat.etale.{t} R).ind.{t} (CommAlgCat.of R Rsh))
    (_hRsh_res : ∃ e : κsep ≃+* ResidueField Rsh, ∀ x : R,
      e (algebraMap (ResidueField R) κsep (residue R x)) =
        residue Rsh (algebraMap R Rsh x)) :
    Module.FaithfullyFlat R Rh ∧
      Module.FaithfullyFlat Rh Rsh ∧
      (maximalIdeal R).map (algebraMap R Rh) = maximalIdeal Rh ∧
      (maximalIdeal R).map (algebraMap R Rsh) = maximalIdeal Rsh ∧
      (maximalIdeal Rh).map (algebraMap Rh Rsh) = maximalIdeal Rsh ∧
      (∀ n : ℕ, Function.Bijective
        (Ideal.quotientMap ((maximalIdeal R ^ n).map (algebraMap R Rh))
          (algebraMap R Rh) Ideal.le_comap_map)) ∧
      ∃ (ι : Type t) (x : ι → Rsh), ∀ n : ℕ,
        letI : Algebra (R ⧸ maximalIdeal R ^ n)
            (Rsh ⧸ (maximalIdeal R ^ n).map (algebraMap R Rsh)) :=
          Ideal.Quotient.algebraQuotientOfLEComap Ideal.le_comap_map
        ∃ b : Module.Basis ι (R ⧸ maximalIdeal R ^ n)
            (Rsh ⧸ (maximalIdeal R ^ n).map (algebraMap R Rsh)),
          ∀ i, b i = Ideal.Quotient.mk _ (x i)
```

> The maps from a local ring to its henselization, from its henselization to its strict
> henselization, and from the original ring to its strict henselization are formally étale. With
> the discrete topology on each source, they are formally smooth for the adic topology defined by
> the maximal ideal of the corresponding target.

```lean
public theorem henselization_formallyEtale_and_isFormallySmooth
    (R Rh Rsh : Type u)
    [CommRing R] [IsLocalRing R]
    [CommRing Rh] [IsLocalRing Rh] [Algebra R Rh]
    [CommRing Rsh] [IsLocalRing Rsh] [Algebra R Rsh] [Algebra Rh Rsh]
    [IsScalarTower R Rh Rsh]
    (κsep : Type v)
    [Field κsep] [Algebra (ResidueField R) κsep]
    [IsSepClosure (ResidueField R) κsep]
    [IsHenselization R (ResidueField R) Rh]
    [IsHenselization R κsep Rsh]
    [IsLocalHom (algebraMap Rh Rsh)] :
    Algebra.FormallyEtale R Rh ∧
      Algebra.FormallyEtale Rh Rsh ∧
      Algebra.FormallyEtale R Rsh ∧
      (letI : TopologicalSpace R := ⊥
       letI : DiscreteTopology R := ⟨rfl⟩
       letI := (maximalIdeal Rh).adicTopology
       letI : IsTopologicalRing Rh := (maximalIdeal Rh).ringFilterBasis.isTopologicalRing
       letI : IsLinearTopology Rh Rh := (maximalIdeal Rh).isLinearTopology
       IsFormallySmooth (algebraMap R Rh)) ∧
      (letI : TopologicalSpace Rh := ⊥
       letI : DiscreteTopology Rh := ⟨rfl⟩
       letI := (maximalIdeal Rsh).adicTopology
       letI : IsTopologicalRing Rsh := (maximalIdeal Rsh).ringFilterBasis.isTopologicalRing
       letI : IsLinearTopology Rsh Rsh := (maximalIdeal Rsh).isLinearTopology
       IsFormallySmooth (algebraMap Rh Rsh)) ∧
      (letI : TopologicalSpace R := ⊥
       letI : DiscreteTopology R := ⟨rfl⟩
       letI := (maximalIdeal Rsh).adicTopology
       letI : IsTopologicalRing Rsh := (maximalIdeal Rsh).ringFilterBasis.isTopologicalRing
       letI : IsLinearTopology Rsh Rsh := (maximalIdeal Rsh).isLinearTopology
       IsFormallySmooth (algebraMap R Rsh))
```

**`isNoetherianRing_iff_henselizations_and_completion_properties`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Henselian/PermanenceOfProperties/NoetherianCompletions.lean, line 641](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/RingTheory/Henselian/PermanenceOfProperties/NoetherianCompletions.lean#L641). References: EGA IV (18.6.6), “(iv), (v)”; EGA IV (18.8.8), “(iv)”.

> A local ring is Noetherian if and only if its henselization is, if and only if its strict
> henselization is. In that case the latter two rings have complete Noetherian local completions;
> completion does not change the henselization, both completion maps are flat, the map from the
> completed ring to the completed strict henselization is formally smooth, strict henselization
> commutes with base change to the completion, and completing once more gives the completed strict
> henselization.

```lean
public theorem isNoetherianRing_iff_henselizations_and_completion_properties.{u, v, w}
    (R Rh Rsh : Type u)
    [CommRing R] [IsLocalRing R]
    [CommRing Rh] [IsLocalRing Rh] [Algebra R Rh]
    [CommRing Rsh] [IsLocalRing Rsh] [Algebra R Rsh]
    (κsep : Type v) [Field κsep] [Algebra (ResidueField R) κsep]
    [IsSepClosure (ResidueField R) κsep]
    [IsHenselization R (ResidueField R) Rh]
    [IsHenselization R κsep Rsh] :
    let Rhat := AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal R) R
    let Rhhat := AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal Rh) Rh
    let Rshhat := AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal Rsh) Rsh
    ((IsNoetherianRing R ↔ IsNoetherianRing Rh) ∧
      (IsNoetherianRing R ↔ IsNoetherianRing Rsh)) ∧
    (∀ [Algebra Rh Rsh] [IsScalarTower R Rh Rsh]
      [IsLocalHom (algebraMap Rh Rsh)], IsNoetherianRing R →
      ∃ (hRhatLocal : IsLocalRing Rhat)
        (hRhhatLocal : IsLocalRing Rhhat)
        (hRshhatLocal : IsLocalRing Rshhat),
      letI : IsLocalRing Rhat := hRhatLocal
      letI : IsLocalRing Rhhat := hRhhatLocal
      letI : IsLocalRing Rshhat := hRshhatLocal
      IsNoetherianRing Rhhat ∧ IsNoetherianRing Rshhat ∧
      IsAdicComplete (maximalIdeal Rhhat) Rhhat ∧
      IsAdicComplete (maximalIdeal Rshhat) Rshhat ∧
      ∃ e : Rhat ≃+* Rhhat,
        (∀ x : R, e (algebraMap R Rhat x) =
          algebraMap Rh Rhhat (algebraMap R Rh x)) ∧
        RingHom.Flat (algebraMap Rh Rhhat) ∧
        RingHom.Flat (algebraMap Rsh Rshhat) ∧
        (∃ f : Rhat →+* Rshhat,
          (∀ x : R, f (algebraMap R Rhat x) =
            algebraMap Rsh Rshhat (algebraMap R Rsh x)) ∧
          (letI : TopologicalSpace Rhat := (maximalIdeal Rhat).adicTopology
           letI : IsTopologicalRing Rhat :=
             (maximalIdeal Rhat).nonarchimedean.toIsTopologicalRing
           letI : IsLinearTopology Rhat Rhat := (maximalIdeal Rhat).isLinearTopology
           letI : TopologicalSpace Rshhat := (maximalIdeal Rshhat).adicTopology
           letI : IsTopologicalRing Rshhat :=
             (maximalIdeal Rshhat).nonarchimedean.toIsTopologicalRing
           letI : IsLinearTopology Rshhat Rshhat := (maximalIdeal Rshhat).isLinearTopology
           IsFormallySmooth.{w} f)) ∧
        (let algRhRhat : Algebra Rh Rhat :=
            (e.symm.toRingHom.comp (algebraMap Rh Rhhat)).toAlgebra
         letI : Algebra Rh Rhat := algRhRhat
         ∃ hRhatMapLocal : IsLocalHom (algebraMap R Rhat),
           letI : IsLocalHom (algebraMap R Rhat) := hRhatMapLocal
           ∃ algKhat : Algebra (ResidueField Rhat) κsep,
           letI : Algebra (ResidueField Rhat) κsep := algKhat
           (∀ x : ResidueField R,
             algebraMap (ResidueField Rhat) κsep
                 (ResidueField.map (algebraMap R Rhat) x) =
               algebraMap (ResidueField R) κsep x) ∧
           IsSepClosure (ResidueField Rhat) κsep ∧
           ∃ hTensorLocal : IsLocalRing (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh),
           letI : IsLocalRing (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh) := hTensorLocal
           IsHenselization Rhat κsep (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh) ∧
           ∃ ef : AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh))
               (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh) ≃+* Rshhat,
             ∀ x : Rsh,
               ef (algebraMap (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh)
                   (AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh))
                     (Rhat ⊗[Rh] Rsh))
                   (Algebra.TensorProduct.includeRight x)) =
                 algebraMap Rsh Rshhat x))
```

>
> * the fiber `B ⊗_A κ(p)` is isomorphic, as a `κ(p)`-algebra, to the (finite) product
>   `∏ᵢ κ(qᵢ)` of the residue fields;
> * each extension `κ(qᵢ)/κ(p)` is separable algebraic;
> * the `qᵢ` are exactly the primes of `B` lying over `p` (`q` is injective and its
>   range is the set of primes lying over `p`).
>
> The `κ(p)`-algebra structure on each residue field `κ(qᵢ)` is the canonical one
> induced by `Ideal.ResidueField.map` from the structure map `A → B`.

```lean
theorem IsFilteredColimitEtale.exists_fiber_algEquiv_pi_residueField
    {A : Type u} {B : Type v} [CommRing A] [CommRing B] [Algebra A B] [IsNoetherianRing B]
    (hcolim : IsFilteredColimitEtale.{u, v, x, y} A B) (p : Ideal A) [p.IsPrime] :
    ∃ (r : ℕ) (q : Fin r → Ideal B) (hq : ∀ i, (q i).IsPrime)
      (hlo : ∀ i, (q i).LiesOver p),
      -- (1) product decomposition of the fiber algebra, and
      -- (2) separable algebraicity of each factor:
      (letI : ∀ i, (q i).IsPrime := hq
       letI : ∀ i, Algebra p.ResidueField (q i).ResidueField := fun i =>
         (Ideal.ResidueField.map p (q i) (algebraMap A B) (hlo i).over).toAlgebra
       Nonempty
         (TensorProduct A p.ResidueField B ≃ₐ[p.ResidueField]
           (∀ i, (q i).ResidueField)) ∧
       (∀ i, Algebra.IsSeparable p.ResidueField (q i).ResidueField) ∧
       (∀ i, Algebra.IsAlgebraic p.ResidueField (q i).ResidueField)) ∧
      -- the `qᵢ` are precisely the primes of `B` lying over `p`:
      Function.Injective q ∧
      (∀ q' : Ideal B, ∀ _ : q'.IsPrime, q'.LiesOver p ↔ ∃ i, q i = q')
```
