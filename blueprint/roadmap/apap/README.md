# Kelley-Meka bound

[YaelDillies/apap](https://github.com/YaelDillies/apap) says in its README:

> The purpose of this repository is to *digitise* some mathematical definitions, theorem statements and theorem proofs.

Its blueprint, by Thomas Bloom and Yaël Dillies, read at commit [b401f6e2](https://github.com/YaelDillies/apap/tree/b401f6e22417e6dbe6f87d6620571ea6c513aed3) (2026-11-23): one article for each of its 37 nodes whose Lean declarations it names and marks formalized, or in Mathlib. Its other 12 nodes have no article, and a section of only those is left out: the map records what the project contains, not what it plans. A statement for which the blueprint marks no proof `\leanok` says so. Dependencies are the blueprint's `\uses`: a statement's under *Depends on*, a proof's under *Proof depends on*.

The text is the blueprint's, under its Apache-2.0 license: each page has a section's prose, remarks and examples in the blueprint's order, with each node's statement and proof where the node stands.
