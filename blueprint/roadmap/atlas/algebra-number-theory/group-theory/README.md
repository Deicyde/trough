# Group Theory

1,663 results of ATLAS's vault, 1,231 proved and 432 stated but not proved, in this subject's topics.

Group theory studies symmetry through groups, actions, subgroups, quotients, and structural decompositions.

No single field dominates the subject; the largest,
[characters of finite groups](part-01/characters-of-finite-groups/README.md), has about an eighth of its articles. Its largest
topic leads up to [Burnside's $p^a q^b$ theorem](part-01/characters-of-finite-groups/burnside-s-p-a-q-b-theorem.md),
that a group of order $p^a q^b$ is solvable, and the field also has the
[Frobenius determinant theorem](part-01/characters-of-finite-groups/frobenius-group-determinant.md) on the
factorization of the group determinant and the
[McKay conjecture](part-01/characters-of-finite-groups/mckay-conjecture.md), the Cabanes–Späth theorem that a finite
group and the normalizer of a Sylow $p$-subgroup have equally many irreducible characters of degree prime to $p$. The
theory of [finite simple groups](part-01/finite-simple-groups/README.md) includes the Gorenstein–Walter theorem on simple
groups with dihedral Sylow $2$-subgroups, and [soluble finite groups](part-01/soluble-finite-groups/README.md), the second
largest field, are studied through [formations and Fitting classes](part-01/soluble-finite-groups/formations/README.md).

On the infinite side, [geometric group theory](part-01/geometric-group-theory/README.md) covers growth, amenability,
hyperbolicity, Thompson's group $F$ and
[property (T)](part-01/geometric-group-theory/property-t-for-automorphism-groups-of-free-group.md) for
$\operatorname{Aut}(F_n)$ with $n \ge 6$. [Free groups](part-01/free-groups/README.md) appear with their subgroups and
automorphisms, and [presentations and decision problems](part-01/presentations-and-decision-problems/README.md) include
[braid and Artin groups](part-01/presentations-and-decision-problems/braid-and-artin/README.md), a finitely presented group
with unsolvable [word problem](part-01/presentations-and-decision-problems/word-problem-for-groups.md) (Novikov and
Boone) and finitely presented infinite simple groups. [Permutation groups](part-01/permutation-groups/README.md) run from
Frobenius groups to highly homogeneous and cofinitary groups on infinite sets, and in
[group rings](part-02/group-rings/README.md) Gardam's unit in $\mathbb{F}_2[P]$, for a torsion-free group $P$, disproves
[Kaplansky's unit conjecture](part-02/group-rings/kaplansky-s-unit-conjecture.md).
