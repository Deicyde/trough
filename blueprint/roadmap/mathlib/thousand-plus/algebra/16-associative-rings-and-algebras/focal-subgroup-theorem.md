# Focal subgroup theorem

**The Focal Subgroup Theorem**

For a Sylow p-subgroup P of a finite group G, `P ∩ G' = P*`,
where `P*` is the focal subgroup of `P`.

```lean
Subgroup.commutator_inf_eq_focalSubgroup.{u_1} {G : Type u_1} [Group G] {p : ℕ} [Fact (Nat.Prime p)]
  (P : Sylow p G) [(↑P).FiniteIndex] : commutator G ⊓ ↑P = (↑P).focalSubgroup
```
