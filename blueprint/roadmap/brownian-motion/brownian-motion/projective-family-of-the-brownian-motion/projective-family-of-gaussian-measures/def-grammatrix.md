# Gram matrix

Let $v_1, \ldots, v_n$ be vectors in an inner product space $E$. The Gram matrix of $v_1, \ldots, v_n$ is the matrix in $\mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ with entries $G_{ij} = \langle v_i, v_j \rangle$ for $1 \leq i,j \leq n$.
