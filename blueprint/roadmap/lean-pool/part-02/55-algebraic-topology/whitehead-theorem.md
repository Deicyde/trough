# Whitehead's theorem for CW-complexes

> Formalizes Whitehead's theorem — a continuous map between CW-complexes that induces isomorphisms on all homotopy groups is a homotopy equivalence — including the supporting theory of CW-complexes, relative homotopy groups, the homotopy extension property, and compression of maps.
