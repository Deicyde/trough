# The Clifford algebra of the gamma matrices

The module `Physlib.Relativity.CliffordAlgebra`, [Physlib/Relativity/CliffordAlgebra.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/Physlib/Relativity/CliffordAlgebra.lean): 49 declarations (1 instance with no name), 35 with a docstring, 1 listed as significant.

> The real algebra generated by the gamma matrices in the Dirac representation is isomorphic to the
> Clifford algebra of the Minkowski quadratic form.

## i. Overview

> This file obtains the gamma matrices in the Dirac representation from the endomorphisms
> `Fermion.Dirac.gamma`. The algebra equivalence `Fermion.Dirac.endEquivMatrix` changes their chiral
> coordinates to Dirac coordinates, and the lemmas `γ0_eq_matrix` to `γ3_eq_matrix` give their
> entries.
>
> The gamma matrices obey the anticommutation relations `γ^μ γ^ν + γ^ν γ^μ = 2 η^μν`, where `η` is
> the Minkowski metric of signature `(+,-,-,-)`. The Clifford algebra of the quadratic form
> `diracForm` is the real algebra generated by four elements subject to these relations and to no
> others. Sending its generators to the gamma matrices gives an algebra homomorphism
> `ofCliffordAlgebra` onto the real algebra `diracAlgebra` generated by the gamma matrices. The main
> result is that this homomorphism is an isomorphism. In other words, every identity between
> polynomial expressions with real coefficients in the gamma matrices follows from the
> anticommutation relations.
>
> The proof counts dimensions. The Clifford algebra has dimension `2 ^ 4 = 16`, one for each subset
> of its four generators. The algebra `diracAlgebra` is the image of the Clifford algebra, so its
> dimension is at most 16. It contains the 16 products of gamma matrices with increasing indices,
> which are linearly independent, so its dimension is exactly 16. Hence `ofCliffordAlgebra`, a
> surjective linear map between two spaces of the same finite dimension, is injective.

## ii. Key results

> - `γ0`, `γ1`, `γ2`, `γ3` : the gamma matrices in the Dirac representation.
> - `γ_anticomm` : the anticommutation relations of the gamma matrices.
> - `γ5` : the chirality matrix `i γ0 γ1 γ2 γ3`.
