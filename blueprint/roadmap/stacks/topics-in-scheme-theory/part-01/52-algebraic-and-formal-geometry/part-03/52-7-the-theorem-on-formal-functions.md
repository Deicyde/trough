> canonical map `(Rf_*K)^∧ ⟶ Rf_*(K^∧)` is invertible, so `Rf_*(K^∧) = Rf_*K = (Rf_*K)^∧`: the
> unit `K ⟶ K^∧` is invertible on a derived-complete `K`.
>
> The general case of Tag 0A0G is not proved; see
> `isIso_derivedCompletionPushforwardComparison_of_isIso_map_unit` for the criterion that is, and
> the module documentation for the state of the four inputs of the source's proof. Three of them
> ([Stacks, Tags 0A0F, 0A0E, 0A07]) are now available; what is missing is the base case
> `K = 𝒪[n]` of the projection formula [Stacks, Tag 0944] on a ringed topos — the dévissage from
> it to a strictly perfect complex is available, and takes that case as a hypothesis — together
> with the identification of `Lf^*` of a Koszul complex with a Koszul complex. That is also why
> part (3) of [Stacks, Tag 099N] is registered here only for a derived-complete object.
