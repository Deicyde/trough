# Convex Three-Distance Degree-Six Theorem and Exceptional-Word Closures

> Proves that every finite cyclically strictly convex planar configuration has a vertex incident to at most six edges from its three largest distance classes. The proof selects a maximal-gap vertex and coordinated majorants, closes all exceptional inner branches, performs the five-row enumeration, and transports all thirteen canonical cover words to four shared full-two-rung, anti-saturation, terminal-cage, and four-edge-cage predicates. It also proves exceptional-count rigidity and an explicit heptagon refutation of a tempting stronger majorant claim, with rational regression witnesses. This settles the all-n k=3 instance of the paper's suggested degree-2k convex bound; Morić--Pritchard proved the corresponding top-three edge bound for sufficiently large n. It does not settle the general-k suggestion or the general Erdős problem 132.
