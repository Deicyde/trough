# Strong PNT (Math Inc)

The blueprint of Strong PNT (Math Inc), read at commit [2f5835c3](https://github.com/math-inc/strongpnt/tree/2f5835c322314f55f1026ec2f139d704b7c45c69) (2025-09-10): one article for each of its 615 nodes that the project has in Lean or Mathlib has. Its other 6 nodes have no article, since the blueprint names no formalized Lean declaration for them: the map records what the project contains, not what it plans. A node the blueprint marks `\leanok` is formalized here, its proof too when that is marked, and `\mathlibok` puts it in Mathlib. Dependencies are the blueprint's `\uses`: a statement's under *Depends on*, a proof's under *Proof depends on*.

The project gives no license, so this chapter has each node's title, Lean names, marks and dependencies, and links to its text in the blueprint, but not the text.
