> **Stacks 09GG.** A finite extension `E/k` is algebraic, and an extension `E/k` is algebraic if
> and only if every simple subextension `k(α)/k` generated by some `α ∈ E` is finite.

```lean
theorem Algebra.isAlgebraic_of_finite_and_isAlgebraic_iff_forall_finiteDimensional_adjoin
    (k E : Type*) [Field k] [Field E] [Algebra k E] :
    (FiniteDimensional k E → Algebra.IsAlgebraic k E) ∧
      (Algebra.IsAlgebraic k E ↔ ∀ α : E, FiniteDimensional k k⟮α⟯)
```

The attribute's comment: “the field `k(α₁, …, αₙ)` is `IntermediateField.adjoin k (Set.range α)`”.

> Let `k` be a field and `α₁, …, αₙ` elements of an extension field `L`, each algebraic over
> `k`. Then `k(α₁, …, αₙ) / k` is a finite extension. This is the field-adjoin form of the
> statement; `fg_adjoin_of_finite` is the ring-adjoin version, about `k[α₁, …, αₙ]` as a finitely
> generated `k`-module.

```lean
theorem IntermediateField.finiteDimensional_adjoin_range_of_isAlgebraic {K L : Type*} [Field K]
    [Field L] [Algebra K L] {n : ℕ} (α : Fin n → L) (hα : ∀ i, IsAlgebraic K (α i)) :
    FiniteDimensional K (IntermediateField.adjoin K (Set.range α))
```
