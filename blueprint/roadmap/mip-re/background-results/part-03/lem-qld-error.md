# The error of the Pauli basis test

Say that a function $f(\varepsilon, m, d, q)$ has the *shape of Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md)* if there are constants $a \geq 1$ and $0 < b < 1$, chosen before the parameters, such that

$$
0 \leq f(\varepsilon, m, d, q) \leq a (md)^a \bigl( \varepsilon^b + q^{-b} + 2^{-bmd} \bigr)
$$

whenever $0 \leq \varepsilon \leq 1$, $m, d \geq 1$ and $q \geq 2$. These functions are closed under sums, nonnegative constant multiples, products, polynomial prefactors $(md)^r$ and real powers $t \mapsto t^B$ for $0 < B \leq 1$; they include $\varepsilon$, $md/q$, and $q^{-B}$ and $2^{-Bmd}$ for every $B > 0$; and no positive constant has the shape. Every error of the soundness chain has the shape: $\delta_Q$ of Lemma [Combined $XZ$ point measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-combined-points.md), $\delta_P$ of Lemma [Pairs of lines](../part-02/lem-qld-pairs-of-lines.md), $\delta_{\mathrm{GS}}$ and $\delta_{\mathrm{ld}} = \delta_{\mathrm{sim}}(q, 4m, d, 1, \delta_{\mathrm{GS}})$ of Lemma [A global padded polynomial PVM](lem-qld-global-pvm.md), $\delta_S$ of Lemma [Global robustness in both register versions](lem-qld-global-robustness.md) at $\delta_{\mathrm{ld}}$, and, at $\delta = \delta_S(\delta_{\mathrm{ld}})$, the constant $X$ of Lemma [Self-consistency of the exact Pauli observables](lem-qld-pauli-selfcons.md) and the two bounds of Lemma [The swap isometry](lem-qld-swap.md). Let $\delta_{qld}(\varepsilon, m, d, q)$ be the smaller of $4$ and $\delta_2 + \sqrt{\eta_1}$ if $48md \leq q$ and $2\sqrt{X} + 2X < 1$, where $\eta_1$ is the bound of item 1 of Lemma [The swap isometry](lem-qld-swap.md) on the squared distance and $\delta_2$ the bound of its item 2, and let it be $4$ otherwise. Then $\delta_{qld}$ has the shape of Theorem [Pauli basis test / quantum low individual degree test](../part-01/thm-qld.md): there are universal constants $a \geq 1$ and $0 < b < 1$ such that $\delta_{qld}(\min(\varepsilon, 1), m, d, q) \leq a (md)^a (\varepsilon^b + q^{-b} + 2^{-bmd})$ for every $\varepsilon \geq 0$, $m, d \geq 1$ and $q \geq 2$.

## Proof

Write $S_b = \varepsilon^b + q^{-b} + 2^{-bmd}$. On the range, $S_b$ is antitone in $b$, since $\varepsilon \leq 1 \leq q$, and $(md)^a$ is monotone in $a$, since $md \geq 1$; so two functions of the shape are both below the shape at the larger $a$ and the smaller $b$, which gives sums, constant multiples and polynomial prefactors. For $0 < B \leq 1$ the map $t \mapsto t^B$ is subadditive, while $a^B \leq a$ and $((md)^a)^B \leq (md)^a$, so $(a (md)^a S_b)^B \leq a (md)^a S_{bB}$; square roots are $B = 1/2$. Each term of $S_b$ is at most one, so $S_b \leq 3$, which gives products. The base cases are direct: $\varepsilon \leq \varepsilon^{1/2}$ on $[0, 1]$, and $md/q = (md) q^{-1}$. A positive constant $c$ does not have the shape: at fixed $m$ and $d$ the shape tends to $a (md)^a 2^{-bmd}$ as $\varepsilon \to 0$ and $q \to \infty$, and that is below $c$ once $md$ is large.

The chain applies these operations a fixed finite number of times, so $b$ stays universal. Its one external input is the seeded soundness error at $(q, 4m, d, 1)$, which is $A (4md)^A (x^B + q^{-B} + 2^{-4Bmd})$ at the error $x$ it is fed, for the constants $A$, $B$ of Lemma [Absorbing the simultaneous reduction's error](../part-01/lem-lidt-ldc-error.md); since $(4md)^A = 4^A (md)^A$ and $2^{-4Bmd} \leq 2^{-Bmd}$, it has the shape whenever $x$ does. Item 1 of Lemma [The swap isometry](lem-qld-swap.md) bounds the squared distance by $\eta_1 = 2 - 2\sqrt{1 - \eta}$ with $\eta = 2\sqrt{X} + 2X$, which is at most $2\eta$ for every $\eta \geq 0$, whether or not $\eta < 1$, the square root of a negative number being $0$. Outside the regime the trivial bound costs nothing: a nonnegative function bounded by a constant $C$, and at most a function $h$ of the shape wherever some function $g$ of the shape is below one, is at most $h + C g$ everywhere, and so has the shape. Here $h = \delta_2 + \sqrt{\eta_1}$, $C = 4$ and $g = 48md/q + 2\sqrt{X} + 2X$, which is below one only where $48md \leq q$ and $2\sqrt{X} + 2X < 1$. Finally, the shape is monotone in $\varepsilon$, which gives the bound at $\min(\varepsilon, 1)$ for every $\varepsilon \geq 0$.
