# 1729 does not imply 817

There exists a magma that satisfies equation 1729 but not equation 817.

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof depends on

- [Existence of partial solution](partial-exist.md)
- [Enlarging $L'_0$](enlarge-l0.md)
- [Enlarging $S'$](enlarge-s.md)
- [Enlarging $\diamond$](enlarge-op.md)
- [Chain of partial solutions](chain.md)

## Proof

Starting from [Existence of partial solution](partial-exist.md) and applying [Enlarging $L'_0$](enlarge-l0.md), [Enlarging $S'$](enlarge-s.md), [Enlarging $\diamond$](enlarge-op.md) alternatingly, one can find a chain of partial solution that are total in the limit. The claim now follows from [Chain of partial solutions](chain.md).
