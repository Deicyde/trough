# The constraint lists of the auxiliary checks, as programs

Polynomial-time functions build each list of Lemma [The auxiliary checks as linear constraints](../part-04/lem-intro-aux-cons.md) — window equalities, readable guards, equalities on a coordinate set, dual equalities, the input's constraints re-indexed, the swapped orientation, and the comparisons of the auxiliary checks — from the lengths and offsets in unary, the readable conditions as Booleans, the coordinate sets as masks, the kernel generators and the input's constraints.

## Depends on

- [The auxiliary checks as linear constraints](../part-04/lem-intro-aux-cons.md)

## Proof

Unit vectors are shifts of one another, a coordinate set selects among them, and every other list is a concatenation of placed vectors.
