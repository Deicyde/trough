# Polynomials

This section contains facts about polynomials that are used in the rest of the library, and also definitions for computable representations of polynomials.

- [Multilinear Extension](def-multilinear-extension.md)
- [Multilinear Extension is Unique](thm-multilinear-extension-unique.md)

We note that the Schwartz-Zippel Lemma is already in Mathlib.

- [Schwartz-Zippel Lemma](thm-schwartz-zippel.md)

Computable univariate and multilinear polynomials come from the CompPoly library. They are represented by arrays of coefficients, or, for multilinear polynomials, dually by their evaluations on the Boolean hypercube.

- [Computable Univariate Polynomials](def-computable-univariate-polynomials.md)
- [Computable Multilinear Polynomials](def-computable-multilinear-polynomials.md)
