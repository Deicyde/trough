\begin{theorem}[Complex.one-add-prime-cpow-ne-zero]
\label{Complex.one_add_prime_cpow_ne_zero}
\lean{ArithmeticFunction.Complex.one_add_prime_cpow_ne_zero}
% at /home/runner/work/trough/trough/library/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/IwaniecKowalskiCh1.lean:1060.0-1082.59
\leanok
For $1<\Re(s)$ and $p$ prime, we have that $1+p^{-s}\neq 0$. The naming convention is to mimic
    \begin{verbatim}
      Complex.one_sub_prime_cpow_ne_zero
    \end{verbatim}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Suppose for contradiction $1+p^{-s}=0$, then $|p^{-s}|=1$. However, this can not happen per
    \begin{verbatim}
      Complex.norm_prime_cpow_le_one_half
    \end{verbatim}
\end{proof}
