# Dirichlet's unit theorem, structural form

The unit group of the ring of integers of a
number field is the product of its torsion subgroup and the free abelian group generated by the
fundamental system: a unit corresponds to its unique decomposition as a root of unity times a
product of powers of fundamental units (`NumberField.Units.exist_unique_eq_mul_prod`).

```lean
NumberField.unitsMulEquivTorsionProdMultiplicative.{u_1} (F : Type u_1) [Field F] [NumberField F] :
  (NumberField.RingOfIntegers F)ˣ ≃*
    ↥(NumberField.Units.torsion F) × Multiplicative (Fin (NumberField.Units.rank F) → ℤ)
```
