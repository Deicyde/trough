# Middle exactness of completion for a Noetherian middle module

For every ideal I of a commutative ring R and R-linear maps f : M → N and g : N → P, if the image of f is the kernel of g, g is surjective and N is Noetherian over R, then the induced maps on their inverse-limit I-adic completions are exact at the completion of N.
