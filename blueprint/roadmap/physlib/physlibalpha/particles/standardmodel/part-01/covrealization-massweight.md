# The mass-weight grading of the field algebra

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.CovRealization.MassWeight`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/MassWeight.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/CovRealization/MassWeight.lean): 32 declarations, 30 with a docstring.

## i. Overview

> The elements of the field algebra of a given mass weight form a submodule, which is
> exactly the span of the words in the covariant generators of that total weight
> (`massWeightSubmodule_eq_span`). Weight-homogeneous elements supercommute,
> the gauge and Lorentz actions preserve the weight, and consequently an invariant
> element decomposing into components of pairwise distinct weights has invariant
> components.

## ii. Key results

> - `massWeightSubmodule_eq_span` : the weight grading of the field algebra

> **The weight grading of the field algebra.** The submodule of elements of the
> field algebra of mass weight `w` is exactly the span of the words in the covariant
> basis generators of total weight `w`.

```lean
lemma massWeightSubmodule_eq_span (w : ℕ) :
    h.massWeightSubmodule w = Submodule.span ℂ
      {x | ∃ gl : List Generators,
        (gl.map Generators.weight).sum = w ∧ (gl.map h.generatorVal).prod = x}
```

## D. Invariance of the weight components

> The gauge and Lorentz actions preserve the mass weight: they carry each covariant
> tower into combinations of towers of the same derivative order. The weight
> components of the field algebra are independent, so an invariant element that is
> a sum of components of pairwise distinct weights has invariant components.
