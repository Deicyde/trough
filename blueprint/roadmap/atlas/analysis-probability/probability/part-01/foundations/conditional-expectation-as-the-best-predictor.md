## Orthogonality of the conditional-expectation residual

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure
on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, let $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a
sub-σ-algebra, and let $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable with respect to $\mu$.
Write $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}]$ for the conditional expectation of $Y$ with respect to
$\mu$ given $\mathcal{G}$. Then for every strongly $\mathcal{G}$-measurable
$h : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ that is square-integrable with respect to $\mu$, the residual
$Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}]$ is orthogonal to $h$:
$\int (Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}]) \, h \, d\mu = 0$.

## Pythagorean identity for the conditional mean

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure on a
measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, let $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a
sub-σ-algebra, and let $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable with respect to $\mu$.
Write $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}]$ for the conditional expectation of $Y$ with respect to
$\mu$ given $\mathcal{G}$. Then for every strongly $\mathcal{G}$-measurable
$g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ that is square-integrable with respect to $\mu$,
$\int (Y - g)^2 \, d\mu = \int (Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}])^2 \, d\mu +
\int (\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}] - g)^2 \, d\mu$.

## The conditional mean minimizes the expected squared error

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure
on a measurable space $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$, let $\mathcal{G} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ be a
sub-σ-algebra, and let $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable with respect to $\mu$.
Write $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}]$ for the conditional expectation of $Y$ with respect to
$\mu$ given $\mathcal{G}$. Then for every strongly $\mathcal{G}$-measurable
$g : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ that is square-integrable with respect to $\mu$,
$\int (Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid \mathcal{G}])^2 \, d\mu \le \int (Y - g)^2 \, d\mu$.

## The regression function is the Bayes-optimal regressor

Let $\mu$ be a finite measure
on a measurable space $\Omega$, let $X : \Omega \to \mathcal{X}$ be a measurable map into a
measurable space $\mathcal{X}$, and let $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be square-integrable with
respect to $\mu$. Write $\mathbb{E}[Y \mid X]$ for the conditional expectation of $Y$ with
respect to $\mu$ given the σ-algebra generated by $X$. Then for every measurable
$h : \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ such that $h \circ X$ is square-integrable with respect to $\mu$,
$\int (Y - \mathbb{E}[Y \mid X])^2 \, d\mu \le \int (Y - h \circ X)^2 \, d\mu$.
