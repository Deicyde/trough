# The joint spectral measure of a commuting family

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ProbabilisticTheory.HilbertSpace.JointSpectral.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/JointSpectral/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/PhyslibAlpha/ProbabilisticTheory/HilbertSpace/JointSpectral/Basic.lean): 17 declarations (2 instances with no name), 7 with a docstring.

> A commuting family of bounded operators closed under adjoints has a joint spectral measure on the
> Gelfand spectrum of the C⋆-algebra it generates.

## i. Overview

> The closed ⋆-subalgebra of `B(H)` generated by a family of operators that commute with each other
> and with each other's adjoints is a commutative C⋆-algebra `A`. By Gelfand duality it is the
> algebra of continuous functions on its character space, so the inclusion `A → B(H)` is a unital
> ⋆-homomorphism from `C(X, ℂ)`, where `X` is the character space. Its spectral measure is the joint
> spectral measure of the family: every operator of the family is the integral of its Gelfand
> transform, and every operator commuting with the family commutes with the spectral measure.

## ii. Key results

> - `CommutingStarFamily` : a commuting family of operators closed under adjoints.
> - `CommutingStarFamily.algebra` : the commutative C⋆-algebra generated by the family.
> - `CommutingStarFamily.spectralMeasure` : the joint spectral measure, on the character space.
> - `CommutingStarFamily.boundedIntegral_gelfandTransform` : each operator of the algebra is the
>   integral of its Gelfand transform.
> - `CommutingStarFamily.spectralMeasure_commute` : the spectral measure commutes with every operator
>   commuting with the family.
