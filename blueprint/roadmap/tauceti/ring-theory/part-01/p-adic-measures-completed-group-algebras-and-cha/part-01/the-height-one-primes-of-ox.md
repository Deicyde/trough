# The height-one primes of `O⟦X⟧`

Over a discrete valuation ring `O` with uniformiser `ϖ`,
complete for its maximal ideal, a prime ideal of `O⟦X⟧` has height one if and only if it is
generated by `C ϖ` or by an irreducible polynomial distinguished at the maximal ideal.

```lean
PowerSeries.height_eq_one_iff_of_isDiscreteValuationRing.{u_1} {O : Type u_1} [CommRing O]
  [IsDomain O] [IsDiscreteValuationRing O] [IsAdicComplete (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal O) O] {ϖ : O}
  (hϖ : Irreducible ϖ) (𝔭 : Ideal (PowerSeries O)) [𝔭.IsPrime] :
  𝔭.height = 1 ↔
    𝔭 = Ideal.span {PowerSeries.C ϖ} ∨
      ∃ F, F.IsDistinguishedAt (IsLocalRing.maximalIdeal O) ∧ Irreducible F ∧ 𝔭 = Ideal.span {↑F}
```
