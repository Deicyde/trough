# The global gauge group SU(n)

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.GlobalGaugeGroup.SU`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GlobalGaugeGroup/SU.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/GlobalGaugeGroup/SU.lean): 11 declarations, 10 with a docstring.

> The special unitary group, as a global gauge group.

## i. Overview

> The gauge groups of the strong and weak interactions are `SU(3)` and `SU(2)`, and `SU(n)`
> is the gauge group of Yang–Mills theory with `n` colours. It is the group of unitary
> `n × n` complex matrices of determinant one. Both conditions are closed, so `SU(n)` is a
> closed submonoid of matrices. It is closed under the conjugate transpose, which is the
> inverse of a unitary matrix, so an invertible matrix lies in the global gauge group `SU(n)`
> exactly when it is special unitary.
>
> Every traceless anti-hermitian matrix `X` is a generator of `SU(n)`. The exponential
> `exp (t X)` is unitary, its conjugate transpose being `exp (−t X)`, and has determinant
> `exp (t tr X) = 1`. The hermitian generators of physics, such as the Gell-Mann matrices for
> `SU(3)`, are `−i X`.
>
> The scalar matrices in `SU(n)` are the multiples of the identity by an `n`-th root of unity.
> They form the centre `ℤ_n` of `SU(n)`, generated by the multiple of the identity by the primitive
> root of unity `exp (2 π i / n)`.

> A generator of `SU(n)` is traceless. The determinant of `exp (t X)` is `exp (t tr X)`, and
> it is one for all real `t`, so the derivative `tr X` of this function at `t = 0` vanishes.
