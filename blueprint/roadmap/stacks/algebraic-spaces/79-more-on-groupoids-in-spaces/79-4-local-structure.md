> **Stacks 0CK9 (Local structure).**  Let `(U, R, s, t, c, e, i)` be a groupoid in algebraic
> spaces over `S`, let `ū` be a geometric point of `U`, and let `A = 𝒪_{U, ū}`,
> `B = 𝒪_{R, e(ū)}` and `C = 𝒪_{R ×_{s, U, t} R, (e, e)(ū)}` be the étale local rings, with the
> homomorphisms `s, t : A → B`, `c : B → C`, `e : B → A`, `i : B → B` and `(e, e) : C → A` induced
> by the structure morphisms.  Then:

> 1. `A`, `B`, `C` are local rings and `s, t, c, e, i, (e, e)` are local homomorphisms;
> 2. `e ∘ t = 1` and `e ∘ s = 1`;
> 3. `B = s(A) ⊕ I = t(A) ⊕ I` for `I = ker (e : B → A)`;
> 4. `𝔪_B ⊗ B + B ⊗ 𝔪_B` is a maximal ideal of `B ⊗_{s, A, t} B`, the localization at it has

> **The arrow `I/I² ⊕ I/I² → J/J²` coming from `J = (I ⊗ B + B ⊗ I)C` is an isomorphism.**
> At a geometric identity of a groupoid in algebraic spaces, the sum of the maps induced by the
> two projections is a bijection `I/I² ⊕ I/I² → J/J²`; its inverse is induced by the two
> retractions `C → B` carried by `a ↦ (a, e(s a))` and `a ↦ (e(t a), a)`.

```lean
theorem bijective_cotangentPairs :
    Function.Bijective ((firstCotangent G z).coprod (secondCotangent G z))
```

> **Stacks 0CKA, composition.**  Let `(U, R, s, t, c, e, i)` be a groupoid in algebraic spaces
> and `ū` a geometric point of `U`, with étale local rings `A = 𝒪_{U, ū}`, `B = 𝒪_{R, e(ū)}` and
> `C = 𝒪_{R ×_{s, U, t} R, (e, e)(ū)}`, `I = ker (e : B → A)` and `J = (I ⊗ B + B ⊗ I)C`.  The
> map `I/I² → J/J²` induced by `c` is the diagonal `(1, 1) : I/I² → I/I² ⊕ I/I²` followed by the
> map `I/I² ⊕ I/I² → J/J²` coming from `J = (I ⊗ B + B ⊗ I)C`, the sum of the maps induced by
> the two projections.

```lean
theorem cotangent_composition :
    compositionCotangent G z =
      ((firstCotangent G z).coprod (secondCotangent G z)).comp
        (LinearMap.id.prod LinearMap.id)
```
