# Finite-dimensional quantum systems

The module `Physlib.QuantumMechanics.FiniteTarget`, [Physlib/QuantumMechanics/FiniteTarget.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/2a1dfe43134b49b643642f13d33031754021bb5d/Physlib/QuantumMechanics/FiniteTarget.lean): 7 declarations, 6 with a docstring.

> A `FiniteTarget` consists of a finite-dimensional Hilbert space together with a self-adjoint
> Hamiltonian. Its Hamiltonian determines a unitary time evolution through Stone's correspondence.
>
> Physical examples of such systems include:
> - Spin systems.
> - Tight binding chains.
