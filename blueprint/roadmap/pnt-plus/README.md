# PNT+

[AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd) says in its README:

> The objective of this project is to formalize in Lean the Prime Number Theorem (with classical error term), as well as related results such as the Prime Number Theorem in Arithmetic Progressions.

Its blueprint, read at commit [b47896b3](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/tree/b47896b36cc6e36559189281330930ecf25fae7a) (2026-11-24): one article for each of its 383 nodes whose Lean declarations it names and marks formalized, or in Mathlib. Its other 20 nodes have no article, and a section of only those is left out: the map records what the project contains, not what it plans. A statement for which the blueprint marks no proof `\leanok` says so. Dependencies are the blueprint's `\uses`: a statement's under *Depends on*, a proof's under *Proof depends on*.

The text is the blueprint's, under its Apache-2.0 license: each page has a section's prose, remarks and examples in the blueprint's order, with each node's statement and proof where the node stands.
