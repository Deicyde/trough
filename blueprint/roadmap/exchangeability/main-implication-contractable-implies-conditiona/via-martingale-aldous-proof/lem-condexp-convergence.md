# Conditional expectation convergence

For $k \le m$ and measurable $B$: $\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{1}_{X_m \in B} \mid \mathcal{F}_m] = \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{1}_{X_k \in B} \mid \mathcal{F}_m]$ a.s.
