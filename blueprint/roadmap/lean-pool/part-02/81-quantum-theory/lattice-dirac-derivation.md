# Cubical lattice Dirac factorization and its continuum limit

> Studies a quartic two-field lattice action on the 18-neighbour cubic stencil with fixed numerical constants. On a fixed period-two background, its quadratic fluctuation symbol splits into a continuum Klein-Gordon symbol and a fourth-order lattice remainder. The four-component Dirac Hamiltonian squares to that continuum symbol. Two flavour copies of a cubical Clifford representation, built from signed cube incidence maps, are equivalent to the standard alpha/beta Dirac representation. Under the stability bound (dt w)^2 <= 4, with continuum frequency w, a first-order branch solves the centred time recurrence using the continuum symbol. Under the same bound the exact lattice recurrence has a defect proportional to dt^2 times the spatial remainder. At fixed momentum the branch generator tends to the Dirac Hamiltonian as the time step vanishes. The project also proves local continuity for the linearized lattice dynamics, the continuum limit of the lattice current, an orthonormal Fourier frame on finite abelian groups, and spin one-half rotation generators. Free continuum identities include Lorentz boosts, the Pauli-Lubanski invariant and the Gordon decomposition. All results are about this lattice model and the free four-component continuum operator. They do not establish properties of physical electrons.
