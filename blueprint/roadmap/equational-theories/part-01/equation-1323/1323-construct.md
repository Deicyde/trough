# Construction of 1323 magmas

Suppose that $M$ is a magma such that

$$
R_{Sy} L_{Sy} = 1\qquad\text{(2)}
$$

and

$$
L_{y} R_{y} = R_{Sy}\qquad\text{(3)}
$$

hold. Then the magma satisfies 1323.
