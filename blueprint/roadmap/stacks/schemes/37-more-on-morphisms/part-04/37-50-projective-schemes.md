**`AlgebraicGeometry.exists_sheafGradedAlgebra_of_isProjective`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Projective/ProjectiveOverAmpleBase.lean, line 245](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Projective/ProjectiveOverAmpleBase.lean#L245). The attribute's comment: “clause (8), reached from clause (1)”.

> **Stacks Tag 0B45, clause (1) implies clause (8).** A projective morphism exhibits its source
> as the relative homogeneous spectrum of a quasi-coherent graded `𝒪_S`-algebra generated in degree
> one whose degree-one piece is of finite type.
>
> `AlgebraicGeometry.IsProjective` *is* a closed immersion `j : X ⟶ 𝐏(ℰ)` over `S` for a
> quasi-coherent `ℰ` of finite type, and `𝐏(ℰ)` *is* `Proj_S(Sym(ℰ))` by definition
> (`Stacks.SymGraded.schemeProjectiveBundle`), so no presentation of projective space as a relative
> Proj is needed: `exists_sheafGradedAlgebra_of_isClosedImmersion` applies to `j` at once. Its three
> hypotheses on `Sym(ℰ)` are proved —
> `Stacks.SymGraded.isGeneratedInDegreeOneAt_schemeSheafGradedAlgebra`,
> `Stacks.SymGraded.quasiCompact_schemeProjectiveBundleToBase`, and, for the degree-one piece,
> `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.symmetricAlgebraGenIso`, which identifies `Sym¹(ℰ)` with `ℰ`.

```lean
theorem exists_sheafGradedAlgebra_of_isProjective {X S : Scheme.{u}} {f : X ⟶ S}
    (hf : IsProjective f) :
    ∃ A : SheafGradedAlgebra.{u} S,
      Stacks.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjRepresentsF1.IsGeneratedInDegreeOne A ∧
        (A.piece 1).IsFiniteType ∧
        ∃ e : X ≅ (relativeProj A).scheme, e.hom ≫ (relativeProj A).toBase = f
```

**`AlgebraicGeometry.isProjective_tfae_of_exists_isAmple`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Projective/ProjectiveOverAmpleBase.lean, line 376](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Morphisms/Projective/ProjectiveOverAmpleBase.lean#L376).

> **Stacks Project, Tag 0B45.** Let `S` be a scheme carrying an ample invertible module and let
> `f : X ⟶ S` be a morphism of schemes. The following are equivalent:
>
> 1. `f` is projective;
> 2. `f` is H-projective;
> 3. `f` is quasi-projective and proper;
> 4. `f` is H-quasi-projective and proper;
> 5. `f` is proper and `X` carries an ample invertible module;
> 6. `f` is proper and carries an `f`-ample invertible module;
> 7. `f` is proper and carries an `f`-very ample invertible module;
> 8. `X` is, over `S`, the relative homogeneous spectrum of a quasi-coherent graded `𝒪_S`-algebra
>    generated in degree one over its degree-zero part whose degree-one piece is of finite type.
>
> **Nothing is assumed beyond the source's own hypothesis on the base.** Conditions (1) to (7) are
> `AlgebraicGeometry.isProjective_tfae_seven_of_exists_isAmple`; (8) ⟹ (1) is the source's
> citation Tag 0B3U, `AlgebraicGeometry.isProjective_relativeProj_toBase`; and (1) ⟹ (8) is the
> source's other citation, Tag 0801, applied to the closed immersion `X ⟶ 𝐏(ℰ)` that clause (1)
> *is*, which is `AlgebraicGeometry.exists_sheafGradedAlgebra_of_isProjective` above.

```lean
theorem isProjective_tfae_of_exists_isAmple
    {X S : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ S)
    (hS : ∃ (M : S.Modules) (hM : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule S M),
      letI : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule S M := hM
      Stacks.Ample.IsAmple S M) :
    List.TFAE
      [IsProjective f,
        IsProjectiveMorphism f,
        IsQuasiProjective f ∧ IsProper f,
        IsHQuasiProjective f ∧ IsProper f,
        IsProper f ∧
          ∃ (L : X.Modules) (hL : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule X L),
            letI : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule X L := hL
            Stacks.Ample.IsAmple X L,
        IsProper f ∧
          ∃ (L : X.Modules) (hL : AlgebraicGeometry.SheafOfModules.IsInvertible X.presheaf
              X.ringCatSheaf.2 L),
            letI : AlgebraicGeometry.SheafOfModules.IsInvertible X.presheaf
              X.ringCatSheaf.2 L := hL
            Stacks.RelativeAmple.IsRelativelyAmple f L,
        IsProper f ∧
          ∃ (L : X.Modules) (hL : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule X L),
            letI : Stacks.Ample.IsInvertibleModule X L := hL
            Stacks.Tag01VMVeryAmple.IsRelativelyVeryAmple f L,
        ∃ A : SheafGradedAlgebra.{u} S,
          Stacks.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjRepresentsF1.IsGeneratedInDegreeOne A ∧
            (A.piece 1).IsFiniteType ∧
            ∃ e : X ≅ (relativeProj A).scheme,
              e.hom ≫ (relativeProj A).toBase = f]
```

> **Stacks Project, Tag 0B46, clause (2).** Let `S` carry an ample invertible sheaf. If
> `f : X ⟶ S` is projective and `g : Y ⟶ X` is projective, then `g ≫ f : Y ⟶ S` is projective.
>
> This is the source's proof. Condition (5) of Tag 0B45 at the base `S` makes `f` proper with `X`
> carrying an ample invertible sheaf; the same condition at the base `X` makes `g` proper with `Y`
> carrying an ample invertible sheaf; a composition of proper morphisms is proper (Tag 01W3); and
> condition (5) read backwards at the base `S` returns projectivity of `g ≫ f`. Condition (5) is
> part of the unconditional cycle of Tag 0B45, so nothing is assumed.

```lean
theorem isProjective_comp_of_hasAmpleInvertibleSheaf
    {X Y S : Scheme.{u}} (g : Y ⟶ X) (f : X ⟶ S)
    (hS : HasAmpleInvertibleSheaf S) (hf : IsProjective f) (hg : IsProjective g) :
    IsProjective (g ≫ f)

References: EGA IV (9.6.4).

> **[Stacks, Tag 0D2S].**  Let `f : X ⟶ S` be a proper morphism of schemes, let `ℒ` be an
> invertible `𝒪_X`-module and let `y` be a point of `S` such that `ℒ_y` is ample on the fibre `X_y`.
> Then `y` has an open neighbourhood `V ⊆ S` such that `ℒ|_{f⁻¹V}` is ample relative to `f⁻¹V / V`.
>
> **No hypothesis on `S`**:  this is [Stacks, Tag 0D2N],
> `Stacks.AmpleFibreNeighbourhood.exists_mem_isRelativelyAmple_of_isAmple_fibre`, with its
> Noetherian hypothesis removed by the limit argument described in the module documentation.  The
> statement is otherwise the same, down to the spelling of the hypothesis and of the conclusion, and
> invertibility of `ℒ_y` is an instance rather than a hypothesis for the same reason as there.
>
> The base is replaced by an affine — hence quasi-compact and quasi-separated — open neighbourhood
> `A` of `y`, which is legitimate in both directions: the hypothesis transfers by
> `Stacks.AmpleFibreLimit.isAmple_fibre_morphismRestrict`, and the conclusion transfers back along
> the isomorphism of arrows `AlgebraicGeometry.morphismRestrictRestrict` by
> `Stacks.AmpleFibreLimit.isRelativelyAmple_pullbackModule_of_commSq`, whose two horizontal
> morphisms are in this instance isomorphisms.

```lean
theorem exists_mem_isRelativelyAmple_of_isAmple_fibre {X S : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ S)
    [IsProper f] (L : X.Modules) [IsInvertibleModule X L] (y : S)

    ∃ V : S.Opens, y ∈ V ∧

```
