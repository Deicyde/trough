# The Quillen–Suslin theorem (Quillen patching, Horrocks and Bass)

> The Quillen–Suslin theorem and the commutative algebra behind it, on Mathlib's carriers. For a commutative ring A, the monic polynomials form a submonoid of A[X], and the localisation A⟨X⟩ at them is a faithfully flat A-algebra; for a field k, k⟨X⟩ is a field, and for a principal ideal domain k, k⟨X⟩ is a principal ideal domain (Quillen's Lemma 2). Modules extended from a base ring are defined, and an A[X]-module is extended from A exactly when it is A[X] ⊗ M(0); extended modules stay extended after base change, in one and in several variables. Quillen's dilation lemma is proved for a not necessarily commutative A-algebra R and f ∈ A; for a unit θ of R_f[X] there is k such that θ(g₁X) θ(g₂X)⁻¹ comes from a unit of R[X] with constant term one whenever g₁ − g₂ ∈ fᵏA. Finitely generated projective modules over two comaximal localisations R_f and R_g that are isomorphic over R_fg glue to a finitely generated projective R-module with the given localisations. For a finitely presented A-module N and an A-algebra B, the polynomials over B ⊗ End(N) act on B[X] ⊗ N, bijectively for flat B, which turns automorphisms of extended modules into units of a polynomial ring. With the dilation lemma this proves Quillen's patching theorem. For a finitely presented A[X]-module M, the a ∈ A such that M becomes extended after inverting a form an ideal (the Quillen ideal), and M is extended from A as soon as it is extended after localising at every maximal ideal of A; the same holds for finitely presented modules over A[X₁, …, Xₙ], by induction on n through A[X₁, …, Xₙ₊₁] ≅ A[X₁, …, Xₙ][X]. Horrocks' theorem follows. Over a local ring, a finitely generated projective A[X]-module that becomes free over A⟨X⟩ is free; over any ring, such a module that becomes extended from A over A⟨X⟩ is extended from A, and one that becomes free over A⟨X⟩ is free (Quillen's form). Bass's theorem holds too. Finitely generated projective A-modules that become isomorphic over A⟨X⟩ are isomorphic. The Quillen–Suslin theorem follows by induction on n; over a principal ideal domain k, every finitely generated projective k[X₁, …, Xₙ]-module is free. Over a Dedekind domain B, every finitely generated projective B[X₁, …, Xₙ]-module is extended from B. Suslin's completion theorem follows; over a principal ideal domain k, every unimodular vector over k[X₁, …, Xₙ] is the first column of an invertible matrix. Little Horrocks' global theorem holds too; a unimodular vector f over A[X] whose head coefficients, for formal degrees bounding the degrees of its entries, generate the unit ideal of A is sent to f(0) by an invertible matrix over A[X]. Suslin's r! theorem is proved for factorial-shaped exponents; for a unimodular row (a₀, …, a_r), r ≥ 1, and exponents m such that max(r − i, 1) divides m(σ i) for some permutation σ, the row (a₀^m₀, …, a_r^m_r) is the first row of a matrix of determinant one. In particular (a₀, a₁, a₂², …, a_rʳ) is completable, and the full divisibility statement r! ∣ ∏ mᵢ holds for r ≤ 2 (Swan–Towber for r = 2).
