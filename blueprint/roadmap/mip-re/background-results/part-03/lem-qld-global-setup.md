# Admissible padded setup

Assume $m, d \geq 1$, $m \mid q$ and $4m \mid q$ — the last follows from $m \mid q$ when $4m \leq q$, in particular when $48md \leq q$, the field having characteristic two — and let $(\psi, M_A, M_B)$ be a projective strategy for the Pauli basis test whose answers are in the format its questions prescribe (Lemma [Axis degree for expanded line measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-axis-degree.md) makes this a normalization). Write a point of the padded space $\mathbb{F}_q^{4m}$ as $u = (x, z, \alpha, \beta, w)$ with $2m - 2$ dummy coordinates $w$. The *padded strategy* is a strategy for the seeded low individual degree test (Definition [Seeded CL low individual degree test](../part-01/def-lidt-cl.md)) at parameters $(q, 4m, d, 1)$, played on the expanded state $\hat{\psi}$ of Lemma [Padded line POVM](lem-qld-padded-lines.md). Each player answers a point question $u$ with the sandwich $\widehat{M}^{X, x}_a \widehat{M}^{Z, z}_b \widehat{M}^{X, x}_a$ of their expanded point measurements, coarse-grained along $(a, b) \mapsto \alpha a + \beta b$ and extended by the identity to the combining coefficients' register — a measurement that ignores $w$ — and a line question with the padded line measurement of Lemma [Padded line POVM](lem-qld-padded-lines.md) at the canonical base point of the line, averaged over the raw directions that produce the question, its polynomial outcome resized to the format of the question. Every answer has the format the decider expects, and on an axis-parallel line the resizing loses nothing. Both measurements are POVMs; the dilation to a projective strategy is performed once, when the soundness theorem is applied (Lemma [A global padded polynomial PVM](lem-qld-global-pvm.md)). Moving the base point of a line question to the sampled point along the line does not change the padded line measurement, so what the decider reads is the padded line measurement at the sample's own data, coarse-grained by evaluation at the parameter of the sample's point — the quantity Lemma [Padded line POVM](lem-qld-padded-lines.md) controls. The Lean constructs it for a projective strategy in any bipartite model, the padded strategy being a pair of families of POVMs in the players' algebras of its register model; the statement above is its tensor-product instance.

## Proof

Definitions, and two invariances. The pasted line measurement is unchanged by moving the padded point along the padded line, because each block of the point either moves along its own subline or does not move at all (Lemma [The sublines distribution](../part-02/lem-qld-sublines.md)); and the resizing of an axis-line answer to degree $d$ is invisible on the support of a legally supported strategy (Lemma [Axis degree for expanded line measurements](../part-02/lem-qld-axis-degree.md)), while the resizing of a diagonal answer to degree $4md$ is a genuine padding. For the divisibility: $q$ is a power of two, the size of a finite field of characteristic two, so its divisor $m$ is one too, $m = 2^j$, and $4m = 2^{j+2}$ divides $q = 2^n$ exactly when $j + 2 \leq n$, that is when $4m \leq q$; and $4m \leq 48md$ for $d \geq 1$.
