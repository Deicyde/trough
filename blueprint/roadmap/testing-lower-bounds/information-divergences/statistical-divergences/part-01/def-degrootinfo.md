# deGrootInfo

The DeGroot statistical information between finite measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ for $\pi \in [0,1]$ is $I_\pi (\mu, \nu) = \mathcal I_{(\pi, 1 - \pi)}(\mu, \nu)$ .
