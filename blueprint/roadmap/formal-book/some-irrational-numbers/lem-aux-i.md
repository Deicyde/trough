# lem_aux_i

For some fixed $n \ge 1$, let

$$
f(x) = \frac{x^n (1-x)^n}{n!}.
$$

- (i) The function $f(x)$ is a polynomial of the form $f(x) = \frac{1}{n!} \sum_{i=n}^{2n} c_i x^i$, where the coefficients $c_i$ are integers.
