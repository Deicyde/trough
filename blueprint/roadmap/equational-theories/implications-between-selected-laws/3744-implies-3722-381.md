# 3744 implies 3722, 381

[E3744](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?3744) $({x \diamond y = (x \diamond z) \diamond (w \diamond y)})$ implies [E3722](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?3722) $({x \diamond y = (x \diamond y) \diamond (x \diamond y)})$ and [E381](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?381) $({x \diamond y = (x \diamond z) \diamond y})$.

- [Equation 381](../selected-laws/part-02/eq381.md)

## Proof

By hypothesis, one has

$$
x \diamond y = (x \diamond z) \diamond (w \diamond y)
$$

for all $x,y,z,w$. Various specializations of this give

$$
\begin{aligned}
x \diamond y &= (x \diamond z) \diamond (y \diamond y)  \\
x \diamond z &= (x \diamond z) \diamond (x \diamond z)  \\
(x \diamond z) \diamond y &= ((x \diamond z) \diamond (x \diamond z)) \diamond (y \diamond y) .
\end{aligned}
$$

(381-2) gives [E3722](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?3722), while (381-1), (381-2), (381-3) gives

$$
x \diamond y = (x\diamond z) \diamond y
$$

which is [E381](https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/implications/?381).
