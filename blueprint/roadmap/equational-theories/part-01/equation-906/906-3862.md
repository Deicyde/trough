# 906-3862

For finite magmas, equation 906 implies equation 3862,

$$
(x \diamond (x \diamond x)) \diamond x = x \diamond x.\qquad\text{(5)}
$$

## Depends on

Its statement uses no other node of the blueprint.

## Proof

Observe from [(2)](README.md) that

$$
L_x S^2 x = L_x (L_x x \diamond Sx) = x
$$

while from [(3)](README.md) we have

$$
L_{L_x Sx} S^2 x = L_x Sx \diamond S S x = L_x^{-1} Sx = x.
$$

Thus

$$
L_x S^2 x = L_{L_x Sx} S^2 x = x
$$

and hence by the $n=2$ case of [Edge disjointness of left cycles](https://github.com/teorth/equational_theories/blob/2ddb16c4c4c036242ad3e18167579b57cac2a006/blueprint/src/chapter/906.tex#L16)

$$
L_x x = L_{L_x Sx} x
$$

giving the claim.
