> **Stacks Project, Tag 0BL7.** The internal hom between two finite étale schemes over `X` is
> represented by a finite étale scheme over `X`, compatibly with every base change: the
> representing object is exhibited by an isomorphism of functors on the whole of `(Over X)ᵒᵖ`.

```lean
public theorem exists_finiteEtale_internalHom (X : Scheme.{u}) (U V : Over X)
    [IsFinite U.hom] [Etale U.hom] [IsFinite V.hom] [Etale V.hom] :
    ∃ W : Over X, IsFinite W.hom ∧ Etale W.hom ∧
      Nonempty
        (yoneda.obj W ≅
          (Limits.prod.functor.obj U).op ⋙ yoneda.obj V)
```

> **Stacks Project, Tag 0BNB.** For a connected scheme `X` and a geometric point `x` of `X`,
> the category `FÉt_X` of finite étale covers of `X` together with the geometric fibre functor
> `F_x` is a Galois category.
>
> The six axioms are: finite limits and finite colimits of `FÉt_X`
> (`Stacks.FundamentalGroup.hasFiniteLimits_finiteEtaleOver` and
> `Stacks.FundamentalGroup.hasFiniteColimits_finiteEtaleOver`); every cover is a finite coproduct
> of connected ones (`AlgebraicGeometry.exists_finiteCoproduct_isConnected_finiteEtaleOver`);
> finiteness of every geometric fibre (`AlgebraicGeometry.finite_finiteEtaleFiber`); that `F_x`
> reflects isomorphisms (`AlgebraicGeometry.reflectsIsomorphisms_finiteEtaleFiberFunctor`); and
> exactness of `F_x`, whose limit half is
> `AlgebraicGeometry.preservesFiniteLimits_finiteEtaleFiberFunctor` and whose colimit half is
> `Stacks.FundamentalGroup.preservesFiniteColimits_finiteEtaleFiberFunctor`.

```lean
instance isGaloisCategory_finiteEtaleFiberFunctor {X : Scheme.{u}} [ConnectedSpace X]
    (x : GeometricPoint X) : IsGaloisCategory (finiteEtaleFiberFunctor x)
```
