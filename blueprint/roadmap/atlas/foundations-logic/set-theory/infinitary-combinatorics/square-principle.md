## Square sequence

Jensen's square principle sequence:  C = (C_α : α < κ⁺) is a □_κ-sequence: for every limit
 α < κ⁺, C_α is club in α of order type at most κ, and the family coheres —
 C_β = C_α ∩ β whenever β is a limit point of C_α.
