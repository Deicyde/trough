# An extriangulated category with no triangulated embedding

> Over every field, an Aronszajn tree gives one essentially small hereditary Krull–Schmidt extriangulated category with enough injectives that has no fully faithful extriangulated embedding into a triangulated category in any universe. Every countable family of objects lies in an extension-closed subcategory with an algebraic realization. For inverse systems of vector spaces over a field indexed by a nonempty well-ordered set, with surjective transition maps, surjectivity of the limit projections characterizes embeddings and algebraic realizations.
