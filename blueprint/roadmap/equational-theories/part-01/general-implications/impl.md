# Implication

A law $E$ is said to *imply* another law $E'$ if $\{E\} \models E'$, or equivalently:

$$
G \models w  \simeq  w' \implies G \models w''  \simeq  w''' \hbox{ for all magmas } G
$$

Two laws are said to be *equivalent* if they imply each other.
