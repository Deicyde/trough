# Projective limit

A measure $\mu$ on $E^T$ is the projective limit of a projective family of measures $P$ indexed by finite sets of $T$ if, for every finite set $I \subseteq T$, the projection from $E^T$ to $E^I$ maps $\mu$ to $P_I$.
