# Existence of a finitely presented non-sofic group

> Constructs a finitely presented group that is not sofic, resolving the question whether every group admits arbitrarily accurate finite permutation approximations. The development combines an explicit elementary group with property T, a finite-table construction of a finitely presented group, finite-model expansion, and a local obstruction to sofic approximation.
