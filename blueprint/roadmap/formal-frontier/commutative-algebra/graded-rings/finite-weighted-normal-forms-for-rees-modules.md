# Finite weighted normal forms for Rees modules

For any ideal I of a commutative ring R and any R-module M, a natural I-filtration has finitely generated Rees module exactly when one finite family of elements x_i and natural weights w_i satisfies H.N n = sum_i I^(n-w_i) • R x_i for every natural n, with truncated natural subtraction.
