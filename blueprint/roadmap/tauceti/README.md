# Tau Ceti

[Tau Ceti](https://taucetiproject.org/), read at commit [ace60277](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/ace60277c4c5ba2166795686bca1af4052290145) (2026-11-07), with its roadmaps at [eaf17b10](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/eaf17b10d9267c0e3cdd3b62dd5e8983e47c690d): 2,733 articles on 3,922 of its declarations, in a part for each of the library's top-level areas. 1,377 are the declarations a roadmap's status names as a result or a notable definition. A model writes the statuses and names at most five results in each, so the other 2,545 are results Tau Ceti's own docstrings name: a theorem whose docstring opens with a bold title naming a result after a person ("Burnside's pᵃqᵇ theorem", "Schur-Weyl duality"), a title such as "The cellular approximation theorem", or a short title pinned to a textbook theorem; a definition that carries such a theorem as data; and the four theorems Tau Ceti's website shows. The forms of one result share an article. The 11 names the statuses give that Tau Ceti does not have at this commit have no article. A roadmap's plan, its suggested signatures and its frontier say what Tau Ceti may add, so they are left out, and so is the statuses' prose: a model writes it, and Tau Ceti does not check it.

Each of the 126 roadmaps whose status names a declaration has a section, in the part where most of its articles are: what its status names, and the named results in a module, or else a folder of modules, where only this roadmap's status names a declaration. The other named results are in 109 sections by area of modules; an area with fewer than 3 of them joins its part's *Other results*.

Each article's text is its declaration's docstring and signature, under Tau Ceti's Apache-2.0 license; a docstring that opens with a bold title, or with bold words its first sentence goes on from, gives the article its title. Dependencies come from Tau Ceti's declarations (`lean/tauceti/TauCetiDeps.lean`): under *Depends on*, the articles that a statement reaches through the statements of declarations not mapped here, and under *Proof depends on*, those a proof reaches through any chain of Tau Ceti declarations not mapped here. A theorem or definition that reaches `sorry`, itself or through a declaration mapped here, says so, and one stated from a proposition Tau Ceti defines says what it assumes.
