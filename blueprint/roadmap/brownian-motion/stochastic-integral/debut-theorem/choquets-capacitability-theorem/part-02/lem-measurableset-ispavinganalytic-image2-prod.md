# MeasurableSet.isPavingAnalytic_image2_prod

Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a measurable space and $s$ be a measurable set of $\mathcal{X} \times \mathbb{R}$. Then $s$ is $(M_{\mathcal{X}} \times I_{\mathbb{R}})$-analytic.
