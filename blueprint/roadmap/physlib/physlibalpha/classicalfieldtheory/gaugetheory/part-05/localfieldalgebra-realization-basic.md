# Invariants descend along realizations

The module `PhyslibAlpha.ClassicalFieldTheory.GaugeTheory.LocalFieldAlgebra.Realization.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/LocalFieldAlgebra/Realization/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/LocalFieldAlgebra/Realization/Basic.lean): 12 declarations, 12 with a docstring.

> An invariant in the image of a realization of the covariant or the local field algebra is the
> image of an invariant of the covariant field algebra.

## i. Overview

> Let `k` be a realization of the covariant field algebra of a field datum `T` in an algebra `B`.
> It is an algebra map, equivariant for the ordinary gauge group and for the Lorentz group. Suppose
> `k a` is invariant under both groups. We show that `k a = k a'` for some `a'` invariant under both
> groups in the covariant field algebra. So every gauge and Lorentz invariant expression of a
> realization is written by an invariant covariant expression.
>
> The proof averages `a` over both groups. By
> `GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.finiteSubrep`, the element `a` lies in a finite-dimensional
> subspace `W`, stable under both groups, on which both act continuously. For a compact gauge group
> the Haar average gives a Reynolds element `r₁` of the gauge group on `W`, and the Casimir
> projection gives a Reynolds element `r₂` of `SL(2,ℂ)` on `W`. Their product `r` is a Reynolds
> element for the joint action, so `a' = r • a` is invariant under both groups. The map `k`
> intertwines the actions of `r₁` and `r₂`. On the invariant vector `k a` they act as the sums of
> their coefficients, which are one, so `k a' = k a`.
>
> The theorem needs three conditions. The gauge group must be compact, the Lorentz representation
> of every species continuous, and the gauge and Lorentz representations of every species must
> commute (see `GaugeFieldData.GaugeLorentzCommute`).
>
> The same holds for a realization `k` of the whole local field algebra, now with the jet gauge
> group acting on the target. The image `k x` is invariant under the jet gauge group and the
> Lorentz group exactly when `k x = k a'` for some `a'` of the covariant field algebra invariant
> under the ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group. The reverse direction holds because the
> jet action on the covariant field algebra factors through evaluation. The forward direction has
> three steps.
>
> * The local field algebra is generated by the gauge-field symbols `∂_s A_μ` and the covariant
>   field algebra. The matter symbols `∂_s ψ` are polynomials in the covariant towers `∇_l ψ` and
>   the gauge-field symbols, by the span lemma
>   `GlobalGaugeGroup.adjoin_symbols_eq_adjoin_covDerivIter`.
> * So `k x` lies in the subalgebra generated by the realized gauge-field symbols and the image
>   `S` of the covariant field algebra. The elements of `S` are fixed by the pure jets and commute
>   with the gauge-field symbols, which come from the central connection factor. The
>   classification `GaugeBoson.invariant_mem_adjoin_fieldStrength` then places the
>   jet-invariant `k x` in the subalgebra generated by the realized field-strength tower and `S`,
>   which is the image of the covariant field algebra. So `k x = k c` for a covariant `c`.
> * `exists_mem_invariants_map_eq`, applied to the restriction of `k` to the covariant field
>   algebra and to `c`, gives the invariant `a'`.

## A. Descent for products of groups

**`Representation.IsReynolds.exists_mem_invariants_map_eq_prod`**, a lemma, [line 109](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/LocalFieldAlgebra/Realization/Basic.lean#L109).

> The descent property for a representation of `H₁ × H₂` with Reynolds elements for both
> factors on the same subspace. If `f` intertwines each factor with a representation on `B`, the
> vector `a` lies in the subspace, and `f a` is invariant under both representations on `B`, then
> `f a` is the image of an invariant vector. The representations on `B` need not commute.

```lean
lemma IsReynolds.exists_mem_invariants_map_eq_prod {ρ : Representation k (H₁ × H₂) V}
    {W : Submodule k V} {r₁ : MonoidAlgebra k H₁} {r₂ : MonoidAlgebra k H₂}
    (hr₁ : IsReynolds (ρ.comp (MonoidHom.inl H₁ H₂)) W r₁)
    (hr₂ : IsReynolds (ρ.comp (MonoidHom.inr H₁ H₂)) W r₂) {σ₁ : Representation k H₁ B}
    {σ₂ : Representation k H₂ B} {f : V →ₗ[k] B}
    (hf₁ : ∀ h v, f (ρ.comp (MonoidHom.inl H₁ H₂) h v) = σ₁ h (f v))
    (hf₂ : ∀ h v, f (ρ.comp (MonoidHom.inr H₁ H₂) h v) = σ₂ h (f v)) {a : V} (ha : a ∈ W)
    (h₁ : f a ∈ σ₁.invariants) (h₂ : f a ∈ σ₂.invariants) :
    ∃ a' ∈ ρ.invariants, f a' = f a
```

## B. Reynolds elements for the joint action

**`GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.exists_isReynolds_finiteSubrep`**, a lemma, [line 138](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/LocalFieldAlgebra/Realization/Basic.lean#L138).

> For a compact gauge group, the joint action of the gauge and Lorentz groups has a Reynolds
> element on `finiteSubrep`, the product of the Reynolds elements of the two groups.

```lean
lemma exists_isReynolds_finiteSubrep [CompactSpace G.group] (hL : T.LorentzContinuous)
    (hC : T.GaugeLorentzCommute) (a : T.LocalCovFieldAlgebra) :
    ∃ (r₁ : MonoidAlgebra ℂ G.group) (r₂ : MonoidAlgebra ℂ SL(2,ℂ)),
      Representation.IsReynolds ((repGaugeLorentz T hC).comp (MonoidHom.inl _ _))
        (finiteSubrep hL hC a).toSubmodule r₁ ∧
      Representation.IsReynolds ((repGaugeLorentz T hC).comp (MonoidHom.inr _ _))
        (finiteSubrep hL hC a).toSubmodule r₂
```

## C. Invariants descend along realizations

**`GaugeFieldData.LocalCovFieldAlgebra.Realization.exists_mem_invariants_map_eq`**, a lemma, [line 169](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/ClassicalFieldTheory/GaugeTheory/LocalFieldAlgebra/Realization/Basic.lean#L169).

> Invariants descend along realizations. If the image `k a` of a covariant expression under a
> realization is invariant under the gauge and Lorentz groups, it is the image of a covariant
> expression invariant under both groups. This needs a compact gauge group, continuous Lorentz
> representations, and commuting gauge and Lorentz representations of every species.

```lean
lemma exists_mem_invariants_map_eq [CompactSpace G.group] (hL : T.LorentzContinuous)
    (hC : T.GaugeLorentzCommute) (k : Realization T hGL B repGauge repLorentz)
    (a : T.LocalCovFieldAlgebra) (h₁ : k.toAlgHom a ∈ repGauge.invariants)
    (h₂ : k.toAlgHom a ∈ repLorentz.invariants) :
    ∃ a' : T.LocalCovFieldAlgebra, a' ∈ (repValue T).invariants ∧
      a' ∈ (repLorentzGroup T hGL).invariants ∧ k.toAlgHom a' = k.toAlgHom a
```

> The gauge and Lorentz invariants of a realization of the local field algebra. The image
> `k x` is invariant under the jet gauge group and the Lorentz group exactly when it is the image
> of a covariant expression invariant under the ordinary gauge group and the Lorentz group. This
> needs a compact gauge group, continuous Lorentz representations, and commuting gauge and
> Lorentz representations of every species.

```lean
lemma mem_invariants_iff_exists_mem_invariants [CompactSpace G.group]
    (hL : T.LorentzContinuous) (hC : T.GaugeLorentzCommute) (x : T.LocalFieldAlgebra) :
    k.toAlgHom x ∈ repJet.invariants ∧ k.toAlgHom x ∈ repLorentz.invariants ↔
      ∃ a' : T.LocalCovFieldAlgebra, a' ∈ (LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repValue T).invariants ∧
        a' ∈ (LocalCovFieldAlgebra.repLorentzGroup T hC.gaugeLorentzCompatible).invariants ∧
        k.toAlgHom (a' : T.LocalFieldAlgebra) = k.toAlgHom x
```
