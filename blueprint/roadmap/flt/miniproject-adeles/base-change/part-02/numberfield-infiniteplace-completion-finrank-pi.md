# NumberField.InfinitePlace.Completion.finrank\_pi\_eq\_finrank\_tensorProduct

For a fixed infinite place $v$ of $K$, we have $\text{dim}_{K_v} \prod_{w\mid v} L_w = \text{dim}_{K_v} L\otimes_K K_v$.
