# `MO` is the quotient of the Lazard ring by the `2`-series

the kernel of
`ofLazardRing : L →+* MO` is the ideal generated by the coefficients of `[2](https://taucetiproject.org/docs/references.html#ref_X)` for the universal
formal group law over `L`.

```lean
TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.ker_ofLazardRing :
  RingHom.ker TauCeti.ExponentTwoLazardRing.ofLazardRing =
    Ideal.span
      (Set.range fun n => (PowerSeries.coeff n) (TauCeti.LazardRing.formalGroup.nsmulSeries 2))
```
