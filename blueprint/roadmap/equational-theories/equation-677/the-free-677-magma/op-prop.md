# Properties of operation

Let $x,y \in M_X$ be such that $x \diamond y = z$. Then either

$$
x, y < z = (x,y)
$$

or

$$
x, z < y = (z, (x \diamond z) \diamond x).
$$

In particular, $x$ is strictly upper bounded by one of $y,z$.
