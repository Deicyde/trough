# Omitted variables form a transcendence basis over the source fraction ring

In the same five-universe commutative-domain setting and under the full
canonical fraction-renaming scalar action,
[`MvPolynomial.isAlgebraic_adjoin_fractionRing_map_rename_compl`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/FractionRingTranscendenceBasis.lean)
proves that `K` is algebraic over the `L`-subalgebra generated by the
complementary localized variables, and
[`MvPolynomial.isTranscendenceBasis_fractionRing_map_rename_compl`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/FractionRingTranscendenceBasis.lean)
identifies that family as a transcendence basis over `L`. Under the same
hypotheses,
[`MvPolynomial.isFractionRing_adjoin_fractionRing_map_rename_compl`](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomials/FractionRingTranscendenceBasis.lean)
makes `K` the fraction ring of that generated `L`-subalgebra, for its canonical
inclusion into `K`. The adjoin is over
the source **fraction ring**, not the coefficient ring. Neither fraction-ring
realization needs a `Field` instance, and neither variable type need be finite.
The [direct-import examples](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/MultivariatePolynomialsTests/FractionRingTranscendenceBasis.lean)
include the nonempty complement of `Unit → Bool`, `() ↦ false`, over `ℤ`,
as well as arbitrary fraction-ring realizations.

*Quoted from the headline results in [README.md](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/blob/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a/README.md#headline-results) of [FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials) at commit [724d9804](https://github.com/FormalFrontier/multivariate-polynomials/tree/724d9804aa6e853d9fe34f6a35c5c0d35c7f8c6a), Apache-2.0.*
