# CPlus

The subgroup $C^+\subseteq(\mathcal O_{K^+})^\times$ is generated by $-1$ and the standard real cyclotomic units:

$$
C^+=\bigl\langle -1,\ \varepsilon_2,\ldots,\varepsilon_{(p-1)/2}
\bigr\rangle.
$$
