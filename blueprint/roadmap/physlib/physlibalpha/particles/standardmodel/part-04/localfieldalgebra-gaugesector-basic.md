# The gauge sector

The module `PhyslibAlpha.Particles.StandardModel.LocalFieldAlgebra.GaugeSector.Basic`, [PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/GaugeSector/Basic.lean](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/GaugeSector/Basic.lean): 15 declarations, 15 with a docstring.

> The field-strength towers of the Standard Model, their laws, and the submodules they span.

## i. Overview

> The gauge sector of the covariant field algebra of the Standard Model is generated by the
> field-strength towers `gaugeFieldStrength`, indexed by ordered tuples of
> covariant-derivative directions, two covector indices and a functional on the gauge algebra.
> They transform under the global gauge group through the adjoint action on the dual value index,
> under the Lorentz group as the covariant derivatives of a two-index Lorentz tensor, they
> commute with one another, are antisymmetric in the two covector indices, and each tower is a
> `massWeightPoly`-eigenvector of weight `2 * (2 + n)` (mass dimension `2 + n`).
> In prose the tower `gaugeFieldStrength` is written `F`.

## ii. Key results

> - `LocalCovFieldAlgebra.gaugeFieldStrength` : the field strength and its covariant derivatives.
> - `GaugeSector.repGauge_F`, `GaugeSector.repLorentz_F`, `GaugeSector.massWeight_F`,
>   `GaugeSector.F_comm_F`, `GaugeSector.F_antisymm` : the laws of the field-strength towers.
> - `GaugeSector.gaugeAlgebra` : the algebra generated by the field strength and all its
>   covariant derivatives.
> - `GaugeSector.gluonField`, `GaugeSector.wField`, `GaugeSector.hyperchargeField` : the gluon,
>   `W`-boson and hypercharge contributions to the field strength and its covariant derivatives.
> - `GaugeSector.derivSubmodule` : the span of the field-strength symbols with `n` covariant
>   derivatives.

```lean
lemma repLorentz_F (Λ : SL(2,ℂ)) (n : ℕ) (l : Fin n → (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3))
    (μ ν : Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3) (φ : Module.Dual ℝ GaugeAlgebra) :
    repLorentzGroup Λ (gaugeFieldStrength l μ ν φ) =
      ∑ p : Fin n → (Fin 1 ⊕ Fin 3),
        (∏ i, (((SL2C.toLorentzGroup Λ).1 (p i) (l i) : ℝ) : ℂ)) •
      ∑ a, (((SL2C.toLorentzGroup Λ).1 a μ : ℝ) : ℂ) •
      ∑ b, (((SL2C.toLorentzGroup Λ).1 b ν : ℝ) : ℂ) • gaugeFieldStrength p a b φ
```

## B. The individual gauge-group contributions to the field strength

> The field-strength symbol family `gaugeFieldStrength` packages together the contributions of
> the three factors of the gauge group. Evaluating it on the coordinate of `GaugeAlgebra.stdBasis`
> dual to a Gell-Mann direction, a Pauli direction, or the `u(1)` direction isolates the
> gluon, `W`-boson, and hypercharge contributions individually.

### C.2. Closure of the derivative submodules under the gauge and Lorentz groups

**`StandardModel.GaugeSector.derivSubmodule_map_repGauge_le`**, a lemma, [line 249](https://github.com/leanprover-community/physlib/blob/d6a3586eb33124da3d6740c9e609d8adbf87e651/PhyslibAlpha/Particles/StandardModel/LocalFieldAlgebra/GaugeSector/Basic.lean#L249).

> The image of a derivative-`n` submodule under a gauge transformation lies inside the
> same submodule. Each generator `gaugeFieldStrength l μ ν φ` is sent by `repGauge_F` to another
> generator `gaugeFieldStrength l μ ν φ'` with the same derivative slots and covector
> indices.

```lean
lemma derivSubmodule_map_repGauge_le (n : ℕ) (g : GaugeGroupI) :
    (derivSubmodule n).map (repGaugeGroupI g) ≤ derivSubmodule n
```
