# Grothendieck universes

> Grothendieck universes in Mathlib's model ZFSet of ZFC, with their closure properties (subsets, pairs, ordered pairs, unions, ranges, products, function sets, and the bound card x < card U for x ∈ U). Every universe U is the von Neumann stage V\_(rank U), by ∈-induction; its cardinal is a regular strong limit, so U = V\_(card U).ord. Conversely V_ω and V_κ for κ inaccessible are universes, giving Williams's classification of the universes as V_ω and the V_κ with κ inaccessible. The cardinal of a universe is inaccessible exactly when the universe contains ω, and Kruse's criterion x ∈ U ⇔ x ⊆ U ∧ card x < card U holds. Universes are totally ordered by membership, V_ω is the least one, the intersection of a nonempty set of universes is its least element, a set lying in some universe lies in a least one, and every set lies in a universe exactly when every cardinal lies below an inaccessible cardinal.
