# Chirotopes, line arrangements and flag systems of maps

> Chirotopes of rank r in the three-term Grassmann–Plücker form, monotone arrangements, and flag systems acted on by the cartographic group. The determinant signs of a spanning family over an ordered field form a chirotope, and reorientation and relabelling preserve that property. A monotone arrangement is simple exactly when its sign function is nonzero on every triple of distinct elements. For straight lines of strictly decreasing slope, that function equals the determinant signs of the line vectors. With at least two lines it is a realizable rank-three chirotope. Flag-system connectedness is equivalent to graph preconnectedness. The orientation double cover is orientable and, for nonempty systems with fixed-point-free involutions, is connected exactly when the original is connected and nonorientable. For closed-surface flag systems it doubles the natural cardinality of each cell type. Nonempty connected flag systems are classified by conjugacy classes of subgroups of the cartographic group. For nonempty connected closed-surface maps it characterizes orientability by even monodromy. It characterizes regularity using automorphisms, normal subgroups, and involutions satisfying the distant commutation relations.
