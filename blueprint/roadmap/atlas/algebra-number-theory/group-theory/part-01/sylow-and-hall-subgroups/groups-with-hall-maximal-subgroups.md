## Hall subgroup (Kourovka Problem 19.109)

A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is a Hall subgroup
when its order $|H|$ is coprime to its index $|G : H|$, where an infinite order or index is
counted as $0$.

## Pronormal subgroup

A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is pronormal in $G$ when for every
$g \in G$ there is an element $x$ of the subgroup $\langle H, gHg^{-1} \rangle$ generated by $H$
and $gHg^{-1}$ such that $xHx^{-1} = gHg^{-1}$.

## Second maximal subgroup

A subgroup $K$ of a group $G$ is second maximal when it is a
maximal subgroup of a maximal subgroup of $G$: there is a maximal subgroup $M$ of $G$ with
$K < M$ and no subgroup $L$ of $G$ with $K < L < M$.

## Zenkov's question on Hall maximal subgroups (Kourovka Problem 19.109)

For every
non-abelian finite simple group $G$, all maximal subgroups of $G$ are Hall subgroups if and only
if every second maximal subgroup of $G$ is pronormal in $G$.

## Negative answer to Zenkov's question (Kourovka Problem 19.109)

It is not true that for every
non-abelian finite simple group $G$, all maximal subgroups of $G$ are Hall subgroups if and only
if every second maximal subgroup of $G$ is pronormal in $G$.

The group $\mathrm{SL}_2(2^{11})$, in which every second maximal subgroup is pronormal
(V. N. Tyutyanov, 2018), is a counterexample.

## Hall subgroup (Kourovka 17.92)

A subgroup $H$ of a group $G$ is a Hall subgroup when its
order $|H|$ is coprime to its index $|G : H|$.

Here $|H|$ and $|G : H|$ are natural numbers, taken to be $0$ when infinite.

## Composition factor of a group (Kourovka 17.92)

For groups $G$ and $S$, $S$ is a
composition factor of $G$ when there are subgroups $H$ and $K$ of $G$ such that $H$ is subnormal in
$G$, $H \cap K$ is normal in $H$, and the quotient $H/(H \cap K)$ is a simple group isomorphic to
$S$.

not soluble and every maximal subgroup of $G$ is a Hall subgroup.

## Maslova's theorem on groups with Hall maximal subgroups

Every non-abelian composition factor
of a finite non-soluble group all of whose maximal subgroups are Hall subgroups is isomorphic to
$L_2(7)$, $L_2(11)$ or $L_5(2)$, so the first half of the answer to Kourovka 17.92 holds. Here
$L_n(q)$ is the projective special linear group $\mathrm{PSL}_n(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$, the
quotient of $\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$ by its centre.

Maslova (2012) proved this; the Kourovka Archive gives these three groups as the answer to 17.92.

## The three answer groups of Kourovka 17.92 occur

Each of $L_2(7)$, $L_2(11)$ and $L_5(2)$ is a
composition factor of some finite non-soluble group all of whose maximal subgroups are Hall
subgroups, so the second half of the answer to Kourovka 17.92 holds. Here $L_n(q)$ is the
projective special linear group $\mathrm{PSL}_n(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$, the quotient of
$\mathrm{SL}_n(\mathbb{Z}/q\mathbb{Z})$ by its centre.

Each of the three groups is itself non-soluble with all of its maximal subgroups Hall
(Levchuk-Likharev, Tyutyanov, as recalled by Maslova and Revin); with Maslova's theorem the three
groups are exactly the non-abelian composition factors.
