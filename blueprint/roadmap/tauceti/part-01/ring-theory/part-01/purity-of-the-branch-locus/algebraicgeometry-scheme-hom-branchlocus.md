# AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.branchLocus

The **branch locus** of `f`: the image of the ramification locus, the points of `Y` over which
`f` is ramified at some point.

```lean
AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Hom.branchLocus.{u} {X Y : AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme} (f : X ⟶ Y) : Set ↥Y
```
