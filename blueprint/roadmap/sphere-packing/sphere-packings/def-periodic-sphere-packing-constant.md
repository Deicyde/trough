# Periodic-sphere-packing-constant

The periodic sphere packing constant is defined to be

$$
\Delta_{d}^{\text{periodic}} := \sup_{\substack{P \subset \mathbb{R}^d \\ \text{periodic packing}}} \Delta_P
$$
