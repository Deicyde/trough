# eGamma

The $E_\gamma$ or hockey-stick divergence between finite measures $\mu$ and $\nu$ for $\gamma \in (0,+\infty)$ is $E_\gamma(\mu, \nu) = \mathcal I_{(1,\gamma)}(\mu, \nu)$ .
