# Effective binary matrix arithmetic and elimination

Uniform polynomial-time ambient programs compute binary vector addition and dot products, matrix application, transposition and multiplication, a basis of the span of a supplied family, solutions of consistent binary linear systems, inverses of nonsingular binary matrices, and generators of matrix kernels. Explicit output dimensions are supplied in unary. Basis rows retain coefficient certificates in the original family; the returned vectors are independent, span exactly that family, and number at most the ambient dimension. Inverse output uses canonical binary coordinates and satisfies both multiplication identities.

## Proof

Reduce each candidate against the stored pivots, XORing its coefficient certificate at every step, and append a nonzero remainder at its first nonzero coordinate. Later rows vanish at every earlier pivot. This triangular invariant proves independence; reduction preserves membership in every subspace containing the stored rows, which proves equality of spans. Reducing a target with an initially zero certificate gives its coefficients in the original generating family. Apply this solver to the columns of a matrix and to each standard basis vector to construct an inverse. Each reduction preserves or shrinks both row widths, and insertion adds a row of size bounded by the candidate's size. These bounds prove polynomial runtime on all raw inputs, including malformed ones.
