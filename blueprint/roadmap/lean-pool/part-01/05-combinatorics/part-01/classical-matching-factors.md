# Classical matching and factor theorems in finite graphs

> Develops augmenting-path and odd-component certificates for maximum matchings, bipartite matching-cover duality, perfect matchings in bridgeless cubic graphs, Tutte's f-factor criterion, and decompositions of even-regular graphs into two-factors. Includes the finite Dilworth and Mirsky partition theorems as an order-theoretic companion. All graph results use Mathlib's SimpleGraph and matching predicates. Hall's and Tutte's one-factor theorems are imported from Mathlib; the general matching and factor conclusions are proved here. The collection integrates five Palomar-registered developments and does not claim new mathematical results or a formalization of polynomial-time algorithms.
