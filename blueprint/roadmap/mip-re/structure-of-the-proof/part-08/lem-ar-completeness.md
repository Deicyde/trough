# Answer-reduction completeness

A perfect PCC input witness gives a perfect typed answer-reduction witness, using the complete honest proof tuple and one combined measurement rule on both players. All queried formats are satisfied; mismatched proof branches use a whole legal zero tuple. Apply detyping completeness, including its identical-operator property, to obtain the exterior PCC witness. In the ambient form, for $\lambda,\mu\geq1$: an input within `inBudget` $\lambda$ $\mu$ $n$ with $\left\vert {\mathcal{D}} \right\vert\leq\sigma$ and a value-$1$ PCC strategy at the answer cut $2^{(\lambda n+1)^\mu}$ gives the answer-reduced verifier a value-$1$ PCC strategy at the answer cut $32(k+1)(m'+7)^2$, and so at the output bound. Two hypotheses on the PCP decider are used: its field is the Shoup field the sampler downsizes along (`ShoupField`), and, for the output bound, its parameters are polynomial in $(\log n,\log T,Q,\sigma)$ (`ParamsBound`, as in Lemma [Answer-reduction construction](lem-ar-construction.md)).

## Proof

Each player measures the oracularized game's honest strategy (Lemma [Completeness of oracularization, at the level of games](../part-06/lem-oracular-completeness.md)) at the oracularized question its seed's oracle half determines, and post-processes the outcome classically: an oracle answering the pair $(a,b)$ at seed $x$ holds a PCP proof of $(x,a,b)$ from Theorem [The bespoke PCP for deciders](../part-07/thm-pcp-decider.md), whose first two answer polynomials are the low-degree encodings of $a$ and $b$; an isolated player $v$ answering $a$ holds the low-degree encoding of $a$ at copy $v$ and zero elsewhere; each answers every low-degree question honestly, with the values at a point and the restrictions to a line. A measurement read through a question map and a question-dependent answer map stays projective, PCC when pairs of positive weight are read as pairs of positive weight, and of value $1$ when the old strategy's operators for rejected pairs multiply to zero there and every accepted old pair is read as an accepted new one. Two typed questions of positive weight are read off one seed: their oracle halves are an oracularized pair of positive weight, whose checks (equal roles give equal answers, an oracle's component matches the isolated player's, the oracle's pair is accepted) are exactly the consistency the five steps of the answer-reduced predicate need, and their PCP halves are questions of one PCP vector, where the seeded test (Definition [Seeded CL low individual degree test](../../background-results/part-01/def-lidt-cl.md)) accepts honest answers of polynomials of individual degree at most $7$, the base points the presentation outputs being already canonical. The oracle's check passes at every point because the input decider accepts within the PCP's time bound $T=2^{(Q+5)(\mu+1)}$, $Q=(\lambda n+1)^\mu$: it runs within $2^Q(\left\vert {d} \right\vert+1)^\mu$ on every input, and the answers are already cut at $2^Q$, so the timeout of Lemma [Timeout-compatible oracle PCC completeness](https://github.com/vidick/MIPRE-formalization/blob/5926bdff5db621f44682a101007ef2fe3ae8d970/blueprint/src/content/06_proof_structure.tex#L5391) is here a consequence of determinism rather than a truncation. The PCP's validity needs $2\lceil\log n\rceil\leq T$, which is where $\lambda,\mu\geq1$ enters. Honest answers are at most $32(k+1)(m'+7)^2$ bits long, the answer cut, where the detyped decider's predicate is the typed game's; detyping completeness (Lemma [Detyping PCC completeness](../part-01/lem-detype-completeness.md)) gives the output's strategy.
