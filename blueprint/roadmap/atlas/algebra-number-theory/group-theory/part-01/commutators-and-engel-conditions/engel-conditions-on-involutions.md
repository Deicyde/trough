## Involution-generated 2-groups with the Engel condition on involutions

A group $G$ has
this property when every element of $G$ has order a power of $2$, $G$ is generated by its
involutions, and $[[x, u], u] = 1$ for every $x \in G$ and every involution $u \in G$, where
$[x, u] = x^{-1} u^{-1} x u$.

## Kourovka Problem 18.57 on the derived length of involution-generated 2-groups

The
proposition (Kourovka 18.57, D. V. Lytkina) that there is a natural number $d$ such that
$G^{(d)} = 1$ for every finite group $G$ in which every element has order a power of $2$, which is
generated by its involutions, and in which $[[x, u], u] = 1$ for every $x \in G$ and every
involution $u \in G$; here $[x, u] = x^{-1} u^{-1} x u$ and $G^{(d)}$ is the $d$-th term of the
derived series of $G$.

## Negative answer to Kourovka Problem 18.57

There is no natural number $d$ such that
$G^{(d)} = 1$ for every finite group $G$ in which every element has order a power of $2$, which is
generated by its involutions, and in which $[[x, u], u] = 1$ for every $x \in G$ and every
involution $u \in G$; here $[x, u] = x^{-1} u^{-1} x u$ and $G^{(d)}$ is the $d$-th term of the
derived series of $G$. Thus the derived length of such groups is unbounded.
