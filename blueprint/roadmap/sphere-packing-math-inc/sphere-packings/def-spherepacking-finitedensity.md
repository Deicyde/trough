# SpherePacking.finiteDensity

The *finite density* of a packing $\mathcal{P}$ is defined as

$$
\Delta_{\mathcal{P}}(R):=\frac{\mathrm{Vol}(\mathcal{P}\cap B_d(0,R))}{\mathrm{Vol}(B_d(0,R))},\quad R>0.
$$
