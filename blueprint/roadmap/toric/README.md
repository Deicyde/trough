# Toric varieties

The blueprint of Toric varieties, read at commit [5956b802](https://github.com/YaelDillies/toric/tree/5956b80249ec5416a266f68b5142606b060d7628) (2026-11-06): one article for each of its 66 nodes whose Lean declarations it names and marks formalized, or in Mathlib. Its other 79 nodes have no article, and a section of only those is left out: the map records what the project contains, not what it plans. A statement for which the blueprint marks no proof `\leanok` says so. Dependencies are the blueprint's `\uses`: a statement's under *Depends on*, a proof's under *Proof depends on*.

The text is the blueprint's, under its Apache-2.0 license: each page has a section's prose, remarks and examples in the blueprint's order, with each node's statement and proof where the node stands.

The presentation is inspired by (Cox et al. 2011), but we do not aim to follow it very closely. For example, the chapters below do not match those of (Cox et al. 2011).

- [Algebra](algebra/README.md)
- [Convex geometry](convex-geometry/README.md)
- [Scheme theory](scheme-theory/README.md)
- [Toric varieties](toric-varieties/README.md)
