# Finiteness of the coinvariant algebra

For a finite group acting on a finitely generated
module, the coinvariant algebra `Sym M ⧸ A₊ · Sym M` is a finite `R`-module: it is of finite type,
and generated by nilpotent, hence integral, elements.

```lean

  {G : Type u_2} {M : Type u_3} [CommRing R] [Group G] [Finite G] [AddCommMonoid M]
  [_root_.Module R M] (ρ : Representation R G M) [Module.Finite R M] :
  Module.Finite R (SymmetricAlgebra R M ⧸ ρ.symmetricAlgebraHilbertIdeal)
```
