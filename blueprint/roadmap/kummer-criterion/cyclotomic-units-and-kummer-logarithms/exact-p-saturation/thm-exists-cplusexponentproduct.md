# exists-CPlusExponentProduct

Every element of $C^+$ admits an exponent-product expression.

## Depends on

- [CPlusExponentProduct](def-cplusexponentproduct.md)

## Proof

Induction over membership in the subgroup generated by $-1$ and the $\varepsilon_a$: each generator trivially has such an expression, the identity is the empty product, and the set of elements admitting an expression is closed under multiplication (add the exponent vectors) and inverses (negate them).
