# derivative

Let $F$ be a quasimodular form. We define the (normalized) derivative of $F$ as

$$
F' = DF := \frac{1}{2\pi i} \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} z} F.\qquad\text{(34)}
$$
