> **Stacks 05WJ.** Some projective ideal of a commutative ring is not locally free as a
> module.

```lean
theorem exists_projective_ideal_not_locallyFree :
    ∃ (R : Type u) (_ : CommRing R) (I : Ideal R),
      Module.Projective R I ∧ ¬ Module.IsLocallyFree R I
```

## Tag 05WK, a lemma

[Tag 05WK](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05WK) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.Chow.chowClass_overProdOpens_section_ne_zero`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Degrees/ProductOfPuncturedSchemes.lean, line 455](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/AlgebraicGeometry/Chow/Degrees/ProductOfPuncturedSchemes.lean#L455).

> **Stacks 05WK.** Let the base be a situation for Chow homology with vanishing dimension
> function, the case of the spectrum of a field. Let `X i` be proper over the base, each with a
> section `P i` and a point `Q i` whose residue field has degree two over the base, and let `U i`
> be the complement of `Q i`. The sections assemble into a section of the product of the `U i` over
> the base, and the prime cycle of each of its points is not rationally equivalent to zero: the
> class of `P 1 × ⋯ × P n` is nonzero in the zeroth Chow group of `U 1 ×_S ⋯ ×_S U n`.

```lean
theorem chowClass_overProdOpens_section_ne_zero (C : ChowSituation.{u})
    (hδ : ∀ s : C.S, C.dimensionFunction s = 0) {n : ℕ} {X : Fin n → Scheme.{u}}
    (f : ∀ i, X i ⟶ C.S) [∀ i, IsProper (f i)] (P : ∀ i, C.S ⟶ X i)
    (hP : ∀ i, P i ≫ f i = 𝟙 C.S) (Q : ∀ i, X i) (hQ : ∀ i, (f i).residueDegree (Q i) = 2)
    (U : ∀ i, (X i).Opens) (hU : ∀ i, (U i : Set (X i)) = {Q i}ᶜ) :
    ∃ σ : C.S ⟶ (overProd fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i).left,
      σ ≫ (overProd fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i).hom = 𝟙 C.S ∧
        (∀ i, σ ≫ overProdπ (fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i) i ≫ (U i).ι = P i) ∧
          ∀ s₀ : C.S, ∃ α : CycleGroup C (overProd fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i).hom 0,
            α.1 = @Function.locallyFinsuppWithin.single _ _ ℤ
                (Classical.decEq (overProd fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i).left) _ (σ.base s₀) (1 : ℤ) ∧
              QuotientAddGroup.mk' (rationalEquivalences C
                (overProd fun i ↦ (U i).ι ≫ f i).hom 0) α ≠ 0
```

## Tag 05WL, a lemma

[Tag 05WL](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/05WL) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.Examples.exists_countable_projective_directSum_isFiniteLocallyFree_not_isLocallyFree`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/Examples/Algebra/CountableProjectiveNotLocallyFree.lean, line 319](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/5ed82d14634e8d9576bb844c7bd4e3da66d450e7/StacksFrontier/Examples/Algebra/CountableProjectiveNotLocallyFree.lean#L319). The attribute's comment: “the summands are finite locally free but NOT of rank one, which is the one way this is weaker than the tag; the countability of the ring and the direct-sum presentation are as the tag states them. The rank-one case is unreachable from this construction: the summands `εₙ R` localize to zero at every prime containing `εₙ`, a Boolean ring of 𝔽₂-sequences has no invertible ideals at all, and the mechanism that defeats local freeness here — every element of a localization of the direct sum is annihilated by a nonzero scalar — is incompatible with rank-one summands, which are faithful at every prime; by Bass's theorem on big projective modules a rank-one example needs a non-noetherian ring of unbounded dimension. It therefore requires the source's own second construction, `B_∞ = colim (A_1 ⊗_ℚ ⋯ ⊗_ℚ A_n)` with the invertible `L_i = Γ(U_i, ℒ_i)`, whose failure of local freeness is read off from the vanishing of higher Chern classes of a direct sum of invertible sheaves on a product of curves — the machinery of Stacks 02TA, 02TH, 02TJ and 02TM, of which only 02TH is available unconditionally in this repository”.

> **Stacks 05WL, with finite locally free summands in place of rank one.** There is a countable
> ring `R` and a projective `R`-module which is a direct sum of countably many finite locally free
> modules but is not itself locally free.
>
> The summands `εₙ R` have rank one at the primes avoiding `εₙ` and rank zero at the others, so
> they are finite locally free but not of rank one as the tag asks; the rank-one case belongs to the
> source's second construction and is not formalized here. See the implementation notes of
> `StacksFrontier.Examples.Algebra.CountableProjectiveNotLocallyFree`.

```lean
theorem exists_countable_projective_directSum_isFiniteLocallyFree_not_isLocallyFree :
    ∃ (R : Type u) (hR : CommRing R),
      letI : CommRing R := hR
      Countable R ∧
        ∃ L : ℕ → ModuleCat.{u} R,
          (∀ n, Module.IsFiniteLocallyFree R (L n)) ∧
            Module.Projective R (DirectSum ℕ fun n => L n) ∧
              ¬ Module.IsLocallyFree R (DirectSum ℕ fun n => L n)
```
