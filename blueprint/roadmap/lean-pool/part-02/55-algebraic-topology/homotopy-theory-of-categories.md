# Basic localizers and the homotopy theory of small categories

> Grothendieck's homotopy theory of small categories, after Pursuing Stacks and Cisinski. The classes W₀ (functors bijective on connected components) and W₁ (functors inducing an equivalence of fundamental groupoids) are compared, and a functor between groupoids is an equivalence exactly when it is bijective on connected components and on automorphism groups; fibrations of groupoids have an exact sequence of fibre, total groupoid and base. Basic localizers are defined, the minimal one W∞ is the intersection of all of them, W₀ and W₋₁ are basic localizers, Quillen's Theorem A holds for any basic localizer, and every functor with an adjoint lies in every basic localizer. For presheaves, the adjunction between the category-of-elements functor and its right adjoint is constructed. Direct categories are those with a well-founded relation of non-identity morphisms; those of finite ranks are characterised by direct Reedy structures with natural-number degrees, and a partial order is direct exactly when it is well-founded. Non-singular presheaves are those whose category of elements is thin, and categories of elements give discrete fibrations. In a cofibration category, Brown's factorization lemma holds between cofibrant objects. For a map of abelian groups d, the gr-category of two-term complexes has π₀ the cokernel and π₁ the kernel of d.
