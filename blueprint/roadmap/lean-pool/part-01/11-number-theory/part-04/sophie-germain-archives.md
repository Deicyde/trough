# Statements read in Sophie Germain's manuscripts

> Lean proofs of selected statements and modern consequences from three volumes of Sophie Germain's papers at the Bibliothèque nationale de France, digitised in Gallica (Français 9115, Français 9118, NAF 4073), following the modernised readings of the edition germain-project. In number theory, Manuscript C on 2z^{2n} = x^{2n} + y^{2n} (the statement for odd n > 1 fails at x = y = z = 1, solutions with coprime x and y are odd, a common divisor of x² + y² and Φ divides n, with n = 5, x = 1, y = 3 as a case where 5 divides both), the working notes on quadratic residues (every non-square integer is a non-residue of some prime, the false extension refuted by −5, −17, −21, 3 a primitive root of primes 12n + 5 with 3n + 1 prime, every non-residue of a Fermat prime a primitive root), the drafts to Gauss (Germain's generalisation of art. 357 deduced from it, the passage x ↦ −x, the first case of Fermat's equation for exponent p − 1), the primality of p⁴ + 4 only for p = 1 (by the Sophie Germain identity, which Mathlib has), the edition's list of reduced forms of determinant −295, and the note of uncertain authorship in NAF 4073 that the triangular number of a square is the sum of the squares of two consecutive triangular numbers, with its generalisations (T(mn), a triangular system for odd power sums, and Lagrange's S_3 = T_n² as the only case of S_p = T_n^q beyond S_1 = T_n, through Levi ben Gershon's 2^p + 1 = 3^q). In mechanics and geometry, Euler's 1779 memoir on the vibrating lamina and Germain's « Remarques » on it (the six end conditions and their determinants, the first root of cos ω cosh ω = 1, the lamina on three supports, the second family of § 51 shown to be a genuine mode against the « Remarques », the free lamina touched by a stylet), the plate equation of the memoir for the Institut, and Euler's theorem on normal sections, the mean curvature as the mean over all directions and the sum of inverse radii on the sphere. Also the Sophie Germain chapter of the edition's exercise book.
