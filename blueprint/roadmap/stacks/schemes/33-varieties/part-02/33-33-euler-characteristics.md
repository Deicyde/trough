```lean
public theorem eulerCharacteristic_add_of_shortExact (k : Type u) [Field k] {X : Scheme.{u}}
    (f : X ⟶ Spec (.of k)) [IsProper f] {S : ShortComplex X.Modules} (hS : S.ShortExact)
```

> **[Stacks, Tag 0AYT] (4): the Euler characteristic of a coherent module whose support has
> dimension at most zero is the dimension of its space of global sections.** For
> `f : X ⟶ Spec k` proper and `ℱ` coherent on `X` with `dim (supp ℱ) ≤ 0`,
>
>   `χ(X, ℱ) = dim_k H⁰(X, ℱ)`.
>
> This is the source's "we have (4) by definition of the Euler characteristic `χ(X, ℱ)` and (3)":
> `AlgebraicGeometry.eulerCharacteristic` is by definition the alternating sum
> `∑ⁿ (-1)ⁿ dim_k Hⁿ(X, ℱ)`, clause (3) kills every term with `n > 0`, and a sum supported on `{0}`
> is its value there.

```lean
theorem eulerCharacteristic_eq_finrank_cohomologyOverField_zero_of_supp_le_zero (k : Type u)
    [Field k] {X : Scheme.{u}} (f : X ⟶ Spec (.of k)) [IsProper f] (F : X.Modules)
    [F.IsCoherent] (hF : topologicalKrullDim (_root_.SheafOfModules.supp F) ≤ 0) :
    eulerCharacteristic k f F = (Module.finrank k (cohomologyOverField k f F 0) : ℤ)
```

> **The Euler characteristic is unchanged by extension of the ground field.** Let `k'/k` be an
> extension of fields, let `X` be a proper scheme over `k`, let `ℱ` be a coherent `𝒪_X`-module and
> let `ℱ'` be the pullback of `ℱ` to a base change `X'` of `X` to `k'`. Then
> `χ(X, ℱ) = χ(X', ℱ')`.

```lean
public theorem eulerCharacteristic_baseChange_field
    (h : IsPullback g' f' f (specMapAlgebraMap k k')) [IsProper f] (F : X.Modules)
```

> **[Stacks, Tag 0BEK].** Let `k` be a field, let `f : Y ⟶ X` be a morphism of schemes proper
> over `k` and let `G` be a coherent `𝒪_Y`-module. Then `χ(Y, G) = ∑ᵢ (-1)ⁱ χ(X, Rⁱf_*G)`, the
> sum being over the nonnegative degrees, with no hypothesis.
>
> The coherence of `Rⁱf_*G` that the right-hand side needs in order to name `χ(X, Rⁱf_*G)` is
> [Stacks, Tag 02O5], in the form
> `AlgebraicGeometry.ProperCoherenceWitness.higherDirectImageIsCoherent`.

```lean
public theorem eulerCharacteristic_eq_finsum_higherDirectImage
    (k : Type u) [Field k] {Y X : Scheme.{u}}
    (f : Y ⟶ X) (g : X ⟶ Spec (.of k))
    [IsProper g] [IsProper (f ≫ g)]
    (G : Y.Modules) [G.IsCoherent] :
    eulerCharacteristic k (f ≫ g) G =
      ∑ᶠ i : ℕ, (-1 : ℤ) ^ i *
        @eulerCharacteristic k _ X g _
          (((Scheme.Modules.pushforward f).rightDerivedPlus (i : ℤ)).obj G)
          (ProperCoherenceWitness.higherDirectImageIsCoherent Y X f
            (LocallyOfFiniteType.isLocallyNoetherian g) (IsProper.of_comp f g) G
            inferInstance i)
```
