## Tag 00WV, a definition

[Tag 00WV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00WV) in the Stacks Project.

**`Stacks.Sites.Site.IsContinuous`**, a structure in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/FamilySite.lean, line 128](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/FamilySite.lean#L128).

> A functor `u : C ⥤ D` between sites is *continuous* (Stacks 00WV) if for every covering
> `{V_i → V}_{i ∈ I}` of `C`
> 1. the family `{u(V_i) → u(V)}_{i ∈ I}` is a covering of `D`, and
> 2. for every morphism `T → V` of `C` the morphism `u(T ×_V V_i) → u(T) ×_{u(V)} u(V_i)` is an
>    isomorphism.
>
> The fibre products in clause (2) exist by axiom (3) of `C`, resp. of `D` applied to the covering
> of clause (1).

```lean
structure IsContinuous (SC : Site.{w} C) (SD : Site.{w} D) (u : C ⥤ D) : Prop
```

## Tag 00WW, a lemma

[Tag 00WW](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00WW) in the Stacks Project.

**`CategoryTheory.Functor.isSheafFor_op_comp_of_preservesLimit_cospan`**, a theorem in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous/SheafRestriction.lean, line 59](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Continuous/SheafRestriction.lean#L59). The attribute's comment: “u^p of a sheaf is a sheaf, for the sheaf condition on the coverings of the sites”.

> **Stacks 00WW.** Let `J` and `K` be precoverages on `C` and `D` such that the fibre products
> `T ×_V Vᵢ` of a `J`-covering `{Vᵢ ⟶ V}` with any `T ⟶ V` exist (axiom (3) of a site, Stacks
> 00VH), and let `u : C ⥤ D` be continuous in the sense of Stacks 00WV:
> `hcov` says that the image `{u(Vᵢ) ⟶ u(V)}` of a `J`-covering is a `K`-covering, and `hpb` that
> `u` carries each fibre product `T ×_V Vᵢ` to a fibre product. If a presheaf of sets `F` on `D`
> satisfies the sheaf condition for every `K`-covering, then `u^p F = u.op ⋙ F` satisfies it for
> every `J`-covering.
>
> The remaining axioms of a site (isomorphisms and composites of coverings are coverings, and
> coverings are stable under base change) are not used, so they are not assumed.

```lean
theorem isSheafFor_op_comp_of_preservesLimit_cospan (J : Precoverage C) (K : Precoverage D)
    [J.HasPullbacks] (u : C ⥤ D)
    (hcov : ∀ ⦃V : C⦄ ⦃R : Presieve V⦄, R ∈ J V → R.map u ∈ K (u.obj V))
    (hpb : ∀ ⦃V : C⦄ ⦃R : Presieve V⦄, R ∈ J V → ∀ ⦃Vᵢ : C⦄ ⦃f : Vᵢ ⟶ V⦄, R f →
      ∀ ⦃T : C⦄ (g : T ⟶ V), PreservesLimit (cospan g f) u)
    (F : Dᵒᵖ ⥤ Type w) (hF : ∀ ⦃U : D⦄ (S : Presieve U), S ∈ K U → Presieve.IsSheafFor F S)
    ⦃V : C⦄ (R : Presieve V) (hR : R ∈ J V) : Presieve.IsSheafFor (u.op ⋙ F) R
```

> **Stacks 00WW**, for the Grothendieck topologies generated by the coverings. Let `J` and `K`
> be precoverages on `C` and `D` whose coverings have fibre products with arbitrary morphisms and
> are stable under base change (axiom (3) of a site, Stacks 00VH), and let `u : C ⥤ D` be
> continuous in the sense of Stacks 00WV (`hcov`, `hpb`, as in
> `CategoryTheory.Functor.isSheafFor_op_comp_of_preservesLimit_cospan`). Then restriction along `u`
> carries sheaves for the topology generated by `K` to sheaves for the topology generated by `J`,
> that is, `u` is `CategoryTheory.Functor.IsContinuous`.

```lean
theorem isContinuous_toGrothendieck_of_preservesLimit_cospan (J : Precoverage C)
    (K : Precoverage D) [J.IsStableUnderBaseChange] [J.HasPullbacks] [K.IsStableUnderBaseChange]
    [K.HasPullbacks] (u : C ⥤ D)
    (hcov : ∀ ⦃V : C⦄ ⦃R : Presieve V⦄, R ∈ J V → R.map u ∈ K (u.obj V))
    (hpb : ∀ ⦃V : C⦄ ⦃R : Presieve V⦄, R ∈ J V → ∀ ⦃Vᵢ : C⦄ ⦃f : Vᵢ ⟶ V⦄, R f →
```

**`CategoryTheory.Functor.lanCompPresheafToSheafIsoSheafPullback`**, a def in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Pullback.lean, line 80](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/Pullback.lean#L80).

> **Stacks 00WY**.
> For a continuous functor `u : C ⥤ D` between sites and a presheaf `G` on `C`,
> `(u_p G)^# ≅ (u_p (G^#))^# = u_s (G^#)`, naturally in `G`: the composite of
> `u_p = u.op.lan` with sheafification on `D` is isomorphic to sheafification on `C` followed by
> the sheaf pullback `u_s = u.sheafPullback`. The right adjointness of `u^s` needed to form
> `u.sheafPullback` is Mathlib's instance built from `u_p` and sheafification on `D`, so it is
> not a hypothesis here.

```lean
noncomputable def lanCompPresheafToSheafIsoSheafPullback
    {C : Type u₁} [Category.{v₁} C] {D : Type u₂} [Category.{v₂} D] (A : Type u₃)
    [Category.{v₃} A] (J : GrothendieckTopology C) (K : GrothendieckTopology D) (u : C ⥤ D)
    [u.IsContinuous J K] [HasWeakSheafify J A] [HasWeakSheafify K A]
    [∀ F : Cᵒᵖ ⥤ A, u.op.HasLeftKanExtension F] :
    u.op.lan ⋙ presheafToSheaf K A ≅ presheafToSheaf J A ⋙ u.sheafPullback A J K
```

## Tag 00WZ, a remark

**`QuasiMorphismOfSites.IsQuasiContinuousPrecoverage`**, a def in StacksFrontier (drafted, not yet reviewed), declared in [StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/MorphismOfSites/QuasiMorphism.lean, line 397](https://github.com/AxiomMath/Stacks/blob/c496df0d9beea9deaeabcea8492605f9a0a2313b/StacksFrontier/CategoryTheory/Sites/MorphismOfSites/QuasiMorphism.lean#L397). The attribute's comment: “the definition of quasi-continuous, for sites given by precoverages”.

> **Stacks 00WZ (definition).** A functor `u : C ⥤ D` between sites, presented by precoverages
> `JC` and `JD`, is *quasi-continuous* if for every covering `𝒱 = {V_i → V}` of `C`:
> * (1') the image family `{u(V_i) → u(V)}` is tautologically equivalent to a covering of `D`; and
> * (2) for every morphism `T → V` of `C` the canonical morphism
>   `u(T ×_V V_i) → u(T) ×_{u(V)} u(V_i)` is an isomorphism, i.e. `u` carries the fibre square
>   `T ×_V V_i` (which exists when `JC` has pullbacks along coverings) to a fibre square of `D`.
>
> Unlike `IsQuasiContinuous`, nothing here asks `C` or `D` to have all fibre products; the two
> notions agree for pretopologies on categories with fibre products
> (`isQuasiContinuousPrecoverage_toPrecoverage_iff`).

```lean
def IsQuasiContinuousPrecoverage {C : Type u} [Category.{v} C] {D : Type u} [Category.{v} D]
    (u : C ⥤ D) (JC : Precoverage C) (JD : Precoverage D) : Prop
```
