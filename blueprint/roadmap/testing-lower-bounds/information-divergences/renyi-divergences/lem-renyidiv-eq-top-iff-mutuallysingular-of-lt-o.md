# renyiDiv_eq_top_iff_mutuallySingular_of_lt_one

For $\alpha \in [0, 1)$ and finite measures $\mu, \nu$,

$$
R_\alpha(\mu, \nu) = \infty \iff \mu \perp \nu \: .
$$
