# Topology

387 results of ATLAS's vault, 325 proved and 62 stated but not proved, in this subject's topics.

Topology studies continuity, open sets, compactness, connectedness, and invariants preserved by homeomorphism.

[Knot theory](knot-theory/README.md) is the largest field, with nearly half of the subject's articles. It starts from
[knots, links and ambient isotopy](knot-theory/knots-links-and-ambient-isotopy.md) and
[knot diagrams](knot-theory/knot-diagrams.md), and goes on to geometric questions:
[ropelength and crossing number](knot-theory/ropelength-and-crossing-number.md), the
[distortion of knots](knot-theory/distortion-of-knots.md) and the
[quadrisecant theorem](knot-theory/quadrisecant-theorem.md), with [slice knots](knot-theory/slice-knots.md)
on the four-dimensional side.

[General topology](general-topology/README.md) covers dimension theory and the limits and products that build new
spaces, and [descriptive set theory](descriptive-set-theory/README.md) characterizes Baire space, Cantor space and the
rationals and studies the [Banach–Mazur game](descriptive-set-theory/banach-mazur-game.md).
[Algebraic topology](algebraic-topology/README.md) includes the
[Hurewicz theorem](algebraic-topology/hurewicz-theorem.md) in degree one and the
[homotopy groups of spheres](algebraic-topology/homotopy-groups-of-spheres.md), and
[manifolds and geometric topology](manifolds-geometric-topology/README.md) runs from the
[classification of surfaces](manifolds-geometric-topology/classification-of-surfaces.md) to the
[Poincaré conjecture](manifolds-geometric-topology/poincar-conjecture.md) in dimensions three, four and at least
five and the [annulus theorem](manifolds-geometric-topology/annulus-theorem.md) in dimensions at least four. The
[Hilbert cube](continua-infinite-dimensional/hilbert-cube.md) and
[absolute retracts](continua-infinite-dimensional/absolute-retracts.md) belong to continuum theory and
infinite-dimensional topology.
