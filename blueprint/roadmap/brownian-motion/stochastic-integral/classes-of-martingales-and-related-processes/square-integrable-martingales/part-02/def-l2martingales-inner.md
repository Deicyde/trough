# L2Martingales.inner

We define an inner product on $\mathcal{M}^2$ by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\langle M, N \rangle_{\mathcal{M}^2} = \mathbb{E}[M_\infty N_\infty]
\: .
\end{aligned}
$$
